Legend:
- **Official** = formally recognized generation/unit/branch by COVER Corp.
- **Fan** = widely recognized pair/unit that fans colloquially use.

- **[R]** = Retired/Graduated member.
- **[A]** = Affiliate member (streaming activities concluded, still affiliated).
- **[D]** = Defunct group/unit.
