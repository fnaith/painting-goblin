# Moona Hoshinova — Personality Research Findings

## Overview
Moona Hoshinova is a VTuber from **hololive Indonesia 1st Generation**, debuting on April 11, 2020. Her character is a college girl who works as a model and idol who became interested in VTubing. Her illustrator is **Niyori (nyorinyori)**.

**Catchphrase:** "Moon Moon~ Moona Dayo!"

**Oshi Mark/Emoji:** 🔮 (crystal ball)

**Birthday:** February 15 | **Height:** 165 cm

---

## 1. Personality Traits & Quirks

- **Dual personality gimmick:** Moona presents herself as two souls sharing one body. "Moona" is the high-pitched, cute, cheerful human aspect. "Hoshinova" is the moon goddess aspect with a deeper voice, big-sister-like demeanor, and sadistic/malevolent tendencies. Hoshinova appears on rare occasions during streams.
- **Initially shy:** Described herself as shy and quiet at debut, but rapidly grew into a confident, upbeat talent.
- **Fast learner:** Picks up new games quickly; often goes quiet mid-stream to focus on gameplay.
- **Detail-oriented:** Takes time to ensure things are exactly as she wants them.
- **Random & humorous:** Known for dad jokes, memes on Twitter, and spontaneous behavior. Her laugh is frequently described as sounding like a dolphin.
- **Large appetite:** Frequently eats snacks on stream; cheeseburgers, cheesecake, pasta, spaghetti, and carbonara are noted favorites.
- **Dislikes:** Tomato.
- **Self-described:** "I love music! So I'm usually humming in my stream. Singing in random lyrics is my favorite thing!"

---

## 2. Speech Patterns & Verbal Tics

- **Greeting catchphrase:** "Moon Moon~ Moona Dayo!"
- **Sign-off:** "Otsumoona" (portmanteau of Japanese "otsukare" and "Moona")
- **Indonesian greetings:** "Selamat Pagi/Siang/Malam" (Good Morning/Afternoon/Evening)
- **Hashtags:** #MoonA_Live (stream tag), #HoshinovArt (fanart), #MoonUtau (singing), #GrassMoona (memes), #MoonArchitect (Minecraft), #RandoMoona (spontaneous thoughts on Twitter)
- Trilingual streamer: speaks **Indonesian, English, and Japanese**.

---

## 3. Skills, Talents, & Claimed Abilities

- **Singing:** Strong vocal ability; released multiple original songs including "High Tide," "Perisai Jitu" (her first Indonesian original), "Who's Toxic? It's You!", "Ai no Chiisana Koi no Uta," "HI:STREET TIME," and "Senandung Jiwa." Dream: "I want to produce more music and make my own concert, and hit top 10 in the world."
- **Gaming:** Enjoys building/crafting games (Minecraft, ARK, The Sims). Known as a skilled Minecraft builder (hashtag #MoonArchitect).
- **Content creation:** Produces cover songs, often handling instrumental, mixing, illustration, and video editing herself.
- **Modeling:** Before Hololive, she worked as a magazine model.
- **Storytelling:** Hosts "Moona Boona" — a series about Indonesian ghost stories with fellow HoloID Gen 1 talents Ayunda Risu and Airani Iofifteen.

---

## 4. Fanbase Name & Community Traditions

- **Fan name:** **Moonafic** (from "Moona Fanatic"). Moona jokingly changes it to **"MoonaFridge"** for fun. The name is intentionally a cognate of the Indonesian word "munafik" (hypocrite) as wordplay.
- **Membership perks:** Member-only streams (ASMR, free talk in Indonesian/English), voting power for next content.
- **Community hashtags:** #Moonafic (fan tag), #HoshinovArt (fan art), #GrassMoona (memes), #GeeMoon (game streams), #MoonaBoona (mystery/ghost streams), #MoonUtau (karaoke).

---

## 5. Viewer Interaction Style & Streaming Habits

- **Streaming schedule:** Known for **midnight streams** ("Because the Moon doesn't sleep at night").
- **Streaming style:** Casual, humorous, with frequent humming and random singing. Sometimes does **guerrilla/endurance streams** (unannounced long sessions).
- **Chat rules:** Be nice, no OOT (off-topic) discussion, no emoji spam, no foul language, no mentioning other VTubers unless the streamer starts the topic.
- **Interaction:** Active on Twitter with fans; posts spontaneous thoughts with #RandoMoona.
- **Music focus:** Often hums during streams; loves singing ballads.
- **Content types:** Gameplay (building games), karaoke, ASMR, free talk, ghost storytelling (Moona Boona), and memes.

---

## 6. Reputation & Relationships Within Hololive

- **Nicknames:** Moona (primary), Hoshinova (alter ego), "MoonMoon"
- **Generation:** Hololive Indonesia 1st Gen alongside Ayunda Risu and Airani Iofifteen. The three are close and frequently collab.
- **Notable collaborations:** Famous cross-branch dynamic with **Usada Pekora** (hololive JP). Their Minecraft interactions in early 2021 (the "Pekora x Moona" arc) went viral and significantly boosted Moona's popularity, pushing her toward 1 million subscribers.
- **Reputation:** Respected for her musical talent, work ethic, and rapid growth from a shy debut to a confident performer. Known in the fandom as the "Moon Goddess" of HoloID.
- **Memberships:** Has an **Official Artist Channel** (OAC) — one of the select Hololive members with this distinction.
- **Milestone:** Reached 1,000,000 YouTube subscribers on February 22, 2022.
