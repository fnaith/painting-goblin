# Moona Hoshinova — Official Setting & Character Design Research

## Overview

Moona Hoshinova (ムーナ・ホシノヴァ) is a Virtual YouTuber under **Hololive Indonesia Generation 1** (unit name: **AREA 15**), managed by Cover Corporation's Hololive Production. She debuted on **April 11, 2020** alongside Ayunda Risu and Airani Iofifteen as part of the inaugural Hololive ID generation.

## Official Lore / Background Story

According to the Hololive official website:

> A college girl who works as a model and idol, but later got interested to become a VTuber too.

Her in-character persona has a dual-personality mechanic:
- **Moona** — High-pitched, child-like voice; the "idol" personality.
- **Hoshinova** — Deeper, older-sister-like voice with sadistic tendencies; described as Moona's "another personality."

This is acknowledged in her official Q&A:
> "Why you sometimes have a different voice? — Because there are 2 people in this body, Moona and Hoshinova. If you heard a high pitch like a kids, probably that's Moona. But if you heard a low pitch like a big sister, probably that's Hoshinova."

## Character / Model Design

### Visual Theme
- **Primary colors:** White, blue, and silver (moon-inspired palette)
- **Outfit:** A sleek white and blue asymmetrical dress with starry/moon motifs, thigh-high boots, and a large ribbon/choker accessory
- **Hair:** Long silver-white hair with blue gradient tips, styled with a side braid
- **Eye color:** Blue/purple
- **Accessories:** A crescent moon hair ornament, star-shaped accessories, a choker
- **Overall theme:** Celestial / moon goddess / space idol aesthetic

### Model Variants
- Original 2D Live model (debut)
- 3D model (revealed December 2020)
- Various costume variants released over time

### Illustrator
Her character design was created by **Nyori** (Nyorinyori), referred to as "Nyori Mama" by Moona.

## Catchphrases & Branding

### Catchphrase / Tagline
> **"Moon Moon~ Moona Dayo!"**

This is her official greeting/tagline displayed on the Hololive website.

### Intro / Outro Phrases
- Her streams typically begin with her signature "Moon Moon~ Moona Dayo!" greeting.
- She often uses Indonesian and English interchangeably in her streams.

### Channel Branding
- Channel name: **Moona Hoshinova hololive-ID**
- Her channel banner and emotes heavily feature moon and star imagery.
- Her membership is named after her fan base "Moonafic."

## Stream Content & Personality

### Regular/Specialty Streams (per official website)
- Loves playing games, especially building games (notably Minecraft — hashtag #MoonArchitect).
- Enjoys singing ballads.
- Enjoys making dad jokes and memes on Twitter.
- Described as "pretty random."
- Her laugh is compared to a dolphin (self-acknowledged).

### Official Q&A Highlights
- **Comedian or Idol?** "No, I'm an Idol."
- **Why midnight streams?** "Because the Moon doesn't sleep at night."
- **Schedule adherence?** "Not exactly, sometimes I do a guerilla… endurance."
- **Fan name origin:** Moonafic, from "Moona Fanatic." Jokingly also called "MoonaFridge."
- **Penguin plushie:** Named Pororo — "you can see it but you can't touch it. He's mine."

## Dream / Ambition

> "I want to produce more music and making my own concert, it would be great if my music can hit top 10 in the world!"

## Notable Facts

- Moona and Hoshinova are presented as two personalities sharing one body — a distinctive aspect of her character.
- She is part of the well-known Hololive ID x JP collaboration group with Usada Pekora (the "PekoMoona" dynamic).
- She gained significant international visibility through her interactions with Hololive JP members and participation in Hololive Minecraft server events.
- She has released original songs including "High Tide," "Perisai Jitu," "Who's Toxic? It's You!," "Senandung Jiwa," and the album "ORBITURE."
