## 1. Personality Traits & General Demeanor

- **Embodiment of Chaos**: Lore describes her as the concept of Chaos itself, birthed by the world. Her catchphrase "WAZZUP!! It's your worldwide Rat Idol --- Hakos Baelz!" and the descriptor "Chaos. Mayhem. Entropy... All in the form of a cute little rat" set the tone.
- **Reverse-expectation "Normal"**: Despite her chaotic lore, Baelz is described as a "reverse-case Watson Amelia" — she initially appeared incredibly chaotic (rivaling Kureiji Ollie in early weeks) but turned out to be the most "normal" member of Council, often becoming the victim of her genmates' antics.
- **Bright & Loud, but Introverted**: Her on-stream persona is bright, loud, and high-energy, yet this contrasts with an introverted personality off-stream. This manifests in awkward/shy interactions with other Hololive and Holostars members.
- **Australian Pride**: Frequently demonstrates pride in being Australian — she introduced the "BAE-GEMITE" segment where she makes other Hololive members taste Vegemite.
- **"Zoomer" Knowledge**: Has limited pop culture reference knowledge, earning the nickname "zoomer" from fans, considered the opposite of "old boomer" Noir Vesper.
- **Chaotic Couple with IRyS**: Shares a well-known "on-again, off-again" relationship with IRyS — they frequently "divorce" and "remarry" depending on their mood. They share many tastes and opinions but also dispute often.

## 4. Known Quirks, Habits & Recurring Behaviors

- **Extreme Fear of Horror**: Has a well-documented, extreme weakness to horror games. Paranormal elements particularly terrify her. Notable: her Outlast stream where she cried and "couldn't feel her left arm." Despite this, she cleared Resident Evil Village with Ceres Fauna watching for support. She loves FNAF lore (binges Game Theory videos) but refuses to play the games.
- **Arachnophobia**: Expressed strong discomfort with spiders; notably called out Haachama's habit of eating spiders while in Australia.
- **Upside-Down Text Writing**: A signature quirk — flipping text vertically as a nod to Australia.
- **Febaerary**: Celebrated her birthday (Feb 29 — a leap day birthday) for the entire month of February in 2022.
- **Coffee Enthusiast**: Loves coffee and has expressed desire to do coffee review streams and create her own coffee drink.
- **Fried Cheese Lover**: Favorite food is fried cheese.
- **Console Gamer**: Prefers console gaming over PC because "there are fewer buttons to press."
- **Music Taste**: Loves anime songs, J-pop, K-pop, hardstyle, and EDM.
- **Anime Preferences**: Her **favorite anime** is *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood*. Her **top 3 anime duos** are:
  1. Izaya & Shizuo (*Durarara*)
  2. Edward & Alphonse Elric (*Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood* — her all-time favorite)
  3. Tanya & X (*Youjo Senki*)
- **Rat Cheese Association**: Fans spam 🧀🧀🧀 in chat while waiting for streams, referencing mice/rat-cheese myths.

## 6. Energy Level & Entertainment Style

- **Energy Level**: Very high. Baelz brings loud, bouncy, chaotic energy to streams, often ratcheting up the excitement rapidly.
- **Entertainment Style**:
  - Combines over-the-top dramatic introductions with genuine, vulnerable moments.
  - Self-deprecating humor about being "the most normal one" caught in chaos.
  - Karaoke streams are a major draw — singing and dancing are listed as her favorite weekend activities.
  - Horror game streams become comedy gold due to her extreme fear reactions.
  - "BAE-GEMITE" tasting segments showcase her Australian pride.
- **Collab Dynamic**: Often plays the straight~~man~~rat or victim in collabs with other chaotic members. Her relationship with IRyS (the "BaeRyS" dynamic) is a recurring entertainment pillar.
- **Watches Most (non-EN)**: Apart from hololive EN members, she watches **Oozora Subaru**, **Yukoku Roberu**, and **Houshou Marine** most frequently.
- **Achievements**: Debuted August 23, 2021. Reached 1 million subscribers on December 12, 2024.
- **Goals & Dreams**:
  - Dance in 3D (achieved at hololive 4th fes. 2023)
  - Attend a live voice actor event
  - Do many collaboration streams
  - Voice act in an anime
  - Create her own coffee drink
- **Creator Notes**: The instrumental for her debut original song **"PLAY DICE!"** was composed by **Camellia**. She also has an **official artist YouTube channel** for posting covers and original music.
