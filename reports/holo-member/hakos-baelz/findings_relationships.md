# Hakos Baelz — Notable Relationships & Collaborations

## 1. Hololive English -Council- (Original Generation Group)

Baelz debuted on **August 23, 2021** as a member of **hololive English -Council-**, the second generation of Hololive English. She was designated as the **Chairperson of the Council** (the embodiment of Chaos), alongside:

| Member | Concept | Role Description |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Ceres Fauna** | Nature | Keeper of "Nature" — calm, motherly presence |
| **Ouro Kronii** | Time | Warden of "Time" — perfectionist, often tsundere |
| **Nanashi Mumei** | Civilization | Keeper of "Civilization" — forgetful, soft-spoken |
| **Tsukumo Sana** | Space | Speaker of "Space" — chaotic tall energy (graduated in 2022) |

**Council Group Dynamics:**
- Baelz was appointed Chairperson by the Gods but takes a "hands-off approach," much like her fellow members who have no intention of contending with each other.
- Despite being the embodiment of Chaos, Baelz is ironically the **most "normal" member of the group**, often becoming the **victim of her genmates' antics** and getting stuck in the middle of their chaotic interactions.
- The Council members participated in a panel at **Anime NYC 2021** together.
- On **July 20, 2022**, the Council (plus IRyS) revealed "Beeg Smol" 3D VR models, with Baelz's VR model designed by **Tsukumo Sana**.
- The Council released a group cover of **"Hikaru Nara"** and participated in various group collabs.

---

## 2. Hololive English -Promise- (Post-Council Unit)

After **Tsukumo Sana's graduation** in 2022, the remaining Council members (Fauna, Kronii, Mumei, Baelz) were merged with **IRyS** (who debuted earlier under "Project: Hope") to form the new unit **hololive English -Promise-** on **October 9, 2023**.

**Key Promise Group Activities:**
- Released the original song **"Connect the World"** (June 17, 2023) as a group
- Released **"Our Promise"** (June 18, 2024) — the unit's theme song
- Performed together at **hololive 4th fes. "Our Bright Parade"** (March 2023), where Baelz debuted her main 3D costume and idol 3D costume
- **"ENigmatic Recollection"** series — a multi-chapter group TTRPG campaign (Chapters 1-3)
- Collaborated on **hololive English concerts** (1st Concert "Connect the World," 2nd Concert "Breaking Dimensions," 3rd Concert "All for One")

## 3. Key Duo / Pairing Dynamics

### BAERyS (Baelz × IRyS)
- The most prominent and chaotic duo pairing in Hololive English.
- Described as being in a "chaotic couple relationship" — they share many tastes and opinions but also dispute frequently.
- Running gag: Baelz and IRyS are constantly "**divorcing**" and "**remarrying**" depending on their mood.
- Known for their high-energy banter and bickering that fans affectionately follow.
- Baelz and IRyS are often grouped together as the "VSinger + Rat" chaotic energy duo.

### Baelz × Ouro Kronii
- Kronii mixed Baelz's "Happy Halloween" cover, showing a collaborative creative relationship.
- Often featured together in Council/Promise group dynamics with a "time vs chaos" thematic contrast.
- Known for teasing each other; Kronii's perfectionist nature clashes humorously with Baelz's chaotic energy.

### Baelz × Ceres Fauna
- Fauna served as emotional support during horror game streams.
- Baelz accompanied Fauna during her playthrough of *Resident Evil 7* in exchange for Fauna watching Baelz clear *Resident Evil Village*.
- Fauna's calm, motherly "Nature" persona complements/contrasts with Baelz's "Chaos" persona.

### Baelz × Nanashi Mumei
- Fellow Council/Promise member with a "Civilization vs Chaos" thematic dynamic.
- Mumei's soft-spoken, forgetful nature contrasts with Baelz's loud chaotic energy.

### Baelz × Tsukumo Sana (Graduated)
- Sana designed Baelz's VR model, showing a close creative bond.
- Sana's "Space" concept and Baelz's "Chaos" concept were thematically linked as fundamental forces.

## 6. Running Jokes & Memorable Bits

- **"Divorcing and remarrying" IRyS** — The ongoing bit where Baelz and IRyS cycle through couple-status changes.
- **"Febaerary"** — Baelz celebrated her February 29 birthday for the entire month of February 2022.
- **"Rat" / "Chaos Rat"** — Her primary fan identity; fans are called "Brats" (a portmanteau of "rat" and "brat").
- **Australian pride — Vegemite** — Baelz frequently promotes Australian culture, most notably through her "BAE-GEMITE" taste-test program.
- **"Zoomer" knowledge gap** — Baelz doesn't get many pop culture references, leading her to be called a "zoomer" (opposite of Noir Vesper's "boomer").
- **Upside-down text** — Her first tweet was "dnssɐʍ" (wassup), a nod to being from Australia "the land down under."
- **Horror game weakness** — She is famously terrified of horror games (especially paranormal elements), leading to memorable panic-filled streams.
