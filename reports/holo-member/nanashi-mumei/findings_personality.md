## Appearance & Design

- Designed by illustrator **azure**
- Predominantly white, brown, and earth-tone color palette
- Large floppy **brown beret** with ear/flap details
- Loose-fitting hooded **poncho/cape** in cream/off-white with brown trim
- Brown corset/bodice with earthy green accents
- Dark brown shorts or leggings
- Tall brown boots
- A **feather quill** tucked behind her ear — referencing her role as a guardian of civilization who records history
- Owl motif throughout (she is a traveling owl personification)

- **Casual Outfit (Casual Clothes)** — modern streetwear style; a cropped jacket, skirt, and legwear

### Hairstyle
- Long **dark brownish-black hair** with lighter brown highlights

## Voice Characteristics & Speaking Style
- **Soft, gentle, airy voice** — often described as "fluffy" or "cozy"
- Speaks with a warm, conversational tone; can shift to an energetic higher pitch
- Known for "gremlin mode" — when excited or amused, her voice rises in pitch and speed, becoming chaotic
- Has a distinct **laugh** — often a high-pitched "hehehe" or sudden giggles
- Voice can drop dramatically when switching to her "dark side" persona — becomes deeper, more measured, with a cold/creepy edge
- Frequently pauses mid-sentence when she forgets what she was saying (playing into her forgetful character)
- Tends to trail off or restart sentences
- Uses the catchphrase greeting: **"Oh hi! Hoo's this? Nanashi Mumei!"**

## Personality Traits

### Core Personality (Gentle / Airheaded)
- Described as a **gentle, lovely girl** who relishes everyday life (per official bio)
- **Extremely forgetful** — constantly forgets what she was saying, what she's doing, or even her own name. "Mumei" literally means "nameless" in Japanese, and she leans into this heavily
- **"Fluffy" and relaxed** — has a calming, comfy energy in most streams
- **Curious and intellectual** — loves "obtaining knowledge" (listed as a like); she's a history-themed character ("Guardian of Civilization")
- **Clumsy** in a charming way
- **Friendly and open** — wants to connect with viewers ("Hoomans")

### The "Dark Side" Persona
- Mumei is known for a **sharp contrast** between her usual gentle self and sudden **disturbing, horrifying moments**
- References the fact that as the Guardian of Civilization, she has witnessed the **rise and fall of countless civilizations** — including all the war, bloodshed, and atrocity
- "Dark Mumei" or "Civilization Mumei" speaks in a **cold, deadpan, emotionless tone** or with a **sinister cheerfulness**
- Famous moments include:
  - Describing in vivid detail how civilizations destroy each other, with a cheerful smile
  - Monologues about the futility of human achievement
  - Jokes about "collecting" things in ways that sound like genocide
  - During horror game streams, she often becomes eerily calm rather than scared
- This duality is one of her most defining and popular traits — the adorable girl who can switch to a chilling, ancient being in an instant
- The community often marks this with chat spam like "oh no" or "there she goes" when the dark side emerges

### Other Quirks
- Has a **love for vegetable broth** (listed as an official like — somewhat unusual/meme-worthy)
- **Hates printers** — a running joke; she has expressed genuine frustration with printers on stream
- **Hates mud and mosquitoes** (official dislikes)
- Enjoys **drawing** (often shares her own art on stream)
- Enjoys **singing** — has a warm, gentle singing voice; released multiple original songs
- Enjoys **gaming** — plays a variety of games but is notably bad at some (part of her charm)

### Health & Lifestyle Details

- **Sleep issues**: Mumei is a light sleeper and mentioned it had been "decades" since she last felt rested. She relies on melatonin supplements.
- **Back/neck pain**: Suffers from shoulder and upper-back pain due to poor posture. Visited a chiropractor for treatment.
- **Blood tests**: Takes yearly blood tests that consistently show vitamin deficiencies.
- **Driving**: Drives a car. Famously reversed into a McDonald's fence — an incident that became a running joke.
- **Education**: Has a GPA of approximately 3.6. Claims she returned to school "every century," from kindergarten through high school.
- **Eyesight**: Despite being an owl, she has poor eyesight and wears contact lenses.

### Owl Physiology (Lore/In-Universe)
- **Head rotation**: Can rotate her head 270 degrees in owl form.
- **Feather trophies**: The feathers on her head are canonically "trophies" from past conquests.
- **Century reset**: Undergoes a "reset" every 100 years, which contributes to her memory loss.

### Equipment / Streaming Setup
- **PC**: Custom-built, ~$3000 — RTX 3070 Ti, Ryzen 9 5900X, 32GB RAM
- **Microphones**: Evolved from Blue Yeti → RØDE NT1-A → Shure SM7B
- **Keyboard**: Started with blue mechanical switches, later switched to red switches for quieter operation

## Streaming Style & Archetype

### Archetype
- **"Fluffy Friend" / "Healing" VTuber** — her baseline is calm, relaxing, nurturing; she makes viewers feel safe
- **"Horrifying Ancient Being"** — the dark-side switch adds a deep lore/creepypasta layer
- **"Comfy Gremlin"** — when excited, she becomes chaotic, loud, and funny
- **Artist VTuber** — frequently draws on stream (drawing streams are common)
- **Karaoke/ASMR adjacent** — known for soft singing voices; some streams are purely chill/karaoke

### Content Mix
- **Gaming streams:** Minecraft (on the Hololive EN server), horror games, party games with friends
- **Drawing/Art streams:** Lives where she creates art, often while chatting
- **Karaoke/Singing streams:** Covers, original songs, fan requests
- **Chill / "Just Chatting" streams:** Talking with Hoomans, answering questions, sharing stories
- **Collaborations:** Regularly collabs with other Hololive EN members (Mori Calliope, Takanashi Kiara, Ninomae Ina'nis, Gawr Gura, Watson Amelia, IRyS, Ouro Kronii, Hakos Baelz, Ceres Fauna, etc.)
- **Zatsudan (free talk) streams:** Casual conversations with fans

### Notable Characteristics
- Uses **owl emojis (🦉)** and **brown heart (🤎)** frequently
- Her background motif heavily features **feathers, history books, quills, and owl imagery**
- Has a tendency to **zone out** or get distracted mid-stream
- Genuinely emotional and caring — has cried thanking fans
- Has a surprisingly **good singing voice** with a warm, airy tone

## Key Lore
- **Official Role:** "The Guardian of Civilization" — a traveling owl who has witnessed all of human history
- **Concept:** She was once an observer of civilizations, but now enjoys participating and interacting with people
- **Council/Promise affiliation:** Originally part of "Hololive English -Council-" (alongside Kronii, Fauna, Bae, Sana, and Mumei representing Civilization). After Sana's graduation, the remaining members formed "Hololive English -Promise-" with IRyS
- **The name game:** "Nanashi" = nameless; "Mumei" = nameless/unknown. Her name doubly means "nameless," reinforcing the theme that she has forgotten her own identity across millenia of observing civilizations
- **Graduation:** As of the official site, she is listed as [Alum], indicating she has graduated from Hololive
