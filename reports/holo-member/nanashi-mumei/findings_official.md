## Official Character Lore

> *"The Guardian of Civilization, a traveling owl who has borne witness to numerous events. Even though glimpses of past memories — seen and forgotten throughout her endless journey — occasionally surface, she is now a lovely girl who relishes each and every day. When once she was just an observer, she now enjoys her everyday interactions with civilizations and its people. And soon the ordinary, ever-present daily life, shall also weave itself into the tapestry of civilization."*

— Official hololive website description

### Core Concept

- **Personification of human civilization** — Mumei is not merely a guardian of civilization; she *is* the embodiment of civilization itself. She has existed across all of human history, witnessing its rise and fall.
- **A traveling owl** — Her owl form associates her with wisdom, observation, and silent watchfulness.
- **Observer-turned-participant** — Originally a detached observer of humanity, she now chooses to actively engage with civilization and its people.
- **Amnesiac themes** — She carries fragmented memories of events she has witnessed across history; forgetting things is a core character trait (and listed among her dislikes).

## Hololive EN Lore / Worldbuilding Role

### Council Era (2021–2023)

Mumei was part of **Hololive English -Council-** , a generation whose members personify fundamental cosmic concepts:

| Talent | Concept |
|---|---|
| Tsukumo Sana (graduated 2022) | Space |
| Ceres Fauna (graduated 2025) | Nature |
| Ouro Kronii | Time |
| **Nanashi Mumei** | **Civilization** |
| Hakos Baelz | Chaos |

The Council lore positions these five as primordial entities who watch over and maintain different aspects of reality.

### Promise Era (2023–present)

On October 9, 2023, COVER Corporation announced the formation of **hololive English -Promise-** , merging the remaining Council members (Fauna, Kronii, Mumei, Baelz) with **IRyS** (formerly Project: HOPE). The unit's lore describes them as:

> *"Once mere observers of humanity from a distance, the members of hololive English -Promise- now set upon a 'life-sized adventure' on this world's stage, seeking answers to their own 'promises'."*

Mumei's official page now lists her unit as hololive English -Promise-.

## Design & Visual Themes

### Color Scheme

- **Primary colors**: Warm browns, cream/off-white, and muted earth tones
- **Accent colors**: Red (ribbon, eye accents), dark brown/black
- **Hair**: Long brown hair with lighter brown/cream streaks, styled with braids and loose sections
- **Eyes**: Gold/amber with a distinctive warm glow

### Outfit (Default)

- Brown and cream-colored dress with a nature/civilization motif
- A red bow/ribbon accent
- Brown boots
- Owl-themed accessories and feather-like elements
- A hood/capelet that evokes an owl's silhouette
- Feather motifs throughout the costume

### Alternative Outfits
- Multiple casual and formal outfits released over her career, including glasses variants and 3D models.

## Mascot / Companion

Mumei does **not** have a separate distinct mascot companion character. However:

- **She herself IS the owl** — her character is literally a "traveling owl" taking human form. This differs from talents who have a separate plushie/mascot (e.g., Gawr Gura with Bloop).
- The **owl motif** is pervasive in her design, lore, and branding.
- Her catchphrase greeting includes an owl hoot reference: *"Oh hi! Hoo's this? Nanashi Mumei!"*
