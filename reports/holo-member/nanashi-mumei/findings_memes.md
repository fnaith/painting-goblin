## Famous Memes

### 1. "I'm going to commit a war crime"
One of Mumei's most viral memes. During a drawing stream, she casually said something to the effect of committing a war crime in her soft, gentle voice, shocking viewers. This became emblematic of her "dark side" — the contrast between her cute appearance/voice and her macabre, psychopathic thoughts.

### 2. Mumei's "Dark Side" / Psychopathic Tendencies
- In drawing streams, she consistently draws Tim Burton-esque, demonic, or cursed imagery regardless of the original subject.
- Her duality: soft-spoken, gentle "Guardian of Civilization" vs. a being fascinated by the grotesque who has witnessed the worst of humanity across millennia.
- Fans theorize this is what observing the accumulated sins of human civilization for thousands of years does to a person.

### 3. "Forgor" and "Rember" (I Forgor 💀)
Mumei's birdbrained, forgetful nature became a running joke. She frequently forgets things mid-stream — leading to the "I forgor 💀" meme (from the broader KnowYourMeme trend). She admitted in streams that she has no recollection of "60 percent" of what gets clipped.

### 4. "Oh hi!" / "Hoo's this?"
Her catchphrase greeting, used at the start of streams. Became an instantly recognizable meme in the HoloEN community.

### 5. "It's moomin' time!"
Her signature phrase to start streams, with many variations like "Are you ready to moom?" or "It's time to moom!"

### 6. "See Mu-meiter!"
A farewell play-on-words coined by Ouro Kronii at the end of the first Council collaboration stream. Adopted by Mumei and Hoomans.

### 7. Friend the Paper Bag
- Her mascot "Friend" — a paper bag with Norse runes (ᚠᚱᛁᛖᚾᛕ = "FRIEND") and eye holes.
- Friend is canonically untrustworthy, conning Hoomans, fighting dirty, and trying to steal Mumei's powers.
- Mumei keeps Friend "folded away" to prevent mischief ("forced hibernation").

### 8. "Hoomans"
The fan name itself became a meme — Mumei's fans are literally "owl noises + humans." Used in countless community jokes and fanart.

### 9. Mumei's "Cursed" Drawings
Her Passpartout: The Starving Artist streams produced notoriously disturbing "art" that became meme templates. She gave permission for Hoomans to use her drawn artwork as avatars.

### 10. The Haato-Mumei "War"
In March 2022, Mumei and Akai Haato engaged in a Twitter "war" (playful threats). Mumei claimed it had been "multiple centuries" since she last participated in a war, referencing her 1346 Black Death era fleas anecdote.

## Viral / Legendary Streams

### **First Karaoke Stream (Sep 28, 2021)** — "【UNARCHIVED】what"
- Guerrilla, unarchived karaoke stream.
- Over 30,000 concurrent viewers.
- "Mumei" trended worldwide on Twitter: #13 worldwide, #7 in the US, #1 in Canada.
- Source: https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Nanashi_Mumei#Legendary_Streams

### **Monetization Celebration Stream**
- Mumei was so overwhelmed by donation speed that she couldn't read chat.
- She stated viewer time is "genuinely as valuable as money" — a heartfelt moment contrasting her dark side.
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQAlW4yR28k

### **1 Million Subscriber Karaoke (Jan 26, 2024)**
- Achieved 1M subscribers during an unarchived karaoke stream.
- First member of Council/Promise to reach this milestone.
- Source: https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Nanashi_Mumei

### **Graduation Stream (Apr 28, 2025)**
- Pre-recorded 3D event; 124,095 peak viewers.
- $22,639 in Superchat revenue (plus $39,817 from the farewell chatting stream the day before).
- Featured all current HoloEN members performing her song "mumei."
- Stream was temporarily hit by a Sony Music copyright takedown (retracted after 5 days).
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tTABc1SlO8U

## Classic Interactions with Other Hololive Members

### With Ouro Kronii
- Kronii coined "See Mu-meiter!" during the first Council collab.
- Frequently collabed as generation-mates; the Kronii-Mumei dynamic (Warden of "Time" + Guardian of "Civilization") was a fan favorite.

### With IRyS
- Fellow members of hololive English -Promise- (formed Oct 2023 alongside Fauna, Kronii, Bae).
- Their contrasting personalities (IRyS's chaotic energy vs. Mumei's low energy) created comedic moments in collabs.

### With Ceres Fauna
- Fauna developed a protective "big sister" attitude toward Mumei due to her soft nature.
- Fauna and Mumei both got 4th costumes revealed on the same day (Feb 24, 2024).

### With Hakos Baelz
- Fellow Council/Promise member.
- Bae described Mumei as having "the cutest voice in Council" at debut.

### With Tsukumo Sana
- Sana designed the "Beeg Smol" 3D VR models (Jul 2022) including Mumei's.
- Sana was featured in Council's first anniversary song "Rise" even after her own retirement.

### With Takanashi Kiara
- First non-Council collab (Sep 28, 2021) playing Minecraft.
- Kiara called Mumei "Moomsies."

### With Inugami Korone
- Mumei's oshi (favorite). She "fangirled" when first meeting Korone in the Hololive Minecraft server.
- Korone was the first Hololive member Mumei was interested in.

### With Gawr Gura
- Collabed in Fortnite (Apr 15, 2022).
- Known for entertaining but nonsensical conversations due to both having chaotic energy.

### With Mori Calliope
- Had amusing awkward conversations where neither fully knew what to say.

### With Akai Haato
- The Twitter "war" (Mar 2022) is legendary: playful threats back and forth, Mumei referencing her ancient past.

## Other Iconic Moments

- **Black Death Fleas (1346):** Mumei claimed she was collecting fleas "for something" in 1346 — the same year the Black Death is believed to have begun.
- **Belly Button Paradox:** She has a belly button despite claiming she was not born.
- **Water Tank:** Mumei says she drinks from a water tank in her mouth, which must be refilled — explaining why viewers never see her drink.
- **Aphantasia:** Mumei suspects she has aphantasia (inability to visualize mentally), which affects her drawing style.
- **Poor Eyesight:** Despite being an owl, she has poor eyesight and wears contact lenses.
- **Grandfather Owl:** Thought Mumei was a fitness influencer on Instagram.
- **74k Merch:** Special merchandise for 774k subscribers (774 = "na-na-shi" in goroawase).
- **Emo Outfit (Feb 2024):** Her 4th costume was punk/emo themed, called "MOOM" with a gothic metal font shirt.
