## Official Lore / Bio

> *"Welcome to your rouge awakening! This is Akai Haato!"*

_A sassy kouhai. She is often prickly, but she'll try to get a lot of attention from those who have made friends with her. She loves red ribbons and heart shapes, and she'll wear them often in her hair and on her clothes._
— hololive Official Website

## Personality

- **Akai Haato:** Classic tsundere — teases her fans ("kimo ota"), flusters easily, calm and relaxing in Minecraft streams, referred to as "seiso" (pure) by management.
- **Haachama:** An "Eldritch Baby" alter-ego — childish, impulsive, unsettling, known for boundary-pushing content. Greets with *"Haachama-chama~!"*
- **Shared traits:** Infamous "Haato Cooking" streams (unholy food combinations), ear-splitting screams ("Haato's blessings"), chaotic energy.

## The Haachama Saga

A narrative horror storyline (2020–2021) depicting Akai Haato and Haachama as two personalities/sisters battling for control. The story involved fan participation (#ONLYHAACHAMA, #FREEHAATO, #COEXIST). Privated in March 2021 due to hololive policy changes, but later partially restored. Haato described the arc as a step toward her dream of making anime.

## Trivia

- Haato's name translates to "Red Heart" (Akai = red, Haato = heart).
- She is 1/4 British and studied abroad in Australia (virtual Australia lore).
- Cannot eat eggs, kiwi, or avocado due to allergies.
- Lost YouTube verification after changing channel name to "Haachama Ch."
- Shares the same illustrator (Haruyuki) with VTubers Utsugi Sawa and Quon Tama.
- One of the few hololive members to moderate the official hololive Reddit subreddit.
- Her channel was demonetized for over a year (Sep 2019–Feb 2020), which ironically freed her to create the wilder "Haachama" content.
- Theme music: Dvorak - Symphony No. 9 (From the New World) Movement 4.
