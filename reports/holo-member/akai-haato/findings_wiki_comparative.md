## 4. Cross-Source Contradictions

### 4.1 Age
| Source | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **hololive.wiki** | 19 years old |
| **Fandom wiki** | Not listed |
| **Existing report** | 24 years old (lore) |

The hololive.wiki age (19) likely reflects an earlier character concept that was later aged up; the existing report's "24 (lore)" reflects the more recent official profile. This discrepancy is not acknowledged in either wiki.

### 4.2 Twitter account creation date
| Source | Date |
|--------|------|
| **hololive.wiki** (sidebar) | May 20, 2018 |
| **Fandom wiki** | May 21, 2018 |
| **Existing reports** | Not specified |

A one-day difference -- likely a timezone artifact, but worth documenting.

### 4.3 "I'm Haachama" catchphrase origin
| Source | Dating |
|--------|--------|
| **Fandom wiki** | "Became prominent in January 2020" |
| **Existing reports** | General reference to early 2020 / the Australia arc |

Both sources agree on the general timeframe but the fandom wiki provides a more precise month.

### 4.4 Costume naming convention
| Costume | hololive.wiki | Fandom wiki |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Apr 1, 2022 outfit | "Fifth Live2D outfit" | "Fifth 2D costume" |
| Jul 9, 2022 model update | "Main Live2D outfit updated to Ver.3.0" | "3.0 model update" |
| Jul 9, 2024 outfit | "Sixth Live2D outfit" | "Sixth 2D costume (waitress)" |
| Sep 6, 2024 outfit | "Seventh Live2D outfit" | "Chamao" |

The fandom wiki assigns thematic/stylized names (e.g., "Chamao", "waitress", "spiderweb") while hololive.wiki uses ordinal numbering. Existing reports use neither scheme.

### 4.5 Bilibili subscriber milestone
- **hololive.wiki** lists 100,000 bilibili subscribers on **Mar 1, 2019**
- Existing reports make no mention of bilibili subscriber milestones
- Fandom wiki does not mention this milestone

### 4.6 RED HEART full release date
- **Fandom wiki** states the full version of RED HEART was released on **January 10, 2021**
- **hololive.wiki** lists the YouTube release as **June 06, 2020** (which appears to be the premiere/MV date) and digital release as **Feb 24, 2023**
- Existing reports give only "mid-2020" -- the distinction between the MV premiere and the "full version" release is absent
