## Official Lore

- A kid dragon who came to Japan from another world as a **language exchange student**
- Holds a deep interest in human culture
- She is a dragon with a strict code of honor
- Keeps her human form through sheer force of willpower
- True form is that of a **7-meter-tall dragon**
- Human form has exaggerated proportions because "it would be hard to survive in the human world without them"
- Started working for Hololive to pay for **YAGOO**'s hospital bills after she crushed him by accident
- Originates from the **"United States of Dragon America" (USDA)**
- Catchphrase: **"Good morning, motherf\*ckers!"** / **"Good morning, everyone! Wake up, wake up, wake up!"**

## Character Design

- Dragon-themed VTuber with horns and tail

- Main designer: yaman\*\*
- 3D model revealed June 27, 2020

## AsaCoco

- Daily morning news show covering Hololive happenings
- Aired at **6:00 AM JST** on weekdays
- First streamed **December 29, 2019** (the day after debut)
- Featured parody "commercials" for made-up AsaCoco products
- AsaCoco **V2** (launched June 7, 2020) included English lessons for Japanese audience
- Weekend Hololive news handled by Sakura Miko's *AsaMiko*

## Key Trivia

- **Completely bilingual** — fluent in both Japanese and English; also some Spanish
- Became the **biggest Super Chat earner on YouTube** in 2020
- Heavily **influential in the creation of Hololive English** (EN)
- Graduated from **university twice**
- Referred to as **"Kaichou"** (Chairman/Boss) by fans and fellow talents, regardless of seniority
- The fan tradition of sending **blue Super Chats to "flush"** when a talent steps away started with Tatsunoko
- Participated in the "Holohouse" project with Suisei and Kanata
- Her channel remains archived post-graduation; monetization was removed Oct 1, 2021
