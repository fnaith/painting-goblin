## AsaCoco Live News & The "AsaCoco" Drug Meme

- Coco's daily 6 AM JST weekday morning news show, first streamed Dec 29, 2019 (the day after debut)
- Featured parody "commercials" for made-up AsaCoco products (a fictional mind-altering drug)
- AsaCoco varieties: tail buttplug, drinks, gas canisters, injections, lotions, anti-smoking patches, eyedrops with blades, pills to slip into drinks of non-viewers
- Overdose symptoms: Ahegao, incoherent babbling, repeating "Ochinchin", addiction, dilated pupils, instant muscle/bust/butt growth
- Asa means "morning" in Japanese; "Asacoco" can be read as "morning hot chocolate" or "morning insanity"
- AsaCoco V2 launched June 7, 2020 — added English lessons
- Holiday episodes: special Christmas/Halloween editions

## Reddit Meme/Shitpost Review

- Started mid-2020, became a staple series
- Showcases memes from r/Hololive
- Regularly features Holopro talents as guests curious about overseas fan reactions
- Served as bridge between JP and overseas fanbases (Coco translating/explaining context)
- Also featured Japanese fan memes from Twitter from late Dec 2020
- Copyright issues in late Nov 2020 led to temporary hiatus
- Featured "Shitty Japanese" (poorly-translated Japanese) memes

## The Anti Incident & Controversies

- April 18, 2020: Received YouTube livestreaming ban (first of several content-policy incidents)
- During the ban, AsaCoco continued as prerecorded videos with English captions
- Stream ban lifted April 23, 2020
- Sept 27, 2020: 3-week suspension alongside Akai Haato (returned Oct 19)
- Pushed boundaries frequently: hair removal streams, toilet speedrunning RTA, flipping the bird in thumbnails
- Higher-ups told her not to mention/collab with Projekt Melody again after suggesting it

## Graduation & Legacy

- June 9, 2021: Announced graduation in emotional stream
- July 1, 2021: Graduation live stream peaked at 491,000+ concurrent viewers — largest in VTuber history at the time
- Every active Hololive member shared first impressions and memories
- 4th Gen (holoForce) members read handwritten letters:
  - Watame: "Thanks to you, Coco, I can stand here without faltering"
  - Luna: Drew crude picture saying "大スキココちゃ" (Wuv you, Cococha)
  - Towa: "The 4th Generation may look like it has four members from now on, but there will always be five of us"
  - Kanata: "KanaCoco is forever! Let's keep it going!" — described as "bickering married couple"
- Final stream ended with 3D performance of "Shiny Smily Story", "Kiseki Musubi", and "Weather Hackers"
- Channel remains archived post-graduation (monetization removed Oct 1, 2021)
- Profile remains on Hololive official site as "OG Alumnus"

## Famous Anecdotes

- **$9,000 Bed**: After describing needing sleeping medication due to uncomfortable futon, viewers superchatted enough for a $9,000 bed
- **The Tail**: Her tail is allegedly fake and "buttplugged" onto her; her mouse cursor becomes a tail buttplug during Reddit meme reviews
- **Scratched Horn**: Right horn has yellow scratch mark from bumping into doorframes due to clumsiness adjusting to human form
- **No Nintendo Switch**: Refused to buy one calling it "normie"; Kanata eventually gifted her an Animal Crossing-themed Switch for 1M subs
- **Coco Kaine**: Proposed Hololive USA with joke character "Coco Kaine" (cocaine pun) — overweight, middle-aged smoker; her designer drew a young version that got more likes than Coco's own art
- **RTA Bathroom Record**: Claims to hold world record in bathroom speedrunning
- **Holohouse**: Proposed and helped establish a shared living space near Cover office with Suisei and Kanata
- **T-shirt Competition**: Started a T-shirt sale competition against Kanata after realizing her merch wasn't selling well (lost)
- **Taste of Humans**: When asked if she's eaten humans and how they taste, she was ready to answer but jumped to next topic
- **Hololive EN Creation**: Heavily influential in proposing Hololive English; began teasing EN branch after ID debut; created short commercial promoting EN auditions
- **Biggest Super Chat Earner**: As of Aug 2020, became the biggest Super Chat earner on YouTube globally

## Fan Culture (Tatsunoko)

- Official fan name: Tatsunoko (たつのこ) — "Little Dragons"
- Blue Super Chat "flush" tradition started with Tatsunoko (even Coco doesn't remember how it started)
- Kiryukai (桐生会) — membership/fan group name
- Known for extreme loyalty and massive Super Chat donations
- Fan hashtag: #たつのこ
