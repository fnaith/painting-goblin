## Speech Quirk / "Luna-speak"

Luna speaks with a distinctive baby-talk lisp, replacing 'r' sounds with 'w' sounds in English transliteration. She frequently adds **"nano"** and **"nanora"** to the end of sentences, which has become her most recognizable auditory hallmark.

A Reddit bot called **"Nanofy_Bot"** exists that converts comments to mimic her speech pattern:
- https://github.com/Rapha-Y/Nanofy_Bot

A voice button website called **"Luna Button"** collects her catchphrases as sound clips:
- https://nanora.moe

## Official Catchphrases / Memes (from hololivepro.com)

The official site explicitly documents these as Luna's catchphrases and memes:

| Catchphrase | Meaning | Context |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| **"Minna, oryu?"** | "Are you there, everyone?" | Greeting her Lu-knights at the start of streams |
| **"Nan nanora?"** | "What's that all about?" | When angry at someone or something (Lu-knights, a game, etc.) |
| **"Yare yare nanora"** | "Good gwief" | When she proved someone wrong and they apologized |
| **"Kusee nora"** | "This stinks" | When encountering something stinky mid-stream |
| **"Ochi wa nee nora"** | "There is no punchline" | When people ask for a punchline but there isn't one; used in her own streams and collabs |
| **"Luna no kachii!" / "Nna-tan no kachii!"** | "Victory is mine!" | When she wins at something |
| **"Nna-shoi!"** | "Nna-hup!" | Used when hitting enemies in games or jumping. Fans also use it as a censor word for naughty topics, describing it as "a surge of joy coming straight from the heart" |
| **"Nnayuki"** | Her version of "Konayuki" | A play on the song "Konayuki" (Powder Snow) by Remioromen |

## Notable Anecdotes & Trivia

### Q&A from Official Profile
- **Age**: Claims to be 0 years old (but also a "sexy adult pwincess")
- **Musical instruments**: Plays Electone (electronic organ) and a little flute
- **Strongest subject**: Claims to read kanji the best out of all hololive members
- **KAATSU training**: Does KAATSU (blood flow restriction) training to gain strength
- **Hobbies**: Gardening when it gets warm; stays indoors during winter
- **Moon accessories**: Her moon-shaped accessories are said to be the crest of a country in another world (in-character lore)

### Gen-mate Relationships
- Part of **hololive 4th Generation** alongside:
  - Tsunomaki Watame
  - Tokoyami Towa
  - Amane Kanata
  - Kiryu Coco (graduated)
- Frequently collabs with **Houshou Marine** — the clips above show a running gag of Marine both bullying and being bullied by Luna
- Has interacted in Minecraft with **Tokino Sora** (hololive's first-generation talent, the "founder")
