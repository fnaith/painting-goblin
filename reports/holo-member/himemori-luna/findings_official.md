## hololive 4th Generation Background

hololive 4th Generation (ホロライブ4期生) is the 4th wave of VTubers under hololive Japan. Original members included:

- **Amane Kanata** (天音かなた) — debuted December 27, 2019 (graduated December 27, 2025)
- **Kiryu Coco** (桐生ココ) — debuted December 28, 2019 (graduated July 1, 2021)
- **Tsunomaki Watame** (角巻わため) — debuted December 29, 2019
- **Tokoyami Towa** (常闇トワ) — debuted January 3, 2020
- **Himemori Luna** (姫森ルーナ) — debuted January 4, 2020 (final member of 4th Gen to debut)

The 4th Gen group single is "Kiseki knot" (キセキ結び), released June 25, 2021.

## Character Design Theme & Concept

- **Theme:** A 0-year-old princess from the "Candy Kingdom" — an otherworldly realm
- **Design motifs:** Pink color scheme, crown, moon-shaped accessories (said to be the crest of her home country), candy/sweet imagery
- **Visual style:** Extremely cute, childlike, all-pink outfit with a royal aesthetic
- **Heterochromia:** Right eye is pink, left eye is green (used in a stream bit called "Today's Wink Time")
- **Persona:** Acts in a spoiled, affectionate, childlike manner. Behind the cuteness, she has a mischievous, competitive, and composed personality. Known for rapid-fire talking when frustrated or excited.
- **Voice:** Childlike, high-pitched, with a distinctive speech pattern. Also has a mature alternate voice called "Anemori" that appears during certain song performances.

### Verbal Tic

- Frequently ends sentences with **"nanora"** (なのら)
- Stream greeting often includes stretching "Nnaaaaa~"
- Self-introduction: **"Hororaibu yon kisei Himemori Luna nanora~"** (ホロライブ4期生姫森ルーナなのら～)

### Greetings & Farewells

- **Greeting:** "Minna~ Oru~?" → Fans reply: "Oru yo~"
- **Farewell:** "Otsu Luna" (おつルーナ) — portmanteau of "Otsukaresama" + "Luna"; also used by fans
- **Night stream farewell:** "Guddonaito" (グッドナイトー) — "Good night"

### Viewer Interactions

- Fans called **Lu-knights** (ルーナイト) answer Luna before streams with "Nna... Nna..."
- Members-only chat badges feature the candy emoji 🍬
