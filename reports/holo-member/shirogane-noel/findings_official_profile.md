## Personality & Traits (from official sources)

- **Archetype:** Fluffy, easy-going but muscle-brained female knight ("ゆるふわ脳筋女騎士")
- **Known as:** "Iron stomach great demon" (鉄の胃袋大魔神) — can eat unlimited gyūdon (beef bowls)
- **Hobbies:** Eating, gaming, watching anime
- **Special skills:** "Massuru Metronome" (super-speed arm muscle flexing), reading aloud, ASMR
- **Favorite food:** Gyūdon (beef bowl), meat in general
- **Close bond with:** Shiranui Flare (fellow Hololive 3rd Gen member); also noted close to Houshou Marine ("NoeMari")
- **Streaming focus:** Action/RPG games, English study, chatting, ASMR, drinking chats (evening sake), karaoke, reading aloud
