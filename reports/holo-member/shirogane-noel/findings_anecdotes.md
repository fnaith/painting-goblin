## Famous / Legendary Stream Moments

### 1. "Getting Over It" Endurance Saga
- Noel attempted to clear *Getting Over It with Bennett Foddy*, a game notorious for its difficulty and frustration.
- Her first attempt failed, but she returned for a **second "revenge" endurance stream** that lasted many hours.
- She showed extreme persistence even while visibly frustrated and exhausted, finally clearing the game after hours of struggle. This became a defining example of her stubborn determination.

### 2. 3D Debut (November 17, 2019)
- Her main 3D model was revealed, created by ぽんぷ長 (@paint002).
- Coincided with hitting the 100,000 subscriber milestone during the stream itself.

### 3. "Nonstop Story" — Hololive's First Live Concert (January 24, 2020)
- Participated as a member of Hololive Fantasy in Hololive's first-ever live concert at Toyosu Pit, Tokyo.
- Debuted her 3D idol outfit on stage.
- Her vocal trainer later critiqued her performance, which Noel openly shared with fans — showing her humility and drive to improve.

### 4. Neptunia Virtual Stars (Announced January 28, 2020)
- Noel appeared as a guest VTuber character in the game **Neptunia Virtual Stars** alongside Tokino Sora, Roboco, Sakura Miko, Shirakami Fubuki, Minato Aqua, Yuzuki Choco, Inugami Korone, and Houshou Marine.

### 5. #Gensokyo Holoism (July 10, 2020)
- Noel voiced the Touhou character **Saigyouji Yuyuko** in this Hololive Touhou collaborative project, which featured a full audio drama and music album.

### 6. Custom Platinum Rings with Flare (February 2021)
- Noel and Shiranui Flare got **custom-made platinum rings together**, further symbolizing their exceptionally close bond.

## Notable "Oops" / Funny Failure Moments

### 1. ASMR Content Purges & Demonetization (YouTube Policy)
- Noel was one of Hololive's primary ASMR-focused members, known for her calming voice.
- On **January 1, 2020**, her YouTube channel was **demonetized** specifically due to her ASMR content. She confirmed this on Twitter, noting that all her videos had green "ad-friendly" marks, yet the demonetization happened without warning. She called YouTube's moderation "emotionally unstable."
- On **January 31, 2020**, her channel was successfully remonetized after one month.
- YouTube later continued harshly taking down her ASMR streams, forcing her to pivot away from it as a main content pillar.

### 2. The Messy Room Incident
- In Noel's first marshmallow-reading collaboration with Shirayuki Mishiro (from Noriopro), multiple fan submissions brought up the topic of Noel's famously messy room.
- This became a recurring gag among her fanbase.

### 3. Self-Acknowledged Singing Shortcomings
- Despite her beautiful speaking/ASMR voice, Noel has been open about her vocal limitations in singing. She shared that even her old vocal trainer expressed concerns about her performance at Nonstop Story.
- She continues to practice and improve, often joking about it during karaoke streams.

## Heartwarming Stories & Fanbase Interactions

### 1. The Danchou-Fan Bond (Knight's Order)
- Noel calls her fans her "knight order" and treats the relationship as a guild leader commanding loyal knights.
- She has a very calming, gentle stream presence that creates a safe and relaxing environment for viewers.
- Known to take her time during chat streams, slowly reading topics and immersing listeners.

### 2. Deep Bond with Shiranui Flare
- Noel and fellow Hololive Fantasy member Shiranui Flare share an exceptionally close relationship.
- They took a vacation trip together and often share stories of off-stream interactions.
- Noel becomes unusually emotional (both tears and temper) when it comes to matters involving Flare.
- Fans frequently celebrate their dynamic (known as "FlareNoel").

### 3. Big Sister Energy
- Frequently takes care of other members during collaborations.
- Considered one of the "big sister" types among Hololive talent.
- Known for her gentle, patient demeanor.

## Character Lore & Knight Theme

### The "Danchou" Persona
- Noel is the **guild leader** of the Knight's Order of Shirogane.
- Her catchphrase "All hustle, all muscle!" reflects her lore as a knight who tries to muscle through problems.
- Her design by Watao incorporates silver/white knight armor motifs.
- Her signature greeting "Konbanmassuru" is a pun fusing "konbanwa" (good evening) with "massuru" (muscle).

### Canon Strengths
- She is canonically one of the physically strongest Hololive members, fitting her knight persona.
- Despite her warrior exterior, she is gentle, caring, and big-sisterly.

### Personality Traits
- Determined and persistent (will not stop until she achieves her goal)
- Gentle, patient, and caring
- Often airheaded/"fluffy" — hence the "meatheaded knight" description in her official bio
- Gets emotional about people she cares about (especially Flare)

## Trivia & Fun Facts
- Her favorite food is **gyūdon** (beef bowl) from any chain store, along with meat in general.
- She has a **younger brother** she adores, who frequently appears in her stories.
- She joined Hololive partly because her oshi is **Nakiri Ayame** (Hololive 2nd Gen).
- Her messy room is a running joke among fans.
- She is a moderator on the official Hololive subreddit.
- Her YouTube channel is verified.
- She often does **yakiniku (grilled meat) eating streams** and **drinking chat streams** due to her high alcohol tolerance.
- Her content is evenly spread across gaming, chatting, karaoke, and eating streams.
- **Bust size claim:** Noel claims to have K-cup breasts, which fans jokingly call "Knight-cup" (a pun on her knight theme).
- **School nickname:** During her school years, she was called "The Running T*ddy Cow" by classmates, a crude nickname referencing her pace and figure.
- **Alcohol metabolism:** Despite having an ALDH2 deficiency (genetically predisposed to poor alcohol metabolism), Noel can drink heavily, surprising even herself.
