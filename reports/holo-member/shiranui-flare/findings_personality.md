# Shiranui Flare (不知火フレア) — Personality Research Findings

## Basic Profile
- **Generation:** Hololive 3rd Generation (Hololive Fantasy)
- **Debut:** August 7, 2019
- **Character:** Half-elf (half human, half elf)
- **Birthday:** April 2, 1798 (221 years old)
- **Height:** 158 cm
- **Illustrator:** lack
- **Fairy companion:** Kintsuba
- **Oshi Mark/Emoji:** 🔥 Fire

---

## Personality Description
Flare is regarded as the definitive **big sister figure** of her generation and often of Hololive as a whole. She projects a **cool, mature, and collected** demeanor — rarely losing herself to frustration or rage on stream, even during notoriously difficult games like *Getting Over It* or *Dark Souls*.

She is **understanding and dependable**, frequently looked up to by other members. In the Hololive Minecraft and ARK servers, she is known for leaving gifts and valuables for others and willingly helping with projects.

Despite her cool exterior, Flare is also **playful and silly** — she frequently launches into unscripted gags and skits during solo streams. She switches between formality and goofiness, using very polite speech when meeting new people or reading superchats (where she bows in gratitude for each one).

---

### Hidden Side
Despite her mature "big sister" image, Flare has a noticeably **childish and girly** side that emerges in specific contexts:
- **Horror streams:** Her genuine fear reactions and screaming contrast sharply with her usual composure
- **Drunk streams:** When drinking, she becomes loose, rambling, and unfiltered — her favorite way to drink is to get drunk and start talking freely (sometimes mentioning other members' bodies)
- **Playful outbursts:** She spontaneously does silly voices, dances, and comedic skits during solo streams

### "Harem" Reputation
Other Hololive Fantasy members have described Flare as having a "harem" — her kindness and mature aura attract many admirers within and outside Hololive. Tokino Sora has publicly expressed admiration for Flare's versatile voice and mature presence.

### Male Persona
Flare has a male persona named **Homura** (another design by her illustrator lack), sometimes referenced in alternate-universe fan content. This is distinct from her main half-elf character.

### Architectural Sense
In Minecraft, Flare is known for having good architectural sense — her builds are well-designed and aesthetically pleasing, contributing to her reputation as a dependable builder in Hololive server projects.

## Typical Stream Behavior
- **Long, late-night streams** — often 3–4+ hours, starting late
- **Three content pillars:** Gaming, singing (karaoke), and chatting
- **Gaming:** Enjoys action games (Dark Souls, Sekiro, Devil May Cry) and sandbox/survival games (Minecraft, ARK)
- **Singing:** Known for wide vocal range; holds regular karaoke streams
- **Chat streams:** Laid-back, often with drinks; discusses recent events
- **Collabs:** Frequent offline/online collaborations; movie watchalongs
- **Giving nature:** In multiplayer games, she routinely leaves gifts and valuables for others
- **Superchat etiquette:** Bows and thanks for every superchat; mentions color for purple/red ones

---

## Special Skills & Talents
- **Three-octave vocal range without falsetto** — can sing in a wide variety of tones and pitches
- **Drawing skill** — occasionally draws alongside other members, showing notable artistic talent
- **Versatile singing** — equally comfortable with cool/deep-voice songs and silly denpa-style songs

## Key Trivia
- **Not a dark elf** — Flare repeatedly emphasizes that she is a **normal** half-elf with human and elf parents
- **Popular among women** — her charm makes her well-liked by female fans and fellow female talents
- **Reddit moderator** — she is one of the moderators on the official Hololive subreddit
- **3D debut hashtag** trended worldwide on Twitter
- Her original design was phased out after receiving her New Year's outfit
- **Male persona "Homura"** — a separate design by lack, sometimes used in alternate-universe fan concepts
- **Architectural sense** — known for well-designed Minecraft builds on the Hololive server
- **Helped sister discover Hololive** — Flare is close with her younger sister, whom she helped introduce to Hololive

- **Twitch channel** — streams occasionally on Twitch (@shiranuiflare_hololive)
