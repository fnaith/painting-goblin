## Official Bio / Lore

> *"Noon-nui! This is hololive 3rd Gen's Shiranui Flare!"*

**Backstory:** A half-elf born to a human and an elf. Usually enjoys life as she pleases. She wants to be a source of comfort — viewers can rely on her whenever they're in trouble. She may be passionate and overly sentimental, but praising her brings her joy, even if also a little embarrassment!

**Companion:** A fairy named **Kintsuba** (きんつば) that follows her everywhere.

**Greeting catchphrase:** "Hehehe! Let's get along!"

## Character Design Motifs

### Original Outfit
- **Theme:** Flame/fire motif (name "Flare" + fire emoji 🔥)
- **Palette:** Predominantly red, black, and white
- **Key elements:**
  - Long blonde hair with a prominent ahoge (cowlick)
  - Dark/black pointed half-elf ears
  - Red-and-black asymmetrical combat/bandolier-style top
  - Black detached sleeves with red accents
  - Short black skirt with red flame-like trim
  - Thigh-high black boots with red accents
  - A flame-shaped accessory/clip in her hair
- **Weapon/accessory:** Bandolier across the chest (decorative, not functional); combat-boot styling reinforcing a fighting-game aesthetic

### New Year Outfit (January 1, 2020)
- Traditional Japanese-style kimono/long-sleeved outfit (furisode)
- Red and white color scheme with flame patterns
- Used as her primary streaming outfit for an extended period

### Casual Outfit (November 14, 2020)
- Casual sailor-style dress
- Laid-back, everyday look

### Main Outfit Redesign (December 12, 2021)
- Updated main outfit by lack, replacing the original as her primary design
- Retains the fire/flame motif with refined details

### Casual Dress (September 2023)
- Alternate casual look distinct from the 2020 sailor-style dress

### Roomwear (June 2024)
- Loungewear / home relaxation outfit

### Hololive Fantasy Outfit (August 2024)
- Generation-themed outfit reflecting the "Hololive Fantasy" unit identity

### Festival 2025 Outfit (September 2025)
- Event/concert-themed outfit for Hololive festival appearances

### 3D Model
- **Default 3D:** Designed by lack
- **1M Subscriber 3D outfit:** Concept by Saeki Yahiro
- **Live 3D outfit:** Designed by ordan (https://twitter.com/ordan)
- **1M subscriber 3D outfit "Luminous":** Concept by Saeki Yahiro
- **figma figure** — Announced in 2021 (prototype shown), one of several Hololive figma releases

### Overall Design Language
Flare's character design leans heavily into a **fantasy-action** aesthetic — half-elf traits (pointed ears), flame motifs (name, accessories, color palette), and a slight combat/bandolier styling. The red/black/white color scheme is consistent across outfits. The fire (🔥) is the universal symbol representing her.

## Personality Traits (from wiki)

- Calm, collected, mature — the "big sister" of 3rd gen (and often of all Hololive)
- Playful and silly in solo streams; does spontaneous skits and gags
- Very formal and polite when interacting with new people
- Bows while reading superchats; reads every one when time allows
- Dependable and generous — frequently leaves gifts/valuables for others in Minecraft and ARK servers
- Known for long streams (3-4+ hours) starting late at night
- Enjoys action games (Dark Souls, Sekiro, Devil May Cry) as well as sandbox games (Minecraft, ARK)
- Holds regular karaoke streams showcasing her wide vocal range
