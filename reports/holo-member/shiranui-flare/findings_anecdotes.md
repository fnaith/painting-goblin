## Overview

Shiranui Flare is a half-elf (human x elf) and a member of **Hololive 3rd Generation** ("Hololive Fantasy"). She debuted on **August 7, 2019**. Her birthday (lore) is **April 2, 1798** (she is 221 years old). Her height is 158 cm. Her fairy companion is named **Kintsuba**. Designed by illustrator **lack**. Her catchphrase greeting is **"Noon-nui!"** (こんぬい～), a portmanteau of "konnichiwa" and "Shiranui."

## Memorable Streaming Moments & Incidents

1. **Getting Over It — Comforting Noel:** During Shirogane Noel's endurance stream of Getting Over It, Flare joined to accompany her. When Noel lost significant progress, Flare called her to comfort and cheer her up. After Noel finished, Flare closed her own game immediately to congratulate her — a defining example of her big-sister personality.

2. **3D Debut Postponement:** Originally scheduled for March 29, 2020, her 3D reveal was postponed due to COVID-19. It was re-announced June 10 and successfully held on June 13, 2020. The debut hashtag trended worldwide.

3. **Long Late-Night Streams:** Known for streaming 3–4+ hours, often starting late at night. She plays action games (Dark Souls, Sekiro, Devil May Cry) and has played multiplayer games like Minecraft and ARK.

4. **Wide Vocal Range Karaoke:** Her karaoke streams showcase a three-octave range without falsetto. Her setlists range from cool songs to silly denpa-style songs and sudden comedic skits.

5. **Subscriber Milestone Karaoke:** She hit **1,000,000 subscribers on June 15, 2023 during a karaoke stream** — a fitting milestone for her singing-focused channel.

6. **Superchat Ritual:** Flare bows in gratitude every time she reads a superchat and mentions the color if it is purple or red.

## Personality & Mannerisms

- **Big sister figure** — calm, collected, dependable. Rarely loses her temper or shows frustration.
- **Playful** — frequently launches into gags and skits mid-stream.
- **Formal/polite** — uses very polite speech with new people.
- **Greeting:** "Konnui!" (こんぬい～)
- **Farewell:** "Otsunui!" (おつぬい～)
- **Popular among women** — her charm is noted to extend to female fans.
- **Not a dark elf** — repeatedly emphasizes she is a "normal half-elf with human and elf parents."

## Fan Community Perception & Common Themes

- **"Konnui"** — her unique greeting is widely recognized and memed
- **Half-elf/debate about being a dark elf** — a running gag in the community
- **"Elfriend" culture** — fans identify strongly with the Elfriend mascot (the little bird)

## Lore & Backstory (Used in Fan Content)

- Half-elf born from a human and elf, **born April 2, 1798** (making her 221+ years old in lore)
- Her fairy companion is named **Kintsuba**
- Lives a leisurely life doing as she pleases
- Her design by **lack** features flame-themed motifs (her namesake "Flare")
- Frequently depicted in fan works wielding swords or in RPG fantasy settings, playing into her half-elf origin
- Fan nickname **"Shiranui Homura"** (another of lack's designs) spawned alternate-universe fan concepts
- Her signature "Noon-nui"/"Otsunui" greetings are integral to fan-made memes and sticker sets
