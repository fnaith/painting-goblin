# Research Plan: Hololive Member Shiranui Flare

## Main Research Question
Gather comprehensive information about Hololive member **Shiranui Flare (不知火フレア)** — including official character settings, personal traits, catchphrases, notable anecdotes, and relationships — for use in creating a fan/doujin game.

## Subtopic 1: Official Profile & Character Design
- Hololive debut date and generation
- Character designer (illustrator) details
- Official bio: height, birthday, stated likes/dislikes, theme
- Character design elements (outfit motifs, color scheme, weapon/accessories)
- VTuber model details

## Subtopic 2: Personality, Catchphrases & Traits
- Character personality traits and persona
- Catchphrases and verbal tics
- Typical behavior during streams
- Special skills or quirks
- Fan nickname(s) and their origins

## Subtopic 3: Notable Events, Anecdotes & Relationships
- Memorable streaming moments
- Notable achievements (milestones, events participated in)
- Relationships with other Hololive members (key interactions, collabs)
- Any appearance in official Hololive games or media
- Fan community perception

## Synthesis Plan
All findings will be combined into a structured report organized by topic, with specific citations of sources and their URLs. This will provide a comprehensive character reference for game development.
