# Ookami Mio (大神ミオ) — Official Research Findings

## Agency & Affiliation
- **Agency:** Cover Corp (hololive production)
- **Branch:** hololive (Japan)
- **Unit:** hololive GAMERS
- **Generation:** Debut as part of the hololive GAMERS subunit alongside Shirakami Fubuki (who debuted the same day)

## Debut & Channel Creation
- **YouTube Channel Created:** December 1, 2018
- **Twitter Account Created:** November 16, 2018
- **Debut Date:** December 7, 2018 (introductory stream with Shirakami Fubuki)
- **3D Model Debut:** June 16, 2019
- **3D Swimsuit Debut:** September 11, 2019
- **1 Million Subscribers:** June 7, 2022
- **1.25 Million Subscribers:** December 29, 2024

## Basic Stats
- **Birthday:** August 20
- **Height:** 160 cm (per official site; historical inconsistency noted at 155 cm and 165 cm)
- **Age:** Not officially disclosed (wolf girl / young adult archetype)
- **Species:** Wolf girl (black-haired wolf girl)

## Character Design Concept
- **Theme:** Wolf girl — black hair with wolf ears and tail; said to appear randomly and often found near shrines
- **Color Scheme:** Primary colors include black (hair), white/cream (outfit base), with accent colors like red (ribbon/accessories) and gold/yellow (trim)
- **Outfit Design:**
  - Main outfit: A white/cream-colored kimono-style top with a red ribbon/obi, dark skirt, thigh-high stockings, and geta sandals. Features wolf ears and a fluffy wolf tail.
  - Has numerous alternate costumes: casual parka/loungewear, New Year's kimono, beret/cardigan casual, Christmas variant, Queen of Hearts-inspired, "Another Mion" hooded jacket, red sailor fuku, and a green dress outfit (7th L2D, debuted January 2025)
- **Illustrator:** Izumi Sai (https://twitter.com/AC______) — also known as "Izumi-papa"
- **Live2D Modeling:** rariemonn (v1.0 and v2.0), 入江燈, FELUCCA
- **3D Modeling:** Yatsurugi

## Catchphrases & Greetings
- **Official introduction:** "Hellooowl! It is Ookami Mio!" (English on official site)
- **Japanese intro:** "ホロライブゲーマーズの大神ミオです！" (Full) / "大神ミオだよ！"
- **Evening greeting:** "こんばんみぉーん！" (Konbanmion!) — a portmanteau of "konbanwa" (good evening) and "Mio"
- **Afternoon greeting:** "ひるみぉーん！" (Hirumion!)
- **Morning greeting:** "おはみぉーん！" (Ohamion!)
- **Sign-off:** "おつみぉーん！" (Otsumion!) — a portmanteau of "otsukaresama" and "Mio"

- **Nicknames:** ミオしゃ (Miosha), みおーん (Mion), ミオママ (Mio Mama / Mio-mama) — the latter due to her motherly personality

## Official Bio (from hololivepro.com)

## Personality & Notable Traits (from fan wiki)
- Known as the "mother of Hololive" (Mio-mama) — mature, responsible, kind, often sought for advice
- Easily scared (especially by horror games) but plays them out of a sense of GAMERS responsibility
- Often plays the straight man in comedic situations but also enjoys making jokes
- Skilled at tarot card readings — uses them to give advice to peers and fans
- Plays ukulele and holds singing practice streams
- Known for supporting the "NoPan Health Act" (joking belief)
- Has two male cats: Taiga and Tawa (an Abyssinian)
- Close friend of Shirakami Fubuki — the two were gaming friends before joining Hololive
