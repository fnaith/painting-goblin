## 1. Canon Lore & Character Background

### Official Bio
- **Species:** Wolf-girl (black-haired wolf with wolf ears and tail)
- **Debut:** December 7, 2018 (YouTube / Hololive GAMERS)
- **Birthday:** August 20
- **Height:** 160 cm (historically listed as 155 cm or 165 cm in different places — a known inconsistency even in official materials)
- **Illustrator:** Izumi Sai (aka Izumi-papa / AC______)
- **Live2D Modeling:** rariemonn (v2.0 & v1.0), 入江燈 (v4), FELUCCA (v5, v6)
- **3D Modeling:** Yatsurugi
- **Fan Name:** Mio-fam (Mio Family; ミオファ)
- **Emoji / Oshi Mark:** 🌲 (pine tree — because her hair resembles one)
- **Catchphrases:** "Konbanmion!" (evening), "Ohamion!" (morning), "Hirumion!" (afternoon), "Otsumion!" (farewell)
- **Introduction:** "Hellooowl! It is Ookami Mio!"

### In-Universe Lore
- A black-haired wolf girl who "appeared on our radar randomly" and "often shows up around shrines."
- She likes gaming.
- **Backstory with Fubuki:** Mio was an old gaming friend of Shirakami Fubuki. Wondering what Fubuki was up to, she reached out. Fubuki explained she was in Hololive, Mio thought it sounded fun, and decided to apply as well. This makes her one of the few Hololive members who joined through a personal connection.
- Mio was part of the **Hololive GAMERS** subgroup, the second wave alongside Fubuki, with Nekomata Okayu and Inugami Korone joining shortly after.

## 2. Personality & Character Traits

### "Mio-mama" — The Mother of Hololive
- Mio is widely regarded as the **mother figure** of Hololive due to her mature, responsible, and kind nature.
- Both fans and fellow Hololivers seek her advice on personal matters.
- She jokingly responded to the "Mio-mama" nickname by saying she "doesn't remember giving birth."
- Despite her maternal image, she has stated she had a more aggressive, wild side in her early streaming days — experimenting with many types of content to find her niche.

### Easily Frightened (But Brave)
- Mio is famously scared of horror games, spending most of her playtime terrified.
- However, she believes it is her responsibility as a member of Hololive GAMERS to play them anyway, so she continues to do so.
- This contrast between fear and determination is a recurring source of content.

### Straight Man / Comedy Role
- Mio often plays the "straight man" (tsukkomi) in comedic situations with her peers.
- She is not afraid to make jokes herself, but her natural demeanor anchors chaotic collabs.

### Other Notable Traits
- **Tarot Card Reader:** Mio can perform tarot card readings, which she uses to give advice to fans and fellow Hololivers.
- **NoPan Health Act:** A running bit — Mio is a devout believer in the "NoPan Health Act," claiming it is healthy to sleep without underwear, passed down as wisdom from her mother and grandmother.
- **Ukulele:** Around January 2021, Mio began streaming herself practicing ukulele while singing.

## 5. Notable Anecdotes & Stream Moments

### Hatotaurus — Mio's "Stand"
- **Hatotaurus** (鳩トーラス) is a giant pigeon-bull creature that appears as Mio's mascot / "stand" (JoJo reference).
- First introduced by Fubuki during a "kindergarten" stream.
- Later made into an actual Live2D model using Izumi-papa's artwork.
- Frequently joked about as Mio's Stand (in the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure sense).

### The "Freedom Mio" Declaration
- After her early days of experimenting with content, Mio eventually declared her "Freedom Mio" approach — settling into her own style and using "uchi uchi, uchi da yo!" as her standard greeting.

### Getting Over It Milestone
- Reached 300,000 subscribers while streaming *Getting Over It* — a notoriously difficult and rage-inducing game. The contrast of her calm/maternal persona with this game was memorable.

### Hololive Motherhood Competition
- Reached 500,000 subscribers during this collab — fittingly, a competition about being a "mother."

### Cat Streams (Taiga & Tawa)
- **Taiga** (タイガ): Mio's male cat, known for interrupting streams — knocking things over, drenching tarot cards, scratching her leg.
- **Tawa** (タワ): Second male cat, revealed June 20, 2021. An Abyssinian breed.
- Real cat appearances are a beloved recurring stream element.

### Azur Lane Collaboration
- Mio participated in the "Looking Glass of Fact and Fiction" Azur Lane collab alongside Natsuiro Matsuri, Nakiri Ayame, Shirakami Fubuki, Murasaki Shion, Tokino Sora, and Minato Aqua (November 26 – December 11, 2019).

### Height Inconsistency Meme
- Different official sources have listed Mio's height as **155 cm**, **160 cm**, and **165 cm** at various times. This inconsistency is a running joke among fans.

### Splatoon Powerhouse
- Despite being self-described as bad at most games, Mio is **extremely good at Splatoon** — a contrast that surprises viewers.

### Earth Defense Force Fan
- Mio is a huge fan of the *Earth Defense Force* series, frequently streaming it.

### "Mio is cute" Meme
- Whenever Mio is away from stream (break, technical issues, etc.), viewers spam "ミオかわいい" (Mio is cute) and super chats in chat.
