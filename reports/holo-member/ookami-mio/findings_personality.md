## Personality & Characteristics

### Core Traits
- **Mature and responsible** — frequently described as the "mother of Hololive" by both fans and fellow talents.
- **Extremely kind** — many Hololive members and fans seek her out for personal advice. She offers tarot card readings to peers and viewers alike.
- **Easily scared** — especially during horror games, which she still plays out of a sense of duty as a member of GAMERS.
- **Straight man / tsukkomi** — often plays the grounded role in comedic situations, but is not afraid to joke herself.
- **Wild streak** — in her early days she was more aggressive/experimental with content; this side occasionally resurfaces, leading to kusoge (trash game) streams.

### Voice
- Characterized as a warm, mature, slightly low-pitched feminine voice, often described as soothing and "motherly."
- Known for her distinctive elongated greeting: "Konbanmion!" (こんばんみぉーん).

### Signature Greetings & Farewells
- **Greeting (evening):** こんばんみぉーん！(Konbanmion!)
- **Greeting (afternoon):** おひるみぉーん！(Ohirumion!)
- **Greeting (morning):** おはみぉーん！(Ohamion!)
- **Farewell:** おつみぉーん！(Otsumion!)
- **Self-intro:** うち うち、うちだよ！(Uchi uchi, uchi da yo!) — uses "uchi" (an informal "I/me") as a stylistic choice after her "Freedom Mio" declaration.
- **Full intro:** ホロライブゲーマーズの大神ミオです！

## Relationships & Collaborations

### Shirakami Fubuki — Closest Friend
- Mio and Fubuki were gaming friends **before** joining Hololive. Mio reached out to Fubuki wondering what she was up to, Fubuki explained Hololive, and Mio applied.
- Fubuki created the chibi "Miochun" (ミオチュン) design.
- Frequent collab partners; the two are often paired as "White Fox × Black Wolf" (白狐×黒狼). A dedicated collaboration line launched in May 2026.

### Hololive GAMERS (core unit)
- Mio is a member of **hololive GAMERS** alongside Shirakami Fubuki, Nekomata Okayu, and Inugami Korone.
- The group has original songs including "We are GAMERS!!!!" and "To Be Continued...."

### FAMS / OKFAMS / SMOK Units
- **FAMS**: Fubuki, Ayame, Mio, Subaru (initials-based unit name).
- **OKFAMS**: Adds Okayu and Korone to FAMS.
- **SMOK**: Subaru, Mio, Okayu, Korone (without Fubuki and Ayame).
- **TsuneMOS (常MOS)**: Tokoyami Towa, Mio, Okayu, Subaru.

### AyaFubuMi (Irohanihoheto)
- Official subunit with Nakiri Ayame and Shirakami Fubuki.
- Released original song "Irowanioedomo" (2026).

### Other Notable Relationships
- **Oozora Subaru** — frequent collab partner, part of FAMS and SMOK.
- **Nakiri Ayame** — part of FAMS and AyaFubuMi unit, close friendship.
- **Tokoyami Towa** — part of TsuneMOS unit.
- **Hakui Koyori** — collaborated on the holo*27 project cover of "Animal."

## Streaming Style & Content Themes

### Common Game Genres
- **Nintendo games** (especially Splatoon, which she is exceptionally good at despite her general lack of gaming skill).
- **Indie games** — frequent indie game playthroughs.
- **Earth Defense Force series** — she is a huge fan.
- **Kusoge (trash games)** — her "wild side" emerges for these.
- **Horror games** — despite being easily scared, plays them out of GAMERS responsibility.

### Unique Content
- **Tarot card readings** — offers readings for viewers and fellow talents; one of the few Hololive members who can do this.
- **Ukulele karaoke streams** — began practicing ukulele in early 2021 and holds singing streams with it.
- **Morning Mio (朝ミオ)** — morning streams with dedicated hashtags #朝ミオ and #朝ミオニュース.
- **PON Flash News (#ポン速)** — news-style segments.

### Streaming Personality
- Calm and nurturing during normal streams, but can become loud and reactive when scared or excited.
- Often has her cat Taiga appear on stream, sometimes causing chaos (knocking over tarot cards, scratching).
- Frequently plays the straight man to more chaotic members.

## Fan Community Memes & Inside Jokes

### Hatotaurus (鳩トーラス)
- A towering pigeon/humanoid figure that often appears in Mio's streams.
- First introduced by Fubuki during a kindergarten stream, later made into L2D artwork by Izumi Sai.
- Joked as Mio's "stand" (JoJo's Bizarre Adventure reference).

### The "NoPan Health Act"
- Mio has stated she believes it is healthy to sleep without underwear, claiming this wisdom was passed down from her mother and grandmother.
- Fans refer to this as the "NoPan Health Act" and it has become a recurring joke.

### Miochun (ミオチュン)
- A chibi, bird-like version of Mio drawn by Fubuki.
- Used in fan art and memes as a cuter, smaller version of Mio.

### Chat Memes
- **ミオかわいい (Mio is cute)** — viewers spam this in chat and superchats whenever Mio is away from stream.
- **待🌲 (Mat-**🌲**)** — pun meaning "Waiting," used by viewers while waiting for stream to start.
- **ﾐﾐﾐ… (Mimimimi…)** — SFX for Mio's "laser beam," used in chat when her stream opening begins.
- **Otsumion! / Konbanmion!** — viewers echo her greeting/farewell catchphrases back to her.

### Mio-mama (ミオママ) / Mio Mom
- The most widespread fan nickname. Mio has jokingly responded that she "doesn't remember giving birth."
- Chinese fans call her 澪媽 / 狼媽 (Mio Mom / Wolf Mom).

### Height Inconsistency Meme
- Mio's listed height has varied across sources: 155cm (some wikis), 160cm (official site), and formerly 165cm (early official site). Fans joke about this inconsistency.

### Taiga and Tawa (Cats)
- **Taiga**: male cat known for interrupting streams and causing mischief (knocking things over, scratching).
- **Tawa**: male Abyssinian cat, revealed June 2021.
