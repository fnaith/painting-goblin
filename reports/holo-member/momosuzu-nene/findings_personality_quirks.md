## Personality Traits

- **Genki / High-energy** — Bubbly, cheerful, always enthusiastic on stream
- **Clumsy (dere/dokkan)** — Known for innocent mistakes (e.g., wrote "IM NENE" in glowstone in Minecraft, which read "ENENMI" from outside; fans compared it to EMINEM)
- **Persistent / Determined** — Applied to hololive **four times** before finally getting accepted into Gen 5
- **Playfully smug / competitive** — Cute smugness when winning games; pouts dramatically when losing
- **Affectionate / flirty** — Playfully flirts with fellow Holomems (Lamy, Ina, etc.)
- **"Love Warrior"** — Official epithet; describes her as having a "quiet fire in her heart"
- **Hydrohomie** — Drinks water constantly on stream; keeps a water gallon image on screen
- **Leeroy Jenkins energy** — Known for running headfirst into danger in collab games (e.g., Craftopia boss room)
- **Inclusive** — Encourages multi-language chat; knows a few English and Spanish phrases, earning the nickname "Worldwide Nenechi"

## Physical / Mannerism Quirks

- Often pouts cutely when she loses games

- Gut-punches new members (virtually) as a "NenePro" initiation ritual
