## Official Lore / Backstory

> "A girl who came from another world in order to become an idol. She loves singing and dancing, and drawing. She is training hard every day to become someone loved by many."

- She hails from "Planet Tao Tao" (also referred to as "another world")
- Her catchphrase/greeting is **"Kon-Nene~!"**
- Original bio mentioned she came from another world wearing a Chinese dress to chase her idol dream, and that her nickname in her hometown was "Taoling"
- She originally used the verbal tic "aru" (ある) at the end of sentences, which she later dropped (claiming she says it too fast to be heard)
