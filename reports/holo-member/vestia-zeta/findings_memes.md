## 2. Personality & Character Traits

- **Agent persona:** Plays up the "secret agent" theme — infiltrating the virtual world to collect data. Her lore makes her a clumsy rookie agent (codename V.7) who struggles when things don't go by the manual.
- **Cat-like curiosity:** Adopts an adorable, curious personality similar to cats.
- **Fearless:** Not afraid of horror games (Outlast 2, DEVOUR collab with Kaela & Kobo). Dislikes backseating and stream sniping.
- **Soft-hearted:** Admits she cries easily at sad scenes.
- **Pon (clumsy):** Frequently makes mistakes in games, leading to the common chant of "Pon" from chat.
- **Vintage & jazz lover:** Loves vintage things and jazz music; sang jazz songs on an unarchived karaoke stream.
- **Multilingual communicator:** Can speak Indonesian, English, Japanese, and Korean at varying levels.

## 3. Running Gags & Memes

### "Pon" (Clumsiness)
Zeta's defining comedic trait. She frequently fumbles or makes silly mistakes during gameplay, and viewers chant "Pon" (a Japanese onomatopoeia for a slip-up). This is actively embraced and referenced everywhere — the Hololive Fan Wiki even notes "the common use of 'Pon' by the Zecretaries whenever such moments occurs."

### "o7" Salute Emoji
Chat uses "o7" in the waiting room and throughout streams as a salute — referencing both the military salute gesture and Zeta's codename V.7 (V + 7 = o7 visually).

### "Mission Sutaato" (Mission Start)
Her catchphrase greeting: "a-a-a Mission Sutaato, Halo nama ku Vestia Zeta dari Hololive ID Gen 3" — delivered with a slightly broken/adorable Japanese-English-Indonesian mix.

### "Otsuzeta" (Sign-off)
A portmanteau of "otsukare" (Japanese for "good work") and "Zeta," used by both Zeta and her viewers as a farewell.

### Calling Kaela "Mommy"
Zeta frequently refers to fellow ID Gen 3 member Kaela Kovalskia as "Mommy" — an ongoing running gag in their interactions.

### Bazo the Deer
Zeta created a cute deer companion character named **Bazo** to keep her company. Bazo is treated almost like a mascot and occasionally makes appearances or is referenced.

### ZecretDialogue (Interrogation Series)
A recurring Q&A series framed as an "interrogation" where Zeta asks "Would you rather" questions. She often tries to gaslight or mischievously argue viewers into picking her preferred answer.

### Jump King: No Rage Challenge
Her Jump King "No Rage Challenge" stream is noted as **"by far the shortest stream [to exist]"** — she presumably either cleared the notoriously difficult game very fast or gave up almost immediately, either way becoming a meme.

### Zeta-nyan (Cat Persona)
Her love of cats and cat-like demeanor has led to "Cat" being listed as an official nickname. She often makes cat sounds and mannerisms.

## 4. Notable Clips & Moments

### Debut (March 25, 2022)
- Hit 100K subscribers **during** her debut stream itself.
- Reached 200K just **2 days later**.
- Reached 300K within 11 days (during a Valorant collab with Kobo Kanaeru).
- Showed off her multilingual ability (ID, EN, JP, KR) during debut.

### Horror Game Fearlessness
- Played **Outlast 2** with minimal fear, showing a tough side.
- **DEVOUR collab** with Kaela & Kobo — Zeta stayed calm even in scary late-game sections, contrasting with her usual cute persona.

### 3D Model Debut (October 15, 2023)
- A major milestone; she performed and interacted in full 3D for the first time.
- Released her debut original song **"You're Mine"** the same day.

### The Great Museum Frame Story
Zeta mentioned that one of her drawings was **framed and displayed in a museum**. She even explained the framing process and shared she has experience making her own frames.

### Acute Sense of Smell
She has mentioned having an unusually strong sense of smell, which has come up in multiple streams as a quirky personal trait.

### Her Pet Turtle
Zeta owned a pet turtle when she was younger — a small bit of cute personal history.

## 6. Relationships with Other Members

### hololive Indonesia 3rd Generation
- **Kobo Kanaeru** — Fellow ID Gen 3. They have done collabs together (Valorant, etc.). Kobo is the chaotic "tenggak" (drinking) gremlin, creating a fun dynamic with Zeta's agent persona.
- **Kaela Kovalskia** — Fellow ID Gen 3. Zeta calls her "Mommy." They have a close, playful dynamic. Kaela is the blacksmith/gamer grinder, complementing Zeta's clumsy agent.
- The three are collectively known as **"holoID Gen 3"** or **"Holoh3ro"** (their unit name).

### Airani Iofifteen (Iofi)
- Iofi has drawn artwork of Zeta (noted in the wiki gallery). Iofi is the artistic member of ID Gen 1, so art collabs happen naturally.

### Cross-Branch Collabs
- Participated in **"Mr. Schadenfreude"** cover with EN (Axel Syrios, Banzoin Hakka, Ouro Kronii, Mori Calliope) and ID members (Kobo Kanaeru, Ayunda Risu, Moona Hoshinova).
