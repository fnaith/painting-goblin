# Vestia Zeta - Research Findings: Personality, Characteristics & Streaming Style

## Personality Overview
- **Agent persona:** Plays up the "secret agent" theme — infiltrating the virtual world to collect data. A clumsy rookie agent (codename V.7) who struggles when things don't go by the manual.
- **Cat-like curiosity:** Adopts an adorable, curious personality similar to cats. Often makes cat sounds and mannerisms (Zeta-nyan).
- **Fearless:** Not afraid of horror games (Outlast 2, DEVOUR collab). Dislikes backseating and stream sniping.
- **Soft-hearted:** Admits she cries easily at sad scenes.
- **Pon (clumsy):** Frequently makes mistakes in games — defining comedic trait. Viewers chant "Pon" when she slips up.

**Voice:** Cute, slightly high-pitched. Known for her unique mix of Indonesian, English, and Japanese in speech.

## Streaming Content & Genres
- **Variety gamer:** Plays a wide range of games (Vampire Survivors, Mario Kart, APEX, Valorant, Jump King, horror games)
- **ZecretDialogue series:** Recurring Q&A sessions framed as "interrogation" — "Would you rather" questions, often gaslighting/mischievously arguing with viewers
- Karaoke streams (jazz songs, anime covers)
- Art streams (she draws her own illustrations for covers)
- Collab streams with other HoloID members

## Notable Skills

- **Singing:** Multiple original songs and cover songs; jazz-influenced style
- **Art:** Creates her own illustrations for cover song videos
- **Drawing:** Her artwork was framed and displayed in a museum
- **Frame-making:** Has experience making her own picture frames
