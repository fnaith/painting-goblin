- Official character design, outfit, and visual motifs

- VTuber model and rigging info

## Subtopic 2: Personality, Characteristics & Streaming Style
- Voice traits and speech patterns (known catchphrases)
- Personality description

- Notable skills or talents (singing, gaming, etc.)
- Fan community name and interactions

## Subtopic 3: Memes, Popular Clips & Anecdotes
- Famous moments and clips
- Running gags and memes
- Notable collabs and interactions with other members
- Fan game relevant anecdotes and lore
- Achievements and milestones

## Synthesis
Consolidate all findings into a Chinese-language report suitable for fan-game character design reference.
