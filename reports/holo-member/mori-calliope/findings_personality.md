# Mori Calliope (森美聲 / 森カリオペ) — Personality & Characteristics Research

## Overview

Mori Calliope is a virtual YouTuber (VTuber) and singer affiliated with Cover Corporation's Hololive Production. She debuted on September 12, 2020 as a member of **Hololive English - Myth**, alongside Gawr Gura, Ninomae Ina'nis, Takanashi Kiara, and Amelia Watson. Her character designer is Yukisame.

- **Debut**: September 12, 2020
- **Birthday**: April 4
- **Height**: 167 cm
- **Channel subscribers**: 2.6 million
- **Total views**: 850 million+
- **Unit**: Hololive English - Myth
- **Source**: https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents/mori-calliope/

## Lore & Character Concept

Mori Calliope is described as **"The Grim Reaper's first apprentice."** Due to modern medical care causing a decline in the reaping business, she decided to become a VTuber to harvest souls instead. Her character is reminiscent of a *shinigami* (death god). According to the official profile:

> "Despite the image her hardcore vocals and manner of speech gives off, she's actually a gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends."

## On-Stream Persona vs. Real Personality

### On-Stream Persona
- **Edgy, brash, and profane** — She frequently uses profanity and dark humor on stream, which Katie Gill of *Anime Feminist* described as an exception to the widespread use of Japanese idol themes within Hololive. Her profanity-laden rap music and manner of speech reinforce a "hardcore" image.
- **The "Grim Reaper" shtick** — She plays up death-themed jokes, calls herself "dad" to her fans, and leans into a cool, confident, "I'm the strongest" attitude.
- **Nicknamed "Tsundereaper"** — Fans affectionately call her a "tsundere reaper" for her contrast between tough exterior and soft interior.
- **Persistent and determined** — Famously spent 90+ minutes on a December 2020 livestream repeatedly begging Atlus to let her stream *Persona 3*, a moment Polygon described as a "famous part of her ongoing streaming career." She even created the hashtag #CallioP3 to rally support. The stream drew ~250,000 live viewers. *Two years later*, in January 2023, she finally got to stream the game.

### Real/Off-Stream Personality (as understood from interviews and casual moments)
- **Self-deprecating and humble** — In interviews, she often downplays her own achievements and expresses surprise at her success. In June 2022, she admitted she hadn't realized how big Hololive had become at the time of her infamous Persona 3 stream.
- **Hardworking and perfectionist** — Her Crunchyroll interview (August 2022) emphasized "the power of perseverance." She spoke openly about the struggle of balancing streaming, music production, and personal life.
- **Genuinely caring** — She shows warmth toward her fellow Hololive members, often praising their talents unprompted. The official site describes her as "a gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends."
- **Anxious but driven** — She has mentioned feeling impostor syndrome and anxiety about her career but continues to push through.

## Musical Career Focus

Mori Calliope is primarily a **rapper and singer**, and music is arguably her main focus over gaming streams.

### Style and Influences
- **Rap-centric**: She is known for fast, profanity-laden rap verses. Teddy Cambosa (Anime Corner) said her "style of rapping comes in line with the rise of other large VTubers that are now leaning towards a more Western preference instead of the Japanese idol grassroots."
- **Influences**: She cites **Japanese hip hop** as the spark that ignited her interest in rapping, and the electro-swing duo **Fake Type.** as her primary inspiration.
- **Genres explored**: Hip hop, rap rock, electro-swing, pop, ballad.

### Catchphrases
| Phrase | Context |
|---|---|
| **"Dead Beats, listen up! Your Mori's been working hard for you!"** | Official introductory catchphrase on the Hololive site |
| **"I've been waiting for this"** | Her signature Persona 3 "all-out attack" callout graphic on stream |
| **"Atlus, please let me play Persona 3"** | Infamous repeated plea from her 90-minute Persona 3 begging stream |
| **"WAGWAN" / "What's good, what's happening?"** | Common stream opening greeting |
| **"Dad"** | She frequently refers to herself as "dad" or is called "dad" by fans (in contrast to other members being "mom") |
| **"I'm horny"** (jokingly, in collabs) | Adopted as a running gag in group dynamics |

### Speech Patterns & Accent
- **Native English speaker** with a North American accent, influenced by living in Japan. She speaks fluent Japanese (used extensively in her music and JP collabs), often code-switches between English and Japanese mid-sentence.
- **Vocal fry and lower register** — Her speaking voice is naturally deeper, and she often employs vocal fry for comedic effect or to sound more "menacing."
- **Rapid-fire speech** — When excited, she speaks very fast and tends to ramble.
- **Profanity** — Known for liberal use of swearing (frequently bleeped on stream), which stands out in the Hololive EN space.

## Fanbase Name and Interaction Style

### Fan Name: **Dead Beats**
- Official fan name as listed on the Hololive profile.
- Hashtags: `#calliolive` / `#カリオライブ` (stream tags), `#callillust` / `#カリイラスト` (fan art)

### Interaction Style
- **"Dad" dynamic** — Calliope positions herself as the "dad" of the fanbase, while Dead Beats lean into the "child" role. This contrasts with the more common "mom" or "onee-san" dynamics used by other Hololive members.
- **Call-and-response** — Her songs frequently have audience participation elements (e.g., shouting along with certain lines).
- **Dark humor** — She shares death jokes and morbid humor with Dead Beats, who enthusiastically play along.
- **Genuine gratitude** — She frequently expresses sincere thanks to Dead Beats for their support, especially during milestone streams.
- **Community-driven events** — The #CallioP3 campaign was entirely fan-driven, with Dead Beats spamming art and tweets to pressure Atlus.

## Relationship Dynamics with Other Hololive Members

### Hololive English - Myth
- **Takanashi Kiara**: Calliope's most notable dynamic is with Kiara, forming the popular ship **"Takamori."** They have a frequent "bickering couple" routine on stream, with Kiara playfully flirting and Calliope acting embarrassed or tsundere. They released a collaborative single "Fire N Ice" (2023) and have done many collab streams together.
- **Gawr Gura**: They collaborated on the single "Q" (2021), which charted at #7 on Billboard World Digital Song Sales. Calliope often teases Gura affectionately.
- **Ninomae Ina'nis**: Calliope has frequently praised Ina's art skills. They have a calmer, more relaxed dynamic.
- **Amelia Watson**: Calliope and Ame share a "chaos gremlin" dynamic, often doing unhinged collab streams together.

### Other Hololive Members
- **Hoshimachi Suisei** (JP Branch): A close collaborator. They released "CapSule" (2022) and "Wicked" (2022). Both are music-focused talents with a mutual admiration dynamic. Suisei is one of the JP members Calliope is closest to.
- **Tsunomaki Watame** (JP Branch): Released "Reaper vs. Sheep -Ouen ver.-" and "-Kenko ver.-" (2022). Funny "reaper vs. sheep" rivalry bit.
- **Nerissa Ravencroft** (Advent): Released "OVER//RIDE" (2025) and a theme song for *Chaos Zero: Nightmare*.
- **Tokoyami Towa** (JP Branch): Released "Queen of the Night" (2025).
- **IRyS**: Released "START AGAIN" (2024) as part of a group single.

### General Reputation
- Calliope is well-respected within Hololive for her music production skills and work ethic.
- She often serves as a bridge between the EN and JP branches due to her Japanese fluency.
- She is frequently roped into collabs as the "straight man" (tsukkomi) role, particularly in chaotic EN group streams.

## Visual Design Details

### Name Etymology
- **"Mori"** (森) means "forest" in Japanese.
- **"Calliope"** is the Greek muse of epic poetry — fitting her rap battle / lyrical style.
- Her scythe's official name is **Guillotine**, but fans also call it **"Ricky"** (origin unknown; a community nickname).

*Source: hololivevtuber.fandom.com*

### First holoEN Debut
Calliope was the very first member of Hololive English to debut on September 12, 2020 at 6:00 PM PST.

*Source: hololivevtuber.fandom.com*

### Primary Design (by Yukisame)
- **Color palette**: Black, white, pink, and red — evoking a death/ shinigami theme with gothic lolita and punk influences.
- **Hair**: Long, flowing pink hair (gradient from hot pink to pale pink), styled with a large black beret-style hat with a skull emblem.
- **Eyes**: Heterochromia — one red eye, one blue/purple eye.
- **Outfit**: A black-and-white gothic dress with corset detailing, detached sleeves, and a short pleated skirt. Pink accents on the trim. Thigh-high black boots with platform soles.
- **Horns**: Small black horns protruding from her head, visible above her hairline, reinforcing the demon/shinigami theme.
- **Scythe**: Her signature accessory — a large, ornate scythe (her "reaping tool") that she often holds or has floating beside her. It appears in her 2D model, 3D model, and most of her official art and MV animations.
- **Additional accessories**: A skull choker, cross-shaped earrings, belt with a heart-and-chain motif, detached fingerless gloves.

### Alternate Outfits
- Various costume variants have been released over the years, including a more casual streetwear outfit and formal concert attire.
