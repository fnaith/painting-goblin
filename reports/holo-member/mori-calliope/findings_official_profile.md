## Character Lore

Mori Calliope is described as **"The Grim Reaper's first apprentice."** Due to modern medical care causing a decline in the reaping business, Calliope decided to become a VTuber to harvest souls instead. The ascended souls of people vaporized by wholesome VTuber interactions also go to her. Despite her hardcore vocals and manner of speech, she is a gentle-hearted girl who cares greatly for her friends.

Her character is reminiscent of a *shinigami* (death god).

*Source: [Hololive Production official talent page](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents/mori-calliope/)*

## Hololive English -Myth- Generation Context

Mori Calliope is a member of **hololive English -Myth-**, the first English-language generation of Hololive Production, which debuted September 2020. The five members are:

1. **Mori Calliope** — Grim Reaper's apprentice (rapper/singer)
2. **Takanashi Kiara** — Phoenix/restaurant owner
3. **Ninomae Ina'nis** — Priestess of the Ancient Ones (artist)
4. **Gawr Gura** — Shark from Atlantis (the "smol shark")
5. **Watson Amelia** — Time-traveling detective (retired from regular streaming in 2024)

*Source: [Hololive Production talents page (Myth group)](https://hololive.hololivepro.com/en/talents)*

## Voice Actor / Streamer Identity

Mori Calliope's real name has **not been publicly disclosed** by Cover Corporation. As is standard for Hololive talents, the person behind the character maintains a private identity separate from the character's persona. However, it is widely known that she is a Canadian-born English/Japanese bilingual rapper and singer who previously worked as an English teacher in Japan.

## Music Career Highlights

- **Major label debut:** Signed with EMI Records in April 2022
- **Debut EP:** *Dead Beats* (Oct 17, 2020) — #23 Oricon Digital Albums
