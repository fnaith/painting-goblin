- **September 12, 2020**: Debuted as part of **Hololive English - Myth**, the first English branch of Hololive Production, alongside Gawr Gura, Ninomae Ina'nis, Takanashi Kiara, and Amelia Watson.

  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori_Calliope

  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mori_Calliope

## 2. The Infamous Persona 3 Livestream (Dec 2020)

- **December 3, 2020**: In one of her most legendary streaming moments, Calliope spent **90+ minutes** begging Atlus to let her livestream *Persona 3*. She sat in silence, using only her model and text overlays saying "Atlus, please." The stream drew **~250,000 concurrent viewers** and spawned the hashtag **#CallioP3**.
  - The stream went viral and was covered by Polygon, Dot Esports, and other outlets.
  - **January 2023**: Over two years later, she finally succeeded — she livestreamed *Persona 3* on her channel for the first time after Atlus softened its streaming restrictions.
  - Sources:
    - https://www.polygon.com/2020/12/3/22151131/mori-calliope-vtuber-youtube-livestream-atlus-persona-3
    - https://dotesports.com/streaming/news/two-years-later-mori-calliopes-persona-3-begging-has-finally-paid-off

## 7. Character Lore & Inside Jokes

- **Lore**: Calliope is the Grim Reaper's first apprentice. Due to slow business (people living longer), she became a VTuber to collect souls. Her scythe is named *Guillotine*.
- **Fan Name**: Her fans are called **"Dead Beats"** — a pun on "deadbeat" and her musical beat-making.
- **Takanashi Kiara (collab dynamic)**: Calliope and Kiara have a famously chaotic love-hate (lore-wise: reaper vs. phoenix) dynamic. They collaborate frequently as the **"Takamori"** ship, which is one of the most popular dynamics in Hololive EN.
- **"Guillotine" catchphrase**: Her scythe and a recurring prop in streams.
- **Yung Sh1n1gam1 Bo1**: An April Fools' 2022 alter ego — released a mixtape *My Mixtape* as a parody SoundCloud rapper persona.

## 8. Fan Culture & Running Gags

- **"Dead Beats" as fanbase**: The name has spawned community-wide motifs of skulls, coffins, and death-themed humor.
- **"Rapping skills"**: Calliope is celebrated as one of the few VTubers who primarily raps rather than sings in a traditional idol style, cited by Anime Feminist as "an exception to the widespread use of Japanese idol themes within Hololive."
  - Source: https://www.animefeminist.com/idols-gone-viral/...

- **"Being bad at games"**: A running gag that Calliope is notoriously terrible at video games despite loving them, leading to frequent rage and self-deprecation during gameplay streams.
- **"Ollie/Undertaker" connection**: Her design and death theme frequently attract comparisons to wrestling undertaker gimmicks.
