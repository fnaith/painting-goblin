## Source: Hololive Fan Wiki

## Personality Traits
- **Gamer**: Loves the thrill of winning
- **Gremlin energy**: Noisy, energetic, unpredictable, chaotic, sometimes annoying
- **Obsessive and determined**: Incessant with memes and requests
- **Chaotic Good alignment**: Makes rude jokes occasionally but not mean-spirited; goes to great lengths to make others laugh
- **Gentle side**: Dedicated to her Grems (fans)
- **Good friends with Cecilia Immergreen**: Ceci acts as a foil to her wacky outbursts

- Uses "Honk Shooah" when snoring (referencing "Hawk Tuah")
