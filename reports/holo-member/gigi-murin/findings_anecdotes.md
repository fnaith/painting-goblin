## Notable Events & Memes

### Boat Goes Binted
- Gigi popularized this variant of "Bogos Binted" during a Minecraft stream with Mori Calliope in early 2025
- She was referring to a Minecraft boat
- Repeated the phrase multiple times, spawning fan animations
- She later commented on how the meme became popular, explaining she enjoys reviving memes

### League of Legends Obsession
- Known for constantly trying to convince other Hololive talents to play League of Legends with her
- Most notably, she persistently tried to get Mori Calliope to play LoL
- A fan animation by @noaqi77 about Gigi begging Calliope to play LoL received over 1.2 million views

### Nerissa Ravencroft's Obsession
- On February 10, 2025, Nerissa Ravencroft publicly declared her obsession with Gigi on Twitter/X
- Nerissa stated: "I am becoming obsessed with Gigi, everytime I see a Gigi clip or animation or art I have to like it. I have to. I love her. She is my everything."
- The post received over 34,000 likes

### "The Fister" Title
- Gigi introduces herself with the line "Today you may call me 'The Fister'!"
- This has become a running joke in the community

### D&D Character Sheet Trivia
- During her debut, Gigi used a D&D 5e character sheet format
- She accidentally reversed the Athletics and Acrobatics modifiers and didn't apply negative Intelligence score penalties
- This shows her "not caring about thinking" persona in a real way

### Duo with Cecilia Immergreen
- Known as "the brainrot duo" by fans (🍵👧)
- Cecilia acts as the calm foil to Gigi's chaotic energy
- Their dynamic is a fan-favorite

### Fujoshi Confession
- Gigi has mentioned reading yaoi (BL manga) "for the love of the game"
- The community ran with this, creating memes about her being a fujoshi
