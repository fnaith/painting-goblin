- **Professional**: Known for punctuality with streams and events.

## 4. Memes & Cultural Impact

### "AWSL" — #1 Buzzword of 2019
- Originated from Fubuki's Bilibili streams.
- "AWSL" = "Ah Wo Si Le" (I'm dying / too cute).
- Became Bilibili's #1 buzzword of 2019 with **3.29 million mentions**.
- Cemented her popularity with the Chinese audience.

### "Friend" Meme
- From Minecraft: Fubuki ate another player's flesh in-game and, when confronted, claimed they were "friends" and it was fine.
- The phrase "Friend" became a recurring joke used when someone does something questionable.

### "It's red~" (Shiny Magikarp)
- **The legendary Magikarp hunt** (Pokémon Brilliant Diamond):
  - 27 days of streaming
  - 26 dedicated streams
  - 10,000+ fishing attempts
  - Despair and joy when she finally encountered the shiny.
  - Became one of the most memorable Pokémon grinds in Hololive history.

### Kurokami Fubuki
- Alter ego with black hair, associated with cool/sultry singing performances.
- Represents her "dark side" as a fox spirit.

### Scatman John Impressions
- Fubuki's Scatman John cover/impressions went viral.
- Led to a collaboration with **The8BitDrummer** (popular music YouTuber).

## 7. Interesting Stories & Anecdotes

### The Cardboard Box Era
Early in her career, Fubuki didn't have a proper streaming setup and was known for using a cardboard box as a makeshift monitor stand/streaming backdrop. This evolved into her "box fans" concept in Minecraft, where fans sit in cardboard boxes to watch her.

### The Friend Meat Incident (Minecraft)
In early Hololive Minecraft SMP, Fubuki killed and ate another player's food/meat. When confronted, she simply replied "Friend" as if that justified everything. This became a legendary Hololive meme and is referenced anytime someone in Hololive does something questionable to a friend.

### Gacha Addiction Confession
Fubuki has openly talked about being a gacha game whale, spending significant amounts of money on mobile games. This has become a running joke in the community.

### Bilibili Bridge Builder
Fubuki was instrumental in bridging the Hololive JP and Chinese fanbases via her Bilibili presence. The "AWSL" meme originating from her streams is a testament to her cross-cultural impact.

### From Shy to Pillar
Fubuki has openly discussed her anxiety early in her career. Watching her grow from a nervous streamer to one of the most respected senpai in Hololive is a fan-favorite arc.
