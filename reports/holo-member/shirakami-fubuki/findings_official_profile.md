## Official Character Design

### Appearance
- **Species:** Fox girl (kitsune) — white fox with animal ears and a fluffy tail
- **Hair:** Long white/silver hair with a prominent cowlick/ahoge
- **Eyes:** Blue/teal
- **Outfit colors:** Predominantly white, light blue, and blue accents
- **Clothing:** A white-and-blue Japanese-style outfit / sailor-inspired top with a red ribbon/bow tie, pleated blue skirt, white thigh-high socks, and brown shoes
- **Accessories:** Fox ears with white inner fur, a large fluffy white tail, a small red ribbon at the collar

### Theme Motif
- **Fox (kitsune)** is her core motif — her name "Shirakami" (白上) means "white hair/fur" and "Fubuki" (吹雪) means "blizzard/snowstorm"
- The Japanese onomatopoeia for a fox's bark is **"kon kon"** — which sounds like "corn" → hence her representative emoji is 🌽
- Her catchphrase greeting plays on "kon": "Good **foxy** day!" / "Konban **kitsune**" (good evening + fox)
- She is frequently (jokingly) mistaken for a cat by viewers, which she passionately corrects: **"Neko janai! Kitsune da!"** ("I'm not a cat! I'm a fox!")

### Outfits / Costume Variants
1. **Original (Main)** — White/blue sailor-style top, blue skirt, bow tie (2018)
2. **Casual/2nd outfit** — Pink sweater, scarf, casual skirt (2019)
3. **New Year's kimono** — Traditional Japanese New Year outfit (2020)
4. **Bilibili million subscriber outfit** — Chinese-style dress variation (2020)
5. **Birthday 5th outfit** — Special birthday dress (2020)
6. **"FubukiTEK"** — Cyberpunk/techwear outfit with twin-tail hair option
7. **Main Ver.3.0 refresh** — Updated main outfit replacing the original (June 2023)
8. **3D swimsuit** — Summer/swimsuit 3D model
9. **Kurokami (alter ego)** — Black-haired version with more aggressive/stern personality
- **3D live/idol outfits** — Concert performance costumes

## Official Lore / Character Description

From the official hololive website:

> *"White-haired animal-eared otaku fox. She loves talking with people and will be happy if you give her the time of day. She aims to become a top otaku idol while enjoying each and every day."*

### Extended Character Notes
- Described as a self-proclaimed **otaku** who loves games, anime, and manga
- Known within hololive as the **"Meme Queen"** — famous for short skit videos, memes, and comedy content
- Acts as a **pillar/support figure** within hololive — often helping other members behind the scenes
- Known for being a **whale (high spender)** in gacha/mobile games
- Has an alter ego named **Kurokami Fubuki** (Kuro-chan) — black-haired, more aggressive personality, birthday September 6
- Her manager is referred to as **"Vice-Manager Intelligent Unicorn"** — a corn character that assists with streams
- Holds many variant forms in memes: Yukkuri, Shark, Crab (Karkinos), Pigeon, etc.

## Notes for Fan Game Character Design

- **Color palette:** White (#FFFFFF), light blue (#87CEEB), deep blue (#1E3A8A), red accent (#FF4444), warm brown for eyes
- **Silhouette:** Fox ears + cowlick/ahoge + fluffy tail + skirt are the key identifiable traits
- **Emoji association:** 🌽 is the universal symbol — incorporate corn motifs subtly (buttons, accessories, etc.)
- **Personality:** Friendly, meme-loving, otaku-coded, supportive "big sister" energy but also easily flustered when teased
- **Voice direction:** Energetic, slightly high-pitched, very expressive, often shifts to a lower "Kurokami" tone for jokes
- **Mascots to consider:** Oruyanke (bamboo shoot with fox ears), Su-Corns (corn-headed fans), Fubuchun (bird)
