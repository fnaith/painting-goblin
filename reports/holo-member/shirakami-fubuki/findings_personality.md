## 1. Personality

### Official Description
"Good foxy day! I'm Shirakami Fubuki! White-haired animal-eared otaku fox. She loves talking with people and will be happy if you give her the time of day. She aims to become a top otaku idol while enjoying each and every day."

### Core Traits
- **Initially shy & timid**: Debuted as a fumbling, shy girl who hid off-screen when embarrassed. Quickly ran away from cringe.
- **Now confident & outgoing**: Has grown tremendously—now has the stamina to MC 6-hour events (HoloComi) and sing/MC most of a concert. The "evolved" Fubuki is talkative, quick-witted, and charismatic.
- **Extremely caring**: The "Catherine Mama" of the Hololive ARK servers—helped supply materials to everyone without asking for anything in return. Shouldered the pillar of Hololive in its early days and kept it steady (confirmed by Natsuiro Matsuri).
- **Befriends everyone**: Noted skill at making friends across generations, branches (Holostars included), and even indie/corporate VTubers outside Hololive.
- **Meme-savvy**: Deeply aware of internet memes, eastern and western. Actively participates in the meme ecosystem around her. Posts memes from Reddit on Twitter.
- **Easily embarrassed**: Prone to blushing and stammering when teased—especially by Matsuri, Flare, or in member-only streams.
- **Gets agitated when called a cat**: Frequently mistaken for a cat by new viewers. Will angrily retort: *"NEKO JANAI! KITSUNE JAI!"* ("I'm not a cat! I'm a fox!")—but will also intentionally do cat-like things (nya~) for the bit.
- **Whale in gacha games**: Known for shockingly high spending in FGO, Twisted Wonderland, Arknights, etc. Streams her rolls.
- **Sentimental**: Gets emotional in games when turnip stocks crash (Animal Crossing) or neighbors leave. Shows raw emotion during bad gacha streaks.
- **Prankster**: Enjoys subtle pranks in Minecraft, has a "Mysterious Mystery Mask" persona. Enjoys messing with friends (the Marine-Shion wake-up RTA is a legendary example).

### How Fans Perceive Her
- **"Queen of Memes" / "Meme Queen"**: Her Scatman cover, recorder covers, and general meme-awareness earned her this title.
- **"Friendzone Fubuki"**: Famously friendzones chat. When viewers call her "wife," she replies "Friends!" Coined the term "Waifriend" (hybrid of wife + friend).
- **"FBK" / "Friend"**: Common affectionate nicknames in the community.
- **"Burger Fox"**: From a viral clip where she excitedly ate Wendy's hamburgers in the US, declaring American burgers are "on a whole different level."
- **"Cat" (affectionately/jokingly)**: Fans love to tease her by calling her a cat because she acts cat-like despite being a fox.
- **"Alien" / "Scatman"**: From her meme songs.
- **Pillar of Hololive**: Widely regarded as one of the most important foundational talents who held early Hololive together.

## 3. Talents, Hobbies & Skills

### In-Character (Lore)
- Fox kitsune (white fox with animal ears and tail)
- Self-proclaimed "otaku fox" and "otaku idol"
- Lives in a log cabin near an elf forest (per debut lore)
- Aims to become a top otaku idol

### IRL Talents / Hobbies

#### Music & Singing
- **Original songs**: "Say! Fanfare!", "KINGWORLD", "KONKON Beats", "Hi Fine FOX!!", "VOID Recorder"
- **Rhythm games specialist**: Excels at jubeat (her main), also plays Muse Dash, maimai, SDVX, CHUNITHM, DDR
- Her song "Say! Fanfare!" was added to Muse Dash as free DLC
- Multiple songs added to jubeat Ave. for her birthday

#### Art
- **Skilled artist**: Draws her own thumbnails, mascots, and fan art
- Created mascots used by other members: Hatoutorus, Poyoyo, Gamer Birds (ホロライブゲーマーチュン), Oruyanke
- Posts original artwork on Twitter regularly

#### Gaming
- **Wide genre range**: Plays almost anything—FPS (admits terrible aim), horror (claims to hate but streams often), racing (Mario Kart), survival (ARK), Minecraft
- **Notoriously bad driver**: In Mario Kart and other racing games, she's known for terrible driving skills
- **Gacha whale**: Heavy spender in FGO, Twisted Wonderland, Arknights, Princess Connect, Azur Lane, SINoALICE, Touken Ranbu
- **Rhythm games**: Competent player across multiple arcade rhythm games
- **Notable gaming moments**:
  - Shiny Pokémon hunt in Brilliant Diamond—27 days, 26 streams, 10,000+ fishing attempts for Shiny Magikarp (coined "It's red~" meme)
  - Ark "Dodo Slayer"—caused and then solved the Dodo population explosion
  - Tetris 99 trauma—placed 98th in under 3 minutes, caused her to turn into a "Yukkuri"

#### Other Skills
- **Drawing & MMD**: Creates MMD model animations for short videos
- **MC/Hosting**: MC'd HoloComi (6-hour event), sang and MC'd Nonstop Story concert
- **Meme production**: Makes short skit videos for Twitter/YouTube; deeply meme-literate
- **Eating spicy food**: Can handle super spicy food (proved in Super Spicy Yakisoba MAX END collab)

### Likes & Dislikes
- **Loves**: Green tea (her staple stream drink), black tea, orange juice, fruit gummies, jerky, Yukimi Daifuku, plush toys, reading books
- **Strongly dislikes**: Bitter things
- **Favorite seiyuu**: Nakamura Yuichi
- **Favorite mecha**: SVMS-01E Graham's Union Flag Custom (Gundam 00), Black Sarena (Nadesico)
- **Loves ZOIDS** (owns Gatling Fox set)
- **Oshis in games**: Silverash (Arknights), Leona (Twisted Wonderland), Stefano Valentini (The Evil Within 2)

## 6. Quirks & Unique Traits

### Kurokami Fubuki (Alter Ego)
- "Another me" — same person but completely different personality and speech
- **Personality**: Aggressive, harsh, no tact, verbally strong, hates embarrassing things, pushes work to others
- **Appearance**: Only has 3D body (no 2D), appears in sprite form
- **Birthday**: September 6
- **Favorite drink**: Cola
- **Favorite food**: Meat
- **Fan name**: Servants (Slaves)
- Debuted September 7, 2019 (first appeared February 2019)
- Collabed with Matsuri's alter ego "Natsuiro Matsuhiko"

### Vice-Manager Intelligent Unicorn
- A corn that helps with stream management and game advice
- No gender (is a corn)
- Uses "boku" to refer to self
- Favorite drink: fresh water
- Handles Fubuki's Twitter account sometimes

### Famous Memes / Viral Moments

1. **"I'm. Scatman"** — Her cover of Scatman John, became her most-viewed video. Remixed by The8BitDrummer, spawned thousands of remixes. Fubuki thanked the remixers.

2. **"Burger Fox"** — Eating Wendy's in the US; "American hamburgers are on a whole different level." Poor English pronunciation is part of the charm.

3. **Marine-Shion Wake-up RTA** — Marine and Shion overslept for a collab; Fubuki streamed a 4-hour countdown speedrun of them waking up. #1 trending on JP Twitter. Showcases her trolling + caretaker duality.

4. **Friendzone Fubuki** — Rejects all marriage proposals with "Friends!" Said she'd stab her partner if they cheated.

5. **"It's red~" (赤いなぁ～)** — From her 27-day Shiny Magikarp hunt. Every time she caught a regular red Magikarp she'd sigh "It's red~" — became a catchphrase even other members quoted.

6. **Hiding on Reddit** — Confirmed she has a Reddit account but won't reveal username. Started a massive guessing game in r/Hololive.

7. **"I am NOT a cat!"** — Perpetual denial of being a cat despite cat-like behavior. One of her most iconic reactions.

8. **"Neko janai! Kitsune jai!"** — The full catchphrase when defending her fox identity.

9. **Dodo Apocalypse / Dodo Slayer** — In ARK, accidentally bred too many dodos then exterminated them.

10. **FubukiTEK** — A model variant with twintails and a more "cyber" look.

### Other Unique Quirks
- **Shotacon tendencies**: Admits to liking shota content; asked Okayu and Subaru to do shota impressions. Tries to hide it but doesn't deny it.
- **Kamen Rider fan**: Specifically loves Kamen Rider Kabuto. Has covered Kamen Rider songs.
- **Watched her own mama (Nagishiro Mito) before becoming a VTuber**: Already knew and admired her artist before joining Hololive.
- **Poor sense of direction/timing**: Known for being bad at driving in games, oversleeping (broke a no-oversleeping streak).
- **Falls into food comas**: Overslept after eating too much, leading to the No-Synergy group formation.
- **Traumatized by Tetris 99**: Lost at 98th place in under 3 minutes, turned into a Yukkuri (slow-speaking meme version of herself).
- **Creates furniture/objects of herself**: There is an Air Humidifier shaped like her that dispenses Asacoco liquid.
- **The Many Variants**: Has appeared as a cat, Yukkuri-head, shark, eel, fish, pigeon, crab (Karkanos from ARK), and more.
