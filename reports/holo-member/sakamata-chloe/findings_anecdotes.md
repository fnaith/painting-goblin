# Sakamata Chloe (沙花叉クロヱ) — Notable Anecdotes & Events

## 1. Memorable Moments and Milestones

### Debut & Rapid Growth (2021)
- **November 26, 2021:** holoX debuted with an announcement teaser. Chloe achieved **100,000 YouTube subscribers** the same day.
- **November 29, 2021:** Debut stream. Hit **200,000 subscribers** immediately.
- **November 30, 2021:** Hit **300,000 subscribers** (day 2).
- **December 1, 2021:** Hit **400,000 subscribers** (day 3).
- **December 3, 2021:** Hit **500,000 subscribers** (day 5).
- **December 10, 2021:** Hit **600,000 subscribers** (day 12).
- This made her one of the fastest-growing VTubers at debut, reaching 600K in under two weeks.
- *(Sources: hololive.wiki milestones, @holo_analytics Twitter)*

### 700K–1M Subscriber Milestones (2022–2023)
- **January 6, 2022:** 700,000 subscribers.
- **April 7, 2022:** 800,000 subscribers.
- **February 18, 2023:** Achieved **1,000,000 subscribers** (Silver Play Button) during an **endurance karaoke stream** — a landmark emotional moment for her and her fans.
- *(Source: hololive.wiki)*

### 3D Debut & Anniversary (2022)
- **June 13, 2022:** 3D model debut stream. Chloe cited this as the stream she'd recommend to first-time viewers, saying "I feel like I put a lot of myself into it."
- **November 29, 2022:** 1st anniversary 3D live — revealed her 3D idol outfit.
- *(Source: official Q&A, hololive.wiki)*

### Conclusion of Streaming Activities (2024–2025)
- **November 29, 2024:** During her **3rd anniversary livestream**, Chloe announced she would be **concluding her streaming activities** effective **January 26, 2025**.
- **Reason:** Cited physical health deterioration (cervical spine, back issues) and overworking. She had been pushing herself hard, leading to burnout.
- This made her the **first holoX member** to step back from regular streaming.
- Rather than a traditional "graduation" (full departure from the agency), COVER Corp introduced a new category: **"Affiliate member"** status. Under this arrangement, Chloe remains affiliated with hololive production and may participate in select future projects on a limited basis — similar to "a graduate visiting their alma mater."
- **January 26, 2025:** Final stream — a 3D live performance.
- **March 31, 2025:** First post-retirement return — released a cover of **"Turing Love" (チューリングラブ)** with **Murasaki Shion**, marking her first musical activity since the conclusion stream.
- *(Sources: COVER Corp official note, hololivepro.com announcement, hololive.wiki, fandom wiki)*

### COVER Corp's New "Conclusion of Streaming Activities" Concept
COVER Corp published an official note (note.cover-corp.com) explaining this new departure model:
- Traditional "graduation" meant all activities end (save for a few ongoing projects).
- "Conclusion of streaming activities" ends regular streaming and event participation, but leaves the door open for **limited future appearances** if both the talent and company agree.
- The company emphasized there is **no hierarchy** between "graduation" and "conclusion of streaming activities" — both are equally valid paths.
- *(Source: https://note.cover-corp.com/n/ne3a8b7a553c0)*

## 2. Relationships with Other Hololive Members

### Secret Society holoX (Gen-Mates)
- **La+ Darknesss** (leader), **Takane Lui** (secretary), **Hakui Koyori** (R&D), **Kazama Iroha** (guard captain).
- As the "intern/fixer" of holoX, Chloe occupied the lowest-ranking but most operationally critical role in the group dynamic.
- The gen-mates frequently collabed for group streams, karaoke, and events. Their group dynamic was characterized by chaotic, sibling-like banter.
- holoX had a **serialized official manga**, *"holoX MEETing"* (Shonen Jump Plus, starting October 2022), featuring all five members.
- *(Source: hololive.wiki, Shonen Jump Plus announcement)*

### "Kanaken" (かなけん) — Duo with Amane Kanata
- A well-known pairing between Chloe and **Amane Kanata** (hololive 4th Gen).
- The duo name "Kanaken" combines "Kanata" and "ken" (from Chloe's orca/killer whale motif — "shachiken").
- They released a cover of **"Rabbit Hole" (ラビットホール)** by DECO*27 together.
- Their dynamic was notable for its playful, teasing energy.
- *(Source: hololive.wiki cover discography)*

### "Shioshachi" (塩シャチ) — Pairing with Murasaki Shion
- A fan-favorite pairing with **Murasaki Shion** (hololive 2nd Gen).
- The name combines "Shio" (from Shion) and "Shachi" (orca).
- Known for their contrasting personalities — Shion's bratty/energetic persona vs. Chloe's calmer, tsundere-leaning demeanor.
- On **March 31, 2025** — well after Chloe's retirement from streaming — the duo released a cover of **"Turing Love" (チューリングラブ)**, proving their bond outlasted her regular streaming career.

### Hakui Koyori
- Particularly close gen-mate. They frequently collabed and released a duet cover of **"Identity" (アイデンティティ)** by Kanaria.
- Koyori and Chloe were often seen as a natural pair within holoX, balancing each other's energy levels.

### UMISEA
- Chloe was part of the **UMISEA** unit (an aquatic-themed sub-unit within hololive), which includes talents with ocean/sea motifs.
- This group participated in special events and merchandise.

### Magical Girl holoWitches!
- A cross-generation magical girl unit featuring **Kanata, Miko, Luna, Marine, Shion, and Chloe**.
- Chloe's holoWitches! outfit concept art was drawn by ancotaku.
- *(Source: hololive.wiki)*

### holoX MEETing Manga
- Serialized in **Shonen Jump Plus** (digital edition of Weekly Shonen Jump) beginning October 1, 2022.
- Features all five holoX members in comedic slice-of-life scenarios.
- A significant milestone — being serialized in one of Japan's most prestigious manga platforms.
- *(Source: Shonen Jump Plus Twitter announcement via hololive.wiki)*

## 4. Interesting Stories and Fan-Favorite Moments

### The Debut Eye Mask Incident
- During her debut stream, Chloe didn't realize her Live2D model didn't have an eyepatch/eye mask on it. As a result, she couldn't see the chat comments scrolling by and was visibly confused at times. This became a memorable debut blooper that fans still reference.

### "#orcart" Community Name Origin
- Chloe's fan community is known as "Orcart" (オーカート), a portmanteau blending "orca" (her motif animal) with "heart". The name was coined by fans rather than Chloe herself — she later mentioned that she was not consulted about the name, but accepted it warmly.

### The Orca/Fish Roleplay Consistency
Chloe maintained her orca/killer whale persona with remarkable consistency across her entire streaming career:
- **Greeting:** "Chomp, chomp, chomp! It's time to eat!" / "Baku baku baku~n!"
- **Closing:** "Thanks for the food." (ごちそうさまでした)
- **Fan name:** "Handlers" (飼育員さん / Zookeepers) — those who handle/feed the orca
- **Viewers' waiting message:** `＜＞＜` (ASCII art of a fish)
- **Fan mark:** 🎣 (fishing rod)
- Even her name "Sakamata" refers to a type of fish (orca/killer whale in some Japanese dialects).
- This level of sustained roleplay immersion was a hallmark of her brand.

### Sleepy Girl Persona
- When asked what she does off-stream, her answer was simply: **"I sleep!"**
- She listed "sleep" among her likes, alongside mentaiko, shortcakes, and music.
- Her low-energy/lethargic persona contrasted sharply with the "secret agent fixer" lore, creating an endearing gap moe (反差萌) effect.

### Tsundere Reputation
- The official profile explicitly states she "denies it vehemently" when people say she's guarded and not upfront.
- This official endorsement of her tsundere qualities became a beloved character trait.
- Fans affectionately teased her about it, which she would (in character) deny with increasing intensity.

### 3D Debut as a Defining Moment
- When asked which stream she'd recommend for first-time viewers, she chose her **3D reveal stream**, saying "I feel like I put a lot of myself into it."
- This was notable because most talents typically recommend a debut stream or a karaoke stream — Chloe's choice of the 3D reveal highlighted how much personal effort and emotion she invested in that specific performance.

### Music as Her True Passion
- Unlike many VTubers who treat music as one of many activities, Chloe's official bio and Q&A emphasized **music as her primary creative outlet**.
- She composed music, wrote lyrics, sang, and pursued formal voice training.
- Her three official dreams: (1) Solo live on a big stage, (2) Get a statue figure, (3) Collab with an aquarium — the first and third are music/live-performance related.

### The Graduation That Wasn't a Graduation
- Chloe's departure from regular streaming set a **precedent** within hololive. She was the first talent to use the new "Affiliate member" / "Conclusion of Streaming Activities" framework.
- COVER Corp's detailed note about this new category suggested the company was actively seeking alternatives to permanent graduation, possibly influenced by fan sentiment and talent preferences.
- This created an interesting legacy: even in her departure, Chloe helped define a new path for future talents.

### Hololive's First "Affiliate" Member
- Chloe's post-January 2025 status as an **Affiliate** (rather than graduated) means she retains the potential for surprise appearances, special projects, or anniversary events in the future.
- This "alumni" model was framed by COVER Corp as similar to a graduate visiting their alma mater — a more flexible, less final endpoint than traditional graduation.
