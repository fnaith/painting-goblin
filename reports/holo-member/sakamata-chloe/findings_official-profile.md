## Official Lore — "The Secret Society Fixer"

Official Hololive profile description:

> "Chomp, chomp, chooomp!"
>
> An intern who acts as a fixer and cleaner for Secret Society holoX.
> Calm and composed at all times, she carries out her orders without so much as batting an eyelid.
> She loves music and can be found listening to it every chance she gets.
> And while she denies it vehemently, she apparently tends to come across as guarded and not very upfront with her thoughts, only letting people get very occasional glimpses into how she really feels.

Key character notes:
- She is an **intern** within holoX (lowest rank, but integral)
- Her job is **fixer and cleaner** — handling dirty work, disposing of evidence, eliminating obstacles
- Portrayed as calm, composed, and professional on the surface
- Has a hidden/guarded personality — not upfront with her true feelings
- Loves music intensely (composing, lyric-writing, singing, voice training)
- Denies being guarded despite clear evidence otherwise

- **Illustrator / Character Designer:** パセリ (Parsley / Parsley_F) — https://twitter.com/Parsley_F
- **Hair:** Long silver-white hair with blue/teal undertones, styled with two distinctive fin-like protrusions at the back (evoking orca/killer whale dorsal fins)
- **Outfit:** Black-and-white theme — a fitted dark dress/suit with white accents, layered with translucent blue fabric elements suggesting water/aquatic themes
- **Headwear:** A black peaked cap with a holoX emblem (military/agent aesthetic)
- **Accessories:** Black gloves, asymmetrical legwear (one thigh-high, one knee-high), choker with a blue gem, belt with pouches/tools
- **Color scheme:** Black, white, and blue/teal (orca/ killer whale colors)

- **Fan mark:** 🎣 (fishing rod and fish)

## Theme & Motif Summary

- **Archetype:** Secret agent / marine creature / mysterious intern
- **Secret Society role:** Fixer and cleaner — handles elimination and cleanup for holoX
- **Marine biology motif:** Orca/killer whale (black-and-white coloring, fin-like hair, aquatic accessories)
- **Fishing motif:** Fan mark 🎣, fan name "Handlers" (fishing/handler terminology), fish emoji in chat
- **Name etymology:**
  - 沙花叉 (Sakamata) — also the name of a type of fish (sakamata = killer whale / orca in some Japanese dialects)
  - クロヱ (Chloe/Kuroe) — "kuro" (black) fitting the dark/mysterious theme
- **Personality paradox:** Officially described as "calm and composed" but known to be guarded and not upfront; her streaming persona often shows a much more energetic, emotional, and chaotic side
