## Official VTuber Lore / Character Setting

> *"A cat being raised by an old woman that runs an onigiri (rice ball) store. She streams from the computer in her grandma's room."*

- She is a **nekomata** (a mythical two-tailed cat yōkai), though her design shows a single tail tipped with a bell/fire-like puff.
- Her real birthdate is unknown (she was adopted by her grandmother); her official birthday was set to February 22 because it reads "nyan-nyan-nyan" in Japanese.
- She used to work at the rice ball store run by her grandmother. She moved to the city at the end of November 2019.
- In lore, she has **no human ears** — only cat ears (confirmed in her official Q&A).
- Her catchphrase is **"Mogu mogu~ Okayu!"** (nom nom Okayu), and she greets by saying "Ikuyo~ Mogu mogu~ Okayu!"
