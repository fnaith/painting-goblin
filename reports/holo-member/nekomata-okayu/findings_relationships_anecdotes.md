## 2. Key Relationships

### Inugami Korone (GAMERS pair / closest duo)
- Debut partners as Hololive GAMERS 2nd gen (same day)
- The most iconic pairing — frequently collab together (streaming, singing, events)
- Part of OKFAMS unit together
- Korone is her most frequent collab partner

### Oozora Subaru (OKFAMS / Sashimio duo)
- Co-host in the OKFAMS unit (FAMS + Okayu + Korone)
- Has a duo unit called **Sashimio** (さしみお / Sashimi-O)
- Co-hosted the official Hololive program **WanWanWaoon** alongside Sora and Subaru
- Sang "Sashimio" song together

### Ookami Mio (GAMERS / FAMS / SMOK / 常MOS)
- Fellow GAMERS member
- Part of **SMOK** unit (Mio/Okayu/Korone)
- Part of **常MOS (TsuneMOS)** unit (Towa/Okayu/Subaru + Mio)
- Mio is the "host" of FAMS / OKFAMS

### Shirakami Fubuki (GAMERS / FAMS)
- GAMERS leader/senpai
- Part of FAMS unit
- Fubuki designed the FAMS unit concept

### Nakiri Ayame (FAMS)
- Fellow FAMS member
- Part of the original FAMS quartet

### Tokino Sora
- Co-hosted WanWanWaoon program together
- Has collabbed in various official events

### Hoshimachi Suisei
- Has collabbed together (e.g., karaoke/streaming collabs)
- Appeared in same official events

### Sakura Miko
- Miko designed Okayu's channel logo
- Shared the same illustrator Kamioka Chiroru with Shiina Yuika of Nijisanji (known as "Kamioka Sisters")

### Oozora Subaru (additional)
- Subaru gave Okayu the nickname **"Takada Donji"** after a comedian she resembles

## 5. Memes & Notable Traits

- **"All-affirming cat"** — known for accepting/agreeing with basically anything said to her during streams
- **"Guilty cat"** — tends to act guilty or flustered when teased
- **Flirtatious personality** — frequently flirts with collab partners and chat
- **Self-described masochist** — specifies "mental only," not physical
- **"Fastest responder"** — known among staff as the quickest to reply to messages
- **Cat motif** — raised by an old woman who runs an onigiri store (official bio)
- **Real cats** — owns cats named Temari and Kojirou
