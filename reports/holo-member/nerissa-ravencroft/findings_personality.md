## 2. Official Lore & Character Concept

Nerissa is a demon sealed away by the gods because her singing voice was so beautiful it had the power to drive the entire world mad. While imprisoned, she discovered Japanese otaku culture and became fascinated by idol songs and penlights — making her imprisonment not so bad after all.

Her character archetype is a **demon diva** — elegant, theatrical, and dramatic, with a deep passion for music as her core identity.

---

## 3. Streaming Persona & Character Traits

Based on the official streaming information and widely observed community consensus:

- **Warm & Affectionate** — Greets viewers with "Hiya darlings!" which immediately establishes a familiar, endearing tone. She frequently uses pet names and terms of endearment for her audience.
- **Theatrical & Dramatic** — Embodies a "devilish diva" persona with flair, embracing her role as a grandiose, musical demon.
- **Musically-Obsessed** — Her entire character revolves around singing. She is known for her strong vocal performances, frequent karaoke streams, and deep appreciation for idol music.
- **Otaku/Weeb Energy** — Openly loves anime, manga, and Japanese pop culture. Her lore explicitly mentions discovering otaku culture during her imprisonment.
- **Playful & Teasing** — Often engages in light-hearted banter and teasing with chat and other members.
- **Affectionately "Down Bad"** — Known within the community for being openly thirsty/appreciative of other VTubers, often joking about being romantically interested in her fellow talents.
- **Emotionally Expressive** — Not afraid to show genuine emotion, whether that's laughing, getting flustered, or being sincerely moved by her fans' support.
- **Confident Performer** — Projects self-assuredness when singing and performing, befitting her diva persona.

**Community-perceived personality type:** Warm, theatrical, musically obsessed, playful, emotionally sincere, with a touch of dramatic diva energy. She is often described by fans as a "sweet demon" — intimidating in lore but genuinely caring in practice.

## 4. Talents & Skills

### Singing
- Characterized by a **powerful, rich alto/mezzo-soprano voice** with strong projection
- Frequently sings idol songs, J-pop, and ballads
- Has released original songs: *Prisoner*, *Say My Name*, *In My Feelings* (EP), *START AGAIN*, *Breaking Dimensions*, *Odyssey*, *LIVE IT LOUD!*, *OVER//RIDE*, *All for One*, *Genesis*, *Blue World*, *Unchained*, *What Goes Around*
- Known for her **vocal range and emotional delivery** in both English and Japanese
- Participated in group songs with Advent: *Rebellion*, *Sirens*, *Genesis*

### Languages
- **Fluent English** (primary streaming language)
- **Japanese** — Studied and can speak to a conversational level; often uses Japanese phrases and covers Japanese songs
- Occasionally mixes both languages on stream

### Gaming
- Plays a variety of games including horror titles, RPGs, and party games
- Known for playing games like *Minecraft*, *Mario Kart*, *Among Us*, horror games
- Particularly known for her **karaoke streams** and singing games

### Other Skills
- Appreciated for her fashion sense (evident in her multiple costumes)
- Known to play keyboard/piano (has done keyboard ASMR recital streams)
