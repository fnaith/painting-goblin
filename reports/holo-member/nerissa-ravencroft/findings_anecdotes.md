## Official Bio & Debut
- Part of **hololive English -Advent-** (3rd gen EN), debuted alongside Shiori Novella, Koseki Bijou, FUWAMOCO
- Debut date: July 31, 2023 (first YouTube stream), Twitter debut July 30, 2023
- Character designer: Jako (logo design), Nekoyashiki (member design, 3D model)
- "A beautiful and captivating raven-haired woman who is the wife of Kureiji Ollie" (lore)
- A self-proclaimed "mafia boss" themed idol — her persona blends gothic mafia aesthetics with a singing idol identity

## Pre-Hololive Identity: Qamar Badwan
- Nerissa had an established singing career **before** joining Hololive under the name **Qamar Badwan**
- Performed a theme song for the anime *Nikoniko, Pun (TV)* as part of the unit "Niko Niko Pun Pun"
- ANN encyclopedia lists her under this name for theme song performance credit
