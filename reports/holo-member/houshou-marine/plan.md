# Research Plan: Houshou Marine (Hololive)

## Main Research Question
尋找Hololive的成員Houshou Marine的相關資料，包含官方設定、個人特色、相關軼事等，用於製作同人遊戲的設定。

## Subtopics

### Subtopic 1: Official Profile & Settings
- Basic info: name, birthday, height, debut date, unit/affiliation
- Character design themes (pirate, captain, etc.)
- Official lore/backstory from Hololive
- Fan nickname "Marine" and fanbase name

### Subtopic 2: Personality & Characteristics
- Personality traits (energetic, lewd jokes, charismatic)
- Talents and skills (singing, streaming style, gaming)
- Catchphrases and recurring bits
- Voice characteristics

### Subtopic 3: Notable Anecdotes & Achievements
- Major milestones (subscriber counts, notable streams)
- Memorable events/collaborations
- Relationships with other Hololive members
- Any notable controversies or viral moments
- Music releases and achievements

## Synthesis Plan
Combine all findings into a comprehensive character profile suitable for fan game development, covering: who she is, what makes her unique, and key reference material for game designers.
