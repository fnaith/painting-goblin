## Character Design Themes

- **Pirate captain** — Her core identity. She calls herself the Captain (Senchou) of the Houshou Pirates.
- **Treasure hunter** — Loves jewels, treasure, and money; her dream is to go treasure hunting as a real pirate.
- **Cosplayer** — Her official bio states her pirate outfit is just cosplay; she does not actually own a ship and is working as a VTuber to raise funds for one.
- **Erotic femme fatale** — Described as a "sensual, femme fatale boss" who acts like a big sister and seduces people. Canonically "around 17."
- **Otaku persona** — Known for deep knowledge of 90s/00s anime, manga, doujinshi, and internet culture.

## Official Lore / Backstory

From the official hololive talent page:

> "Ahoy! Captain of the Houshou Pirates, Houshou Marine here! Keep 'er steady!"
>
> Loves jewels, treasure, and money. Her dream is to become a pirate and go treasure hunting. In order to get a pirate ship, she is now stacking up money as a VTuber on the great lands, just like how the other land-lovers do (which also means she is currently just a girl cosplayer).
>
> She acts like a big sis and sometimes seduces people like a succubus does. A sensual, femme fatale boss, and a young one, at that! (Around 17)
>
> Her pirate outfit is just cosplay, and quite lewd when you see it that way.

**Dream (from official site):**
> "The plan is procure a pirate ship, set sail with you guys, and at the end of our journey remark, 'My greatest treasure was this scenery and the friends we made along the way.'"

**Q&A from official site:**
- **"Are you really 17?"** — Yes. (2021 was her third go-around.)
- **"Are you really a pirate?"** — "Piracy isn't restricted to just the crimes or the travels, you know. Piracy is a way of life. And my heart is always sailing the seas. And so I say, I am a pirate."

**Real-world origin:** Marine became interested in VTubing after watching Kaguya Luna, then became a fan of Nijisanji's Tsukino Mito. She cites Shirakami Fubuki and Minato Aqua as inspirations for auditioning for Hololive.

## Costume Variations

Based on official site image galleries, Marine has at minimum the following known costume/outfit variants:

2. **Gothic Lolita Outfit** — Revealed October 2020; gothic-style dress.
3. **Casual / Modern Outfits** — Multiple casual civilian styles (at least 8 variations shown on the official site gallery thumbnails, including different hairstyles and accessories).
