# Houshou Marine (宝鐘マリン) — Personality & Characteristics Research

## Overview
- **Affiliation**: Hololive Production, Japan 3rd Generation "hololive Fantasy"
- **Debut**: August 11, 2019
- **Role**: Self-proclaimed pirate captain ("Senchou") of the Houshou Pirates — doesn't actually own a ship yet
- **Illustrator**: Akasa Ai (Live2D rigging by Ilie Tou)
- **Fan Name**: Houshou no Ichimi (宝鐘の一味 — Houshou's Pirate Crew)
- **YouTube**: 4.32M subscribers, 2.19B views (as of March 2026)
- **Claim**: Most-subscribed active VTuber from Japan; second most-subscribed overall after Gawr Gura

## Personality Traits

### Energetic & High-Energy
- Known for fast-paced delivery in chatting streams
- Boisterous, loud, constantly engaging with chat at high speed
- Frequently shouts "Ahoy!" as her signature greeting
- Described as having a "diverse repertoire of topics" — 90s/00s anime/manga, internet culture, memes

### Lewd Humor ("Ero-Kaiwai" / エロかわいい)
- Marine is famous for her "ero-kawaii" (erotically cute) persona — a central part of her brand
- Openly discusses browsing adult-oriented doujinshi and searching for explicit content
- Listed hobbies on official profile include: "searching for porn, fantasizing, dirty talk"
- Described as acting like a "succubus" who seduces viewers — "a sensual, femme fatale boss"
- Taught "Im horny" by Kiryu Coco — now a self-described catchphrase
- This contrasts with her official age of "17" (her 3rd go-around as of 2021), creating comedic irony

### Charismatic & Entertaining
- Known for her distinctive, loud laugh and expressive voice
- Proactive at organizing collabs and special streams
- Her official message says: "even non-pervs might enjoy my company"
- Described as a "big sis" figure who sometimes seduces like a succubus
- Known for her skills as a digital artist — draws regularly on stream and posts on Twitter

### Tsukkomi / Straight-Woman
- Often plays the tsukkomi (straight-man) role in collabs
- Her dynamic with Usada Pekora is especially famous — Pekora as chaotic prankster, Marine as exasperated reactor
- Sharp wit, quick comebacks, and self-aware meta-humor

## Popular Memes & Running Gags

### "Ero" Moments & Midnight Streams
- Marine's lewd remarks are compiled extensively by fans
- Late-night streams where she becomes more unfiltered — "dangerous" or "unsafe" content
- Paipai Mask — an invented character that became an original song ("How about Paipai Mask?")

### Houshou Disease (宝鐘病)
- Term for becoming obsessed with Marine
- Fans jokingly describe it as an incurable disease that spreads among viewers

### Shark-tooth Marine
- Meme expression depicting Marine with shark teeth
- Represents her more aggressive/savage moments during streams

### Marine MK II
- Refers to alternate outfits or an "upgraded" Marine persona
- Often used in fan edits comparing "normal" Marine vs. "upgraded" Marine

### Marine vs. Pekora Dynamics
- One of the most popular Hololive duos
- Pekora as chaotic gremlin, Marine as exasperated tsukkomi
- Their BridalDream duet song was part of the HoneyWorks collaboration

### "17 Years Old" Running Gag
- Claims to be perpetually 17 (stated 2021 was her "third go-around")
- Official Q&A embraces the joke

### Official Q&A Memes
- "Are you wearing panties?" → "I'm panties head-to-toe"
- "Are you wearing colored contacts?" → "Nay, those are my mesmerizing eyes"
- "Are you really a pirate?" → "Piracy is a way of life"
- "What are your measurements?" → "Would you like to find out with your own hands?"

### Dokuro-kun
- A skull-shaped drinking cup that became a running gag among the crew

### Maringlish / Maripanish
- Speaking English/Spanish by just winging it — comically broken but endearing

## Talents

### Singing — Major Achievements
- **1st solo live**: "Ahoy!! You're All Pirates" (Dec 7-8, 2024, K-Arena Yokohama, ~40,000 attendees over 2 days)
- **Debut album**: *Ahoy!! You're All Pirates♡* (Oct 16, 2024) — peaked #3 on Oricon weekly chart
- "Dead Ma'am's Chest" — composed by **Toby Fox** (creator of Undertale/Deltarune)
- "I'm Your Treasure Box" — composed by **Camellia**
- "Marine Set Sail!!" — composed by **Kohei Tanaka** (known for One Piece, Sakura Wars)
- **First VTuber ever** invited to Fuji TV's FNS Music Festival (Dec 2023) — invited again in 2024
- Original singles: "Ahoy!! We are Houshou Pirates", "Marine Set Sail!!", "Bishoujyo Muzai♡Pirates", "Unison", "I'm Your Treasure Box", "Dead Ma'am's Chest", "How about Paipai Mask?"

### Drawing / Digital Art
- Skilled digital artist — draws frequently on stream
- Streamed "Pictures I've Drawn from Elementary School till Now"
- Shares illustrations on Twitter

### Gaming
- Plays dating sims, shooters, and various games
- Appeared as a character in: Neptunia Virtual Stars, Touhou Spell Bubble, Rakugaki Kingdom, Caravan Stories, World of Warships, White Cat Project, and more

### Other
- Mimicry/impressions listed as a special skill
- Voice role in Ghostbusters: Frozen Empire Japanese dub (2024)
- Voice role in Project Hail Mary Japanese dub (2026)

## Notable Achievements
- Most-subscribed active VTuber from Japan on YouTube (4M+ subs)

- First VTuber to perform on FNS Music Festival (2023, returned 2024)
