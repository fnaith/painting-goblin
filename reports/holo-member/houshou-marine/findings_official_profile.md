# Findings: Official Profile & Settings (Houshou Marine)

## Basic Information
- **Name**: Houshou Marine (宝鐘マリン)
- **Nickname**: Senchou (Captain), Marine
- **Birthday**: July 30
- **Height**: 150 cm
- **Debut**: August 11, 2019 (Last member of hololive 3rd Generation "hololive Fantasy")
- **Unit**: hololive 3rd Generation (with Usada Pekora, Uruha Rushia, Shirogane Noel, Shiranui Flare)

## Official Lore (from hololive website)
"Ahoy! Captain of the Houshou Pirates, Houshou Marine here! Keep 'er steady!"
- Loves jewels, treasure, and money
- Dream is to become a pirate and go treasure hunting
- Working as a VTuber to fund purchasing a pirate ship ("land-lover" cosplayer)
- Acts like a big sis, sometimes seduces like a succubus
- Described as "sensual, femme fatale boss, young at around 17"
- Pirate outfit is cosplay

## Ultimate Dream
