## Character & Persona Details
- **"17 years old"**: Running joke — claims to be perpetually 17 (2021 was her "3rd loop")
- **Self-taught digital artist**: Draws frequently on stream, posts fan art on Twitter
- **Fast-talking, lewd comedian**: Known for openly discussing adult doujinshi, otaku culture from the 90s/00s
- **Maringlish/Maripanish**: Makes up foreign languages on the fly
- **Dokuro-kun**: Skull-shaped drinking cup — famous meme prop among the crew
- **Jack**: Genderbent cool male version of Marine from 3rd Gen Host Club roleplay streams
- **95/100**: How she typically rates herself
- Catchphrase taught by **Kiryu Coco**: "Im horny"
- **Inspirations**: Kaguya Luna (first VTuber interest), Tsukino Mito (Nijisanji), Shirakami Fubuki, Minato Aqua (audition inspirations)
