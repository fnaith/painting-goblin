# Kureiji Ollie — Personality, Memes & Fanbase Research

## Overview

Kureiji Ollie is a female zombie Virtual YouTuber and member of Hololive Indonesia 2nd Generation. She debuted on December 4, 2020. Her illustrator is LAM. She is 162cm tall, birthday October 13, and lore-age 17. Her emoji/oshi mark is 🧟‍♀️.

**Lore:** A zombie high school girl who rose from her grave and headed home using memories from when she was alive. Upon arriving, she discovered her house had been turned into a ramen shop. To reclaim it, she decided to become a VTuber.

---

## Personality

- **Loud, chaotic, energetic** — frequently screams at unexpected events; viewers are warned to lower volume during her streams.
- **Multilingual** — speaks Bahasa Indonesia, English, and Japanese, often code-switching rapidly mid-sentence.
- **Extremely talkative** — the most talkative member of her generation; never misses an opportunity to crack a joke.
- **Caring and attentive** — despite the chaotic exterior, she pays close attention to her friends and supporters.
- **Self-described SIMP** — listed "SIMPing" as a hobby during her debut stream. Shows immense passion and respect for her senpais, often screaming excitedly when they appear in her chat.
- **Impressionist** — imitated 42 hololive talents from all branches plus YAGOO during her debut stream alone.
- **Meme-literate** — frequently uses online slang like "POG", "Yeet", "Skeet", etc.
- **Appreciative of fans** — strongly values Zomrade-made content, sometimes using fan-made intros for her streams.

## Notable Memes

1. **"Annyeonghasseyo Ollie imnida"** — Ollie's frequent Korean greeting became a staple meme, highlighting her multilingual randomness.

2. **"Shat up!"** — Her mispronunciation of "shut up" became an iconic repeated phrase within the community.

3. **Cabbage Hatred** — Ollie openly and intensely hates cabbages (listed in her dislikes). This became a recurring joke, with fans and fellow talents referencing it.

4. **Doko? / Koko** — In Minecraft collabs, she would frequently ask "doko?" (where?) and respond with "koko" (here), creating a repeated call-and-response pattern.

5. **The Sword Through Her Head** — Her character design features a sword impaled through her head buns (and technically her head). The mascot Udin hangs from it. The fact that she's fine because she's a zombie is a running joke.

6. **42 Impressions** — During her debut stream, she imitated 42 different hololive members plus YAGOO, which immediately established her as a superfan and impressionist.

7. **The Ear-Piercing Scream** — Her reaction to senpais appearing in her chat (especially Tokino Sora appearing during debut) became legendary. Viewers are consistently warned to lower volume.

8. **Mismatched Shoes** — Her canonical reason for wearing mismatched shoes is that she "couldn't decide which pair to wear." This quirky lore detail is often referenced.

## Additional Personality Traits (from Supplementary Sources)

### Stream Rules (from hololive.wiki)
Ollie's stream description includes 6 rules:

1. **Don't be a jerk** — be respectful in chat
2. **No spoilers** — unless she asks
3. **Don't backseat game** — let her play, unless she asks for help
4. **Don't bring up other streamers/Livers** — unless related to the stream / or if she mentions them first
5. **No racist, sexist, or any kind of discrimination** — inclusive chat
6. **Follow Ollie's rules** — have fun!

### CDawgVA Meme
Ollie revealed herself as a huge fan of CDawgVA, calling him "Papa." When CDawgVA acknowledged her, she screamed and became extremely flustered — a classic "Ollie fangirling" moment that became a meme crossover between the Hololive and CDawgVA communities.

### Additional Catchphrases (from seesaawiki)
- **"HELLO EVERYNYAA"** — Cat-themed greeting variant
- **"Ore ga zombie de, zombie ga ore de"** — "I am zombie, zombie is me" (Japanese)

### Zombie Persona Depth (from seesaawiki)
- Fully embraces the zombie identity: uses zombie moans, zombie puns, zombie mannerisms
- Despite the zombie theme, her character is "kawaii zombie" rather than "scary zombie" — emphasizing the idol aspect
- Her "zombieness" is played for comedy (body parts falling off, forgetting she's dead, etc.)
