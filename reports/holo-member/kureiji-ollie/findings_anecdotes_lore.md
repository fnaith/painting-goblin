# Kureiji Ollie — Research Findings for Game Character Setting

## Overview

Kureiji Ollie is a VTuber from Hololive Indonesia 2nd Generation (holoro). Her character is a zombie high school girl who rose from the grave, found her house had been turned into a ramen shop, and decided to become a VTuber to earn money to reclaim it. She is known for her extreme energy, loud voice, chaotic humor, and deep love for her senpais and fans (Zomrade).

## Interesting Anecdotes from Streams

### Debut Highlights
- **42 Voice Impressions**: During her debut stream, Ollie imitated the voices of 42 different Hololive members plus YAGOO (the CEO), demonstrating an encyclopedic knowledge of her senpais.
- **Tokino Sora Appearance**: When Tokino Sora (the first Hololive talent) appeared in her debut chat, Ollie became hysterical with joy, screaming and crying — setting the tone for her reputation as the ultimate Hololive fan.
- **Self-Intro**: Introduced herself as a zombie high school girl who "rose from the grave and wants to become a top idol." Her lore: she died, resurrected, found her house turned into a ramen shop, and decided to become a VTuber to raise money to buy it back.

### Signature Behaviors
- **"Always lower your volumes during her streams"**: This warning appears in her stream descriptions because she is notorious for sudden loud screams.
- **Frequent Screaming**: Screams when excited, scared in horror games, or when senpais notice her. Screaming is a core part of her streaming identity.
- **Chaos Gremlin**: Known for chaotic, unpredictable behavior — switching topics rapidly, making random noises, and breaking into song mid-sentence.

### Gaming Moments
- **Horror Games**: Ollie is notoriously scared of horror games. Her reaction streams are legendary for combining genuine fear with loud screaming.
- **Minecraft**: Frequently builds chaotic structures. Known for dying in ridiculous ways.
- **APEX Legends**: Plays with various Hololive members. Her skill level varies wildly between "clutch god" and "bot."
- **Chess**: Occasionally plays chess, which contrasts amusingly with her chaotic persona.

### Memorable Interactions
- **Moona Effect**: Whenever Moona Hoshinova interacts with Ollie, she becomes visibly (audibly) flustered and happy, leading to entertaining streams.
- **Senpai Worship**: Has openly stated that streaming with her senpais is a dream come true. Her genuine emotional reactions to collabs with senpais are a recurring theme.
- **Zomrade Love**: Frequently expresses deep gratitude and love for her fans (Zomrade). Known for reading superchats with genuine emotional reactions.

## Ongoing "Lore" / Storylines

### Core Character Lore
- **Identity**: Kureiji Ollie is a zombie (zombie = "Kureiji" in Japanese pronunciation of "crazy"). She was a normal high school girl who died and resurrected as a zombie.
- **Goal**: To become a top-class idol (zombie idol concept) and reclaim her family home, which was converted into a ramen shop while she was dead.
- **Appearance**: Zombie aesthetic — bandages, stitches, mismatched eyes. Her signature prop is a giant sword that goes through her head.
- **Mascot/Bear**: A stuffed bear named **Udin** sits on the tip of her sword.

### Running Gags
- **Mismatched Shoes**: Ollie is always depicted wearing mismatched shoes — part of her zombie "just got out of the grave" aesthetic.
- **Sword Through Head**: It's canon that she occasionally forgets about the sword and it goes through her head when she turns around too fast.
- **Zombie Noises**: Frequently makes zombie groaning/moaning sounds, especially when tired or pretending to be dramatic.
- **"Zombanwa!"**: Her signature greeting (zombie + "konbanwa" = good evening).
- **"Otsucrazy!"**: Her sign-off (otsucare + crazy).

### Visual Novel: "Days With Ollie"
- **Release Date**: April 22, 2024 (Steam)
- **Genre**: Visual Novel / Interactive fiction
- **Description**: An official visual novel featuring Ollie, where fans can interact with her in a story-driven format
- **Significance**: One of the few hololive members to get an official solo visual novel game

### DECO*27 "Zombie" Collaboration
- Ollie was featured in the chorus of DECO\*27's song **"Zombie"** (2022)
- DECO\*27 is a famous Vocaloid producer — this collaboration gave Ollie significant exposure in the broader Japanese music scene

### World Zombie Day
- Ollie's birthday (October 13) coincides with **World Zombie Day**
- This is considered a cosmic coincidence that perfectly matches her zombie idol persona

### CDawgVA Fan Moment
- Ollie is a known fan of CDawgVA (Chris Broad), the YouTuber known for "Abroad in Japan" and Trash Taste
- She called him "Papa" during an interaction
- When CDawgVA acknowledged her, she screamed and became extremely flustered
- This moment became a notable crossover meme between the Hololive and CDawgVA/Trash Taste communities

### Family Lore Details (from Q&A)
- **Illo**: Older brother who died at age 10 due to illness. Ollie has mentioned missing him.
- **Ollo**: Younger brother, still alive. Ollie is protective of him.
- **Ellie**: Younger sister, still alive.
- **Mama Lily**: Her mother, who gave her the nickname "Kureiji"
- Ollie's entire family are ordinary humans — she is the only zombie

### Twice-Dead
- Ollie has canonically died at least twice in her lore
- This reinforces her "rebirth" ability concept

### Eye Color Trivia
- When Ollie was alive, her eyes were already different colors (heterochromia)
- This means the heterochromia is not a result of being a zombie, but a natural trait

### Spicy Food Paradox
- Ollie likes spicy food but cannot handle it well
- She enjoys the burning sensation despite the pain — a character detail that mirrors her chaotic "pain is pleasure" zombie persona

### Chess Tournament
- Ollie participated in the **Hololive Chess Tournament Arc 2**, showcasing a surprisingly strategic side that contrasts with her chaotic persona
- Chess became a recurring content theme for her

### Multilingual Identity
- Speaks **Indonesian** (native), **English** (fluent), and **Japanese** (conversational).
- Often code-switches between all three in a single sentence.
- Represents the Indonesian VTuber scene prominently in Hololive.
