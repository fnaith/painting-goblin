## 5. Official Lore & Backstory

### Official Introduction (from hololivepro.com)

> **"ZOMBANWA!! SUPER KAWAII ZOMBIE IDOL, KU KU KU KUREIJI OLLIE DESU~~!!"**
>
> A zombie high school girl risen from her grave. She headed home, relying on memories retained from when she was still alive.
> Upon arriving, she discovered it had turned into a ramen shop! And so, in order to reclaim her house, she decided to become a VTuber.
> Ollie has a very big tendency to scream. Always lower your volumes during her streams.

### Expanded Lore (from wiki & official Q&A)

- **Death & Rebirth**: Ollie was a girl who died and later returned as a zombie. She does not remember how she died. She discovered she has the power of rebirth, which she mainly uses to restore hopes and dreams.
- **The Ramen Shop**: The house she lived in before dying was converted into a ramen shop. She became an idol to get it back, but after achieving her goal, she decided to leave it in the hands of the ramen shop.
- **Human Family**: Her family are normal humans, not zombies.
- **Name Origin**: "Kureiji" is a nickname, not her real name — given to her by "Mama Lily" (her mother).

### Official Catchphrase / Greeting
- **"ZOMBANWA!!"** — Her signature greeting, a portmanteau of "zombie" and the Japanese greeting "konnichiwa"
- **"OHAYOLLIE"** — Morning greeting
- **"SUPER KAWAII ZOMBIE IDOL, KU KU KU KUREIJI OLLIE DESU~~!!"** — Full introduction

## 13. Game Character Design Notes (Summary for Implementation)

- **Archetype**: Zombie + Schoolgirl + Idol — a chaotic, high-energy undead idol
- **Key visual hook**: Sword through the head (can be removed/replaced), mismatched shoes, button eye, stitched skin patches, stitched bear mascot (Udin)
- **Signature pose/motion**: Limbs falling off, loud screaming, energetic flailing
- **Color palette**: Red (#FF0000), Black (#000000), White (#FFFFFF), Gray (#808080), Pink (#FF69B4), Purple (#800080)
- **Voice direction**: High-energy, loud, trilingual (Indonesian/English/Japanese), prone to screaming, fast talking
- **Personality keywords**: Chaotic, loud, caring, flirtatious, meme-savvy, superfan, determined
- **Signature catchphrases**: "ZOMBANWA!!", "OHAYOLLIE", "SUPER KAWAII ZOMBIE IDOL"
- **Power concept**: Rebirth / resurrection ability (restoring hopes and dreams), undead durability
- **Mascot companion**: Udin — a stitched blue-and-purple teddy bear with dead eyes and visible stitches
