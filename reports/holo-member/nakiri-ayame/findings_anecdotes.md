## 1. The "低浮上" (Teijoujo / Low-Activity / Ghosting) Reputation

Ayame is famously known within the Hololive fandom for her **low streaming frequency**, often disappearing for weeks or even months at a time between streams. This has become one of her defining traits and a major source of memes:

- Fans frequently joke that Ayame has "ghosted" her own fanbase, referring to her as the "great phantom" of Hololive.
- The term **低浮上 (teijoujo)**, meaning "barely surfacing" or "low-activity," is strongly associated with her. Fans often post "Ayame spotted!" memes whenever she appears online.
- Extended hiatuses became so common that fan accounts and subreddits would treat any Ayame tweet or stream as a rare sighting event.
- Fellow Hololive members frequently tease her about her low streaming activity. Shirakami Fubuki, in particular, has joked about Ayame being hard to pin down for collaborations.
- Despite her low activity, she consistently maintains strong viewer numbers during her rare streams, indicating a highly loyal fanbase.
- Her tendency to vanish and reappear unpredictably has been affectionately nicknamed "Ayame Time" or being on "Ayame Standard Time."

## 4. Memes and Fan Culture

### "Kawayo" (かわ余)
- A portmanteau of **"Kawaii" (かわいい)** and **"Yo" (余)** — the latter being an archaic first-person pronoun that Ayame uses.
- Fans spam "Kawayo" in chat whenever Ayame does something cute. It has become her signature chat meme.

### "Shikigami" Calls
- Ayame's fan "Shikigami" (式神) call-and-response: she shouts "Karma!" and fans respond "Shiranui!" — referring to her two oni/god affiliations.
- When she says "Karma" the chat responds, and when she says "Shiranui" they respond in kind.

### "Ayame Spotted" / Rare Entity
- Because of her low streaming frequency, fan communities developed "sighting" memes — treating Ayame tweets, streams, or appearances in other members' streams as if spotting a rare mythical creature.
- Subreddits and Discord servers would post "AYAME SPOTTED" alerts when she went live.

### "Ojou" (お嬢) Nickname
- Fellow Hololive members often call Ayame **"Ojou"** (young lady/ princess). When asked why in her Q&A, she responded: "I have no idea myself."

### "Poyoyo"
- The mascot character created by Fubuki, often used in fan art and emoji form.

### Nakiri Gang
- Her fanbase is officially called the **Nakiri Gang**, and fans often play up the "gangster" / "delinquent" angle despite Ayame being an oni student council president.

## 7. Controversies and Incidents

Compared to many other Hololive members, Nakiri Ayame has maintained a **relatively controversy-free career**. Key notes:

- **No major scandals** — Ayame has avoided the type of public controversies that have affected other Hololive members (such as contract terminations or suspensions).
- **Hololive China incident (2020)** — As part of Hololive, she was indirectly affected by the fallout of the Taiwan controversy involving Kiryu Coco and Akai Haato, which led to Hololive China's dissolution, but she was not personally involved.
- **Low activity criticism** — The most persistent "issue" around Ayame is fan frustration over her infrequent streaming. Some fans have expressed concern about her health or motivation, but she has never indicated any plans to graduate.
- **No graduation announcements** — As of the latest information, Ayame remains an active (if low-frequency) member of Hololive Production.
- She is widely regarded as one of the most "pure" and uncontroversial members of the agency, and her fanbase is noted for being comparatively chill and welcoming.

## 8. Legacy and Impact

- Nakiri Ayame is one of the remaining few Hololive 2nd Generation members still active (alongside Oozora Subaru and Yuzuki Choco), making her part of the "old guard" of Hololive.
- Her "low-activity queen" reputation has become a unique brand that sets her apart from the typical high-output VTuber model.
- Despite streaming rarely, she has maintained over 1 million YouTube subscribers (achieved in 2020/2021), demonstrating the power of her personality and fan loyalty.
- The "Kawayo" meme remains one of the most recognizable Hololive fan chants.
- Her influence can be seen in how later generations of Hololive members treat streaming frequency — with Ayame proving that quality and personality can matter more than sheer quantity.

## 9. Additional Anecdotes (from Hololive Fan Wiki)

### "Docchi Docchi" Song Origin
During a Minecraft stream, Ayame got lost and didn't know which way to go. She kept asking chat "Docchi? Docchi?" (どっち？どっち？ — "Which way? Which way?"). This became a fan-made remix/MAD (music anime douga) video that went viral. She later officially released it as a song on her YouTube channel. It has since become one of her most iconic meme songs.

### "Shimakeshi" (島壊し / 島潰し) — Island Destroyer
In a Minecraft stream while searching for treasure using a treasure map, she got frustrated and systematically destroyed an entire island in Minecraft block by block. This led to the "Shimakeshi" meme — a portmanteau of "shima" (島, island) and "kowashi" (壊し, destroy), or alternatively "tsubushi" (潰し, crush). Fans created the phrase "You walk into a bar and see Ayame destroying an island" as a running joke.
