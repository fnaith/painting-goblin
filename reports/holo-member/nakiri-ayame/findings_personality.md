**Official intro:** "Greetings, Humans! Yoohoo!"

## Personality Traits

- **Playful and Friendly:** Despite her oni (demon) persona suggesting aloofness, Ayame is exceptionally warm, approachable, and treats her audience as equals. She loves pranking people with will-o'-the-wisps, true to her lore.
- **Easily Amused / Sensitive Funny Bone:** One of her most defining traits — she bursts into cute giggles at the slightest amusement. Bad Japanese puns are a particular weakness, causing her to laugh uncontrollably, bash her desk, and pause the stream to recover.
- **Analytical Approach to Socializing:** She is methodical with new people, often needing ~5 separate encounters to collect enough "data" before she feels comfortable approaching someone. This can cause analytical "meltdowns" around people who prefer immediate closeness (e.g., Houshou Marine).
- **Hidden Temper (Oni Side):** True to her oni nature, she has a violent temper when pushed too far (e.g., physically fighting her father, kicking a hole in a wall when recordings were deleted). However, she strictly maintains a cheerful, positive mood on stream and only shows cute, toothless outbursts to her audience.
- **Former Loner:** Before VTubing, she struggled with social interaction and was a loner. Hololive helped her build self-confidence, open up, and form meaningful bonds.
- **Self-Described Stream Goal:** "I strive to create a warm place on stream where I can smile and have fun together with my viewers!"

---

## Voice Characteristics

- **High-pitched, soft, girly voice** with a somewhat elegant cadence that fits her "ojou-sama" (young lady) image.
- Frequently described by fans as "moe" — sweet, soothing, and slightly nasally in an endearing way.
- Her laugh is distinctive: a light, rapid, infectious giggle that escalates into full laughter, often accompanied by desk-banging.

## Streaming Style & Regular Content

- **Stream Types:** Chit-chat (zatsudan), karaoke, and night-drinking streams are her specialties.
- **Relaxed & Cozy Atmosphere:** She aims for a "warm place" where she and viewers smile together. Streams feel intimate, conversational, and unhurried.
- **Infrequent but Impactful Schedule:** Known for relatively sparse streaming compared to other hololive members, which makes each stream an event. Fans affectionately call her a "phantom" streamer.
- **Fan Interaction:** Engages actively with chat, reacts to comments in real time, and frequently laughs at viewer-submitted jokes and puns.

## Quirks & Habits

- **Wears Glasses IRL:** She has admitted to very poor eyesight and wears glasses off-stream.
- **Pet Cat:** Owns a cat named **Jirusuke**.
- **Bad Sense of Direction:** A running gag; she frequently gets lost in games, leading to the "Docchi Docchi" meme.
- **Twin Katanas:** Her character always carries two named blades — the cursed sword **Rakshasa** (black) and the demon blade **Asura** (red).
- **Collector:** She enjoys collecting shrine stamps (goshuin), watching anime, and visiting shrines.
- **Strict Mother:** She has mentioned having a very strict, strong-headed mother who would correct her manners rigorously.
- **Pretty Cure Fan:** Her favorite series is *Smile Pretty Cure*; favorite characters are Cure Happy, Cure Peace, and Cure Beauty — though she denies being an otaku.
- **Frequently Asked About Her Horns:** She claims viewers ask why she has horns every ~6 months; her standard reply is "Why don't you humans have horns?"
