## 2. Character Lore & Setting

**Official Hololive Bio:**
> "Greetings, Humans! Yoohoo!"
> A kimono-clad Oni girl from the Underworld Academy. As she loves to prank people, she always uses will-o'-the-wisp on them just to have fun. Many may not realize it, but she is actually the president of the student council.

**Key Lore Elements:**
- **Species**: Oni (demon/ogre) girl
- **Origin**: Underworld Academy (冥府学園)
- **Role**: Student Council President (though this is a surprise to most)
- **Personality**: Playful and mischievous — loves pranking people with will-o'-the-wisp (鬼火 / onibi)
- **Greeting**: "Konbanwa, ningen no minasan! Yoohoo!" (Good evening, humans! Yoohoo!)

## 3. Character Design Details

**Theme / Motif:**
- Demon/Oni theme with traditional Japanese aesthetics
- Color palette dominated by black, white, and red (classic oni colors)

**Outfit (Original / Default):**
- Black sailor-style school uniform (seifuku) with a red inner layer
- Long black skirt with red accents and a trailing sheer/translucent fabric element
- White collar with a red ribbon tie
- Black and red geta (traditional wooden sandals)
- Long flowing black hair with a prominent red horn on the left side of her head
- Gold/yellow eyes
- A single visible fang (canine tooth), reinforcing the oni motif

**Accessories:**
- Red horn (signature demon trait)
- Will-o'-the-wisp (鬼火 / onibi) effects in her design — floating blue flames
- Kimono-style elements incorporated into the school uniform base
- Traditional Japanese geta footwear

**Visual Motifs:**
- Oni horn (single, left side)
- Will-o'-the-wisp / ghost flames
- School uniform + traditional Japanese fusion
- Fang/canine tooth

| Phrase | Meaning / Context |
|---|---|
| "Yoohoo!" | Part of her standard greeting |
| "Konbanwa, ningen no minasan!" | "Good evening, humans!" — standard greeting |
| **"Kawayo"** | Portmanteau of *kawaii* (cute) + *"yo"* (余, archaic first-person pronoun she uses) — used by fans when something cute happens |
