# Aki Rosenthal (AkiRose) - Memes, Clips & Community Highlights

**Research compiled: June 2026**

---

## 1. Famous Memes & Running Gags

### Mukiroze
Aki's ARK: Survival Evolved streams gave birth to **Mukiroze**, her overly muscular male avatar. This became wildly popular among fans and fellow Hololive members. Mukiroze is known for:
- Almost always being naked except for a censored "Birthday Suit Pants" skin (started by Kiryu Coco)
- Taming raptors with his bare fists
- Surviving extreme heat naked for a full day
- Taming predators from cliff edges without traps
- Mukiroze's "birthday" is January 29th
- The character was so popular it significantly boosted Aki's viewer count and brought in new fans

### Floating Twin Tails
Aki's trademark pigtails float due to "supernatural power" connected to another dimension. This is a constant visual gag.

### Portal Between Thighs
She can produce objects and materials from between her thighs — the nature of this "portal" is a running joke.

### "No Nose" Meme
Aki's original Live2D model lacked a visible nose, leading to a community meme where fans would "shitpost until she gets a nose." A memorable Reddit post series tracked this.

### Apple Emoji (Oshi Mark)
Her representative emote is the apple (🍎). Her fans are called **Squad Rose** (Rose-tai / ロゼ隊), depicted as sentient apple creatures with knight visors and floating hands.

### Mukiroze Janken
Aki sometimes participates in Tsunomaki Watame's morning show with "Akirose janken" or "Mukirose janken" — the latter is considered impossible to win against.

### Demonetization Incident
Aki was demonetized in December 2019, leading to the creation of a sub-channel. This was part of a wider Hololive demonetization wave that Kiryu Coco famously fought against.

## 4. Memorable Streaming Events

- **June 1, 2018** - Debuted on Mirrativ (using only a phone provided by Cover Corp, with no PC)
- **December 2, 2018** - Hiatus due to traffic accident / leg surgery; hospitalized
- **February 9, 2019** - Returned from hiatus
- **December 9, 2019** - YouTube channel demonetized
- **June 29, 2020** - Released full MV of her original song "Sha-Le-Ys" (name of her home world)
- **January 24, 2020** - Participated in Hololive's first live "Nonstop Story"
- **December 4, 2020** - Revealed fourth Live2D outfit
- **Reached 1,000,000 subscribers** on YouTube (milestone celebrated by the community)

## 5. Personal Trivia & Community Memes

- **Half-elf, half-siren** — mother is a siren, father is an elf
- "Aki Rosenthal" is not her real name — it was given to her by Yagoo to be pronounceable on Earth
- **Tentacle fetish** — Aki has openly admitted this
- **Belly dancing** — Her goal since debut is to be the "world's first virtual belly dancer"
- **Heavy drinker** — Known for loving beer and sake on stream
- **Dad jokes & dirty humor** — Despite her calm image, she has a subtle dirty-minded sense of humor (often pointed out by Natsuiro Matsuri)
- **Directionally challenged** — Gets lost very easily, combined with frequent bad luck
- **Drummer** — Bought her own drum set and practices (though she admits she's not very skilled)
- **Candy-Go-Round** — Participated in the Hololive IDOL PROJECT song with Roboco, Mel, Aqua, and Flare
- **Moderator** — One of the moderators on the official Hololive subreddit
