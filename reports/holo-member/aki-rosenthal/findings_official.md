## 2. Official Lore / Character Setting

### Official Bio (from hololivepro.com)
> "Alona, everyone! This is Aki Rosenthal a.k.a. AkiRose!"
> "A half-elf from another world, she has an insatiable curiosity and cannot resist exploring anything that piques her interest. Her trademark pigtails float due to some sort of supernatural power."

### In-Depth Lore (from Hololive Fan Wiki)
- **Race:** Half-elf, half-siren (mother is a siren, father is an elf)
- **Origin:** From a different universe / another dimension
- **Home world:** Sha-Le-Ys (also the title of her first original song)
- **Real name:** Aki Rosenthal is NOT her real name — it was given to her by Yagoo so she would have an understandable and easily pronounceable name on Earth
- **Floating Twintails:** Connected to another dimension via mysterious power. According to the official site, they are actually "an accessory extension with on-board AI that can freely transform" named **Aik**
- **Thigh Portal:** She can produce objects from between her thighs — the nature of this "portal" is as mysterious as her twintails
- **Goals:** "An AkiRose in every household!" / Wants to meet fans using VR/AR technology / Wants to try doing an entertainment show

### Name Format
- **Unique Western-style naming:** Aki Rosenthal uses Western name order (Given + Family), whereas most other Hololive JP members use Japanese order (Family + Given). This is consistent with her "from another world" lore.

### Additional Fandom Wiki Findings
- **Detachable Pigtails:** In *Holo no Graffiti* shorts, her floating pigtails have been shown as detachable accessories, consistent with the official lore that they are AI-powered accessory extensions named "Aik"
- **"Giving Birth" to Oozora Subaru:** In a *Holo no Graffiti* skit, Aki produced Oozora Subaru from between her thighs (via the Thigh Portal), which the fan community dubbed "giving birth to Subaru"
- **3D Idol Outfit:** Announced March 2025, marking a significant model upgrade

## 3. Yagoo (CEO Motoaki Tanigo) — Evaluation & Connection

- **Name origin:** Yagoo personally gave Aki the name "Rosenthal" so it would be easy for Earthlings to pronounce and understand
- **Concept fit:** Yagoo originally imagined hololive as "streamers that came from a different or parallel worlds and came together as a group" — Aki's half-elf/siren from another dimension lore fits this original vision perfectly
- **General philosophy (from ANN interview 2020):** Yagoo stated that Cover Corp started with AR/VR technology development before pivoting to VTubers. He considers Hololive as making use of both AR and VR technologies. He noted that Tokino Sora was the first talent they worked with.
- **Yagoo's hiring values:** When evaluating talents, Yagoo prioritizes eagerness and determination — being able to stream almost every day requires determination, and he respects that
- **Yagoo's background:** Co-founder and CEO of COVER Corporation (founded June 2016), graduated from Keio University Faculty of Science and Technology, previously worked with Sanrio on character development

## 5. Race / Species Classification

**Aki Rosenthal is a half-elf, half-siren — NOT a fox.**

- **Mother:** Siren (a mythical creature from Greek mythology that lures sailors with song)
- **Father:** Elf (from Western/European fantasy tradition)
- **Visual cues:** Pointed elf ears, floating twintails
- **She is not a fox** (kitsune) — that is a common misidentification. The fox-eared Hololive members include Shirakami Fubuki (白上フブキ, a fox spirit) and others, but Aki Rosenthal's ears are distinctly elf ears.

## 6. Personality & Trivia

- **Streaming style:** Relaxed and calming; known for ASMR, karaoke (nostalgic songs), Let's Plays, and evening drinking chats
- **Weakness:** Gets scared extremely easily — infamous for her screams in horror games (calls herself a "professional screamer")
- **Sense of direction:** Very poor, gets lost easily
- **Alcohol:** Loves beer and sake, often drinks on stream
- **Cooking:** Good at cooking; sometimes streams cooking
- **Fitness:** Actually quite fit — does well in Ring Fit Adventure
- **Belly dancing:** Her goal is to be the world's first virtual belly dancer
- **Drums:** Owns a drum set and practices (though not very skilled)
- **Humor:** Has a subtle dirty sense of humor / dad-jokes that contrasts with her calm image
- **Certifications:** Certified Aromatherapy Instructor
- **Mukiroze:** Her overly muscular male ARK avatar became wildly popular and boosted her career
- **Fans (Rose Squad):** Depicted as sentient apple creatures wearing visors and having floating hands
