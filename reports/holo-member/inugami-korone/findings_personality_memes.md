## 2. Personality Traits

### 2.1 Cheerful & Energetic (Genki)
Korone has a very cheerful and energetic personality. She appears outgoing and playful, often making various noises relating to her in-game actions, adding props on screen (e.g., a coffee mug with a pepper in it), or messing with her model (making copies of it, putting it upside down).

### 2.2 Shy in Large Groups
Despite her genki exterior, she is sometimes shy in collabs with many people. She has slowly become more open to talking with people she is not close friends with over the years.

### 2.3 Eccentric / Does Things at Her Own Pace
She can be described as a bit "eccentric." She once expressed interest in doing a stream about various torture methods. She does not have a set streaming schedule and ends streams when she has had enough rather than for a set amount of time.

### 2.4 Strong Conscience
She felt bad when she finished *Dragon's Lair* with 30 lives instead of the default 5, because it felt like cheating. She later re-uploaded a video of her finishing it with the base 5 lives.

### 2.5 Emotionally Sensitive
She gets very emotional when games deal with themes of death of family members or parting with friends/partners:
- In her **Monster Rancher** stream, she was very sad when she had to freeze her monster due to old age (a monster she initially called ugly but got very attached to).
- In **Klonoa**, she was moved by the ending where the main character is forced to leave his friend behind.

### 2.6 Yandere / Scary Moments
Her "Yubi Yubi" catchphrase involves her telling viewers she will take their fingers and keep them safe in ice water during the stream. This has a playful but dark/yandere undertone. On her debut stream with the other Gamers, Mio asked if the fingers were a hostage, which Korone liked.

### 2.7 Dark Side Korone (enoroK)
After a capture card failure, Korone unveiled **Dark Side Korone** (enoroK), who has an inverted color spectrum and the opposite personality. enoroK scolds Korone for keeping viewers up late, encourages viewers to go to bed early, and returns viewers' fingers instead of taking them (though she did not like being called kind for doing so).

## 3. Famous Memes

### 3.1 "Yubi Yubi" (Finger Finger)
Korone's signature greeting and catchphrase. She tells viewers she will take their fingers at the start of the stream, place them in ice water for safekeeping, and give them back when it ends. The gag evolved into her "Ending Making Time" segments, where something happens to the viewers (often exploding, or the finger turning blue and unrecoverable).

- The "yu.. yu.." chant is used by viewers in chat while waiting for a stream to start.
- The phrase is written with pitch accents: ゆ↑び↓ ゆ↑び↓

### 3.2 "Okayu" — OkaKoro (Okakoro)
Korone is extremely close with **Nekomata Okayu**. The pair is known as "OkaKoro" and were friends even before joining hololive. Korone has said she did not audition for hololive—she got in due to being referred by Okayu. (Alternate origin: Fubuki says she saw them together at an arcade and asked them to join.)

### 3.3 Endurance Streams
Korone is famous for her extremely long endurance streams:
- First endurance stream: August 14, 2019 — **10 hours 45 minutes**
- **Maka Maka stream** (March 2020): **28 hours**
- **24-hour stream with Sakura Miko** (September 2019)
- Generally streams 5–12 hours of the same game

### 3.4 "Goma King" (Sesame Seed Kings)
Early in her career, Korone's chat was so small she thought viewers looked like sesame seeds (goma). Because they were so demanding, she crowned them "kings." The fan name stuck.

### 3.5 Traffic Lights
Korone sometimes displays traffic lights on stream to indicate chat mood:
- **Green**: Doting phase (chat praises her)
- **Yellow**: Nitpicky phase (chat points out mistakes)
- **Red**: Pestering phase (chat is agitated)

### 3.6 "Korone's English"
Korone has a sizable English-speaking fan base and actively studies English. Memorable mispronunciations:
- "Exposito" → sounded like **"X Potato"**
- "As you wish" → sounded like **"as you fish"**
- She has a tradition of yelling **"Hey Guys!"** near the end of streams to engage overseas fans.
- She used to run "Japanese study time" segments where she taught viewers Japanese phrases.

### 3.7 "TAS-kun"
After watching a Tool-Assisted Speedrun (TAS) of Castlevania 4 on YouTube, she played the game for hours and wondered how "Tas-kun" could beat it so quickly—not understanding it was a tool-assisted run.

### 3.8 Nyanpyou
A white cat-like creature holding a sword that appeared at the start of some streams. Korone discovered it in a minigame within the PS2 game *Pyū to Fuku! Jaguar* and played it for **7 hours straight**, then returned for another **12 hours** the next day, and a combined **11 more hours** for 100% completion. She even asked viewers to submit fanart.

### 3.9 Hosoinu & Futoinu
Two dogs used as emotes on her channel:
- **Hosoinu** (thin dog): a failed attempt at drawing her signature
- **Futoinu** (fat dog): based on her proper signature
- The designs are clearly based on Snoopy (Korone is a big fan)
- Illustrator Ordan helped popularize them in chat

### 3.10 563 Yen Donations
Fans like to donate **563 yen** because the numbers spell out her nickname: 5 (Ko) 6 (Ro) 3 (San).

## 4. Notable Streaming Moments

### 4.1 DOOM (2016) Playthrough
Korone played DOOM 2016 on stream and hit **500,000 YouTube subscribers** during her third DOOM session (July 3, 2020). Her playthrough became legendary as she enthusiastically engaged with the game's violence while maintaining her cheerful persona, creating a beloved contrast.

### 4.2 Mario Maker Rage
Korone played *Super Mario Maker* and *Super Mario Maker 2*, where she experienced the infamous "endless" levels created by fans. Her reactions ranged from determined focus to playful frustration, with her chat traffic lights cycling through all colors.

### 4.3 28-Hour Maka Maka Stream
In March 2020, Korone streamed *Maka Maka* (a quirky PS1 game) for **28 hours straight**—one of the most famous endurance streams in hololive history.

### 4.4 Sonic the Hedgehog Ambassador
On June 23, 2022, Korone was officially announced as a **Sonic the Hedgehog ambassador** and received a Sonic-themed costume.

### 4.5 "TV Tokyo" Comparisons
Japanese fans have compared Korone to TV Tokyo because she plays obscure retro games while everyone else is playing trendy titles. For example, when everyone was playing *Animal Crossing: New Horizons*, Korone played a *Gegege no Kitaro* SNES game.

### 4.6 Jump Rope Challenge Milestone
In January 2021, she played *Jump Rope Challenge* on Switch until she hit 1.3 million subscribers.

### 4.7 Sneeze Challenge
In May 2021, she held a guerilla challenge stream: reach 1.5 million subscribers before sneezing. She completed it in just 15 minutes 35 seconds.

### 4.8 Channel Logo
Her channel logo was designed by **Sakura Miko**.

### 4.9 Retro Game Collection
Korone owns an extensive collection of retro consoles: Famicom, Super Famicom, N64, GameCube, Wii, Wii U, Switch, DS (x4), Mega Drive, Sega Saturn, Dreamcast, PS2, PS3, PS4 (x2), PS5, PSP, PC Engine, and PC Engine Mini. She almost always plays original games on original hardware.

## 7. Interesting Anecdotes

1. **Height typo**: During her introduction stream, her height was mistakenly listed as **156 meters** (~512 feet) instead of 156 cm.
2. **She does not like bacon.**
3. **Bakery lore**: She works at a bakery in the city, hence the croissant emoji and bread/pastry theming.
4. **Anti-trendsetter**: She deliberately avoids popular games and plays whatever she finds interesting, typically obscure retro titles.
5. **Nyanpyou obsession**: She played a minigame called "Nyanpyou the Flute Meister" for a total of ~30 hours across multiple streams.
6. **Original song discography**: Includes *SAIKYO TENSAI WONDERFUL WORLD of KORONE*, *Doggy god's street*, *Wonky Monkey*, *TROUBLE "WAN"DER!*, and *Mafiosa*.
7. **2 million subscribers**: Achieved on June 28, 2023.
8. **Kusoge lover**: She loves "kuso-ge" (trash games) and "baka-ge" (stupid games), finding them endearing rather than frustrating.
9. **Battle cry "Horayo!"**: Shouts "Horayo!" (ホラヨ！) during exciting or intense moments on stream.
10. **Fear of bugs**: Afraid of insects, especially centipedes and mosquitoes.
11. **Horror movie fan**: Enjoys horror films including *Human Centipede* and *Saw*.
