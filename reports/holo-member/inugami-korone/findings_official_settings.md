## 2. Official Bio & Lore

**Official catchphrase:**
> "'Ello! Fingers! Give me your fingers!"
> 「ゆ↑び↓ ゆ↑び↓！」(Yubi yubi!)

**Official bio (from hololivepro.com):**
> "A dog from a bakery in the city. She likes to play games during her free time while watchdogging."

**Character lore:**
- Inugami Korone is a dog girl VTuber who works at a bakery in the city. This is why her design and theming incorporate bread and pastry motifs, and her representative emoji is a croissant.
- She is a member of the **Hololive GAMERS** sub-unit, a subdivision of Hololive Production focused on gaming content.
- She streams games during her free time while "watchdogging" (guarding the bakery).
- She originally did not audition for Hololive; she was referred by her close friend Nekomata Okayu (together known as the duo **"Okakoro"**). According to Shirakami Fubuki on a now-private HoloRadio episode, Fubuki spotted Okayu and Korone at an arcade and invited them to join.

## 4. Visual Appearance & Outfit Design

### Default Outfit (Illustrator: Fukahire, Live2D: rariemonn)
- Korone is designed as a **dog girl** with long, light blonde/cream-colored hair with brown dog ears on top.
- She wears a white baker's-style hat adorned with a croissant emblem.
- Her outfit consists of a white-and-brown baker's apron dress over a beige blouse, with a brown-orange pleated skirt section.
- She wears brown boots and carries a croissant motif prominently.
- Her tail is visible and fluffy, matching her dog ears.

### Design Motifs
- **Baker/dog girl** — apron, chef-style hat, croissant accessories
- Color palette dominated by **white, cream, warm brown, and orange**
- Her dog breed (Cavalier King Charles Spaniel) influences the ear and tail shapes
- Hosoinu (thin dog) and Futoinu (fat dog) are mascot characters derived from her own doodles, based on Snoopy

### Alternate & Seasonal Costumes
1. **Second outfit** — Casual wear (by Fukahire)
2. **New Year outfit** — Traditional Japanese kimono-style (revealed Jan 1, 2020)
3. **Nonstop Story idol outfit** — 3D concert costume (by Narumi-Nanami)
4. **Birthday cake accessories** — Added October 1, 2020
5. **Fifth costume** — Revealed July 14, 2021 (by Fukahire)
6. **Sonic the Hedgehog ambassador costume** — Revealed June 23, 2022 (Sega collaboration)
7. **Alice in Wonderland themed costume** — Revealed July 7, 2023
8. **3D Anniversary dog model** — Full furry dog form (3rd Anniversary)
9. **Holonatsu Paradise outfit**
10. **Gamer Fes outfit 2025**

### Other Visual Notes
- **Channel logo** was designed by Sakura Miko.
- **Dark Side Korone ("enoroK")** — An inverted-color alter ego with opposite personality, created spontaneously after a capture card failure. "enoroK" encourages viewers to sleep and scolds Korone for long streams.
- Korone sometimes uses **traffic lights** (red/yellow/green) on stream to indicate chat mood (doting / nitpicky / pestering).

## 7. Streaming Persona & Catchphrases

| Context | Phrase | Romaji / Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting | ぉぁょ | *oayo* — Korone's version of *ohayou* (good morning) |
| Introduction | "Yubi yubi!" / "Fingers!" | Taking viewers' fingers for safekeeping |
| Farewell | おつころーん | *otsukoron* — portmanteau of *otsukare* + *Korone* |
| Viewer waiting | ゆ。。ゆ。。 | *Yu.. Yu..* — first syllable of "Yubi" |
| English section | "Hey Guys!" | Tradition of addressing English-speaking fans |

### Streaming Style
- Known for **endurance streams** (5–12+ hours, famously a 28-hour Maka Maka stream)
- Plays **kusoge** (bad games) and **bakage** (stupid games) with genuine enjoyment
- Refuses to follow trends; famously played a Gegege no Kitaro SNES game while everyone else played Animal Crossing
- Often adds props on screen (coffee mug with pepper, etc.) and distorts her model for comedy
- Emotional when games deal with death, parting, or family themes
- Traffic lights on stream indicate perceived chat mood
- Grew from a very small chat she likened to "sesame seeds" (goma), calling viewers "Goma Kings"
- Actively studies English with a teacher and regularly speaks English to overseas fans
- Name: **563 yen** donations — 5=Ko, 6=Ro, 3=San = "Koro-san"

## 9. Notable Trivia

- **Channel logo** was designed and drawn by Sakura Miko.
- **Fans donate 563 yen** because 5=Ko, 6=Ro, 3=San spells "Koro-san".
- During her debut stream, her height was mistakenly listed as **156 meters** (not cm).
- She **does not like bacon**.
- Shares illustrator (Fukahire) with Civia (Hololive China), Ayano Date, and multiple other VTubers.
- Owns an extensive **retro game console collection**: Famicom, Super Famicom, N64, GameCube, Wii, Wii U, Switch, DS (x4), Mega Drive, Sega Saturn, Dreamcast, PS2, PS3, PS4 (x2), PS5, PSP, PC Engine, PC Engine Mini.
- Frequently plays games on original hardware rather than re-releases.
- Her mascots **Hosoinu** (thin dog) and **Futoinu** (fat dog) started as her own doodles; later received full designs.
- **Nyanpyou** — a white cat-like creature holding a sword from a PS2 mini-game that Korone played obsessively for hours.
