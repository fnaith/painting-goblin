## 3. ICONIC STREAMING MOMENTS

### "Eekum Bokum" (Aug 2020)
- During a Banjo-Kazooie stream (Aug 25, 2020), Korone repeatedly said "Eekum Bokum" whenever collecting a Mumbo Token
- YouTuber 2ManySnacks created an animated meme video (Aug 28, 2020) that went massively viral
- A second animation "NomNomNom" followed (Sep 19, 2020)
- Despite the meme's popularity, Korone later forgot how to say "Eekum Bokum" and produced increasingly garbled versions

### "X-Potato" (Exposito)
- During the credit roll of her Blasphemous stream, she tried to pronounce the boss name "Expósito" via Google Translate
- Mistakenly pronounced it as "X-Potato" — became one of her most famous memes
- Fans use "X-Potato" as a stand-in label for Korone's viewers

### "As You Fish" (Mispronunciation)
- During her "Japanese study time" segment, she tried to teach the phrase "as you wish"
- It came out sounding like "as you fish" — another beloved Engrish moment

### Dark Side Korone — "enoroK" (Capture Card Failure)
- After a capture card failure during a stream, Korone unveiled her inverted-color alter ego "enoroK"
- enoroK has the opposite personality: scolds Korone for keeping viewers up late, encourages sleep, and returns viewers' fingers instead of taking them
- enoroK did not like being called "kind" for returning fingers
- Demonstrated Korone's improvisational skills with technical issues

### Traffic Lights (Started during Assassin's Creed Valhalla)
- Korone introduced colored traffic light indicators on stream reflecting her perception of chat mood:
  - **Green:** Doting phase — chat showers praise
  - **Yellow:** Nitpicky phase — chat points out mistakes
  - **Red:** Pestering phase — chat agitated, telling her to hurry
- Became a recurring visual gag on her streams

### "Hey Guys!" and Japanese Study Time
- Tradition of yelling "Hey Guys!" near the end of streams to address English-speaking fans
- Started during her Siren stream
- Also included "Japanese study time" segments teaching viewers a word/phrase
- These segments have been largely discontinued

### "Eating Mike Tyson's Ass"
- Korone was the most frequent target of a donation troll who donated to English-illiterate VTubers to prank them into reading his name
- She smartened up and twisted the name into variations like "Eating Mike Tyson's Biohazard"
- Notable example of her adaptability to chat culture

### End Credits / "Ending Making Time"
- Her stream end segments originally featured a drawn caricature of viewers getting their finger back, but something went wrong (finger turned blue/unrecoverable)
- Replaced by "Ending Making Time" where viewers often explode or meet untimely ends

### Doom Eternal "DOOG" Easter Egg
- An easter egg in Doom Eternal's DLC "The Ancient Gods: Part 1" referenced Korone
- Pressing the chainsaw button 4 times on the rune slot screen produced a "DOOG Eternal" logo
- Removed in patch 3.1, likely due to copyright concerns with Cover Corp.
- Nod to Korone's avid use of the chainsaw in Doom (2016)

### Dragon's Lair Guilt
- Finished Dragon's Lair with 30 lives instead of default 5 — felt she cheated
- Later uploaded a video finishing legitimately with only 5 lives
- Demonstrates her strong conscience

### Monster Rancher Emotional Moment
- Got deeply sad when she had to freeze her monster due to old age
- The monster was one she initially called ugly but got very attached to
- Highlights her emotional expressiveness

### Tas-kun
- After seeing a Tool-Assisted Speedrun (TAS) of Castlevania 4 on YouTube
- Played the game for hours wondering how "Tas-kun" could beat it so fast
- Endearing misunderstanding of TAS terminology

## 6. NYANPYOU — THE OBSESSION

One of Korone's most famous prolonged segments:

| Session | Date | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| First playthrough | Dec 30, 2019 | 7 hours |
| Second playthrough | Dec 31, 2019 | ~12 hours (completed game) |
| Third + Fourth | Jan 2020 | ~11 hours combined (100% completion) |

**Details:**
- Played "Pyū to Fuku! Jaguar: Ashita no Jump" (PS2 game)
- Within it, a mini-game called "Nyanpyou the flute Meister" (ニャンピョウのフエマイスター)
- Nyanpyou is a white cat-like creature holding a sword
- Korone became obsessed, played it for ~30 hours total
- Asked fans to submit Nyanpyou fanart which she showcased on stream
- Nyanpyou became a recurring prop/emote on her streams (though now rarely used)

## 7. HOSOINU AND FUTOUINU — MASCOT CHARACTERS

| Character | Name Meaning | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| **Hosoinu (細い犬)** | "Thin dog" — drawn with thin lines | Failed attempt at drawing her signature |
| **Futoinu (太い犬)** | "Fat dog" — drawn with thick lines | Based on her actual signature |

**History:**
- Originally just her doodles, not intended as mascots
- Ordan (Tokino Sora's illustrator) started using them in chat, and they caught on
- Later received full fleshed-out designs by illustrator
- Design is clearly based on Snoopy (Korone is a big Snoopy fan)
- Hosoinu was fleshed out first; Futoinu followed because people felt bad it was left out
- Now used as channel emotes and stream props

## 12. NOTABLE TRIVIA & PERSONAL DETAILS

- **Health concern:** Kiryu Coco revealed Korone sleeps only ~2-3 hours a day between bakery work and streaming
- **Broken finger:** Once broke her ring finger gaming; doctor refused to believe her. Later found she was practicing a handspring for Hololive Fes stage performance
- **Martial arts:** Trained in boxing — once punched her chat (Listener-kun) for backseating during Super Mario World
- **American pop culture fan:** Loves South Park, Jurassic Park, Phineas & Ferb, Gravity Falls, Michael Jackson, SpongeBob SquarePants, and Boba Fett (has a large collection of Boba Fett merchandise)
- **Favorite game:** Ganbare Goemon
- **Favorite console:** Dreamcast
- **Kansai dialect:** Accent influenced by grandmother's Kansai dialect and parents' unknown dialect
- **Search history leak:** Accidentally leaked; contained Pentatonix, Rocky Horror Show, "top 16 torture methods," "slow execution methods," and "Cut Wound Ouch"
- **YouTube AI issues:** Due to COVID-19, YouTube AI auto-tagged all her videos with CDC COVID-19 tab (likely due to name similarity to "Corona"). She also posted positive comments about YouTube in descriptions to prevent AI from ending streams
- **"Korone Virus":** Fans adopted this term — replaced "Korone" with other VTuber names in usernames
- **Doom Eternal easter egg:** "DOOG Eternal" chainsaw reference — later removed due to copyright
- **Personality evolution:** Was quieter and more reserved in early streams/collabs; became more open and energetic over time. She credits Hololive's friendly atmosphere and her friendship with Okayu
- **AirServer glitches:** Screen mirroring software sometimes glitches, turning her model into a giant AirServer icon
- **Speaker user:** Took 2 years after debut to admit she normally plays game audio from speakers instead of headphones
- **Two pairs of ears:** Has dog ears on top AND human ears (unlike Okayu who only has one pair)
- **Voice cameo:** Voiced a character in Yo-Kai Watch♪ episode 94 (Mar 3, 2023)
- **Horror preferences:** Fan of horror movies including *Human Centipede* and *Saw*
- **Fear of insects:** Afraid of bugs, especially centipedes and mosquitoes
- **Heavy Rain finger scene reaction:** During a *Heavy Rain* stream, when a character cuts off their own finger, she was unfazed — consistent with her "Yubi Yubi" finger-collecting persona
- **Roomania 203 confidence issues:** During a Roomania 203 playthrough, she shared personal struggles with confidence, adding depth to her usually genki persona
- **Okayunyumu! VN (2024):** Appeared in Nekomata Okayu's visual novel developed by ENTERGRAM, released November 15, 2024
- **"Korone" etymology (fan theory):** Fans speculate her name resembles "chocolate cornet," a pastry — fitting her bakery theme
