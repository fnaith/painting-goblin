## 2. Retro Gaming Obsession (Core Fan Game Angle)

Korone is primarily known as a **variety gaming streamer** with a heavy focus on **retro/classic games**. This is the single most important characteristic for a fan game:

- **DOOM** — Her playthrough of DOOM (1993) became iconic, spawning the **"DOOG"** meme (DOOM + Dog). Fan art often depicts her in Doomguy's armor.
- **Banjo-Kazooie (N64)** — Her playthrough produced the famous **"Eekum Bokum"** meme, where she imitated the Mumbo Token collection sound. A fan animation by 2ManyStacks has 2.8M+ views.
- **Mario Maker** — She has played extensive Super Mario Maker, showcasing her platforming skills.
- **Sonic the Hedgehog** series — Frequently played on stream.
- **Minecraft** — Regular hololive server content.
- **Among Us** — Participated in hololive x Among Us collab (2022).
- **Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes** — Her stream with Mori Calliope spawned the **"I'm Die, Thank You Forever"** meme.
- **Other retro/nostalgia games**: N64 titles, classic platformers, and obscure retro games are her staple content.

**Takeaway for game design:** A pixel-art retro-style game (platformer, boomer shooter, or collectathon) would align perfectly with her brand.

## 3. Endurance Streaming & "No Sleep" Persona

- Known for streaming **extremely long sessions**, sometimes exceeding **24 hours consecutively**.
- Fans and other hololive members express concern for her health; jokes that she "cannot sleep" or "doesn't understand the concept of sleeping."
- This intense dedication / endless energy is a major part of her character.

**Takeaway for game design:** Could be represented as a stamina mechanic where Korone never tires, or an "endurance mode" / marathon level.

## 5. Relationship with Nekomata Okayu

- **Nekomata Okayu** is Korone's closest friend and fellow hololive GAMERS member.
- They previously **lived together**.
- Frequently stream together ("OkaKoro" content).
- Fans ship them as having a sisterly/domestic bond; often drawn together in fan art.
- Okayu is a cat (nekomata), Korone is a dog — classic cat-dog dynamic.

**Takeaway for game design:** Okayu could appear as a support character, co-op partner, or NPC.

## 6. English Ability & Western Appeal

- Frequently attempts to speak English on stream, often using **Google Translate**.
- Endearing broken English phrases ("I'm die, thank you forever") are beloved by international fans.
- One of the most popular hololive talents with Western audiences due to her efforts to bridge the language gap.

**Takeaway for game design:** Could include Engrish dialogue options, Google Translate-style text, or bilingual in-game text.

## 7. Dog-like Behavior & Bakery Theme

- Official description: "A dog from a bakery in the city. She likes to play games during her free time while watchdogging."
- Dog mannerisms: playful, loyal, energetic, excitable tail-wagging energy.
- Bakery theme: She works at a bakery, so bread/pastry motifs appear in her branding.
- Her fan name "Koronesuki" sounds like "Korone suki" ("I like Korone").

**Takeaway for game design:** Bakery-themed levels, bread-based power-ups, or dog mechanics (fetch, digging, barking).
