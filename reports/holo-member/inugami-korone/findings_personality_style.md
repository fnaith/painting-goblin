## 2. PERSONALITY TRAITS

### Cheerful and Energetic
Korone has a very cheerful and energetic personality. She appears outgoing but can be shy in large collabs, though she has grown more open over the years.

### Eccentric / Does Things at Her Own Pace
She operates on her own wavelength. Examples:
- Wanted to do a stream about various torture methods
- Will turn attempts to end a stream into extended talking with viewers
- Has no set schedule — ends streams when she feels like it, not at a predetermined time
- When everyone played Animal Crossing on Switch launch, she played a Gegege no Kitaro SNES game instead
- Japanese fans compare her to TV Tokyo: airing her own content while everyone else covers major events

### Playful and Chaotic
- Makes various noises relating to in-game actions
- Adds random props on screen (e.g., coffee mug with a pepper in it)
- Messes with her Live2D model (copies of it, turning it upside down)
- Uses "traffic light" indicators on stream: green = chat doting phase, yellow = nitpicky phase, red = pestering/agitated phase (started during Assassin's Creed Valhalla stream)

### Emotionally Expressive
- Finds weird-looking things "cute"
- Gets very emotional over games dealing with death of family members or parting with friends
- Got deeply sad in Monster Rancher when she had to freeze her monster due to old age (a monster she initially called ugly but got attached to)
- Cried at the ending of Klonoa

### Strong Conscience
Felt bad when she finished Dragon's Lair with 30 lives instead of default 5, feeling she cheated. Later uploaded a video of her finishing it legitimately with only 5 lives.

### Duality — "enoroK" (Dark Side)
After a capture card failure, Korone unveiled "Dark Side Korone" (enoroK) — opposite personality with inverted colors who scolds Korone for keeping viewers up late and returns viewers' fingers instead of taking them.

## 3. CATCHPHRASES AND VERBAL TICS

### "Yubi yubi!" (ゆ↑び↓ ゆ↑び↓)
- Means "Finger finger!" — her signature greeting
- She claims she will take viewers' fingers at the start of a stream, place them in ice water for safekeeping, and return them at the end
- Origin: Before debut, her fingers got tired from responding to tweets and she wished she had 100 of them
- Viewers in chat say "Yu.. Yu.." (ゆ。。ゆ。。) while waiting for a stream to start, completing "Yubi!" when it begins
- Her ending segments used to feature a drawn caricature of viewers getting their finger back, but something goes wrong (finger turns blue/unrecoverable). Replaced by "Ending Making Time" where viewers often explode.

### Greeting: "ぉぁょ" (oayo)
- Korone's version of "ohayou" (good morning), written with small characters.

### Farewell: "おつころーん" (Otsukoron)
- Portmanteau of "otsukare" (good work) + "Korone"

### "Ello! Fingers! Give me your fingers!"
- The English version in her official bio

### "Hey Guys!"
- She used to yell this near the end of streams to address her English-speaking fans. Started during her Siren stream. Also began "Japanese study time" segments here (teaching viewers a Japanese word/phrase; now largely discontinued).

### "Koronesuki" / "Goma King"
- Calls fans "Koronesuki" (Loves Korone) and "Goma King" (sesame seed kings — because her chat was so small they looked like sesame seeds, and since they were demanding, she dubbed them "kings").

## 4. STREAMING CONTENT

### Retro Games / Retro Gamer
- Known as a massive retro game enthusiast who owns numerous consoles: Famicom, Super Famicom, N64, GameCube, Wii, Wii U, Switch, DS (x4), Mega Drive, Sega Saturn, Dreamcast, PS2, PS3, PS4 (x2), PS5, PSP, PC Engine, PC Engine Mini
- Almost always plays original games on original hardware rather than re-releases

### Kusoge / Bakage (Bad/Stupid Games)
- Loves playing intentionally bad or silly games ("kusoge" = crap game, "bakage" = stupid game)
- Laughs at them instead of getting frustrated

### Endurance Streams
- Famous for VERY long streams — typically 5–12 hours of the same game
- Milestone endurance streams: 10h45m (first endurance, Aug 2019), 24-hour stream with Sakura Miko (Sep 2019), 28-hour Maka Maka stream (Mar 2020)

### Notable Games Played
- **Doom (2016)** — famously played through the entire game on stream; hit 500k subs during third Doom stream
- **Dragon's Lair** — completed with self-imposed restrictions
- **Klonoa** — emotional ending reaction
- **Monster Rancher** — got attached to monsters
- **Nyanpyou** — a mini-game within PS2 "Pyū to Fuku! Jaguar" that she played for 7+12+11 hours, getting 100% completion and asking fans to submit Nyanpyou fan art
- **Slither.io** — hit 700k subs during this stream
- **Spider-Man** — hit 400k subs during this stream
- Assassin's Creed Valhalla (introduced traffic lights)
- Various SNES, Famicom, Sega Saturn, Dreamcast titles

### Doesn't Follow Trends
Actively avoids what's popular. When everyone played Animal Crossing, she chose an obscure SNES game. Fans compare her to TV Tokyo for this trait.

### Sonic the Hedgehog Ambassador
In June 2022, Korone was officially announced as a Sonic the Hedgehog ambassador and received a Sonic-themed costume.

## 7. DOG / INU-THEMED MANNERISMS

### In-Universe Identity
- She is a dog (Cavalier King Charles Spaniel) who works at a bakery
- Age is given in dog years (95 = ~13 human years)
- Her name means "Dog God" (Inugami) + "Korone"
- Official bio: "A dog from a bakery in the city. She likes to play games during her free time while watchdogging."

### Dog-Themed Characters
- **Hosoinu** (細い犬 = thin dog) and **Futoinu** (太い犬 = fat dog) — two Snoopy-like dogs that appear as props/emotes. Hosoinu originated from a failed attempt at drawing her signature. Both were later fully designed and became mainstays.
- **Nyanpyou** — a white cat-like creature that was a recurring character (despite being a cat, coexists with her dog theme)

### Behavioral Dog-isms
- Makes playful barking/animal noises
- "Watchdogging" terminology in official bio
- Protective/possessive of viewers (taking their fingers for "safekeeping")
- Energetic and loyal (dog-like traits)
- Dog model added for 3rd anniversary (3D model)

### Bakery/Bread Theme
- Represents with a croissant emoji 🥐
- Works at a bakery (in lore)
- Bread and pastry theming around her character design
