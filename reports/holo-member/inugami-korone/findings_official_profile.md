## Summary

Inugami Korone is a hololive GAMERS VTuber who debuted April 13, 2019. She is characterized as a dog from a bakery who loves gaming. Her official fan name is "Koronesky" (or ころねすきー in Japanese). Her signature motifs include croissants (🥐), dog imagery, and her iconic catchphrase asking viewers for their "fingers." She is illustrated by Fukahire and stands at 156 cm with an October 1 birthday. As of mid-2026, she has approximately 1.5M followers on X (Twitter).
