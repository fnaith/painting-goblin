## Character Design & Appearance

- **Hair:** Light brown, worn in a side ponytail with an ahoge (cowlick) that changes shape with mood
- **Eyes:** Light blue
- **Key accessory:** Black ribbon with green stripes and a white cat head charm (the cat is named **Sunagimo**)
- **Outfit:** Short orange top with inner black layer and green ribbon; pleated white skirt with orange trim and green/black ribbon
- **Color scheme:** Orange, white, black, green — festival/summer vibes
- **Motif:** Summer festival ("Matsuri" = festival), lanterns, cheerleading

## Mascot

- **Ebifrion** — part fried-shrimp ("ebifurai") + part lion ("lion"). Speaks in Kansai dialect with an old man's voice. Can be seen on Matsuri's pajama outfit.

## Personality Profile

Matsuri is known as an energetic, expressive, and chaotic cheerleader. Key traits:

- **Outward persona:** Rowdy, tomboyish, playful, occasionally chaotic (infamous for groping other members, doing "pee-holding" streams, etc.)
- **Inner persona:** Emotionally intelligent, sensitive, sweet, serious, hardworking — often revealed during late-night audio-only Twitcasting streams
- **Reliable senpai:** Frequently cited by juniors as a senpai they respect and can rely on
- **Deeply caring:** Treasures her fans, interacts with them constantly via Twitter self-searches, expressive about her love for her viewers

## Trivia

- Her first original song "Kimi to Nagameru Natsu no Hana" was written by a fan for her first birthday
- Fans often donate **7216 yen** — number wordplay: 7=na(na), 2=tsu ("two"), 1=i(chi), 6=ro(ku) → "Natsuiro"
- Allergic to dogs (despite loving them) and citrus fruits
- Favorite foods: donuts, sea urchin, sweets, ramen
- Has a younger brother and a pet Pomeranian named Kuu-chan (short for "Cream")
- Her mother "Mamatsuri" has appeared on stream
- Open about using her real voice when requested, but prefers to use her character voice on stream
