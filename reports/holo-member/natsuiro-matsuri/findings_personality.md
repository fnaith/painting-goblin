## Official Bio

> "Wasshoi! hololive's symbol of purity and everyone's idol, Natsuiro Matsuri here!"

A rookie member of her school cheerleading team. Cheerful, energetic, and affectionate personality — gets along with everyone and has lots of friends. Loves festivals, events, and other fun things.

**Debut:** June 1, 2018 (hololive 1st Generation)
**Birthday:** July 22
**Height:** 152 cm
**Illustrator:** Minamura Haruki
**Dream:** To be the top idol & voice actress
**Fan Name:** Matsurisu (Matsuri + "risu" = listener; also "risu" = squirrel, so fans are represented as squirrels)
**Oshi Mark:** 🏮 (lantern / festival)

## Core Personality Traits

### 1. Energetic & Chaotic
- Lively, outgoing, and chaotic personality. Very loud and playfully boisterous.
- Has lots of VTuber friends outside of hololive.
- Self-describes as "seiso" (pure/innocent), but her actions often contradict this — e.g., groping Hoshimachi Suisei upon first meeting, hosting pee-holding streams, reading Comic LO on stream.

### 2. Sensitive & Kind-Hearted (Behind the Chaos)
- Underneath the outrageous behavior, she is genuinely sensitive and kind-hearted.
- Often reveals her vulnerable side during late-night Twitcast (audio-only) streams before sleeping or in the bath — candid conversations about her inner thoughts.
- Called Inuyama Tamaki (independent VTuber) late at night to talk through worries as both an individual and an idol.

### 3. Reliable & Respected Senpai
- Frequently named by newer hololive members as the senpai they respect and can rely on.
- Has taught APEX Legends to juniors like Uruha Rushia and Kiryu Coco.

### 4. Affectionate Towards Fans
- Treasures her fans deeply; consistently interacts via self-searching on Twitter.
- Expresses love for fans without reserve in streams and tweets.
- When fans donate a Super Chat, chat responds with "うん" (Uh-huh) — a meme born from a drunk call with Nijisanji's Yashiro Kizuku.
- Fans donate a "sneeze fee" (くしゃみ代) whenever she sneezes on-stream.
- Popular donation amount: 7216 yen (7=Na, 2=Tsu, 1=I, 6=Ro — a pun on "Natsuiro").

## Quirks & Habits

### On-Stream Behaviors
- **Pee-holding streams:** Drinking game where participants drink water on certain triggers (e.g., losing a game) and resist going to the bathroom. Originally a concept from Inuyama Tamaki.
- **Self-searching on Twitter:** Regularly searches her name to interact with fan posts.
- **Late-night Twitcast:** Audio-only candid streams (bath/before sleep) where she opens up about personal thoughts.
- **Favorite Super Chat color:** Red — but tells viewers not to push themselves financially.

### Food Obsessions
- **Donuts** — especially *Young Donuts* brand (official promotion campaign with Shirakami Fubuki).
- Ramen, sweets in general.

### Relationships
- **Shirakami Fubuki (NatsuFubu):** Extremely close; has confessed her love for Fubuki many times. Frequent collaborations.
- **Himemori Luna (FestivalLuna):** Very close; received matching pajamas from Luna.
- **Rikkaroid (MatsuRikka):** Singing pair with Holostars member; first hololive/holostars 3D collab.

### Mascot
- **Ebifrion:** Part fried-shrimp (ebi-fry), part lion. Speaks with Kansai dialect and an old man's voice. Visible in her pajama outfit.

## Game Character Design Notes

- **Core archetype:** Cheerleader / festival idol — energetic, chaotic, affectionate
- **Visual motifs:** Lantern (🏮), festival colors (summer), squirrel (fans), fried-shrimp lion (Ebifrion)
- **Voice direction:** High-energy, loud, playful; can shift to soft/candid for emotional moments
- **Character arc potential:** Chaotic exterior hiding a sensitive, caring core; reliable senpai dynamic
- **Signature move ideas:** "Wasshoi!" cheer buffs, lantern-based abilities, squirrel-summoning, donut-themed power-ups
- **Catchphrase to include as ability name:** "Konbanwasshoi!" or "Omatsuriwasshoi!"
