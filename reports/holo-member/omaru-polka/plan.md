# Research Plan: Omaru Polka (尾丸ポルカ)

## Main Research Question
Research Hololive member Omaru Polka for use in fan game character setting design.

## Subtopics

### 1. Official Profile & Design
- Official character design, debut date, height, birthday, color scheme
- VTuber model design elements and motifs
- Catchphrases and signature phrases

### 2. Character Personality & Themes
- Personality traits and character archetype
- Circus/performer-themed character elements
- Character lore and backstory
- Relationships with other Hololive members

### 3. Notable Anecdotes & Fan Game Material
- Memorable moments, achievements, and notable events
- Fan game related content or appearances
- Unique quirks that could translate to game mechanics
- Popular memes associated with the character
