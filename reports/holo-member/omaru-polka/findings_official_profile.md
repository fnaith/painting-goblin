## Personality & Traits
- Highly energetic, outgoing, and silly — fitting her circus performer persona
- Speaks extremely fast, often outpacing native Japanese speakers
- Rapid emotional shifts (loud/energetic ↔ quiet/subdued)
- Chaotic, unpredictable streams with frequent topic jumps
- Easily affected by weather / atmospheric pressure
- Known for playing various instruments live: bells, rotary phone, glockenspiel, vibraslap, rubber chickens
- Distinctive laugh often compared to Usada Pekora's
- Often the target of pranks from fellow hololive members

## Membership (Omaru-za おまる座)

### Membership Perks (from hololive.wiki)
- Variety of emotes/emojis for chat
- Monthly ASMR streams (relaxing, "just the two of us")
- Community posts (member-only diaries and videos)
- "A Men○○ Diary by a Men○○" — content that can't be shown publicly
- Occasional project-planning streams
- Work streams and song practice sessions
- Writing poetry and novels on stream
- Occasional display of her computer's recycle bin size
- Irregular members-only meetings

### Stream Rules (from stream descriptions)
- No excessive conversation in pre-stream waiting area
- Avoid unrelated topics during performance
- Don't talk about people not mentioned in the stream
- Don't bring Polka topics into other streamers' chats
- Enjoy Polka's stream atmosphere only within her stream
- Be careful about spoilers; read the room
- Don't overzealously enforce rules on others — getting along is priority
- No strong language, meaningless spam, or comments against Polka's intent

## Other Facts
- Debuted original song **HOLOGRAM CIRCUS** on her debut stream
- Reached 100K subscribers on debut day (before debut even started)
- First 5th Gen member to receive a 3D model (January 2021)
- Reached 1,000,000 YouTube subscribers on July 31, 2022
- Current subscribers: ~1.36M (as of May 2026)
- Hosts "Polka no Densetsu" (Legend of Polka) variety show (since April 2021)
- Hosts "Polka Radio Ka" radio series (since September 2021)
- Mascot character is named **Zain** (座員) — fennec-like creature from Picrew
- Voiced boss character Tania in *Little Witch Nobeta*
- Was made playable in *Disgaea 6* as Circus Mage "Pol-Pol"
- Voiced character Yuzuyuzu in *Dogengers Metropolis* (live-action tokusatsu)
- Collaborated with *Shironeko Project* (White Cat Project) (July-August 2025)
- Featured in upcoming game *"Test Play wo Sabori Sugita RPG"* (2026)
- Participated in Fuji-Q Highland collaboration (Sept-Oct 2021)
- Sweets Paradise collab cafe (Nov-Dec 2024)
- Haneda Airport Garden × Shiranui Construction event (Feb-Mar 2025)
- Tokyo Joypolis "SWEETY PARTY" event as NePoLaBo (May-Jul 2025)
