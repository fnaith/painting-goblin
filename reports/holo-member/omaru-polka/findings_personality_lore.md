### Name Etymology
- **Omaru (尾丸):** "Round tail" — references her fennec fox tail
- **Polka (ポルカ):** Named after the polka dance, which gained popularity in 19th-century European circus performances (in particular, the "circus polka" tradition). Also references her fast-paced, dancing-like speaking style.
- Full name translates loosely to "Round Tail Polka" — a circus performer with a round, fluffy tail doing a polka dance.

### Background Before Hololive
- Before debuting as a VTuber, Polka had experience as a **DJ and MC** at live events
- This experience directly informs her stream hosting ability, her crowd-reading skills, and her comfort with live performance
- Her DJ background is occasionally referenced in her music production and remixes

## 2. Character Lore & Backstory

### Circus Performer Theme
Polka's entire character identity is built around a circus/performer motif:
- She is a **quarter-part fennec fox** who is a **circus-less ringmistress** — she decided to turn Hololive itself into her circus.
- Her representative emoji is the circus tent (🎪).
- Her streams are called "performances" (公演), and her audience are "troupe members" (座員).
- Her costume features circus-ringmaster-inspired design elements: a top hat, tailcoat-like jacket, and a prominent fluffy tail.
- Her last name "Omaru" (尾丸) means "round tail" — she is very proud of her super fluffy tail.

### Origin Story
Polka joined Hololive with the ambition of becoming the "ringmaster of the VTuber world." Unlike a traditional circus ringmaster who commands a fixed troupe, Polka's circus is the entire Hololive organization — her fellow talents are performers in her big top.

### The "Shared Consciousness" Concept
A unique element of Polka's character lore is the concept of **shared consciousness** between Polka and her fans (the Troupe). In her own words: "Troupe members are Polka. By supporting me, you too can become one with Polka." This blurs the line between performer and audience — the Troupe IS Polka, and Polka IS the Troupe.

### Eye Design
Polka's eyes have symbols in the center of the pupil that change depending on her expression. The symbols are based on French card suits (spades, hearts, clubs), with a star replacing diamonds. This ties into her circus/performer theme — playing cards being a classic circus/fairground motif.

### Fennec Fox Heritage
Polka is identified as quarter-part fennec fox. This manifests in her design through large fennec-like ears and her prominent fluffy tail. She has made contradictory comments about having human ears vs. only fox ears, but her 3D model confirmed she has both.

## 3. Personality Traits & Character Archetype

### Core Archetype: Chaotic Ringmaster / Energetic Performer
Polka embodies the **"Chaotic Ringmaster"** archetype — a character who is always performing, always entertaining, but whose energy can be unpredictable and overwhelming.

### Key Personality Traits

**High Energy & Rapid Speech:**
- Polka speaks extremely fast — often faster than even native Japanese viewers can follow.
- Her natural speaking pace is frantic, bouncing between topics rapidly.
- She has been described as having "verbal diarrhea" in the most endearing sense.

**Emotional Roller Coaster:**
- Polka's emotions fluctuate wildly and suddenly. One moment she is extremely loud and energetic, the next she is quiet and subdued.
- This "Emotional Roller Coaster" is a known Polka trait and a source of entertainment for fans.
- She is easily affected by weather, particularly low atmospheric pressure, which triggers headaches and lower energy.

**Unpredictable & Chaotic:**
- She abandoned scheduled streaming early on, dubbing herself the **"Guerrilla Stream Queen"** — announcing streams 15 minutes to 2 hours in advance.
- Her stream times are erratic, ranging from early morning to very late night.
- In games, she starts with a plan but quickly abandons it for whatever catches her attention.
- Her Minecraft projects are famous for stalling due to constant distractions and detours.

**Hardworking Behind the Chaos:**
- Despite the chaotic exterior, Polka works very hard behind the scenes.
- She was involved in designing merchandise for the "Bloom," concert and came up with the idea of metallic floral charms.
- Her "Legend of Polka" weekly series shows her collaborating with Hololive staff (Ms. Izutsu) on produced content.
- She runs "Polka Radio Ka" as a more relaxed, personal project.

**Knowledgeable Otaku:**
- Like Houshou Marine, Polka is deeply knowledgeable about 90s and 2000s anime and otaku culture.
- She is touchy about her age (a running joke in the community).
- Her karaoke repertoire spans many idol groups: Dempagumi.inc, Morning Musume, Momoiro Clover Z, etc.

**Musical & Multi-Instrumental:**
- Polka owns an extensive collection of musical instruments: maracas, guiro, otamatone, cajon, vibraslap, triangle, various whistles, chimes, cowbell, snare drum, tambourine, kalimba, harmonica, ukulele, glockenspiel, accordion, and more.
- The **kazoo** has become her signature instrument — she plays it mid-game, during collabs, and even as a solo in karaoke.
- She owns approximately 10 kazoos.

**Self-Aware & Playful:**
- Polka is aware of her chaotic nature and leans into it as part of her character.
- She engages with foreign fans despite not speaking English, using greetings like "Good morning, good evening, good afternoon!"
- She played Among Us in English with international fans.

**Speech Quirks:**
- A dental procedure in childhood caused a slight speech impediment: her "nya/nyi/nyu/nye/nyo" sounds come out as "ni-tya/ni-tyi/ni-tyu/ni-tye/ni-tyo."
- She frequently uses the syllable "POL" (ぽる) to create portmanteaus: OHAPOL (good morning), HIRUPOL (good afternoon), OYAPOL (good night), POLMAO (POL + LMAO).

### The "Seven Voices" of Polka (from Fandom)
Polka is noted for having seven distinct voice types/characters she can perform:
1. **High-pitched character voice** (used as a VTuber persona)
2. **Natural lower voice** (closer to her real voice)
3. **Narrator voice**
4. **Character impression voices** (various anime/game characters)
5. **Singing voice** (clear and resonant, notably different from speaking voice)
6. **Whispering/ASMR voice** (used in members-only ASMR streams)
7. **Comedy/over-the-top voice** (for skits and impressions)

This versatility has been compared to a **rakugo performer** (traditional Japanese comic storyteller who plays multiple roles using only voice and a fan) — Polka is described as "like a rakugo performer" in her ability to switch characters and maintain a one-person show.

### Polka's "Unit" Concepts (from Seesaawiki)
- **"1 Polka = 100°C"** — A Polka-based measurement system. One Polka is boiling hot (energy level), meaning her energy is always at maximum.
- **"scrap & build !"** — Her philosophy of constantly tearing down and rebuilding, both in content creation and personal growth.
- She treats her content as a **"scrap & build"** cycle — constantly iterating, experimenting, and reinventing her streaming style.

### The "Shared Consciousness" Concept
When asked, Polka describes herself as "neutral" — neither introvert nor extrovert. This aligns with her ability to be incredibly high-energy on stream but also having quieter, more subdued moments.

## 6. Fan Community Perception

### Fan Name & Relationship
- Fans are called **Zain** (座員 / Troupe Members), collectively the **Omaru-za** (おまる座 / Omaru Troupe).
- There is a fan-made Zain mascot (a small fennec-like creature) from a Picrew dress-up maker that was adopted as semi-official by Polka herself.
- The "shared consciousness" concept means fans feel a strong sense of being PART of Polka's performance, not just observers.

### Community Memes & Running Jokes
- **POLMAO** — Portmanteau of POL + LMAO, used by fans to describe laugh-out-loud moments.
- **Polpeller** — Her cowlick; fans treat it as an actual propeller.
- **Guerrilla Queen** — Her unpredictable streaming schedule is a beloved trait, not a flaw.
- **"Polka oru ka?" / "Oru yo!"** — Call-and-response greeting that fans use in chat before streams.
- **Emotional Roller Coaster** — Fans embrace the sudden shifts between high energy and calm.
- **Super Chat Song Copy-Pasta** — During Super Chat readings, fans paste the lyrics in chat: "ｽｰﾊﾟｰﾁｬｯﾄｱﾘｶﾞﾄｰ🎶..."
- **"Po" Language** — After releasing the song "Po", Polka and fans sometimes communicate using only the syllable "po."
- **POLTATO PC** — Describing Polka's old, underpowered computer.
- **"BEEF PC"** — The upgraded computer, described as "beefy" after the Poltato era.
- **Polka = 100°C** — A unit of measurement invented by fans; one Polka equals maximum energy.
- **"Polka is a Rakugo Performer"** — Fan comparison of Polka's voice acting to traditional Japanese rakugo storytelling.

### Community Perception Summary
- Polka is widely regarded as **underappreciated but beloved** — her chaotic streaming style and lack of schedule mean she has a smaller but extremely dedicated fanbase.
- She is seen as **genuinely talented** (singing, instruments, acting, coordination) hiding behind a clown persona.
- Fans appreciate her **hard work** behind the scenes — she is involved in concert production, merchandise design, and content planning beyond just streaming.
- Her **musicianship** (especially the kazoo) is a defining and endearing quirk.
- The **"shared consciousness"** lore creates a unique parasocial relationship where fans feel like active participants in the circus.

## 7. Notable Projects & Series

### Legend of Polka (ポルカの伝説)
- Weekly 10-12 minute videos starting April 29, 2021.
- Format: Hololive staff member Ms. Izutsu brings situations for Polka to react to (e.g., childhood manga, kids asking for advice).
- Highly produced content showcasing Polka's acting and improvisation skills.

### Polka Radio Ka (ポルカラジオカ)
- Weekly short radio project starting September 14, 2021.
- Audio-only, around 10 minutes, covering 1-3 topics.
- More relaxed and personal compared to Legend of Polka and her regular streams.

### 🔴NOW Playlist
- A playlist of games/projects Polka intends to complete.
- Represents her genuine desire to finish what she starts, despite her chaotic tendencies.
