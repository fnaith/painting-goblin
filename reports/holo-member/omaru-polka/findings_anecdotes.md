## Unique Quirks & Character Traits (Game Mechanic Translation Ideas)

### 1. Polpeller (ポルペラ)
- Her cowlick (ahoge) behaves like a propeller, spinning around
- **Game mechanic idea:** Spin attack, helicopter glide, or propeller boost ability

### 2. Kazoo Instrument
- Polka plays the kazoo during streams as a signature bit
- **Game mechanic idea:** Sound-based attacks, buff/debuff via kazoo notes, summoning item

### 3. Guerrilla Stream Queen (ゲリラ配信の女王)
- Known for completely unpredictable, unannounced "surprise" streams
- **Game mechanic idea:** Random appearance/surprise attack element, stealth/invisibility mechanic

### 4. Emotional Roller Coaster
- Alternates between high-energy chaotic chatter and calm, composed conversation in seconds
- **Game mechanic idea:** Dual-mode mechanic (Berserker mode / Calm mode) with different stats or abilities

### 5. Shared Consciousness with Troupe
- Considers her audience (ZA-IN / Troupe members) as one being with herself
- **Game mechanic idea:** Summon/ally mechanic, crowd-based power scaling, shared HP or resource pool

### 6. Circus Ringmaster Theme
- Her entire persona is circus/sideshow themed — ringmaster, acrobatics, fire-jumping
- **Game mechanic idea:** Acrobatic movement, trap/lasso skills, stage-construction abilities, juggling projectiles

### 7. Fennec Fox Traits
- Species is a fennec fox — large ears, small body
- **Game mechanic idea:** Enhanced hearing/sonar, small hitbox, digging/burrowing, sand-related abilities

### 8. "Connection Investigation" Running Gag
- Polka investigating why her internet is bad, only to discover she caused it herself
- **Game mechanic idea:** Self-sabotage/reversal mechanic, "investigation" minigame, debuff that turns into a buff

### 9. Surprise Entrance - "Where's Polka?"
- Streams often start with the audience asking "Where's Polka?" before she appears
- **Game mechanic idea:** Teleportation, hidden appearance, or deployable decoy

### 10. Loves Gold, Popcorn, and Shine Muscat grapes
- **Game mechanic idea:** Collectible bonus items, crafting materials, or currency affinity

## Stream Content Patterns

Polka's content is known for:
- **"Polka no Densetsu" (Polka's Legend)** — Her own variety show segment on her channel (started April 2021)
- **"Anime! Mokepol-chan"** — Original animation corner
- **Gaming streams** — Wide variety of games
- **Karaoke (Utawaku)** — Emotionally charged singing
- **ASMR content** — Posted monthly for members
- **Irregular guerrilla streaming** — No fixed schedule, appears at random times
- **Costume coordination** — One of her stated special skills
