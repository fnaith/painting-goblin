## Memorable Moments & Notable Events

### Debut & Early History
- Debuted as part of Hololive 5th Generation (NePoLaBo) alongside Yukihana Lamy, Momosuzu Nene, Shishiro Botan
- Her debut stream had major connectivity issues — she was attempting to stream using a mobile phone data line, which became a running gag ("Connection Investigation")
- Reached 100K subscribers before her debut even ended (50K goal, but hit 100K)
- First original catchphrase: coined "NePoLaBo" as the generation group name by taking the first two letters of each member's name

### Legendary/Notable Streams (from hololive.wiki)
- **"Omaru Polka's Legend of Connections"** — A stream where Polka investigated bad audio, only to discover she was playing music through her phone that was being picked up by the mic
- **"The 5 o'clock morning stream"** — A famous early-morning stream where Polka just quietly talked and drew

## Unique Quirks & Character Traits (Game Mechanic Translation Ideas)

### 1. Polpeller (ポルペラ)
- Her cowlick (ahoge) behaves like a propeller, spinning around
- **Game mechanic idea:** Spin attack, helicopter glide, or propeller boost ability

### 2. Kazoo Instrument
- Polka plays the kazoo during streams as a signature bit
- **Game mechanic idea:** Sound-based attacks, buff/debuff via kazoo notes, summoning item

### 3. Guerrilla Stream Queen (ゲリラ配信の女王)
- Known for completely unpredictable, unannounced "surprise" streams
- **Game mechanic idea:** Random appearance/surprise attack element, stealth/invisibility mechanic

### 4. Emotional Roller Coaster
- Alternates between high-energy chaotic chatter and calm, composed conversation in seconds
- **Game mechanic idea:** Dual-mode mechanic (Berserker mode / Calm mode) with different stats or abilities

### 5. Shared Consciousness with Troupe
- Considers her audience (ZA-IN / Troupe members) as one being with herself
- **Game mechanic idea:** Summon/ally mechanic, crowd-based power scaling, shared HP or resource pool

### 6. Circus Ringmaster Theme
- Her entire persona is circus/sideshow themed — ringmaster, acrobatics, fire-jumping
- **Game mechanic idea:** Acrobatic movement, trap/lasso skills, stage-construction abilities, juggling projectiles

### 7. Fennec Fox Traits
- Species is a fennec fox — large ears, small body
- **Game mechanic idea:** Enhanced hearing/sonar, small hitbox, digging/burrowing, sand-related abilities

### 8. "Connection Investigation" Running Gag
- Polka investigating why her internet is bad, only to discover she caused it herself
- **Game mechanic idea:** Self-sabotage/reversal mechanic, "investigation" minigame, debuff that turns into a buff

### 9. Surprise Entrance - "Where's Polka?"
- Streams often start with the audience asking "Where's Polka?" before she appears
- **Game mechanic idea:** Teleportation, hidden appearance, or deployable decoy

### 10. Loves Gold, Popcorn, and Shine Muscat grapes
- **Game mechanic idea:** Collectible bonus items, crafting materials, or currency affinity

### 11. Poltato → Beef PC Upgrade
- Her old PC (Poltato) was so underpowered it caused constant stream issues
- In 2022, Tokino Sora and Shishiro Botan gifted her PC parts for an upgrade
- The "Beef PC" represents a major power-up moment in her lore
- **Game mechanic idea:** Equipment upgrade system, power scaling, or temporary "empowered" state

### 12. The "Shared Consciousness" Frame Break
- Polka reads Super Chats and acts as if she and her audience share one mind
- **Game mechanic idea:** Fourth-wall breaking ability, audience participation mechanic, "crowd power" meter

### 13. Hate for Low Pressure / Humidity
- Polka openly struggles with low atmospheric pressure, which causes headaches and low energy
- **Game mechanic idea:** Environmental debuff system — weather affects character stats

### 14. The "Po" Language
- After the song "Po", Polka and fans can communicate using only variants of "po"
- **Game mechanic idea:** Language/communication puzzle, limited-vocabulary challenge mode

### 15. Kazoo Solo Ability
- The kazoo is her signature instrument — used mid-game, in karaoke, in collabs
- **Game mechanic idea:** Sound-based attack (kazoo blast), taunt, or buff ability

### 16. Rakugo-Style Performance
- Polka can switch between 7+ distinct voices/characters, like a traditional rakugo comic storyteller
- **Game mechanic idea:** Character swap mechanic, multiple "stances" with different abilities
