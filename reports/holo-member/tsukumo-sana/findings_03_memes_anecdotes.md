# Tsukumo Sana — Memes, Anecdotes & Trivia

## Famous Memes & Running Gags
1. **"Sana is BEEEG"** — her catchphrase referencing her true cosmic size
2. **"Uranus jokes"** — her membership perk mentions fitting planets into Uranus; she loves this immature humor
3. **Height jokes** — 169 cm with limiter vs 1.7 million km without; she's the tallest EN member
4. **Limiter antenna** — common fan mistake: drawing the antenna while she's at full size (it only appears at human size)
5. **Destroying Earth** — constantly joked about accidentally destroying Earth
6. **Sananara** — her signature farewell portmanteau

## Notable Events
- **Horse Birthing Stream (Membership Opening)**: With chat's help, designed/"gave birth" to a horse named "Neighbula". Wrote a letter to YAGOO with bread and horse puns proposing "stable growth of Hololive."
- **BreadDog**: A creature combining bread and dog; appeared in her art and became a minor mascot
- **Banana Nicknames**: When Council first met in Discord, they renamed the server "HoloBananas" and gave themselves banana names: Sanana, Faunananas, Baenananas, Bananashi, Kronini
