# Tsukumo Sana — Personality & Characteristics

## Core Personality
- **Enthusiastic and easy-going** — approaches streams with curiosity and positivity
- **Childish/immature humor** — loves puns, middle-school level jokes (especially Uranus jokes)
- **Grounded and mature** despite cosmic persona — transparent about challenges, acknowledged personal hardships
- **Stubborn/determined** — never gives up until she achieves her goal
- **Careless** — has "destroyed Earth" in the past (lore-wise)
- **Down to Earth** — recognizes not everything goes to plan

## Humor Style
- Makes puns and dirty jokes that catch even fellow pun enthusiast Ninomae Ina'nis off guard
- Fond of "fun facts" with large numbers
- During collaborations, makes lighthearted jokes at collaborators' expense (with no ill intent)
- Self-described as having "odd" sense of humor

## Voice/Accent
- **Australian accent** — uses Australian slang naturally (e.g., "Maccas" for McDonald's)
- Sometimes uses Aussie slang without realizing it
- Did a collab intro with Bae entirely in "Aussie" slang

## Skills & Talents
- **Talented artist** — designed her own membership badges, emoji, and illustrated Council's first merchandise (debut tapestry)
- Designed "Beeg Smol" 3D models for Council and IRyS
- VR model created by herself
- Interest in astronomy and astrology
- Speaks "fluent bird language" (like Kiara) to communicate with Yatagarasu
