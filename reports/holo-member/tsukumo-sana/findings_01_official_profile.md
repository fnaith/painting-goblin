## Official Lore
A member of the Council and the Speaker of "Space," the very first concept created by the Gods. After being materialized in the mortal realm, she began researching astrology in earnest for one particular reason. However, she soon stumbled unknowingly upon the Forbidden Truth, and was turned into an apostle for the Ancient Ones.

"Space" refers not only to this universe we live in, but all dimensions and matter to exist. It is a concept unbridled by definition, and one that continues to grow in scope limitlessly. As it is yet to be fully explored by man, her seemingly strange behavior and speech can be explained away with a simple, "Humanity simply isn't yet ready to understand."

## Character Design
- Space/astronomy theme with cosmic motifs
- Hair resembles the Milky Way galaxy
- Hair ornaments resemble *sanshoku dango* (three-colored rice dumplings)
- Has a swirly antenna/limiter on her head — when removed, her gravitational field would fling Earth out of orbit
- Accompanied by Yatagarasu, an orange worm-like creature (a three-legged crow from Japanese folklore, whose full form would melt human brains)
- Outfits: Main outfit, New Year kimono, VR model, Halloween costume

## Name Trivia
- "Tsukumo" (九十九) means "ninety-nine" — appears in *tsukumogami* (99-year-old spirits)
- "Sana" is an anagram of NASA
- She reads her name in Western order (Sana Tsukumo), like Kronii, Bae, Calli, and Ame
