# Yukihana Lamy - Personality & Streaming Characteristics

## Personality Traits
- **Kind-hearted and soft-spoken** - Known for her gentle voice and calm demeanor on streams
- **Shy but bold** - While she seems timid at first, she is not afraid to assert herself when needed
- **Easily flustered** - Gets flustered when flirted with by fans or other members (e.g., Matsuri)
- **Motherly ("Mama")** - Often called "Mama" by viewers and other hololive members due to her caring nature
- **Clueless/empty-headed** - Official lore states her serious facade hides a more airheaded side
- **Heavy drinker** - Despite claiming she's not good at drinking, she is one of the heaviest drinkers in hololive
- **Surprisingly healthy** - Despite heavy drinking, she maintains good health
- **Drinks 2+ liters of Contrex water daily**

## Streaming Style
- **Voice**: Soft-spoken, gentle, calm
- **Morning streams**: 9 AM every weekday (chat or games)
- **Night streams**: 6 days/week, 10-11 PM start (drinking & chat, ASMR, games)
- **Long streams**: Often ~5 hours
- **Dislikes**: Horror games (gets scared easily - especially funny with Botan)
- **Likes**: Sandbox simulation games, MMOs, online multiplayer games, Minecraft, ARK

## Catchphrases & Mannerisms
- **Greeting (viewer → Lamy)**: "Konlamy" (こんらみ) - portmanteau of "konnichiwa" + "Lamy"
- **Farewell**: "Otsulamy" (おつラミィ/おつらみー) - portmanteau of "otsukare" + "Lamy"
- **Opening catch**: "Lamyoohoo!"
- **Channel intro**: "Konlamy desu"

## Streaming Rules
- Be nice to other viewers
- No spam or trolling
- Don't bring up other streamers unless she mentions them
- No clipping during streams (only after archive is up)
- All languages welcome if rules are followed

## Fan Culture
- Fans are called "Yukimin" (Snow People)
- Membership name: "Mochimochi Daifuku"
- Members-only content: Song practice, ASMR, watch parties, behind-the-scenes stories
- Known for morning streams as part of her routine
