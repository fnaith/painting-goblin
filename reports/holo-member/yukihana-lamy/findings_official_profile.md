## Official Lore / Backstory
The daughter of a noble snow family living in the vast, white, icy lands far from civilization. Struck by the smiles and vibrant live streams by the talents from hololive, she burst out of the comforts of her home with her companion, Daifuku. Her serious facade belies her clueless and empty-headed side.

- She is a **half-elf** (snow elf) born to a human mother and elf father
- Her companion **Daifuku** is a white creature/companion
- Despite being a snow elf, she **doesn't like cold places**
