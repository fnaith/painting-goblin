# Ouro Kronii — Lore & Backstory Research

## 1. "Warden of Time" Concept

Kronii's official title is **Warden of "Time"** — the overseer and embodiment of the concept of time itself.

**Key lore points:**
- Time is described as "an unwavering, precise entity" — its Warden is "equally cool and impeccable."
- She is the **third concept birthed by the Gods**, and the one "most intrinsically linked with mankind."
- Her personal emoji is the hourglass (⌛).
- "None may escape the sands of time and, indeed, most men do not even wish to be freed from her captivity."
- She picked up her twin blades "almost as a reflex action" and seems to enjoy wielding them.
- Her quiet, aloof personality has never changed over the ages, but she has developed "a little bit of haughtiness and sadistic tendencies along the way."
- She refers to herself as "perfection" and opens streams with **"Kroniichiwa!"**

**Origin of time in-universe:**
- Originally, the concept of time was "but a cog in the wheel, one which only functioned in tandem with others."
- Eventually, humans gave time individual meanings (dawn, dusk, etc.), making it universal.
- Humans became "enslaved to Time in return for empowering her."
- In her present form, she is a "sadistic, prideful warden."

## 2. Role in Hololive's Council / Promise Lore

### Hololive English -Council- (debut group)
Kronii debuted on **August 23, 2021** as part of **hololive English -Council-**, the second generation (or "wave") of Hololive English talents. Council consisted of five members, each embodying a fundamental concept:

| Member | Concept |
|---|---|
| Tsukumo Sana | Space |
| **Ouro Kronii** | **Time** |
| Ceres Fauna | Nature |
| Nanashi Mumei | Civilization |
| Hakos Baelz | Chaos (primordial force) |

**Teaser lore (from "Prelude" and "Omen" videos narrated by IRyS):**
- The gods created four concepts: Space, Nature, Time, and Civilization.
- Chaos (Bae) is a primordial force that predates or exists alongside these concepts.
- Avatars were created for each concept, giving rise to the Council members.

### Hololive English -Promise- (current unit)
- On **October 9, 2023**, after Tsukumo Sana's graduation (July 31, 2022) and the merger with Project: HOPE (IRyS), the remaining active Council members + IRyS formed **hololive English -Promise-**.
- Current Promise members: **IRyS, Ouro Kronii, Hakos Baelz** (Fauna graduated Jan 2025, Mumei graduated Apr 2025).

## 3. Character Origin Story (Debut Stream Summary)

**Debut stream:** August 23, 2021 (originally scheduled Aug 22, delayed 24 hours due to technical issues — jokingly blamed on the "EN curse").

**Debut highlights:**
- Stream title: "The Time Has Come"
- Peaked at **90,000+ concurrent viewers**.
- She had **115,000 subscribers** before the stream even started (though YouTube's anti-bot algorithm later removed many).
- She teased an original song during the stream.
- Closed the stream by singing **"Butterfly"** (Digimon Adventure Japanese OP).

**Official bio (from hololivepro.com):**
> "Kroniichiwa! It's me, perfection. Also synonymous for Ouro Kronii! Time is an unwavering, precise entity, and its Warden - its overseer - is equally cool and impeccable. Occasionally her exquisiteness leads her to decisions and bouts of luck that transcend the understanding of mere mortals. However, even utmost perfection finds no foothold in the face of her mortal enemy—disorder."

## 4. Lore-Related Events & Story Arcs

- **holoMinistry (Jan 25, 2022):** April Fools' genderbent alter-egos of Council. Kronii became **Ouro Krono**, a male version. All Council members swapped profiles/names to the male counterparts for the bit.
- **ENigmatic Recollection (2024):** A Hololive EN-wide story/RPG event where members had guild affiliations. Kronii's alternate persona was **Tam Gandr** (a callback to her rejected surname "Gandr").
- **"The Broken Promise" (Dec 14, 2024):** Promise's 3D musical, a 1st anniversary story event.
- **CouncilRyS RPG:** A fan-made game featuring the Council + IRyS as protagonists.
- **Chrono Gear: Warden of Time:** A fan game specifically about Kronii's lore.

## 6. The Concept of Space-Time and Kronii's Relationship to It

- **Space (Sana) and Time (Kronii) are explicitly paired** as complementary cosmic forces. They were the first two concepts born of the gods, alongside Nature and Civilization.
- Kronii's name itself: **"Ouro"** derives from *ouroboros* (the serpent eating its own tail, symbolizing cyclical time / infinity). **"Kronii"** derives from Greek *chronos* (time).
- Her rejected surname was **Gandr** (from Jörmungandr, the world-serpent that encircles Midgard — another tail-eating serpent symbol). She later used this as **Tam Gandr** in ENReco.
- Her age is listed as **∞ (infinite)** — the oldest member in all of Hololive Production, as she was born simultaneously with the concept of time itself.
- Her birthday is **March 14** (Pi Day — 3.14, a mathematical constant associated with cycles and circles, linking to the ouroboros symbolism).
