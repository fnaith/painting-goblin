# Research Plan: Ouro Kronii (Hololive)

## Main Research Question
Comprehensive research on Hololive member Ouro Kronii — covering official settings, personal characteristics, notable anecdotes, and fan culture — for use in creating a fan game.

## Subtopics

### 1. Official Profile & Design
- Character design details (artist, outfit themes, color scheme)
- Basic profile: height, birthday, debut date, unit/group affiliation
- VTuber model and streaming setup
- Official motto/taglines

### 2. Personality & Character Traits
- The "Time" theme and how it manifests in character
- Personality type (kuudere/perfectionist reputation)
- Catchphrases, verbal tics, speech patterns
- Interactions with fans and other Hololive members

### 3. Lore & Backstory
- In-universe lore: "Warden of Time" concept
- Connection to Hololive's broader mythos (Council / Promise)
- Character origin story as presented in debut

### 4. Notable Anecdotes, Memes & Relationships
- Famous moments / clips
- Relationships with other Hololive members (especially HoloCouncil/Promise)
- Fan culture and popular memes (e.g., "Kronii's feet" jokes, "Kronii weapon" memes)
- Achievements and milestones

## Synthesis Approach
Combine all findings into a comprehensive character reference document suitable for fan game development, organized by category (visual design, personality, lore, fan culture).
