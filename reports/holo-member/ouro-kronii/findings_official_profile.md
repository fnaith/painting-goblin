## 2. Character Design

### Illustrator
- **WADARCO** (twitter: @tsubuanfes) — designed all primary costumes (main, kimono, casual, military, formal).
- **Live2D Modeling** by けっふぃ (@keffiy).

### 3D Model
- Revealed May 25, 2023, during a chatting stream.
- Idol 3D costume debuted at hololive 4th fes. *Our Bright Parade* on March 19, 2023.

### Color Scheme
- **Primary: Blue, white, and aqua/cyan tones.**
- Hair is medium blue with lighter streaks, styled in a long flowing cut with side-swept bangs.
- Eyes are a striking blue.
- Outfit is predominantly white with blue accents, gold trim, and translucent/velvet fabric elements.

### Outfit Themes

1. **Main Outfit (Warden of Time)**
   - White and blue regal military-style coat with gold trim.
   - Hourglass motif accessories (earrings, belt buckle).
   - Thigh-high boots, short shorts.
   - Carries a sword (katana-like blade) as Warden of Time.
   - Sheer fabric panels, corset-like bodice.

2. **Kimono / New Year Outfit (January 15, 2022)**
   - Traditional Japanese kimono in blue and white with gold accents.
   - Floral and geometric patterns.
   - Hair styled up with ornamental hairpins.

3. **Casual Outfit (December 28, 2022)**
   - Relaxed streetwear: oversized blue hoodie, white skirt, sneakers.
   - Hair worn down casually.

4. **Military Outfit (May 12, 2024)**
   - Tactical military-style uniform in blue, white, and black.
   - Fitted jacket, combat boots, utility belt.

5. **Swimsuit (holonatsu Paradise)**
   - Designed by Goomrrat.
   - Blue/white bikini with sheer cover-up.

### VR Models
- "Beeg Smol" 3D VR models designed by Tsukumo Sana (July 20, 2022).
- Additional VR models designed by Amelia Watson and Zania.

## 3. Lore & Personality

### Official Role
> **Warden of "Time"** — The third concept birthed by the Gods and the one most intrinsically linked with mankind. Time is an unwavering, precise entity, and its Warden is equally cool and impeccable.

### Personality
- Confident, self-centered, unafraid to praise herself ("perfection").
- Describes herself as "not a happy person," listing *existential dread* as a hobby.
- Deadpan humor, dry delivery — "cool aunt" or painfully relatable comic relief.
- Haughty and slightly sadistic (per lore), but in reality accommodating and prone to "gap moe" when flustered.
- Hilariously bad luck; her projected confidence is often a coping mechanism.
- Known for explosive reactions when taking damage in games.

### Likes
- Sleeping/napping, walking at night, existential dread, swimming, anime, manga, webtoons.

### Dislikes
- Sleeping/napping, procrastinating, vegetables, working under pressure, rage-inducing games.
- (Sleeping and procrastinating appear in both lists — she's aware of her contradictions.)

### Hobbies
- Games, procrastinating, lying down, watching videos, singing.

## 7. Key Trivia

- Kronii is the **oldest member of Hololive Production** by lore (infinite age).
- Her birthday (March 14) is **Pi Day** (3/14).
- She is **Canadian**, grew up watching YTV, Treehouse, Teletoon, and *Just for Laughs Gags*.
- Earned a driver's license during her time in Hololive.
- Has a pet snake named **Boros** whom she found in the woods and adopted.
- Upon creating her Twitter account, she gained followers so fast that **Twitter temporarily restricted her account** for excessive activity.
- Her mismatched likes/dislikes (sleeping, napping, procrastinating appear on both lists) are a running joke.
- Her room is known as the **"Bunkeronii"** — a cluttered bunker-like space with monitors, snacks, and a general "chronically online" vibe.
- She has a tendency to avoid vegetables (especially green peppers and peanut butter).
- Kronii self-mixed her cover of *MAFIA* (wotaku), handling the audio engineering herself.
- She has mentioned interest in the "villainess" webtoon genre and actively keeps up with Korean webtoon series.
- Referred to her fans as **"second-graders"** (a playful jab).
