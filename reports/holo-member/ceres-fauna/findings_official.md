## Official Lore / Background

> "The Keeper of Nature, a druidic kirin who appeared on the internet to win over humans and convince them to return to nature.
>
> She'll attempt to charm you with her soft voice, comforting presence, and surprisingly deep knowledge of cursed memes, but be wary, because she has a reputation for turning people into trees. Once you enter her forest, you won't want to leave."

She is characterized as a **druidic kirin** — a mythical creature (Japanese qilin) associated with nature, forests, and guardianship.

## Role in Hololive EN Council / Promise

- **Council** was a generation of Hololive English debuted in 2021, themed around cosmic/conceptual forces. Each member represented a concept:
  - Nanashi Mumei (Civilization / Guardian of Civilization)
  - Ceres Fauna (Nature / Guardian of Nature / Keeper of Nature)
  - Ouro Kronii (Time / Warden of Time)
  - Hakos Baelz (Chaos / Warden of Chaos)
  - Tsukumo Sana (Space / Speaker of Space — graduated 2022)
- On **October 9, 2023**, Ceres Fauna along with the remaining Council members (Mumei, Kronii, Baelz) and IRyS were reorganized into the unit **hololive English -Promise-**.
- Fauna's specific role/title within Council mythology is **"Keeper of Nature"** (also referred to as "Guardian of Nature").
