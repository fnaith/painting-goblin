# Ceres Fauna — Memes, Highlights, Anecdotes & Community Culture

## Overview

Ceres Fauna (セレス・ファウナ) is a former Hololive English VTuber who debuted August 23, 2021 as part of **hololive English -Council-** (later **hololive English -Promise-**). She is the Keeper of "Nature" — a druidic kirin (qilin) born 4.54 billion years ago (same age as Earth). She graduated on January 4, 2025.

## 1. Popular Memes & Inside Jokes

### "Konfauna" / "Faunwell"
Her signature greeting is a mashup of "konnichiwa/konbanwa" + "Fauna." Her farewell is analogously "Faunwell." Chat echoes these back to her.

### "Uuuuuu~"
Fauna's iconic verbal tic when embarrassed, flustered, or being cute. It became one of her most recognizable trademarks and is frequently used in fan edits, memes, and emotes.

### "Return to Nature" / Turning People into Trees
A core character bit: Fauna threatens (playfully) to turn people into trees. The official bio says "she has a reputation for turning people into trees. Once you enter her forest, you won't want to leave." This is a constant source of fan art and joke templates.

### "Fauna Bait"
A community term used when Fauna does something deliberately cute, flirtatious, or attention-seeking to get reactions from chat or other talents. It's used both affectionately and humorously.

### "Green" / Nature Motif
Everything ties back to nature — plants, forests, the color green (🌿 is her oshi mark). Fans associate her with plant puns, photosynthesis jokes, and druid/ranger class memes.

### "Mama Fauna" / "Mother Nature"
Her soft, soothing voice and comforting demeanor earned her the "Mama" nickname early on (coined by Hakos Baelz at debut). Her ASMR streams are legendary for their calming effect. However, Fauna herself has said she prefers to be seen as an older sister, not a mother.

### "Not Deer Antlers"
Fauna explicitly clarified her horns are made of tree branches and are **NOT deer antlers**. This became a running correction gag.

### "Beeg Smol"
Her VR model, designed by Tsukumo Sana, came in two sizes — "Beeg" (big) and "Smol" (small). The contrast became a meme template.

### "Emo Phase"
Fauna has openly discussed her teenage emo phase, listing Pierce the Veil, Linkin Park, and My Chemical Romance as favorites. Her 2024 goth outfit reveal and her final original song "It's Not a Phase" (released December 22, 2024) are direct callbacks.

### Elevator Phobia
Fauna has a genuine, intense fear of elevators, which she has discussed on stream. Fans find this endearing and it's a frequent bit during collabs.

### "Vegan Kirin"
As a kirin, Fauna is canonically vegan. This ties into nature-themed jokes about herbivores and plant-based diets.

## 2. Famous Highlight / Clip Moments

### Debut Milestone (Aug 23, 2021)
Reached **200,000 subscribers** on debut day itself. Her debut stream crashed through expectations and established her soft-spoken ASMR-friendly persona immediately.

### HoloweEN Horror Manga Miniseries (Oct-Nov 2022)
Fauna **wrote and produced** a 3-episode animated horror manga miniseries called **"HoloweEN"** featuring voice acting by all Hololive English talents. This showcased her creative writing and project management skills.

### 3D Model Debut on HoloGra (June 4, 2023)
Her main 3D model debuted in episode 211 of the HoloGra anime series, later showcased in full at the hololive 4th fes. "Our Bright Parade."

### 1 Million Subscribers (Dec 28, 2024)
Reached the 1M milestone just **7 days before her graduation** — a bittersweet moment celebrated by the entire community.

### Graduation Stream (Jan 4, 2025)
Her final stream was an emotional farewell that drew massive viewership. The "World Tree" completion in Minecraft and numerous farewell collabs marked her final month.

### Original Songs
- **"Let Me Stay Here"** (May 15, 2022) — her first original solo
- **"Rise"** (Aug 23, 2022) — collab single
- **"Connect the World"** (June 17, 2023) — HoloEN 1st Concert theme
- **"Our Promise"** (June 18, 2024)
- **"It's Not a Phase"** (Dec 22, 2024) — her final original song, a full-circle emo callback

## 3. Memorable Interactions with Other Hololive Members

### Nanashi Mumei (Extreme Protectiveness / Yandere Bit)
Fauna formed an especially close bond with Nanashi Mumei. A running character bit is that Fauna is **extremely protective and possessive** of Mumei, threatening to "return [Mumei] to nature" whenever she gets upset. Fans frequently ship them as "Faumei."

### Ouro Kronii
As fellow Council members, Fauna and Kronii had a strong "bickering friends" dynamic. Their contrasting personalities (calm nature vs. impatient time) generated many funny exchanges.

### Hakos Baelz
Baelz described Fauna at debut as "a natural mama, a soothing beauty, someone who gives the best headpats." Their interactions often leaned into the "chaos rat meets calm nature" contrast.

### Tsukumo Sana
Though Sana graduated before Fauna, Sana designed Fauna's VR model ("Beeg Smol"). This connection is fondly remembered by the community.

### Promise Unit (IRyS, Kronii, Mumei, Baelz + Fauna)
From October 9, 2023, Fauna was grouped into **hololive English -Promise-** alongside IRyS, Ouro Kronii, Nanashi Mumei, and Hakos Baelz. This unit performed together at concerts and in various collabs.

### Council Collabs
As part of the original Council (with Sana, Kronii, Mumei, Baelz), Fauna participated in panels at Anime NYC 2021 and numerous group streams. Their cover of "Hikaru Nara" (Goose house) is a fan favorite.

## 4. Interesting Anecdotes & Stream Stories

- **Age 4.54 billion:** Her canonical age matches the estimated age of Earth. This makes her the third-oldest member in all of Hololive Production lore.
- **Vegan IRL:** Fauna is actually vegan, consistent with her kirin character.
- **Phobias:** Intense fear of **cockroaches** and **elevators**. These have come up repeatedly on stream.
- **Food Preferences:** Loves peaches and golden apples; dislikes mushrooms (as food) and grasshoppers. Drinks tea hot, no milk or sugar — prefers peach tea.
- **Horror Game Paradox:** Enjoys horror games but gets scared very easily, leading to endearing reactions.
- **Kalimba Player:** She plays the kalimba (thumb piano), often incorporating it into streams.
- **Mascot:** Her mascot is named **Nemu**.
- **ASMR Specialty:** Her ASMR streams are considered some of the best in Hololive EN, combining ear cleaning with long-form conversational segments.
- **"I prefer animals to plants":** Fauna has noted that her name is Fauna, not Flora — she represents animal life specifically.

## 5. Fan Community — "Saplings" Culture

### Fan Name
**Saplings** — fans are "Saplings" growing under the care of Mother Nature.

### Membership Tiers ("Faunatics")
1. **Faunatic** — base tier
2. **Sprouted Faunatic** — "Look at you go! You're already a little sprout!"
3. **Blossomed Faunatic** — "Wow! Look at your beautiful flowers!"

### Key Hashtags
| Hashtag | Purpose |
|---|---|
| #Faunline | Stream discussion |
| #FineFaunart | Fan art |
| #Saplings | General fan tag |
| #FirstFaun | Debut tag |

### Oshi Mark
🌿 (herb/leaf emoji)

### Community Personality
Saplings are known for being a relatively chill, wholesome community, reflecting Fauna's own calming presence. They leaned heavily into nature/growth metaphors. During her graduation period, the community organized appreciation projects and celebrated her legacy.
