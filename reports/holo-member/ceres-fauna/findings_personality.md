# Ceres Fauna — Personality & Streaming Characteristics

**VTuber:** Ceres Fauna (セレス・ファウナ)
**Debut:** August 23, 2021
**Graduation:** January 4, 2025
**Affiliation:** Hololive English -Council- (formerly), Hololive English -Promise-
**Fan Name:** Saplings
**Membership Name:** Faunatics
**Oshi Mark:** 🌿
**Illustrator:** Asagi Tōsaka
**Age (lore):** 4.54 billion years (age of Earth)

---

## 1. Personality Traits

### Core Personality
- **Soft-spoken and gentle** — Fauna has a naturally soft, comforting voice that draws people in despite being quiet. Described by fans and peers alike as having a "motherly" or "older sister" aura.
- **Warm and affable** — Generally cheerful, kind, and welcoming to her audience ("Saplings").
- **Slightly mischievous** — Has a playful side; enjoys memes and "cursed" internet humor despite her gentle appearance.
- **Easily embarrassed** — Gets flustered and blushes easily, often using her verbal tic "Uuuuuu~" when embarrassed or shy.
- **Possessive/protective** — Extremely protective of her fans and especially Nanashi Mumei, to the point of playful possessiveness and jealousy. Has a habit of trying to convince people to "return to nature."
- **Can be condescending/assertive** — When angered or faced with something she disapproves of, she can shift to a sharp, condescending tone ("the full brunt of Nature's fury").

### Comparison to Other Members
- Hakos Baelz described Fauna at debut as a "natural mama," a "soothing beauty," and someone who "gives the best headpats."
- Fans frequently compare her to Yukihana Lamy (from Hololive JP) — both share a soft voice, motherly personality, and nature motif.

---

## 2. Voice Characteristics

- **Soft, whispery, ASMR-quality voice** — Her voice is notably quiet, gentle, and calming, making her ASMR streams highly popular.
- **Lore description:** "Her whispers, as an avatar of Mother Nature, have healing properties."
- Can project a more authoritative/condescending tone when needed (e.g., scolding chat or roleplaying as a strict "mother nature").
- Her singing voice is light and sweet; she has released original songs (*Let Me Stay Here*, *It's Not a Phase*, *Our Promise*) and participated in group singles.

## 5. Relationships with Other Hololive Members

### Hololive English -Council- / -Promise-
Fauna was originally part of **Council** (alongside Nanashi Mumei, Ouro Kronii, Hakos Baelz, and originally Tsukumo Sana). After Sana's graduation, the remaining four plus IRyS formed **Promise**.

| Member | Relationship |
|---|---|
| **Nanashi Mumei** | Extremely close. Fauna is famously possessive/protective of Mumei. Their dynamic is often described as "mother/older sister" watching over a chaotic friend. Fauna dotes on Mumei and frequently collabs with her. |
| **Ouro Kronii** | Good friends with teasing chemistry. Their dynamic is playful and often flirty. |
| **Hakos Baelz** | Close friend; Bae has publicly praised Fauna's warmth and "mama" energy. They have a sisterly rapport. |
| **IRyS** | Friendly gen-mate under Promise. Regular collabs and group activities. |
| **Tsukumo Sana** | Close friend during Sana's time in Council. Sana designed a VR model for Fauna. Their dynamic was warm and collaborative. |

### Other Hololive Members
- Has a warm relationship with Mori Calliope, Takanashi Kiara, Ninomae Ina'nis, and Gawr Gura (Myth generation).
- A close friendship with FUWAMOCO (Advent generation), who also drew graduation art for her.
- Maintains friendly relationships with Hololive JP members through collaborative events and concerts.

## 6. Unique Quirks & Habits

- **Vegan** — As a kirin, she is vegetarian/vegan, befitting the nature guardian lore.
- **Phobia of cockroaches** — Intensely afraid of them.
- **Phobia of elevators** — Genuinely scared of using elevators.
- **Plays kalimba** — Enjoys playing the kalimba (thumb piano) as a hobby.
- **Tea enthusiast** — Drinks hot tea (peach tea favorite) without milk or sugar.
- **Horror game fan who hates being scared** — Loves horror games but is easily frightened, leading to amusing reactions on stream.
- **Former emo phase** — Has openly discussed her middle school emo phase, listening to post-hardcore and pop-punk bands.
- **Anime taste** — Favorites include *Neon Genesis Evangelion*, *Madoka Magica*, *Mob Psycho 100*, *Made in Abyss*, *Hunter x Hunter*, *Death Note*.
- **Creator of HoloweEN** — Wrote and produced the HoloweEN horror manga miniseries for Halloween 2022, featuring all Hololive EN members.
- **Nemu mascot** — Her personal mascot character is named Nemu.

## 7. Key Facts

- **1 Million YouTube Subscribers** — Reached on December 28, 2024, just 7 days before her graduation.
- **Original Songs:** *Let Me Stay Here* (2022), *Our Promise* (2024), *It's Not a Phase* (2024), and multiple group songs (*Rise*, *Connect the World*, *Aventure Holic*).
- **3D Model Debut:** June 4, 2023 (on HoloGra episode 211).
- **Fanbase:** Saplings are known for being a cozy, supportive community that mirrors Fauna's own gentle demeanor.
- **Goals stated at debut:** Learn Japanese, learn to sing and create original songs, offline collabs with genmates, learn speedruns, voice act in a video game.
