## 2. Speaking Style & Mannerisms

- **Voice:** High-energy, expressive, often shifts pitch dramatically when excited or frustrated.
- **Gremlin Mode voice:** Described as "a cross between a high-pitched wheeze, reptilian screech, and the final breath of a dying squeaky toy."
- **Laugh:** Loud, wheezy, infectious — frequently devolves into coughing or screeching.
- **Hiccups:** Known for frequent hiccups during streams; viewers spam "HIC" in chat.
- **No filter:** Frequently makes lewd or outrageous remarks. As noted on the wiki: "Amelia has a filter, but most of the outrageous things that are caught in her filter end up being said out loud regardless."
- **Trolling:** Enjoys messing with chat (e.g., darkening Minecraft shaders and claiming she "fixed" them).
- **British accent gimmick:** Started her debut stream with a fake British accent (to satisfy expectations of a Sherlock-like character) before dropping it as a ruse.

## 3. Interaction Style with Fans (Teamates)

- **Highly engaged:** Frequently reads superchats, comments in her own and others' streams.
- **Encouraging chat tags:**
  - **#Amebeliever** — used by chat to encourage her when she's stuck on a challenge.
- **Super Sunday series:** Longest-running series where she dedicates entire streams to reading superchats.
- **Bubb4bot:** Created a separate Twitch channel where fans can submit music requests during her streams.
- **Charity drives:** Hosted hololive's first-ever charity stream (June 2021), raising $235,972.69 for Best Friends Animal Society. Her final week of streaming included a second charity stream raising $214,915.19 for ocean conservation.
- **Fanbase name origin:** "Teamates" — pun on "tea" (British drink she listed as a favorite), but she actually prefers iced tea.
- **Membership tiers:** Investigators → Investamigators → Investamigatorators (alligator pun).

## 5. Known Quirks, Habits & Personality Traits

### "Gremlin Mode"

- Emerges during competitive PvP games (Apex Legends, Valorant).
- Traits: greedy, prideful, unsportsmanlike, highly defensive of own mistakes, hypercritical of teammates.
- Makes streams "unpredictable and very interesting to watch."

### "Ameway" (Stubborn Determination)

- Refuses to quit challenges until completed on her own terms.
- Iconic example: Sun station landing in Outer Wilds — took nearly 3 hours.
- Other examples: Antichamber playthrough, Mario streams.
- Even her retirement from streaming was described by fans as "the Ameway" — she didn't want a full graduation, so she negotiated an "Affiliate" status unique to her.

### Kind & Supportive Side

- Actively watches and supports fellow members' streams.
- Generous with time and resources (tech help, VR model creation for others, hosting events).
- Known for being "sweet, kind, and supportive" as a baseline personality.

### Playfully Lewd / Mischievous

- Named a Minecraft door "Gura's backdoor" with a lenny face `( ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°)`.
- Ground pound joke became iconic.
- Often makes jokes with "inconspicuously lewd undertones."

### Technical & Creative Pioneer

- Drove VR initiatives for Hololive EN — created VR models, the "HoloEN VR Studios" / "Ametaverse."
- Organized the "HoloWorm" tournament (Worms W.M.D.) with 11+ talents.
- Created and directed "Smol Adventures" animated series.
- Developed and released "ChikuTaku" original song + rhythm game on itch.io.
- Hosted "Ame in the A.M." / "Watson Weekly" news show (successor to Kiryu Coco's AsaCoco).

### Other Quirks

- **Likes:** Iced tea, roller coasters, sleeping, FPS games (Apex, Valorant), open-world games (Fallout, Zelda: BotW), animals/marine biology (Animal Review series).
- **Dislikes:** Onions, going outside (despite liking roller coasters).
- **Real-life pet dog Bubba** is her mascot, occasionally heard barking on stream.
- **Height:** 150 cm (4'11") — frequently the subject of "smol" jokes.
- **Hobbies outside streaming:** Emergency medicine studies (backup career before hololive).

## 6. Persona Analysis

### "Chaotic Gremlin" — Accurate Assessment

Amelia's persona is best described as a **balance of sweetness and saltiness**. She oscillates between:
1. **Supportive, kind friend** who helps genmates, hosts charity events, and encourages chat.
2. **Gremlin gremlin** who trash-talks in PvP, makes lewd jokes, trolls chat, and revels in chaos.

### "Detective" Persona

- Official lore: A time-traveling detective from the 1920s investigating rumors of talking animals (Hololive members).
- Pocket watch allows time travel; syringes of "Watson's Concoction" on her leg.
- The detective motif is more of a framing device than a strict roleplay — she drops it frequently for genuine interactions.

### "True Gamer" Persona

- Competitive, salty, toxic-in-a-funny-way during FPS games.
- Fans love the contrast between her normally sweet demeanor and competitive rage.

### "Innovator" Persona

- Pushed boundaries of what a Hololive vtuber could do with VR, animation, game development, and original programming.
- Unofficial tech lead / leader of Myth.

## 7. Retirement (Affiliate Status)

On September 20, 2024, Amelia announced she would end general streaming activities but remain a **hololive Affiliate** — a first-of-its-kind arrangement. She specifically did not call it a "graduation," leaving the door open for future project participation.

**Final stream** (September 30, 2024):
- 136,000+ concurrent viewers at peak
- $96,000+ in superchats
- 1.8 million subscribers at end of stream
