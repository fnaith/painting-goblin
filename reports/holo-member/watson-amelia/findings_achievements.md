# Watson Amelia — Research Findings & Achievements

## Overview

**Watson Amelia** (ワトソン・アメリア) is a VTuber associated with **hololive English**, debuting on **September 13, 2020** as the fifth and final member of hololive English's first generation, **"-Myth-"**, alongside Mori Calliope, Takanashi Kiara, Ninomae Ina'nis, and Gawr Gura.

- **Character:** A self-proclaimed "hololive's #1 detective" who investigates the strange rumors surrounding hololive
- **Designer:** Aoi Nabi (illustration), Naname (Live2D)
- **Height:** 150 cm (4'11")
- **Birthday:** January 6
- **Fan name:** Teamates (a pun on "tea")
- **Emoji:** 🔎
- **Mascot:** Bubba (a dog, based on her real pet)
- **Streaming End:** September 30, 2024 (transitioned to "hololive Affiliate" status, the first -Myth- member to do so)

## 2. Notable Stream Events and Achievements

### First-Ever Hololive Charity Stream (June 5, 2021)
- Organized a **9-hour charity stream** for Best Friends Animal Society (animal charity)
- **First charity stream ever within Hololive/Cover Corp.**
- Initial goal: $15,000 — reached before stream began
- **Final total: $235,972.69 USD**

### Final Week Charity Stream (September 28, 2024)
- Raised **$214,915.19** for ocean conservation
- Featured collaborations with every current hololive English member

### Final Stream (September 30, 2024)
- Peaked at **over 136,000 concurrent viewers**
- Received **over $96,000 in Superchats**
- Announced "Immortalized as Marketable Plushies 4EVER" merch set
- Channel reached **1.8 million subscribers** by stream end

### "Ame in the A.M." / "Watson Weekly"
- Inspired by Kiryu Coco's "AsaCoco" program
- Morning news digest show covering hololive news and stream highlights
- Rebooted as **"Watson Weekly"** in June 2023

### "ChikuTaku" — Original Song & Game (January 2023)
- Released first original song "ChikuTaku" on January 17, 2023
- Also released a rhythm game of the same name on itch.io

### Paladins Announcer Pack (October 2021)
- Hi-Rez Studios released an official **Watson Amelia announcer pack** for Paladins' Absolution update
- Unlockable by playing 5 matches

### HoloWorm Tournament (September-October 2022)
- Organized a Worms W.M.D tournament featuring talents from Hololive EN and Hololive Indonesia
- 11 talents participated over 3 weeks

## 4. Popular Memes

### "Ground Pound" (or "Ground Pounding Your Mom")
- Originated during a **Super Mario Odyssey** playthrough
- Said in a slightly lower-pitched, gremlin-like voice
- Became **almost synonymous with her character**
- Frequently referenced in fan content and by other hololive members

### "Amelia Tech Tips"
- Born from her helping genmates troubleshoot technical issues pre-debut
- Parody of Linus Tech Tips
- Frequently referenced by the community

### "Gremlin Mode" / "True Gamer"
- Used to describe her personality shift during competitive PvP games (Apex Legends, Valorant)
- Characterized by being salty, defensive of mistakes, and highly critical
- Distinctive high-pitched wheeze/reptilian screech voice when in this mode

### "Ame Same" / "AmeSame Fusion Dance"
- The friendly rivalry/ship between Ame and Gura
- "AmeSame" is one of the most recognized duo names in hololive EN

### "HIC"
- Amelia hiccups frequently during streams
- Viewers spam "HIC" in chat
- She jokingly theorized they're "upside-down burps"

### "Smol Ame"
- Chibi design by artist Walfie
- Became massively popular, leading to a series of VR models and animated content
- "Smol Adventures" animated series written and directed by Amelia

### "Ameway"
- Signature stubbornness — doing things *her way*, ignoring chat/game feedback
- Refuses to quit a challenge until completed
- Examples: Outer Wilds Sun station landing (~3 hours), Antichamber playthrough
- Fans use **"#Amebeliever"** in chat to encourage her
- Even her retirement was jokingly called "the Ameway" by fans

### "Bubba" the Dog
- Her real-life dog that can occasionally be heard during streams
- Mascot version designed by Ninomae Ina'nis
- Multiple VR variations created (Dancing Bubba, Bee Bubba, etc.)

## 6. Controversies and Notable Community Interactions

- **No major controversies** are documented in public wiki sources
- Notable for handling sensitive topics with care:
  - During one stream, she discussed a past suicide attempt and urged viewers to seek help, demonstrating her supportive side
- Her **"retirement" (September 2024)** was handled uniquely:
  - Did NOT use the term "graduation" — became an **"affiliate"** instead
  - First -Myth- member to step back from streaming
  - Continued affiliation allows participation in future hololive projects
  - Dismissed rumors of joining hololive staff, but noted she would continue technical support for members
- Known for being kind and supportive off-stream — frequently watches and comments on fellow members' streams
- Praised for her technical expertise in streaming equipment, VR, and creative project management

## 7. Role in Hololive English -Myth-

- **Fifth and final member to debut** on September 13, 2020
- **Unofficial leader** of -Myth- — recognized by genmates for technical knowledge and organizational skills
- **Technical backbone** — helped troubleshoot pre-debut issues, earned "Amelia Tech Tips" meme
- **VR/3D pioneer** — led the charge for VR content in hololive EN, coordinating with fan creators and VRChat
- **Creative director** — wrote/directed "Smol Adventures" animated series, organized charity events, created game (ChikuTaku)
- **Bridge to JP members** — collaborated with Roboco in VR, participated in Holo New Year Cup, co-organized events crossing JP/EN/ID branches
- **First to leave general streaming** — set the precedent of "Affiliate" status rather than full graduation
- Contributed to -Myth- original songs: "Myth or Treat" (2021), "Journey Like a Thousand Years" (2022), "Non-Fiction" (2022)
