## Official Lore / Backstory

### The Time Traveler Detective Concept

From the official hololive website:

> *"Test test, Hello~ Amelia Watson! #1 Detective at your service!"*

> *"Amelia heard strange rumors online surrounding hololive: talking foxes, magical squirrels, superhuman dogs, and more. Soon after beginning her investigation on hololive, and just out of interest, she decided to become an idol herself!"*

> *"She loves to pass her time training her reflexes with FPS games, and challenging herself with puzzle games. 'It's elementary, right?'"*

### Lore Narrative
- Amelia is a **time-traveling detective** from the **1920s**
- She uses a **golden pocket watch** as her time travel device
- She traveled to the present to investigate hololive after hearing strange rumors
- Her catchphrase, "It's elementary," is a nod to Sherlock Holmes ("Elementary, my dear Watson")
- The stethoscope and syringes reinforce the "detective + scientist" hybrid theme
- She often refers to her investigative craft and brews "Watson Concoction" — a mysterious substance

## Debut Stream

### Date & Details
- **Date**: September 13, 2020 (00:04 JST)
- **Title**: "【Debut Stream】The Investigation Begins"
- **Order**: 5th and final member of hololive English -Myth- to debut

### Key Moments
1. **Sleep deprivation**: Had only slept a few hours in the 36 hours prior, having stayed up to watch genmates' debuts and prepare content
2. **British accent ruse**: Began with a fake British accent (suggested by manager Enma to appease viewers expecting a Sherlock-like British character), then revealed it was fake
3. **Puru Puru Pururin**: Sang the *Welcome to the NHK* insert song
4. **Watson Concoction sequence**: Ended with a surreal segment where she "injected" viewers with her concoction, causing bizarre hallucinations
5. **Myth debut collab**: Followed immediately by the first -Myth- collaboration stream where she performed her detective role by investigating the other members

## Key Facts

- **Full name "Amelia H. Watson"** — the middle initial "H" was kept secret until revealed in a membership video after her Affiliate transition
- **January 6 birthday** coincides with Sherlock Holmes' birthday (in some canons), adding to her detective persona
- **First hololive EN member to hold a charity stream** (June 5, 2021, raised ~$236K for Best Friends Animal Society)
- **First hololive EN member to retire from streaming** (September 30, 2024) — introduced the "Affiliate" status as an alternative to full graduation
- **Hosted "Ame in The A.M."** — a morning news show inspired by Kiryu Coco's "AsaCoco"
- **Created "ChikuTaku"** — her own original song and rhythm game (released January 2023 on itch.io)
- **Mascot**: Bubba (based on her real hunting terrier); also has a second dog Mikki/Nikki and a cat named Wellington
- **Known for**: "Gremlin mode" (competitive gaming personality), "Amelia Tech Tips" (helping genmates with tech), FPS games (Apex Legends, Valorant), puzzle games
