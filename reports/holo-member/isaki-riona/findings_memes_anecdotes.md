## Major Memes & Famous Clips

### 1. "變態紳士2號" (Pervert Gentleman No. 2)
- Alongside Koganei Niko and Rindo Chihaya, Riona is part of a trio known for perverted/unhinged behavior
- The "變態紳士" (pervert gentleman) lineage started with Koganei Niko
- Riona earned the title "No.2" for her own contributions to this reputation

### 2. Gomoku (五目並べ) Loss RTA
- Challenged Sakura Miko (known gomoku master) to a game of gomoku
- Lost catastrophically in record time
- Resulted in emergency training streams where she studied gomoku strategies
- Became a running gag about her competitive nature

### 3. 42-Hour Jump King Completion
- Jump King: notoriously difficult rage-game
- Riona streamed it for approximately 41-42 hours total (over multiple sessions)
- Completed it live and burst into tears
- Upon beating the final stage, shouted: "おっぱいがでけぇ！！" (The boobs are huge!!)
- This moment became legendary in the community

### 4. Explosive Sneezes / "ヘキシブオブザイヤー2025"
- Riona has extremely loud, explosive sneezes that startle everyone
- Won "ヘキシブオブザイヤー2025" (Sneeze of the Year 2025) at Ookami Mio's "ヘキシブアワード" (Sneeze Awards)
- The sneezes are so powerful they've become a staple clip

### 5. Chronic Back Pain — "ぎっくり姉妹" (Back Pain Sisters)
- Suffers from chronic腰痛 (lower back pain) from a past injury
- Formed "ぎっくり姉妹" (Back Spasm Sisters) with Takane Lui who also has back problems
- Often streams while lying down or using back support

### 6. "パチンカス" (Pachinko Trash)
- Openly admits to being addicted to pachinko and slot machines
- Frequently discusses her wins and losses at pachinko parlors
- Community finds the contrast between her idol persona and pachinko hobby hilarious

### 7. Luna Knight (ルーナイト)
- Massive fan of Himemori Luna
- Calls herself a "ルーナイト" (Luna Knight)
- Entire family (parents, siblings) are also Luna Knights
- Has done Luna-themed streams and karaoke

### 8. Initial Debut Controversy
- During an early Liar's Bar stream with ReGLOSS members, made insensitive remarks
- Apologized formally afterward
- Community largely forgave her; became a learning moment

### 9. 3D Debut Triumph — "Make it or Break it"
- November 23, 2025 3D debut received massive praise
- La+ (Laplus Darknesss) famously said "ママァ" (mom / mama~) in response
- Dancing skills (jazz, ballet, tap) on full display
- Regarded as one of the most impressive 3D debuts
- Title theme: "Make it or Break it"

### 10a. HoloGra Debut
- Debuted on HoloGra (Hololive's official skit series) on November 30, 2025
- Marked her entry into the broader Hololive variety content scene

### 10b. 500,000 Subscriber Milestone
- Reached 500,000 YouTube subscribers on July 13, 2026
- Celebrated with a special milestone stream
- Half-million milestone within ~1.5 years of debut

### 11. Past Life Speculation (前世)
- Widely speculated that she is the former 立仙愛理 (Ririsu Tessen/Airi Rissen)
- Previously was an AKB48 member
- This explains her excellent dancing and singing ability
