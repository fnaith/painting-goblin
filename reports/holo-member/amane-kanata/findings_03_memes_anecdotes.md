# Findings: Memes, Clips & Anecdotes - Amane Kanata

## 1. PP Tenshi (PowerPoint Angel)
- Used Microsoft PowerPoint during her debut stream, earning the nickname "PPT" (PowerPoint Tenshi)
- Fans joke that PPT can stand for many things:
  - **Perfect Pitch Tenshi** (when singing)
  - **Perpetually Panicked Tenshi** (when playing horror games)
  - **PP Kabaya** (combined with "Kabaya" nickname)

## 2. Sōran Bushi
- Sang the traditional Japanese sea shanty **Sōran Bushi** a cappella during her debut stream
- Effectively became her theme song
- A remixed version serves as standby music on her streams
- Fans perform calls for the song during streams (Dokkoisho! Sooran! Hai! Hai! Ha! Ha!)

## 3. Gorilla / Kana-Gorilla
- Has a grip strength of **50 kg** - far above the average Japanese female (19-30 kg) and even exceeds male average (40-45 kg)
- Kiryu Coco gave her the variation "Kana-Gorilla" after asking Kanata to open tough glass jars
- Has shattered jars she attempted to open
- Broke her controller and keyboard due to her grip strength
- Doctors are unable to explain her strength despite her below-average size
- Almost crushed Houshou Marine's hand

## 4. Kabaya
- Akai Haato accidentally misspelled Kanata's name as "Kabaya" and only noticed after chat started calling her that
- Combined nickname: "PP Kabaya"

## 5. Pop Team Epic Fan
- Big fan of Pop Team Epic
- Invited the series' illustrator Bkub to create stamps for her membership
- Frequently depicted in fanart as Popuko from Pop Team Epic

## 6. Voice Acting / Prank Calls
- Has a notably wide vocal range and can impersonate others convincingly
- Running joke among English fans: Kanata is secretly all Hololive members and voice acts for everyone

## 7. Near Deafness
- Almost deaf in one ear - fans admire her ability to keep spirits high despite this

## 8. Innocent / "Corruption" Worries
- Viewers jokingly worry about Kanata getting "corrupted" by other members
- Especially since she lived with Kiryu Coco
- Known for not understanding adult jokes and avoiding bad words

## 9. Baseball Fan
- Avid baseball fan along with Inugami Korone
- Have attended games together

## 10. Fashion Sense
- Famously lacks fashion sense - Coco claimed to only ever see her wear two outfits
- Her uniform's ribbon is consistently uneven/lopsided

## 11. Membership Name
- Her YouTube membership was named "THE Kanata" after a Twitter poll
- The options were: "Kanatas", "THE Kanata", and "Kanata Fan Club"

## 12. Halo Emoji
- Her representative emote is "dizzy" (💫), because it resembles an angel halo
- Her halo is four-pointed star-shaped, metallic

## 13. Graduation
- Announced graduation on December 2, 2025
- Final stream and graduation on December 27, 2025 (exactly 6 years after debut)

## 14. ORIO Unit
- Formed unit **ORIO** with Tokoyami Towa on February 27, 2022
