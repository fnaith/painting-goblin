## Official Character Setting & Backstory

From the official hololive profile (hololivepro.com):

> *"An angel from the Celestial Academy. She is cheerful in front of close friends but becomes quiet and introverted when in crowds. She stays at home on her days off. She works hard with the goal of holding a concert at Saitama Super Arena someday."*

The fan wiki adds the alternate bio:

> *"An angel from the Celestial Academy. She is now studying how to heal people. To hide her shyness, she pretends to be cool."*

Key character traits:
- Self-described as a **Student Council Secretary**
- Described as a **slightly dorky yet pure-hearted girl**
- Known for her **extraordinary vocal range** — often nicknamed "Perfect Pitch Tenshi" (PP Tenshi)
- Friendly and kind, but shy in crowds; maintains a cool facade to conceal shyness
- Has a notable **lack of fashion sense**
- Likes Japanese idols
- Scored 20 points in a general knowledge test (high score)
- Known for **avoiding adult jokes and bad words**; considered one of the most "pure" members of hololive
- Has near-deafness in one ear but keeps spirits high
- Her catchphrase/greeting at the start of streams: "Hei!" (へい!), leading fans to be called Heimin
- Her tagline closing: "Otsukanata!" (おつかなたー！)
- Famous for singing **"Sōran Bushi"** (a traditional Japanese folk song) during her debut stream, which became her theme

## Notable Trivia
- Has **50kg grip strength**, far above average for Japanese women her age, earning her the nickname "Gorilla" (specifically "Kana-Gorilla" from Kiryu Coco)
- Used Microsoft PowerPoint in her debut stream, earning the nickname "PPTenshi" (PowerPoint Tenshi)
- Shares the same illustrator (Oshioshio) as Nijisanji's Sorahoshi Kirame
- Formed the musical unit **ORIO** with Tokoyami Towa (announced February 27, 2022)
- Has two pet rabbits
- Is a big fan of *Pop Team Epic* and got its illustrator Bkub to create membership stamps
- One of the moderators on the official hololive subreddit
- Her YouTube channel is verified with a customized URL
- Graduated from hololive on December 27, 2025 (6th anniversary of her debut)
