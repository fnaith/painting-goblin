# Amane Kanata (天音かなた) — Research Findings

> Retired Japanese singer, songwriter and virtual YouTuber affiliated with Hololive Production. Debuted on December 27, 2019 as part of Hololive Japan's 4th generation "holoForce" alongside Tokoyami Towa, Tsunomaki Watame, Himemori Luna, and Kiryu Coco. Graduated December 27, 2025. At peak: 1.72M YouTube subscribers, 576M views.

---

## 1. Memorable Livestream Anecdotes

### "PP Tenshi" Origin
- During her debut stream, Kanata prominently used **Microsoft PowerPoint** for presentations, leading fans to coin the nickname "PP Tenshi" (PP天使). This has also been reinterpreted as "Perfect Pitch Tenshi" due to her vocal abilities.
- **Source:** https://realsound.jp/2022/01/post-942262.html

### Superhuman Grip Strength ("Kana-Gorilla")
- Kanata has a measured grip strength of over **50 kg**, exceeding the average adult male. She inadvertently destroyed multiple objects on stream.
- Her genmate Kiryu Coco (who lived with her) gave her the nickname "Kana-Gorilla" after frequently asking Kanata to open tough glass jars.
- **Source:** https://kai-you.net/word/%E5%A4%A9%E9%9F%B3%E3%81%8B%E3%81%AA%E3%81%9F
- **Source (fan wiki):** https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Amane_Kanata

### Sōran Bushi Theme Song
- Kanata sang the traditional Japanese song **Sōran Bushi** during her debut stream, and it effectively became her signature/acapella theme song, with fans performing call-and-response during streams.
- **Source:** https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Amane_Kanata

### Holding Notes for 20+ Seconds
- Kanata was well known for holding notes in online concerts for more than 20 seconds on multiple occasions, showcasing her extraordinary breath control and vocal stamina.
- **Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ubeDFv_61Y0

### Kidney Stone Emergency
- On June 29, 2025, Kanata ended a gaming stream early due to urgent hospitalization. She later held a press conference-style stream revealing she had kidney stones, referring to them as a "bomb" she was still carrying.
- **Source:** https://news.ameba.jp/entry/20250702-87554327
- **Source:** https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/295/4295784/

### Kabaya Nickname Incident
- Akai Haato once misspelled Kanata's name, and viewers in chat began calling her "Kabaya," which stuck as a recurring nickname.
- **Source:** https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Amane_Kanata

### COVID-19 Recovery
- On February 14, 2022, Kanata tested positive for COVID-19, taking a nine-day break from streaming before returning.
- **Source:** https://www.excite.co.jp/news/article/Inside_136810/

## 2. Notable Interactions with Other VTubers

### ORIO Unit with Tokoyami Towa
- On May 2, 2022, Kanata and Tokoyami Towa formed the musical unit **ORIO**, releasing debut single "Over Time" and later an EP of the same name on July 30, 2022. The duo — an angel and a devil — was praised for their perfectly complementary voices.
- **Source:** https://kai-you.net/article/83545
- **Source:** https://realsound.jp/2022/05/post-1022934.html

### Living with Kiryu Coco
- Kanata shared a house with genmate Kiryu Coco (graduated 2021). Their close friendship was widely known, with Coco frequently teasing Kanata about her fashion sense and strength. Kanata wrote and composed "Kiseki Knot" as the holoForce farewell song for Coco's graduation.
- **Source:** https://ototoy.jp/news/102204
- **Source:** https://hololive.wiki/wiki/Amane_Kanata

### Kanaken Unit with AZKi and Sakamata Chloe
- On December 23, 2024, Kanata, AZKi, and Sakamata Chloe (as "Kanaken") released the collaborative song "Tenkai Dokyū Oshigoto Road" and performed a 3D concert together ahead of Chloe's graduation.
- **Source:** https://dengekionline.com/article/202501/29888

### Collaboration with Toby Fox
- Kanata's song **"Fallen Wing"** was co-written by Toby Fox (creator of Undertale/Deltarune) and Camellia. This came about as a counter-offer from Toby Fox after he reached out.
- **Source:** https://www.famitsu.com/news/202403/15337235.html

### Collaboration with Yomiuri Giants
- On August 1, 2023, Kanata served as a live announcer alongside Hakui Koyori during a Yomiuri Giants baseball game as part of a collaboration between the team and Hololive.
- **Source:** https://www.moguravr.com/vtuber-daily-news-564/

### Animelo Summer Live 2024
- On September 13, 2024, Kanata was announced as a performer at Animelo Summer Live's first VTuber concert, alongside fellow Hololive member Ookami Mio.
- **Source:** https://kai-you.net/article/90512

### Blue Journey Project
- In April 2023, Kanata joined the Hololive x EMI Records project **Blue Journey**, performing on songs "Dear Youthful Days", "Astro", and "Mizutamari" alongside various Hololive members.
- **Source:** https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/124063/2

### First Solo Live "LOCK ON" with AZKi
- On August 13, 2025, Kanata performed her first solo live concert at **Ariake Arena** with AZKi as a special guest. It received widespread acclaim for her vocal performance, costume changes, and live band.
- **Source:** https://www.famitsu.com/article/202508/50424
- **Source:** https://www.pashplus.jp/report/428858/

### 3D Debut with 100K+ Concurrent Viewers
- On July 5, 2020, Kanata's 3D debut stream exceeded **100,000 concurrent viewers** — a major milestone.
- **Source:** https://panora.tokyo/archives/7883

### Studio Albums
| Album | Release Date | Oricon Peak | Billboard Japan Peak |
|-------|-------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Unknown Diva** (1st album) | March 13, 2024 | #6 | #7 |
| **Trigger** (2nd/final album) | July 23, 2025 | #5 | #33 |
- **Source:** https://www.oricon.co.jp/prof/789957/rank/album/

### Solo Concert at Ariake Arena
- Kanata performed **"Amane Kanata 1st Solo Live 'LOCK ON'"** at the Ariake Arena on August 13, 2025. This was her first and only solo live concert, and it was met with widespread critical acclaim for her vocal performance, the production value, and the emotional setlist.
- **Source:** https://www.pashplus.jp/report/428858/
- **Source:** https://panora.tokyo/archives/114417

### Overcoming Ménière's Disease
- Kanata was diagnosed with **Ménière's disease in 2017**, leaving her completely deaf in one ear. This prevented her from joining traditional idol groups. Despite this, she became a successful Hololive VTuber and singer, holding 20+ second notes in live concerts.
- **Source:** https://realsound.jp/2022/05/post-1022934.html
- **Source:** https://www.famitsu.com/news/202403/15337235.html

### Songwriting & Composition Credits
- Kanata composed and wrote her own songs **"Betsu Sekai"** and **"Jumanokudo"**, as well as the holoForce group song **"Kiseki Knot"**.
- She wrote lyrics for **"Start Up"**, **"Sekai de Ichiban no Idol"**, and **"Fallen Wing"** (co-written with Toby Fox and Camellia).
- **Source:** https://www.famitsu.com/news/202403/15337235.html

### Game Tie-in: "Memento Mori"
- On June 26, 2025, Kanata was confirmed to perform the tie-in song **"Lumière"** for the gacha game *Memento Mori*, released July 29, 2025.
- **Source:** https://www.4gamer.net/games/590/G059012/20250627030/

### Game Tie-in: "Deep Insanity: Asylum"
- On June 24, 2021, Kanata and Tokoyami Towa were confirmed to perform **"Madoromi"** as the theme song for *Deep Insanity: Asylum*, released on October 13, 2021.
- **Source:** https://app.famitsu.com/20210625_1814659/

## 4. Additional Trivia

- **Pet rabbits:** Kanata has two pet rabbits.
- **Pop Team Epic fan:** She is a big fan of the series and invited illustrator Bkub to create stamps for her YouTube membership.
- **Reddit moderator:** She was one of the moderators of the official Hololive subreddit.
- **Fashion sense:** Known for having a poor sense of fashion — Kiryu Coco claimed she had only ever seen Kanata wear two different outfits.
- **Name poll:** Her YouTube membership was named "THE Kanata" via a Twitter poll (options included "Kanatas" and "Kanata Fan Club").
- **Inspirations:** Cited Eri Kamei as inspiration for her idol work, and Minato Aqua as influence on her VTubing career. Yui Aragaki was cited as her "first love".
- **Age:** Officially 20 years old (17 at debut), height 149 cm.
