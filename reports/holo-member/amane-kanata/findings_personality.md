# Amane Kanata (天音かなた) — Personality & Style Research

## Personality Description

Amane Kanata is a former Hololive Japan 4th Generation (holoForce) VTuber who debuted on 27 December 2019 and graduated on 27 December 2025. Her official bio describes her as:

- **Cheerful and outgoing** around close friends, but **shy and quiet/introverted** in large crowds.
- **Homebody** — stays at home on her days off.
- **Hardworking and driven** — her stated goal was to perform a concert at Saitama Super Arena, which she pursued relentlessly throughout her career.
- **Strong-willed** — despite being diagnosed with Meniere's disease in 2017 (which left her completely deaf in one ear and prevented her from joining traditional idol groups), she persisted in applying to Hololive and built a successful career.
- **"Idol otaku" personality** — she was a known idol enthusiast who cited former Morning Musume member Eri Kamei as her inspiration for becoming an idol, and Yui Aragaki as her "first love." Her reaction to Yui Aragaki's marriage to Gen Hoshino inspired her original song "Betsu Sekai."
- **Surprising physical strength** — known for her grip strength exceeding 50 kg (stronger than the average adult male), which led to her accidentally destroying multiple objects on stream.
- **Nicknamed "PP Tenshi" (PP天使)** — originated from her frequent use of Microsoft PowerPoint during debut streams, which became a beloved quirk.

## Voice Characteristics & Streaming Style

- **Strong vocal performance** — widely praised for her singing ability. She frequently held notes in online concerts for over 20 seconds on multiple occasions.
- **Songwriter & composer** — wrote and composed her own solo songs "Betsu Sekai" and "Jumanokudo," as well as the holoForce group song "Kiseki Knot." She also wrote lyrics for "Start Up," "Sekai de Ichiban no Idol," and "Fallen Wing" (the latter co-written with Toby Fox and Camellia).
- **Expressive range** — her "Lock On" solo concert at Ariake Arena (13 August 2025) received widespread acclaim for her vocal versatility, costume changes, live band performance, and emotional delivery.
- **Streaming quirk** — heavy use of Microsoft PowerPoint in early streams, leading to the "PP Tenshi" nickname.
- **Gaming streamer** — played a variety of games including Dark Souls (as a complete newbie to FromSoftware games).
- **Content variety** — mix of karaoke/ singing streams ("uta-goke"), gaming, chatting, and music production.
- **Podcast host** — started a podcast series "Amane Kanata Wants to Learn Music!" in May 2024.
- **High-pitched, energetic voice** — commonly described as having an "angelic" voice fitting her celestial/angel theme.

## Relationships with Other Hololive Members

### Genmates (Hololive 4th Generation / holoForce)
- **Tokoyami Towa** — very close collaborator. Formed the musical unit **ORIO** together, releasing the EP "Over Time" (2022). Frequently performed duets and collaborated on songs like "Madoromi."
- **Tsunomaki Watame** — fellow genmate; part of holoForce group songs.
- **Himemori Luna** — fellow genmate; part of holoForce activities.
- **Kiryu Coco** — fellow genmate (graduated 2021). Wrote and composed "Kiseki Knot" for Coco's graduation.

### Close Collaborators
- **AZKi** — formed the unit **Kanaken** with AZKi and Sakamata Chloe. AZKi was a special guest at her "Lock On" solo concert. They collaborated on "Tenkaidokyuu Oshigoto Road."
- **Sakamata Chloe** — part of the **Kanaken** unit; performed a farewell 3D concert together in December 2024.
- **Minato Aqua** — cited by Kanata as an influence on her VTubing career. Collaborated on Blue Journey project.
- **Hakui Koyori** — co-hosted live announcing for a Yomiuri Giants baseball game.
- **Ookami Mio** — performed together at Animelo Summer Live (VTuber concert, 2024).

### Broader Hololive
- Participated in **Blue Journey** (Hololive × EMI Records musical project) alongside multiple members.
- Member of **Magical Girl HoloWitches!** with Sakura Miko, Himemori Luna, Houshou Marine, Murasaki Shion, and Sakamata Chloe.
- Frequently involved in Hololive Idol Project ensemble songs with talents across all generations.
- Fanbase name: **Hey-min** (へい民).

## Career Highlights
- 1.72 million YouTube subscribers at graduation.
- Released two studio albums: *Unknown Diva* (2024, #6 Oricon) and *Trigger* (2025, #5 Oricon).
- Performed first solo live "Lock On" at Ariake Arena (August 2025).
- Held notes in concerts for 20+ seconds as a signature vocal feat.
- Announced graduation on 2 December 2025 due to work-related stress and organizational strain; fully left the entertainment industry without plans to reincarnate.
