# Findings: Personality & Streaming Style - Amane Kanata

## Personality
- Extremely sweet, polite, friendly, and demure
- Slightly dorky yet pure-hearted
- Somewhat quiet and reserved compared to her 4th gen contemporaries
- Has a playfully "sadistic" streak - teases her audience and plays pranks
- Markedly shy, conceals this with a "cool facade"
- Innocent and a little naive - viewers jokingly worry about her being "corrupted" by other members (Kiryu Coco, Natsuiro Matsuri)
- Often does not understand adult jokes and avoids bad words
- Known for keeping her spirits high even through bad moments (e.g., near deafness in one ear)

## Singing / Voice
- Well known for **impressive vocal range** - one of the best singers in Hololive
- Can simulate a wide range of voices (child-like, deep male, exaggerated cartoon voices)
- Has successfully made prank calls to other members impersonating other people
- Runs a running joke that Kanata is secretly ALL Hololive members doing voice acting
- Sang **Sōran Bushi** (traditional Japanese sea shanty) a cappella during debut - became her theme song
- The nickname "PP Tenshi" can stand for **Perfect Pitch Tenshi** when she sings

## Intelligence & Knowledge
- Scored a perfect 20/20 on Yuzuki Choco's general knowledge test
- Has excellent memory
- Nicknamed "BB Tenshi" (Big-Brained Angel) by English-speaking fans
- Big fan of Pop Team Epic, Hunter × Hunter, and especially Detective Conan

## Quirks
- Lacks a sense of fashion - Kiryu Coco claimed to have only seen her wear two different outfits
- Lacks a sense for naming things - her YouTube membership poll options were "Kanatas", "THE Kanata", and "Kanata Fan Club"
- Used to call herself "Nyan Nyan" as a kid

## Relationships
- **Kiryu Coco**: Lived together with Coco; best friends; Coco gave her the nickname "Kana-Gorilla"
- **Tokoyami Towa**: Formed the unit **ORIO** together (announced Feb 27, 2022)
- **Inugami Korone**: Both are avid baseball fans and have attended games together
- **Houshou Marine**: Participated in "Cinderella switch" Vol.1 together

## Other Activities
- Moderator on the official Hololive Subreddit
- Has two pet rabbits
- Member of **Magical Girl holoWitches** outfit group
