# IRyS — Official Profile & Lore Research

## Character Lore & Debut Background (Project: HOPE)

IRyS was introduced under **Project: HOPE**, a solo VTuber project from Hololive English. She is described as a **Nephilim** — the offspring of an angel and a demon — who was once the embodiment of hope in "The Paradise." She left Paradise to adventure into the world, delivering hope through her lyrics and songs. Her dual angel-demon nature occasionally manifests in unpredictable, "yabai" (dangerous/surprising) comments, which are framed as an unexpected charm of her duality.

Her catchphrase: *"HiRyS, it's IRyS! Your seiso nephilim here to fill the world with hopium!"*

## Affiliations

- **Hololive English** — the English-language branch of Hololive Production, operated by COVER Corporation.
- **Project: HOPE** — the solo debut project under which IRyS was introduced. She is the only member under this category.
- **hololive English -Promise-** — a merged unit formed from the former Council (Ouro Kronii, Hakos Baelz, Ceres Fauna, Nanashi Mumei) and Project: HOPE (IRyS). As of the research date, Ceres Fauna and Nanashi Mumei are listed as alumni; the active Promise roster includes IRyS, Ouro Kronii, and Hakos Baelz.
- **Note on Advent:** Nerissa Ravencroft and Koseki Bijou are members of **hololive English -Advent-** (debuting July 2023), a separate generation from Promise. They are all part of Hololive English but belong to different sub-units.

## VSinger Aspect

IRyS is positioned primarily as a **VSinger** (Virtual Singer), placing heavy emphasis on music and singing as her core identity. Her lore directly ties her voice and songs to the act of delivering hope. She has released numerous original singles and albums, including *YaBAI*, *Stay Blue*, *Odyssey*, *Carbonated Love*, *Our Promise*, *Connect the World*, *Breaking Dimensions*, *START AGAIN*, *TwiLight*, *LIVE IT LOUD!*, *Escalate*, *Run Back 'Round*, *Bright Tonight*, *BE MY FLAME*, and *All for One*. Her music catalog spans original songs, covers, and collaborative albums such as the *hololive English Eurobeat Remix Album*.
