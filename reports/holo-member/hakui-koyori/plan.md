# Hakui Koyori (博衣こより) Research Plan

## Main Research Question
Hololive member Hakui Koyori's official settings, personal characteristics, notable memes/clips, and anecdotes for doujin game character design.

## Subtopics

### 1. Official Settings & Profile
- Hololive official profile, character design (outfit, color scheme, theme)
- Debut date, generation affiliation (Hololive 6th Gen / Secret Society holoX)
- Basic stats: height, birthday, fan name, hashtags
- Character lore: "ko yo" (koyori = "ko" + "yori"), lab coat theme, assistant/Mad Scientist persona

### 2. Personality & Streaming Characteristics
- Speaking style, voice characteristics
- Typical streaming content (gaming, singing, chatting)
- Interaction with other Hololive members
- Known for certain catchphrases or mannerisms

### 3. Notable Memes, Clips & Anecdotes
- Famous clips and moments
- Recurring jokes/memes in the community
- Notable collabs and interactions
- Any unique quirks or running gags

### 4. Community & Fan Perception
- Fan community characteristics
- Popular fan creations or interpretations
- Potential for game character adaptation notes

## Synthesis Plan
Combine all findings into a comprehensive Chinese report covering all aspects, organized for game development reference.
