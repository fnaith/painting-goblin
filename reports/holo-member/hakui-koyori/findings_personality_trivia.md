# Hakui Koyori - Personality, Strengths & Trivia

Sources: hololive.wiki, hololivevtuber.fandom.com

## Personality
- Self-proclaimed "brains of holoX" but with limited expertise — a running gag
- Insatiable thirst for knowledge; curious researcher type
- Meddlesome but helpful — studies human behavior by getting involved
- Sometimes deliberately messes with people just to see their reactions
- Energetic, chatty, and highly sociable

## Strengths
- Tongue-twisters (速射砲のような早口言葉)
- Good memory
- Skill with numbers and mental games
- Detective skills
- Typing speed
- Voice impersonations
- Singing, planning, MC/hosting, human observation

## Weaknesses
- Horror (scared easily)
- Insects
- Arm strength (weak arms)
- Poor luck
- Mornings (not a morning person)
- Spicy food
- FPS games

## Likes
- Singing, planning, MC work
- Human observation
- Video games
- Crane games
- Theme parks

## Favorite Foods
- Meat, ramen, sake, shirako (cod milt), udon, mayonnaise, fugashi (fried dough snack)

## Favorite Anime/Media
- Macross F, Higurashi no naku koro ni, Gundam Seed, Detective Conan, Saki, Uma Musume, Tokyo Revengers
- Western media: Game of Thrones, Ready Player One

## Trivia
- Had her Twitter account restricted for ~2 weeks immediately after HoloX reveal due to overwhelming interactions from fans
- Shares the same illustrator (Momoco) with Petra Gurin (Nijisanji EN) and Igarashi Rika (Nijisanji)
- The only Hololive member who has links to all other members from all branches in her YouTube channel's "channels" tab
- Uses a paid font called "Panda Bakery" for her schedule and morning show streams
- Her partner/mascot is a **coyote robot**
- Her lore: born on a savannah to coyote parents; instead of inheriting parents' physical traits, her intelligence and "geek" stats were exceptionally high

## Nickname Origin
- "Pink Pervert" (ピンクの変態) — from the official HoloX MEETing manga serialized in Shounen Jump Plus
