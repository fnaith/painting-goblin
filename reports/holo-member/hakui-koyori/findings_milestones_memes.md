## Key Milestones
- **2021:** YouTube channel created Sep 12; Twitter account created Sep 2021
- **Nov 26, 2021:** HoloX officially revealed; Koyori's Twitter immediately restricted due to volume of interactions
- **Nov 28, 2021:** Debut stream — 3rd holoX member to debut; 175,000+ peak concurrent viewers
- **Dec 8, 2021:** Twitter restriction lifted; YouTube monetization approved same day
- **Jan 1, 2022:** New Year's outfit revealed
- **Jun 16, 2022:** 3D model debut
- **Nov 28, 2022:** 1st anniversary 3D live — debuted 3D idol outfit
- **2024:** Regular appearances in Hololive events and collaborations

## Regular Content
- **Asa Koyo (朝こよ):** Weekly morning (JST) Hololive news show — would run twice a week initially, later reduced to once weekly
- Frequent karaoke streams (many milestone celebrations are karaoke endurance streams)

## Memes & Running Gags
1. **"Self-proclaimed brains but limited expertise"** — Official bio gag that she's not actually as smart as she claims
2. **Twitter restriction incident** — Her debut was literally titled "Will my Twitter be unfrozen by the start of the stream!? RTA Start!" (初配信【スタートまでにTwitterの凍結は溶けるのか！？RTA開始！】)
3. **Coyote identity** — Constantly reminding viewers she's a coyote, not a cat or fox
4. **"Kon Koyo!" catchphrase** — Derived from trying to come up with a cool greeting for a week straight
5. **Pink Pervert** — From the official holoX MEETing manga, added as a running character trait

## Notable Interactions & Collabs
- Part of **Secret Society holoX** alongside La+ Darknesss, Takane Lui, Sakamata Chloe, Kazama Iroha
- Featured in official Hololive manga **"holoX MEETing"** serialized in **Shounen Jump Plus**
- Close relationships with all holoX members — the group is known for strong chemistry
- Collab cover "Newton Dance" with Takanashi Kiara (KoaraConnect)
- Collab cover "Monster" with Tokino Sora, Hoshimachi Suisei, Oozora Subaru, Omaru Polka, Sakamata Chloe
- holoX collab cover "Ochame Kinou"
