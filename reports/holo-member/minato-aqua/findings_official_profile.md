## Official Lore

Minato Aqua is a **marine maid-styled virtual maid** who serves at the hololive agency. Her official description states: *"She is trying her very best, yet is still sometimes awkward and clumsy."*

Despite her nautical theme, she has nothing to do with water — the marine motif is purely an aesthetic choice. She aspires to be **"the ultimate idol in the galaxy."**

## Character Design

### Original Design (2018)
- **Illustrator:** Gaou (Twitter: @umaiyo_puyoman)
- **Live2D:** rariemonn (base model)
- **Hair:** Purple hair with twin spiral ponytails decorated with navy-blue ribbons; light-blue streaks in bangs and inner edges of ponytails; light-blue ahoge
- **Outfit:** Marine-themed maid uniform with a white mob cap decorated with an anchor (sometimes described as resembling a "tissue box")
- **Colors:** Navy blue, white, purple, light blue

## Personality & Traits

- **On-stream persona:** Bumbling, airheaded, clumsy, prone to tantrums and hyperventilating; enjoys pranks that almost always backfire; often called "Baqua" (a portmanteau of "Baka" and "Aqua")
- **Off-stream persona:** Kind, responsible, extremely introverted — only opens up while streaming
- **Surprisingly skilled:** Master rank in Apex Legends, Diamond rank in League of Legends; skilled at Sekiro, Dark Souls, and other technically demanding games
- Known for prolific streaming (once streamed 5 times in a single day)

## Trivia

- Her character was designed by Gaou, who also designed other hololive talents
- The audition for her role was announced on Marine Day (July 14, 2018) to match her nautical theme
- Her fanbase frequently calls her "Onion" due to her purple hair resembling a red onion
- Despite being a "maid," her catchphrase "Konaqua" (こんあくあ) is a pun combining "Kon'nichiwa" and "Aqua"
- She was the first hololive 2nd Generation member to reach 1 million subscribers
- Her graduation was due to differences in opinion with management, as stated in her announcement
- Post-graduation, her YouTube channel content remains online
