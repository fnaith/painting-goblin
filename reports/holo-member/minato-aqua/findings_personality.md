# Minato Aqua (湊あくあ) — Personality & Characteristics Research

## Overview

Minato Aqua was a member of **hololive 2nd Generation**, debuting on August 8, 2018 and graduating on August 28, 2024. Her character is a marine maid-styled virtual maid, represented by the ⚓ anchor emoji. She stood 148cm tall, born December 1st, with a fanbase called **Aqua Crew** (あくあクルー).

## Personality

- **Airhead / Clumsy Maid**: Despite her debut as a "pure and innocent maid," Aqua quickly evolved into a lovable airhead who often ends up in embarrassing situations — sometimes deliberately. She has masochistic tendencies and once held a stream dedicated to other Hololive members bullying her.
- **Childish & Emotional**: Known for table-slamming tantrums, random noises ("ora", "rua", "nya"), and screaming "NEEEEEE" when things don't go her way (shared catchphrase with Murasaki Shion).
- **Shy & Introverted (off-camera)** : She joined Hololive specifically to overcome her introversion. She described herself as a hikkikomori. Fellow VTubers report she becomes nervous and barely speaks when meeting in real life. She famously wore a cap everywhere to avoid eye contact (coining the term "Incap").
- **Resilient & Determined**: Despite her childish on-screen persona, she is extremely driven. She felt she had nothing that made her stand out, so she grinded gaming skills to make her streams entertaining. She regularly did endurance streams (e.g., playing until reaching Diamond rank in Apex).
- **Kind & Loyal**: Particularly shown in her friendship with Murasaki Shion — she persistently supported Shion during tough times even when pushed away, which became a turning point in their relationship.

## Streaming Style

- **Greeting**: "KonAqua!" (こんあくあ) — a blend of "konbanwa" and "Aqua"
- **Farewell**: "Otsuaqua~" (おつあくあ)
- **Catchphrases**: "Naruhaya taiki" (なるはや待機) — misread kanji that became a meme
- **SAO reference**: Before starting games she often says "Game no sekai ni link start" (a Sword Art Online reference)
- **Content focus**: Primarily competitive online games (FPS, action), but plays anything. Known for karaoke streams, endurance challenges, and speedruns.
- **Sololive**: Nicknamed "Sololive" due to her tendency to play games alone rather than collab.
- **Aqutan persona**: Occasionally roleplays as "Aqutan" — her little sister character with an extremely innocent loli voice, who is also surprisingly skilled at video games.
