## 2. Memorable Stream Moments

- **Graduation Stream (August 28, 2024):** Her farewell stream drew a peak of **749,854 concurrent viewers** and accumulated **$237,571 USD in Superchat** donations. Over 6 million VOD views within 3 days. This was one of the largest graduation streams in VTuber history.
- **950K+ concurrent viewers** at its peak during the graduation (per Know Your Meme), making it one of the most-watched VTuber streams ever.
- Known for **"stalling laughter"** — her inability to stop laughing during streams, often setting off chain reactions with other Hololive members.
- Frequent **cat persona** roleplay: meowing, cat puns, wearing cat-ear accessories as part of her "marine maid" aesthetic.

## 3. Popular Fan Memes

- **"Onion" vs "Archangel" duality** — Fans depict her as a crying onion who suddenly switches to a cool, angelic mode.
- **IQ 5,000 / Needs Brain Dissection** — The ironic contrast between pretending to be a genius and acting helpless/clumsy.
- **Upah** spam in chat — A nonsense phrase that became a raid/chat spam meme.
- **The Cat Persona** — Aqua inserting "nya" into sentences, roleplaying as a cat, with fans enthusiastically playing along.
- **AKUKIN** — A portmanteau of Aqua and "gold" (kin), used in reference to merch/SC chasing.

## 5. Controversies & Notable Incidents

- **DDoS Attack on 2021 Concert:** In 2021, Aqua's solo concert experienced a DDoS attack. This incident gained significant coverage as a serious disruption of a VTuber live event.
