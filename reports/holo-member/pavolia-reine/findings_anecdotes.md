## 2. Relationships with Other Hololive Members

### Hololive Indonesia (holoro — 2nd Gen)

Reine debuted alongside **Kureiji Ollie** and **Anya Melfissa** as Hololive ID's 2nd generation (holoro). The three have a close sister-like dynamic:

- **Kureiji Ollie** — Reine often serves as the "straight man" (tsukkomi) to Ollie's chaotic energy. Their contrast (elegant peacock vs. zombie gremlin) is a staple of ID collabs.
- **Anya Melfissa** — Reine and Anya share a calmer, more playful rapport. Both are known for refined aesthetics (Reine's peafowl nobility, Anya's keris-wielding mystic).
- **Ayunda Risu & Moona Hoshinova** (ID Gen 1) — Reine frequently collabs with the earlier generation. Moona in particular has interacted with Reine in both ID and EN settings (e.g., Minecraft events).
- **Airani Iofifteen** — Iofi and Reine share an appreciation for art and creativity, occasionally doing drawing/chat streams together.
- **Kaela Kovalskia & Kobo Kanaeru** (ID Gen 3) — Reine acts as a senpai to the 3rd gen, with Kobo often bringing chaotic energy that Reine playfully scolds or teases.

### Hololive Japan

- **Usada Pekora, Shirakami Fubuki, Oozora Subaru** — Reine has participated in multi-branch Minecraft server shenanigans and event collabs (e.g., Sports Festival, Among Us). Her elegant-but-dorky persona fits well with JP members.
- **Houshou Marine** — Both share a love of singing and have interacted during karaoke relay events. Their "ojou-sama" energy overlaps in amusing ways.
- **Nakiri Ayame** — Ayame and Reine have been noted for similar soft-spoken yet mischievous personalities, leading to fan speculation about collab potential.

### Hololive English

- **Takanashi Kiara** — Both are "bird" themed (Kiara = phoenix, Reine = peafowl), leading to the "Bird Up" duo fanon. They have acknowledged the bird connection in streams.
- **Ninomae Ina'nis** — Ina's calm, pun-driven streams align well with Reine's brunch-and-chat format.
- **Ceres Fauna** (graduated) — Fauna and Reine's nature themes and gentle voices made for a natural pairing in the "Nature Sisters" fan concept.

## 3. Memorable Behind-the-Scenes Stories & Trivia

### 3.1 The Turkey Incident (Debut Stream)
During her debut stream, Reine accidentally referred to herself as a "turkey" instead of a peafowl. This became an enduring meme — fans and even other talents occasionally tease her by calling her "Turkey," and it remains one of her most well-known running gags.

### 3.2 Tatang Tambourine
Reine's tambourine is named **Tatang Tambourine** (affectionately "Tatang"). She brings him out during donation-reading segments to provide rhythm and sound effects. Tatang has become a beloved secondary "character" in her streams, with fans treating him as a legitimate part of the Reine experience.

### 3.3 The Watermelon Room
Originated from a simulator game stream where Reine painted walls with alternating watermelon-colored stripes and told chat it was a principal's office where misbehaving viewers would be sent to "reflect." This evolved into a running lore — viewers sometimes joke they are watching streams *from inside* the watermelon room, begging to be let out.

### 3.4 KUSALAND
An extension of the Watermelon Room lore. Reine built an amusement park in a simulator game where the rides are intentionally terrifying/chaotic (rides that throw you off, unsafe loops, etc.). Fans jokingly call it **KUSALAND** — a theme park where "nothing is safe." It is treated as an upgrade or branch of the Watermelon Room concept.

### 3.5 "It's Fine"
A recurring catchphrase Reine uses excessively, often in resignation when a game goes wrong or when she tries to brush off her own mistakes. Fans have compiled montages of her saying "it's fine" in increasingly dire situations.

### 3.6 Engine-Starting Laugh
Many viewers describe Reine's laugh as resembling a car engine struggling to start. This distinctive laugh is frequently mentioned in live chat and fan content, and is considered one of her most endearing quirks.

### 3.8 Dislikes Cleaning (Uses Magic)
Reine has stated she dislikes cleaning, but claims it's not laziness — rather, she used magic to clean back in her home world, so doing it manually is a difficult adjustment. This ties back to her lore as a student from a magic academy with diminished powers.

### 3.9 Favorite Media
- **Anime:** When They Cry (Higurashi), Hell Girl, Another, Great Teacher Onizuka, Great Pretender
- **Games:** Corpse Party, Danganronpa, RPGmaker horror games, party games, platformers, puzzle games, BanG Dream!
- She has a clear preference for horror, mystery, and narrative-driven media.

### 3.10 "I Can't Speak Peafowl"
Despite being a peafowl-themed character, Reine has explicitly stated she *cannot* speak peafowl language (in contrast to other animal-themed members who do animal sounds). This has become a minor self-deprecating joke in the community.
Per her official bio, Reine loves sweets (cookies, chocolate, cake) but is weak against overly spicy food. Brunch streams where she eats while chatting are a regular weekly feature.

## 4. Fan Culture & Community Characteristics

### 4.1 MERAKyats Identity
The fanbase name is a intentional double entendre:
- **Merak** = peafowl (Indonesian)
- **Rakyat** = people/citizen
- **Merakyat** = humble, close to the people

This reinforces Reine's self-image as an elegant but approachable, "dorky" noble. The sign-off "Salam MERAKyats" (MERAKyats greetings) is used at the end of her streams.

### 4.2 Community Tone
- The fanbase leans into the "elegant but unhinged" dichotomy — treating Reine with mock-formal respect ("Lady Pavolia") while knowing she is a dork.
- Watermelon Room and KUSALAND are frequently referenced in superchats and chat.
- Tats (Tatang Tambourine) lore is maintained by fans who send donations asking about him.

### 4.3 Art & Creative Output
- Fan art uses `#Reinessance` (a pun on "Renaissance" + "Reine").
- The fan community produces a steady stream of elegant peafowl imagery alongside "turkey" meme edits.
- Iida Pochi.'s original character design (beautiful, regal, with glasses variants) is beloved and widely replicated in fan works.

### 4.4 Collab Culture
Reine's fans are receptive to cross-branch collabs. There is a notable appetite for:
- "Bird collabs" with Kiara.
- ID generation collabs (holoro reunion).
- Brunch/multi-branch chatting collabs where Reine's relaxed hosting style shines.
