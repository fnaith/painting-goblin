## 1. Signature Memes & Running Gags

### 🦃 The Turkey Incident (Kalkun)
- **Origin:** During her December 6, 2020 debut stream, Reine accidentally referred to herself as a "turkey" instead of a "peafowl" (merak).
- **Impact:** Fans latched onto this immediately. She is now frequently called "Turkey," "Kalkun" (Indonesian for turkey), or "Tante Kalkun" (Aunt Turkey) by chat.
- **Official acknowledgment:** The hololive website FAQ includes "Q: Are you a turkey? A: No?" — confirming the running gag is officially recognized.
- **Nicknames spawned:** Turkey, Kalkun, Tante Kalkun.
- **Status:** One of her most enduring memes; she leans into it with self-deprecating humor.

### 🚗 Car Engine Laugh
- **Origin:** Reine has a distinctive, raspy, loud laugh.
- **Description:** The official hololive website states her laugh "has been perceived as similar to a car engine starting."
- **Gap moe:** The contrast between her elegant peafowl princess appearance and her engine-like laugh is a core part of her charm.
- **Fan reaction:** Viewers often comment "the car engine is starting" or use sound effect memes in chat when she laughs.

### 🍉 The Watermelon Room (Kamar Semangka)
- **Origin:** From a House Flipper (simulator game) stream where Reine painted a room's walls with alternating green and pink stripes (watermelon colors).
- **Gag premise:** She declared it a "detention room" where misbehaving viewers are sent to reflect on their actions.
- **Running joke:** Chat members jokingly claim they are watching the stream FROM INSIDE the watermelon room, begging to be let out.
- **Fan expansion:** The concept has been expanded by fans in art and memes — what exactly happens inside is left to the imagination.
- **Official acknowledgment:** Listed on the hololive website under Catchphrases/Memes.

### 🎢 KUSALAND
- **Origin:** From a simulator game stream where Reine built an amusement park.
- **Gag premise:** KUSALAND is an amusement park where people scream in terror instead of joy. Nothing is safe, and that's a "feature."
- **Relationship to Watermelon Room:** Described as an upgrade or a branch of the Watermelon Room concept.
- **Name origin:** "KUSA" is likely a play on "kusa" (草, Japanese internet slang for "lol," from "warau" → "wwww" → looks like grass).
- **Official acknowledgment:** Listed on the hololive website under Catchphrases/Memes as "(optional) KUSALAND."

### 🥁 Tatang Tambourine
- **Full name:** Tatang Tambourine.
- **What it is:** A physical tambourine Reine uses during donation reading segments on stream.
- **Purpose:** She shakes/makes music with Tatang while reading out superchats and donations.
- **Personality:** Referred to as a "him" — Tatang has become a character in his own right.
- **Catchphrase usage:** "Tatang Tambourine!" is exclaimed during streams.
- **Official acknowledgment:** The FAQ on the hololive site opens with "Q: Who is Tatang!?!??! A: Tatang is my tambourine."

### ✅ "It's Fine"
- **Origin:** Reine says this phrase excessively during streams.
- **Context 1 — Resignation:** When a game goes wrong or she makes a mistake, she says "it's fine" in a tone of surrender.
- **Context 2 — Dismissal:** Trying to wave off her own errors or things going badly.
- **Running joke:** Chat counts how many times she says it per stream.

### 🏥 GWS (Get Well Soon)
- **Origin:** During donation reading streams, viewers submit pickup lines and romantic compliments.
- **The bit:** Most pickup lines are awkward or cringeworthy. Reine responds with "Get Well Soon" (GWS), treating the failed attempt as an illness.
- **Community participation:** Chat joins in with "GWS" whenever a particularly bad pickup line is read.
- **Status:** An enduring donation reading tradition.

## 2. Personality-Based Running Jokes

### Elegant Facade vs. Dork Reality (Gap Moe)
- **The official bio:** "At first glance, she seems to be the elegant type, but people who spend some more time with her will find out that she has more of a dorky personality."
- **The joke:** She starts sentences in a refined, noble tone (ojou-sama speech), then immediately says something goofy or clumsy.
- **Fan response:** The contrast is her primary appeal — "elegant princess who is actually a goofball."

### Weak to Spicy Food
- **The irony:** Reine is Indonesian, and Indonesia is known for its spicy cuisine (sambal, etc.).
- **The joke:** Despite being Indonesian, she cannot handle spicy food at all.
- **Notable moment:** At viewer request, she attempted to eat spicy instant noodles on stream but could not finish them.
- **Contrast with genmates:** Kureiji Ollie and other HoloID members have notably better spice tolerance.

### "Salam MERAKyats!"
- **Sign-off greeting:** Reine ends her streams with "Salam MERAKyats!" ("MERAKyats greetings!").
- **Fan name pun:** MERAKyats = Merak (peafowl) + Rakyat (people/citizens). Also puns on "merakyat" (humble/down-to-earth).
- **Chat response:** Viewers echo "Salam MERAKyats" back in chat at stream end.

## 3. Stream Series Memes & Bits

### YABE (Yet Another Brunch Everyone)
- **Concept:** Weekly weekend brunch stream where Reine eats and chats with viewers.
- **Interactive element:** Viewers are encouraged to also eat their own meals during the stream.
- **Chat food:** Reine draws illustrations of foods mentioned in chat and uses them as stream props.
- **Meme potential:** The cozy communal dining vibe contrasted with threats of the Watermelon Room creates an entertaining dynamic.

### Reine's Classroom
- **Concept:** Reine teaches other Hololive talents useful Bahasa Indonesia phrases.
- **Chaos factor:** Guest talents often ask for questionable phrases, leading to hilarious roleplay scenarios.
- **Cross-branch appeal:** Has featured talents from HoloJP and HoloEN, serving as a cultural bridge.
- **Meme phrases taught:** Some phrases taught in the classroom have become running jokes in the ID community.

## 4. Iconic Moments & Clip Highlights

### Debut Stream (Dec 6, 2020)
- **Key moment 1:** The "Turkey" slip — accidentally calling herself a turkey. Launched the most enduring meme.
- **Key moment 2:** Reached 100K subscribers during debut stream.
- **Significance:** Set the tone for her self-deprecating, honest streaming style.

### House Flipper — Watermelon Room Origin
- **Game:** House Flipper (simulator).
- **What happened:** Reine painted a room with alternating pink and green stripes.
- **The bit:** Declared it a principal's office/detention room for misbehaving chat members.
- **Why it became a meme:** The absurd specificity, the threatening tone delivered in her elegant voice, and the enthusiastic embrace by chat.

### Simulator Game — KUSALAND Origin
- **Game:** A theme park/amusement park simulator.
- **What happened:** Reine built an amusement park where all the rides were dangerous or terrifying.
- **The bit:** She announced it as her personal amusement park where screaming is guaranteed.
- **Evolution:** Became the "upgrade" from the Watermelon Room as a place for "worse" offenders.

### Passpartout Speedrun
- **Game:** Passpartout: The Starving Artist.
- **What happened:** Reine completed the entire campaign in under 3 hours, showcasing genuine drawing talent.
- **Significance:** Proved she is not just a VTuber but a legitimately skilled artist.

### Spicy Noodle Challenge
- **What happened:** Attempted to eat extremely spicy instant noodles at viewer request.
- **Outcome:** Could not finish them — confirmed her weakness to spicy food as genuine.
- **Meme value:** The contrast between her confident start and defeated finish was highly entertaining.
