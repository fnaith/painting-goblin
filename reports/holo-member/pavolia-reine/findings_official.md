### Name Etymology
- **"Pavolia"** — derived from Latin *pavo* (peacock); *Pavo* is also the genus for peafowl
- **"Reine"** — French word for "queen"
- **Nickname "Kanjeng"** — traditional Indonesian honorific for high-ranking nobility

### Notable Facts
- **Tallest active female Hololive VTuber** — at 172 cm, she is the tallest currently active female member (second all-time behind Kiryu Coco at 180 cm, who graduated)
- **Name etymology:** "Pavolia" from Latin *pavo* (peacock); "Reine" from French for "queen"

- **Third bird-themed Hololive member** — after Oozora Subaru and Takanashi Kiara

## 2. Official Lore / Backstory

> **"Lady of the Peafowl."**
>
> While studying at a magic academy, she was involved in an incident and transferred to the human world.
> Since then, she has been unable to use much of her magic.

Key lore elements:
- A student at a **magic academy** in her world of origin
- Was involved in an unspecified **incident** that resulted in her transfer to the human world
- Retains some magic ability, but **significantly diminished** compared to her original power
- Her peafowl/peacock theme connects to her identity as royalty or noble lineage ("princess")

## 4. Character Design Concept

### Theme: Peacock / Peafowl

The design is built around peacock imagery, reflected in:
- **Color palette:** Dark purple, teal/green, gold, and white — mimicking peacock plumage
- **Hair:** Long flowing dark hair with teal/purple gradient tones
- **Eyes:** Striking, multifaceted, reminiscent of peacock feather eyespots

### Default Outfit
- Elegant dress with layered, feather-like skirt sections
- Sheer forearm-length gloves
- Ornate gold jewelry and accessories
- Feather-shaped hair accessories / headpiece
- Peacock feather fan as a signature prop
- Heeled boots with feather accents

### Alternate Outfits
1. **Indonesian Traditional Outfit** — 2nd L2D (revealed August 16, 2021)
2. **New Year Outfit** — revealed February 4, 2023
3. **3D Model** — debuted July 2023
4. **"Our Bright Parade" Idol Outfit** — designed by Mika Pikazo for the hololive ID idol outfit set

### Membership Program: "The Royaltea"
- Members-only live streams, badges, and custom emoji

### Signature Props
- **Tatang Tambourine** (full name: *Tatang Tambourine*) — used during donation readings
- **Peacock feather fan** — elegant hand prop
- **"Chat food"** — hand-drawn illustrations of food mentioned in chat, used as stream props
- **Pen display/tablet** — used for drawing on stream

- **Watermelon Room / KUSALAND:** Fun environmental or gag elements that could appear as side content or special rooms.
