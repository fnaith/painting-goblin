## Character Design & Theme

### Sheep Theme
Watame is a sheep bard (traveling musician sheep). Her entire design revolves around sheep/fluff aesthetics.

### Outfit Description (Default)
- Long, straight hair with a pink/magenta inner color and lighter outer tones
- Sheep-like horns protruding from the sides of her head
- A white dress with fluffy sheep-wool trim at the shoulders and hem
- Detached sleeves with puffy sheep-wool accents
- A pink ribbon/choker at the neck
- She carries a **self-playing harp** (lyre-style instrument), emphasizing her bard/musician identity
- Bare legs with sheep-hoof styled shoes/boots

### Alternative / 3D Outfits
1. **New Year's Kimono** (2021) — Traditional Japanese outfit with sheep motifs
2. **3D Idol Outfit** (2021) — Concert-ready idol costume
3. **Casual Outfit** (2021) — A more relaxed everyday look
4. **Additional Casual Outfit** (2022) — Another everyday variant
5. **"Watame Night Fever!! in Zepp Tokyo" Concert Outfit** (2021)
6. **"Watame Night Fever!! in Tokyo Garden Theatre" Concert Outfit** (2024)
7. **Yukata (holonatsu Paradise)** — Summer festival yukata

### Design Notes
- The ram emoji (🐏) is used instead of the sheep (🐑) because the ram has visible horns that match Watame's character design
- Her hair color is described as having a pink/magenta gradient
- The overall color palette is soft pastels — whites, pinks, and lavender tones

## Official Lore / Backstory

From the official Hololive website:
> A sheep bard that goes around everywhere. She loves singing and is fluffy! YES! FLUFFY! Being a herbivore, she loves potato chips. She makes music and videos together with the members of the Tsunomaki Troupe.

Watame is described as a traveling sheep musician who performs as a bard. She established the "Tsunomaki Troupe" as her performance group.
