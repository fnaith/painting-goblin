# Tsunomaki Watame (角巻わため) — Notable Anecdotes & Trivia

## Overview

- **Generation:** Hololive 4th Generation (holoForce)
- **Debut:** December 29, 2019
- **Character:** A sheep bard who loves singing. Known as the "fluffiest" Hololive member.
- **Illustrator:** Fu-mi (@fuumiisc)
- **Fan Name:** Wata-mates (わためいと)
- **Birthday:** June 6
- **Height:** 151 cm
- **Oshi Mark:** Ram emoji (chosen because it has horns, unlike the sheep emoji)
- **Channel:** [Watame Ch. 角巻わため](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCqm3BQLlJfvkTsX_hvm0UmA)
- **Twitch:** [tsunomakiwatame_hololive](https://www.twitch.tv/tsunomakiwatame_hololive/)

## Signature Catchphrase & Memes

### "Watame wa warukunai yo ne" (Watame did nothing wrong)
- The most famous running gag associated with Watame. The phrase originated from an "apology" video she made after a previous stream had connection issues.
- Variants include "Giri giri warukunai Watame" (Borderline nothing wrong Watame).
- The official Hololive bio includes "Watame did nothing wrong!"
- **"GiriGiri Warukunai Watame" (ギリギリ悪くないWatame)**: A live-action short series launched on January 7, 2022, featuring Watame in live-action skit videos. Hashtag: #ギリわる

### "Watame Have a nice day" Series
- A short-form video series launched on October 25, 2022, featuring various slice-of-life Watame content. Hashtag: #はばない

### "Konban dododo!"
- Her signature greeting, a catchy and unique opener for her streams.

### Nicknames
- **Wataojii (わたおじ)** — "Old man Watame," referencing her tendency to act/talk like an elderly person.
- **Watamelon (わたメロン)** — A portmanteau of Watame and melon.

## Personality & Quirks

### Falling Asleep During Streams
- Watame is well-known for dozing off mid-stream. Her "special skills" in her official profile include "respawning in 8 seconds" (a reference to falling asleep and waking back up).
- She has a habit of getting sleepy during late-night or early-morning streams, leading to fans lovingly watching her nod off.

### Exceptionally Emotional
- Watame is known for crying easily during emotional moments in games (character deaths, weddings, etc.).
- Her sniffling sounds like "slurping ramen" to Western fans; chat often types "time for ramen" when she cries.

### Gentle Disposition
- Uses calm, polite language. Rarely shows frustration during games.
- Known for being warm and friendly among peers.
- Speaks "sheep language" — replaces words with bleats ("mee mee").
- "Netabare mee mee!" is her way of saying "No spoilers!"

### ASMR & Soft Voice
- Relatively new to ASMR but praised for the softness of her voice.

## Relationships with Other Hololive Members

### PPTrio (with Kiryu Coco & Amane Kanata)
- Watame formed the "PPTrio" with fellow 4th Gen members Kiryu Coco and Amane Kanata. This was one of the most iconic subgroups within Hololive 4th Gen.

### Bakatare (with Shirakami Fubuki & Shiranui Flare)
- A collab group with Shirakami Fubuki (GAMERS) and Shiranui Flare (3rd Gen).

### Other 4th Gen (Towa, Luna, Kanata, Coco)
- **Tokoyami Towa:** Fellow 4th Gen; they have a friendly senpai-kouhai dynamic.
- **Himemori Luna:** The "princess" of 4th Gen; Watame often plays a supportive role.
- **Amane Kanata:** PPTrio partner; close friend.
- **Kiryu Coco:** PPTrio leader and close friend. Coco's "AsaCoco" morning show ran after Watame's "WataUta" in the early days.

### Akai Haato
- Paired together after their antics in Minecraft.

### 4th Gen Group Song
- The entire 4th Gen released a collaborative single, **"Kiseki Knot"** (キセキ結び), on June 25, 2021.

## Music Milestones

### Original Songs
- **"Everlasting Soul"** — Released September 10, 2021. One of her most recognized original songs.
- **"Ai-mai Chocolate"** (愛昧ショコラーテ) — First original single, released December 19, 2020.
- **"Melody For You"** (キミだけのメロディ) — Released June 30, 2021.
- **"mayday,mayday"** — Released August 19, 2021.
- **"Kimiiro Hanamizuki"** (君色ハナミズキ) — Released April 22, 2022.
- **"Fins"** — Released June 7, 2023.
- **"Cloudy Sheep"** (曇天羊) feat. Mori Calliope — A notable collab song with Hololive EN's Mori Calliope.
- **"Reaper vs. Sheep"** — A duet with Mori Calliope (two versions: Ouen and Kenko).
- **"Dreamy Sheep"** (夢見る羊), **"Happy day to you!"**, **"sweet night, sweet time..."** and many more.

### Albums
- **"Watame no Uta Vol. 1"** — Released May 31, 2021 (digital). Her first studio album.
- **"Watame no Uta Vol. 2"** — Released December 30, 2021.
- **"Watame no Uta Vol. 2.5"** — EP released December 30, 2022.

### Regular Music Programs
- **Watame no Uta (WataUta):** A daily early-morning program with short song covers. Originally aired before Kiryu Coco's AsaCoco. Later moved to weekly then discontinued.
- **Watame Night Fever:** A 2-hour weekend karaoke stream.

### Solo Live Concerts
- **1st Live "Watame Night Fever!! in Zepp Tokyo"** — October 12, 2021. A major milestone, debuting a new 3D outfit.
- She also held "Watame Night Fever!! in Tokyo Garden Theatre" as a subsequent solo concert.

## Notable Incidents & Moments

### "Watame Did Nothing Wrong"
- The catchphrase became a defining meme. The original context was a technical issue apology that she turned into a running joke.

### Connection Issues / "Kusozako Kaisen"
- Her early streaming career was plagued by poor internet connection, which she even turned into a song: "Kusozako Kaisen Sotsugyou! no Uta" (Graduation from Crappy Connection Song), featured on Watame no Uta Vol. 1.

### Emotional Streaming Moments
- Frequently cries during emotional game scenes (e.g., Final Fantasy IX, Hansel and Gretel). This has become an endearing staple of her personality.

### Falling Asleep Incidents
- Multiple instances of falling asleep during her own karaoke or gaming streams, leading to the "respawning in 8 seconds" joke in her official profile.

### Collab with Mori Calliope
- The "Reaper vs. Sheep" series of songs with Mori Calliope was a notable cross-branch collaboration, producing two versions of the same song.

### Blue Journey Project
- Watame is a participating member of **Blue Journey**, a Hololive music project that released a collaborative album and held a live concert featuring multiple Hololive members across generations.

### Nemu Nemu Wata no Mori (ねむねむわたのもり)
- A Watame-themed Minecraft mod/datapack referenced on the seesaawiki, featuring Watame-themed content within Minecraft.

### NHK Radio Appearance
- On January 9, 2023, Watame appeared on NHK Radio, a notable mainstream media appearance for a Hololive VTuber.

## Trivia

- Watame shares a birthday (June 6) with Astel Leda of Holostars 2nd Generation.
- Her hobbies include eating, sleeping, playing, singing, and getting 100% game achievements/trophies.
- She likes potato chips, games, and "wakipai" (underarm sideboob).
- Her special skills include singing, "respawning in 8 seconds," Horn Drill, and putting on a "high" look (gangimari).
- She has a verified YouTube Official Artist Channel and verified Twitter account.
- Her illustrator Fu-mi also designed Akuma Saki (AXEL-V) and Hakushika Iori (Phase-Connect).
- Her greeting is "Konban dododo!" and her farewell is "Otsunomaki~" (a pun on "otsukaresama" + "tsunomaki").
- Representative emote is the RAM emoji, not the sheep — because the ram has horns matching her character design.
