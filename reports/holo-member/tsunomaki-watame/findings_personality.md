# Tsunomaki Watame (角巻わため) - Personality & Character Traits

## Overview
Tsunomaki Watame is a Hololive 4th Generation VTuber who debuted on December 29, 2019. Her character is a sheep bard who loves singing, and her fan name is "Wata-mates" (わためいと). Her representative emoji is the ram (🐏), chosen for its horns distinguishing it from the hornless sheep emoji.

## Voice & Speaking Style
- Uses calm, polite language; gentle disposition
- Greeting: "Konban dododo!" (こんばんどどど！) and "Hoof, hoof, helloof!"
- Farewell: "Otsunomaki~" (おつのまき～)
- Incorporates sheep-like bleats into speech (e.g., "mee mee" sounds)
- Common phrase: "ネタバレめぇめぇ！" (netabare mee mee!) meaning "No spoilers!" in "sheep language"
- Known for a soft, soothing voice ideal for ASMR content
- Catchphrase: "Watame wa warukunai yo ne" (わためは悪くないよね) — "Watame did nothing wrong"

## Personality Traits
- **Gentle & Warm**: Possesses a gentle disposition, uses calm and polite language. Known among peers as warm and friendly.
- **Emotional / Crybaby**: Exceptionally emotional during sentimental game scenes (deaths, marriages) or special streams — often cries openly and needs to compose herself.
- **Earnest & Dedicated**: Takes vocal lessons weekly; does multiple retakes for song recordings when unsatisfied with results.
- **Shy**: Showed shyness during debut when not singing.
- **Graceful Under Pressure**: Rarely shows frustration during gaming; accepts viewer help gracefully when stuck.
- **Playful**: Loves puns and wordplay, reflected in fan name and program naming.

## Singing Ability
- Extensively music-oriented; widely regarded as an excellent singer
- Known for the phrase "Watame's voice heals" (listeners describe her singing as soothing/healing)
- Weekly singing stream: "Watameh Night Fever!!" (WNF) — weekend 2-hour karaoke streams
- Former weekday program: "Watame no Uta" (Watame's Song Corner)
- Dream: To hold a concert at Tokyo Budokan with only original songs
- Held 1st solo live: "Tsunomaki Watame 1st Live 'Watame Night Fever!! in Zepp Tokyo'" (Oct 12, 2021)
- Has released multiple original songs and albums including "Hop Step Sheep" and "WHAT A MESS!!!"

## Notable Talents & Skills (per official profile)
- **Singing** (primary specialty)
- **Respawning in 8 seconds** (in-game reference)
- **Horn Drill** (Pokémon reference)
- **Putting on a gangimari look** (high/energetic expression)
- **Getting 100% achievements/trophies** in games

## Streaming Style & Content
- **Main Content**: Singing streams and comfy/relaxing game streams
- **Late-night streams**: Often streams all night; described as "perfect for watching before bed"
- **Game genres**: A wide variety — ARK, Apex, Minecraft, Splatoon, Mario Kart, horror games, story-driven games (Final Fantasy IX, Hansel and Gretel)
- **"Tsunomaki Rock Paper Scissors"**: A recurring segment
- **"Ganbaru Watame" (がんばるわため)**: A segment where Watame takes on challenges or tasks sent in by fans
- **Reaction to horror**: Moderately weak to horror; shrieks at jumpscares
- **Emotional gaming**: Cries at emotional story moments; chat often jokes with "time for ramen" (comparing crying sounds to slurping ramen)
- **Mic gathering habit**: Known for collecting microphones; often seen with multiple high-quality mics on stream

## Catchphrase Origin
"Watame wa warukunai yo ne" (Watame did nothing wrong) originated from an apology video about previous stream connection issues. It became her iconic catchphrase.
