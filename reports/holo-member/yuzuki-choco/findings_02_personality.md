# Findings: Personality & Characteristics

## Summary
Yuzuki Choco has a sweet, seductive, motherly voice that blurs the line between erotic and maternal. She is often regarded as the "sexy member" of Hololive.

## Key Traits
- **Voice**: Husky, charming adult voice. Known for ASMR streams (though YouTube demonetized her ASMR content around November 2019 due to strict policies, leading to a backup channel).
- **Persona**: Sweet, seductive, motherly — blurs the line between erotic and maternal. Often called "Choco-sensei" and takes on a teacher/nurturing role.
- **Cooking**: One of the best cooks in Hololive. Runs "Choco Cooking" streaming series where she shares recipes with accompanying pictures. Notably, she dislikes cleaning up after cooking.
- **"Physical Examination" Streams**: Hosts collaboration streams where she "examines" other VTubers' character designs in-character.
- **Spoiled Side**: Despite her nurturing persona, she also likes being spoiled herself and asks for praise from viewers.
- **Humor**: Sense of humor can be morbid at times.
- **Uses -sama**: Uses the "-sama" honorific when referring to other VTubers and her viewers, showing great respect.
- **Picky Eater**: Has a reputation for being a picky eater.
- **Sleep Habits**: Famous for her heavy sleeping. Has a record of oversleeping by 4 hours 22 minutes for a planned stream. Has fallen asleep during streams, and once slept for 2.5 days straight.

## ASMR History
- Was one of the early Hololive members to do ASMR content
- YouTube restricted/disallowed ASMR monetization around Nov 2019
- Created a backup channel for ASMR content
- Later recovered monetization on Feb 1, 2020

## Hobbies & Interests
- **ARK: Survival Evolved**: Self-confessed ARK addict with 500+ hours played
- **H-Doujinshi**: Has admitted to purchasing adult doujinshi, even causing her credit card to be frozen once
- **Cat Lover**: Owns a pet cat named Chocola

## Origin Story
- Got interested in Hololive after watching Tokino Sora's cover of "Sakura no Ame"
- This led her to apply and eventually join Hololive

## Streaming Rules
Choco has established streaming rules for her viewers, including:
- Be careful about spoilers
- Do not talk about other streamers in unrelated contexts
- No self-promotion
- Be respectful to everyone
- Enjoy the stream
