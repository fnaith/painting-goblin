# Findings: Official Profile & Character Setting

## Summary
Yuzuki Choco (癒月ちょこ) is a VTuber belonging to Hololive Production, part of Hololive Japan 2nd Generation.

## Key Facts
- **Name**: Yuzuki Choco (癒月ちょこ)
- **Debut**: September 4, 2018
- **Generation**: Hololive Japan 2nd Generation
- **Character Setting**: School nurse at the Underworld Academy (魔界学校の保健医). She is a demon (NOT a succubus, despite common misconceptions). Extremely popular with male students who constantly visit for checkups under false pretenses.
- **Catchphrase**: "Good evening, my cute students! Choc-on!" (ちょっこーん!)
- **Birthday**: February 14 (Valentine's Day)
- **Height**: 165 cm
- **Illustrator**: Masuishi Kinoto (shima_je)
- **Fan Name**: Chocomates
- **Hashtags**: Stream Tag `#癒月診療所`, Fan Art `#しょこらーと`
- **YouTube**: UC1suqwovbL1kzsoaZgFZLKg
- **Twitter**: @yuzukichococh

## Quirks
- Loves sweets and often leaves candy wrappers on her desk at the Underworld Academy, getting scolded for it.
- Frequently called "Choco-sensei" by fans and fellow Hololive members.
