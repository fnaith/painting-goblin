# Findings: Appearance & Outfits

## Summary
Yuzuki Choco is designed as a demon with distinct visual characteristics, illustrated by Masuishi Kinoto. Her design emphasizes the school nurse character concept with a blend of cuteness and sex appeal.

## Physical Design
- **Species**: Demon (not succubus)
- **Horns**: Black demon horns
- **Wings**: Black demon wings
- **Tail**: Long demon tail that wraps around one leg
- **Hair**: Blonde
- **Eyes**: Heterochromia — one green eye and one blue eye
- **Height**: 165 cm
- **Weight**: 43 kg
- **Bust**: H-Cup
- **Distinct Features**: Very large bust, thin waist, wide hips — an exaggerated hourglass figure

## Original Outfit (Debut — Sep 4, 2018)
- Pink top
- Black skirt
- White lab coat (lined with red) — fitting her school nurse character
- Choker with a heart-shaped charm
- Thigh-high boots
