# Findings: Appearance & Outfits

## Summary
Yuzuki Choco is designed as a demon with distinct visual characteristics, illustrated by Masuishi Kinoto.

## Physical Design
- **Species**: Demon (not succubus)
- **Horns**: Black demon horns
- **Wings**: Black demon wings
- **Tail**: Long demon tail that wraps around one leg
- **Hair**: Blonde
- **Eyes**: Heterochromia — one green eye and one blue eye
- **Height**: 165 cm

## Original Outfit
- Pink top
- Black skirt
- White lab coat (lined with red) — fitting her school nurse character
- Choker with a heart-shaped charm

## Alternate Outfits
Over the years, Choco has received multiple alternate costumes including:
- Maid outfit
- Nurse outfit (alternate version)
- New Year's kimono
- Other seasonal/special outfits
