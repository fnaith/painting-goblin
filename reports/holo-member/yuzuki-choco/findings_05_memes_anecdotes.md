# Findings: Memes & Community Anecdotes

## Common Memes and Running Gags

### "Choco-sensei" Mother/Teacher Figure
The most pervasive meme — she is universally referred to as "Choco-sensei" and the teacher/nurse/mother dynamic is a core part of her identity.

### Succubus Misconception
Despite her official setting being a demon (school nurse of the Underworld Academy), she is frequently mistaken for a succubus due to her seductive voice and appearance. This is a common point of correction among fans.

### Sexy Dynamite Body (SDB)
One of her nicknames, "Sexy Dynamite Body" (often abbreviated as SDB), is frequently used in fan discussions to refer to her exaggerated hourglass figure.

### Early 3D Model Quality
Early Hololive 3D models were inconsistent in quality, and Choco's early 3D model was a subject of jokes/complaints among fans. This is part of a broader Hololive 2nd gen meme about early 3D models.

### Handshake Meme with Hakui Koyori
A notable meme image pairs Choco with Hololive member Hakui Koyori in a handshake/greeting pose.

### "Sexy Member" Typecasting
Choco is frequently typecast as the "sexy" member of Hololive, which she plays into but also subverts with her cooking streams, motherly behavior, and occasional morbid humor.

### ASMR Ban
When YouTube restricted ASMR monetization, it became a notable talking point in the community about how unfair the situation was for Choco. She later recovered monetization on Feb 1, 2020.

## Notable Anecdotes

### Oversleeping Legend
- Once overslept by 4 hours 22 minutes for a planned stream
- Has fallen asleep during live streams
- Famously slept for 2.5 days straight at one point

### H-Doujinshi and the Frozen Credit Card
Choco admitted to purchasing adult doujinshi (H-doujinshi) to the point where her credit card was frozen by the bank, believing it was fraudulent activity.

### Pet Cat Chocola
She owns a cat named Chocola (ちょこら), which occasionally appears in her content.

### Melty Kiss Unit
Formed a musical unit "Melty Kiss" with fellow Hololive member Yozora Mel (癒月ちょことは別のメンバー).

### ARK Addiction
She has admitted to being addicted to ARK: Survival Evolved, accumulating over 500 hours of playtime.

### Hololive Origin Story
She became interested in VTubing after watching Tokino Sora's cover of "Sakura no Ame," which led her to apply for Hololive.
