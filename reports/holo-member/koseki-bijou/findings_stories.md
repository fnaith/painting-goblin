## 1. Official Bio / Character Lore

Formed from the crystallization of all forms of human emotion, Koseki Bijou is **"The Jewel of Emotions."** Over years and years, emotions of beauty and filth alike have woven into her under immense pressure, resulting in an unmatched brilliance. Her ancient, mystic brilliance caused strife between the masses of the greedy, who fought over ownership of her. This led to her being imprisoned in secrecy, far from the reach of humans. It seems as though when she encounters people, and particularly their good emotions, her radiance shines even brighter.

**Tagline:** *"I hope you'll feel my radiance!"*

## 2. Personality & Character Traits

- **Bubbly, friendly, easily excited** — described as having a childlike, infectious energy
- **Gen Z meme expert** — extensive knowledge of internet memes; often leaves older genmates bewildered
- **"Mom Collector"** — uses her cuteness to get other members to agree to be her "mom"
- **Remarkably "seiso" (pure/wholesome)** — refuses to swear on stream; literally says "beep" in place of swear words; uses "dang it!" instead of harsher language; asks Pebbles to refrain from vulgarity in chat
  - Chat emote: **BEEP** (all-caps)
- **Unshakeable calm** — remains collected even during frustrating game challenges that would upset other streamers
- **Self-described "gamer"** — loves RPGs, action games, and FromSoftware titles (Dark Souls trilogy, Elden Ring, Sekiro, Armored Core 6); often creates self-imposed challenge runs
- **Known for game modding knowledge** — famously replaced Sans from Undertale with Mori Calliope's model for her Hololive audition

## 3. Memorable Collabs & Moments

### 3.1 The Bury the Light / Devil May Cry Saga
Bijou discovered the *Devil May Cry* series through memes and fan suggestions. She fell in love with the series, especially Vergil and his theme **"Bury the Light"** by Casey Edwards. This escalated to:
- **Casey Edwards (DMC5 composer) acknowledged her** on Twitter/X and visited her stream
- She sang a mostly-unofficial karaoke cover of "Bury the Light" with altered lyrics referencing her personal memes (e.g., "Sticking Out Your Gyatt For Nerizzler")
- Fan editor Ludokano turned it into a music video
- She calls DMC5 "one of the best action games ever made"

### 3.2 Advent Dynamics
As part of hololive English -Advent-, Bijou's relationships with genmates are central to her content:

- **Shiori Novella:** The "archiver" and storyteller of Advent; Bijou often bounces off Shiori's chaotic energy
- **Nerissa Ravencroft:** The group's musician/singer; Bijou and Nerissa have a playful dynamic with frequent teasing. Nerissa is the subject of the "Nerizzler" meme Bijou popularized
- **FUWAMOCO (Fuwawa & Mococo Abyssgard):** The twin demon guard dogs. Bijou has a particularly close relationship with them, often appearing in their "FUWAMOCO Morning" show and participating in their chaotic collabs
- **Unit fan name:** "Adventrix" (coined by the talents themselves)

### 3.3 Cross-Generation Relationships
- Strong friendship with **Hakos Baelz** (Promise) — they share a chaotic, meme-loving energy
- Close to **Ninomae Ina'nis** (Myth) — Ina has drawn official Bijou art
- Interacts frequently with **Justice** gen members (Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame, Gigi Murin, Cecilia Immergreen, Raora Panthera)
- "Mom collecting" targets include various senpai members who dote on her small stature and cute demeanor

### 3.4 Notable Collab Streams
- **Advent full-group collabs** (marked by chaotic, overlapping conversations)
- **#AdventTakeover** — pre-3D debut promotional artworks showing Advent in Japan
- **Advent x Justice motion comics** — "AdVSJus" series
- **hololive English concerts:** Breaking Dimensions, All for One, Serendipity (upcoming 4th concert)
- Group original songs: *Rebellion*, *Sirens*, *Genesis*, *Unchained*, *Breakout*, *What Goes Around*

## 5. Fan Culture & Inside Jokes

### The Moai Emoji 🗿
Bijou's oshi mark is the Moai (🗿). It originates from her character design — she begins streams in "moai form" (a stone statue) before transforming into her jewel form. Pebbles spam 🗿 in chat during stream intros and as a general meme.

### Pebbles
Her fans are called **Pebbles** — a play on her jewel theme (small pieces of a gem). Fan character OCs are called "pebblesonas" (#pebblesona).

### "Bon Bijou!"
Her signature greeting — a portmanteau of French "bonjour" and her name "Bijou" (which means "jewel" in French). Also uses "Kira-kira Koseki!" (sparkle sparkle Koseki).

### "Biboo later~"
Her farewell phrase; derived from her nickname "Biboo" which is so ubiquitous that even fellow Hololive members sometimes forget her real name is Bijou.

### The "Nerizzler" Meme
Originating from her "Bury the Light" parody lyrics ("Sticking Out Your Gyatt For Nerizzler"), this became an inside joke referencing genmate Nerissa Ravencroft.

### Beep Culture
Bijou's refusal to swear, instead saying "beep," has become a defining quirk. The chat emote **BEEP** is used both when she censors herself and as a general reaction.

### Gen Z Meme Lord
She is known for deploying current internet memes that often go over the heads of her Hololive senpai, creating funny "explaining memes to boomers" moments.

### "Mom Collecting"
A recurring bit where Bijou uses her cuteness and small stature to convince other female Hololive members to become her "mom," who then dote on her.

### Height Jokes
At 140cm, she is the shortest in HoloEN. This is a frequent topic of teasing and affectionate jokes about being "smol," similar to Gawr Gura before her.
