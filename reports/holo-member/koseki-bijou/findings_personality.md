## 2. PERSONALITY TRAITS & QUIRKS

### Core Personality
- **Bubbly & Friendly**: Biboo is consistently described as bubbly, warm, and approachable. She radiates genuine positive energy.
- **Easily Excited**: Gets visibly hyped about games, memes, and interactions with chat.
- **Childlike Antics**: Frequently amuses genmates and viewers with silly, playful behavior. Her innocence is endearing rather than immature.
- **Gen Z Meme Queen**: Has extensive, encyclopedic knowledge of Gen Z internet memes, often leaving her slightly-older genmates and senpais baffled.
- **Mom Collector**: Known for using her cuteness to convince other Hololive members to agree to being her "mom." This has become a running gag.
- **Takes Teasing in Stride**: When she blurts out silly jokes, viewers affectionately tease her, and she plays along.

### The "Seiso" (Pure/Wholesome) Reputation
Despite being deeply entrenched in meme culture, Biboo is one of the **most seiso (wholesome/clean) members** of Hololive English:
- **No Swearing**: She consciously refuses to swear on stream. Instead, she literally says **"beep"** in place of curse words — even when reading text that originally contained vulgar language.
- **"Dang it!"** is her go-to expression of frustration rather than harsher language.
- **Calm Under Pressure**: Known for remaining remarkably composed even during frustrating game challenges that would cause other streamers to rage. This contrast (good at games + stays calm) is a hallmark of her brand.
- **Chat Conduct**: She has an unofficial request that Pebbles (her fans) also refrain from vulgarity in chat. One of her chat emotes is the all-caps **"BEEP"** emote.
- **"BEEP"** has become a meme in its own right — whenever she or chat would normally swear, they say "beep."

### Nickname Absorption
- Her nickname **"Biboo"** has become so universally used (and considered to fit her so perfectly) that many people — including fellow Hololive members — genuinely forget her proper given name is Bijou.

## 3. TALENTS & SKILLS

### Gaming (Primary Content)
- **RPG & Action Game Specialist**: Biboo is exceptionally skilled at action games and RPGs. She is a **huge fan of FromSoftware titles**:
  - Has played all three **Dark Souls** games prior to debut
  - Streamed **Elden Ring**, **Armored Core 6: Fires of Rubicon**, **Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice**
  - Notable omission: Had not played **Bloodborne** as of 2023
- **Self-Imposed Challenges**: Often creates optional challenges to make already-difficult games even harder after clearing them normally.
- **Devil May Cry Obsession**: Introduced to DMC through memes and Pebble suggestions, she fell deeply in love with the series, especially:
  - The character **Vergil** (she is a massive Vergil fan)
  - The song **"Bury the Light"** (Vergil's DMC5 theme)
  - She sang a meme karaoke cover with custom lyrics (e.g., "Sticking Out Your Gyatt For Nerizzler")
  - Composer **Casey Edwards** acknowledged her on Twitter/X and visited her stream
  - She considers **Devil May Cry 5** one of the best action games ever made
- **Game Modding Knowledge**: Demonstrated technical modding skill — e.g., replaced Sans from Undertale with **Mori Calliope** for her Hololive audition.

### Singing & Music
- Regular **karaoke streams**
- **Original songs**: "Freaky Deaky Love," "Unchained," "What Goes Around," "Breakout," "ROCK IN!," "Prisoner," "Genesis," "All for One," "Prism Magic"
- **Group songs** with Advent: "Rebellion," "Sirens," "Breaking Dimensions," "Our Bright Parade"
- Known for the meme "Bury the Light" karaoke cover
- Participated in Hololive concerts

### Other Notable Abilities
- **Deep Meme Knowledge**: Practically a librarian of internet culture
- **Cute/Charming Persona**: Masterful at leveraging her adorable persona for comedic and social effect (e.g., mom collecting)
- **Dual Language Comfort**: Primarily streams in English with occasional Japanese phrases (notably her greeting "Kira-kira Koseki!")

## 4. STREAMING STYLE & CONTENT TYPES

### Typical Stream Format
1. **Opening**: Often starts stream in **"moai form"** (just the Moai 🗿 face on screen)
2. **Transformation Intro**: A short "magical girl"-style animation transforms her from moai statue to full Live2D model
3. **Greeting**: "Kira-kira Koseki!" (キラキラ コセキ!) — "Kira-kira" being Japanese onomatopoeia for sparkling/shining
4. **Introduction**: "Bon Bijou! I am Koseki Bijou, sparkling gem of Hololive English Advent!" — "Bon Bijou" is a pun on French "bonjour" (good day) and her name "Bijou" (French for "jewel")
5. **Content**: Gaming, chatting, karaoke, or collab
6. **Sign-off**: "Biboo later~" or "Byebye!" with her famous big smile

### Content Categories
- **Action Game Playthroughs**: FromSoftware games, Devil May Cry series, other challenging action titles
- **RPG Streams**: Deep RPG gameplay with character build discussions
- **Karaoke Streams**: Singing covers, original songs, and meme songs
- **Chatting / Just Chatting**: Interacting with Pebbles, sharing memes, reacting
- **Collabs**: With fellow Advent members (Shiori Novella, Nerissa Ravencroft, FUWAMOCO) and other Hololive talent
- **Challenge Runs**: Self-imposed difficulty challenges on already-cleared games
- **New Game First Impressions**: Trying games suggested by Pebbles

### Chat Interaction Style
- Very engaged with chat
- Reads and reacts to memes and jokes in real-time
- Encourages wholesome chat atmosphere (beep instead of swear)
- Spam 🗿 emote during waiting/transition screens is a community ritual

## 7. LORE / CHARACTER CONCEPT

### Official Bio (Canon)
> *"Formed from the crystallization of all forms of human emotion, Koseki Bijou is 'The Jewel of Emotions.' Over years and years, emotions of beauty and filth alike have woven into her under immense pressure, resulting in an unmatched brilliance. Her ancient, mystic brilliance caused strife between the masses of the greedy, who fought over ownership of her. This led to her being imprisoned in secrecy, far from the reach of humans. It seems as though when she encounters people, and particularly their good emotions, her radiance shines even brighter."*

### Key Lore Elements for Game Development
- She is an **ancient, sentient gemstone** formed from crystallized human emotions
- Was **imprisoned** due to conflicts over her power/beauty
- Gains strength from **positive emotions** (especially good feelings from people)
- Her "radiance" / glow intensifies with positive emotional energy
- **Age is listed as "1" (hidden)** — suggesting she is ageless/ancient
- The **moai statue** (🗿) is her "default" or dormant form before transforming into her humanoid/Live2D appearance
- Theme of **imprisonment vs. freedom** runs through her backstory
