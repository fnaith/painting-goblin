## 2. CHARACTER LORE & BIOGRAPHY

**Official bio from hololivepro.com:**

> *"Formed from the crystallization of all forms of human emotion, Koseki Bijou is 'The Jewel of Emotions.' Over years and years, emotions of beauty and filth alike have woven into her under immense pressure, resulting in an unmatched brilliance. Her ancient, mystic brilliance caused strife between the masses of the greedy, who fought over ownership of her. This led to her being imprisoned in secrecy, far from the reach of humans. It seems as though when she encounters people, and particularly their good emotions, her radiance shines even brighter."*

**Key lore points for game development:**
- **Origin:** A crystallized being formed from all human emotions (both positive and negative) compressed over immense timespans.
- **Prisoner backstory:** Her brilliance sparked conflict among humans who fought to possess her. She was subsequently imprisoned in secret to keep her away from humanity. This "imprisoned gem" theme is core to her identity.
- **Emotion-powered:** Her radiance (and likely her power) intensifies when she encounters people and their positive emotions. This suggests a game mechanic around emotional energy or "radiance."
- **Tagline identity:** "The Jewel of Emotions" — every aspect of her design and lore orbits this concept.

## 3. VISUAL DESIGN & APPEARANCE

**Character design elements (from official artwork):**

- **Theme:** Jewel/Gem — specifically a multifaceted cut jewel with mystical properties.
- **Color palette:** Deep purples, blues, and pinks with translucent/glass-like accents. Her outfit features crystalline motifs.
- **Hair:** Long, flowing dark hair with jewel-toned highlights (blue/purple), adorned with gem-like accessories.
- **Outfit:** A gothic/jewel-inspired dress with layered fabrics, gemstone embellishments, translucent crystal-like elements, and ornate jewelry.
- **Key accessory:** Large gem/gems embedded in her outfit and hair ornaments. Her design emphasizes faceted, cut-stone shapes.
- **Height:** Notably short at 140 cm (one of the shortest in Hololive), contributing to a cute/compact silhouette.

**Visual motifs to emphasize in fan games:**
- Crystals, gems, faceted shapes, prismatic light effects
- Deep jewel tones (amethyst, sapphire, magenta)
- Translucency and refraction/reflection effects
- Gothic/ornate framing around a cute core

## 9. NOTES FOR FAN GAME DEVELOPMENT

**Potential game design hooks from lore:**
- **Emotion mechanic:** Her power derives from emotions — consider a system where positive/negative emotional states affect abilities.
- **Radiance/brilliance:** A "radiance meter" that grows when interacting positively with NPCs/players.
- **Crystal/gem theming:** Environments, particles, UI elements, and collectibles can all be gem/crystal-themed.
- **Imprisonment origin:** An opening narrative hook — she begins imprisoned/isolated and must be freed.
