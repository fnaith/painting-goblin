# Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame — Identity Research Findings

## Official Status

**Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame is an official Hololive Production talent.** She is a member of **hololive English (EN)**, specifically the fourth English generation known as **-Justice-**, which debuted in June 2024.

She is NOT a fan creation or unofficial character. Her official profile is hosted on the Hololive Production website, and she is managed by Cover Corporation.

## Design Elements

- **Theme:** Scarlet Queen / Harbinger of Order
- **Key visual traits:** Red eyes, long red hair with blue-ish undertones, a prominent ahoge, a blue flame atop her chest
- **Outfit:** Black-and-white attire with belts, red pauldrons, removable sleeves. Wields a large black-and-red sword named "Thorn" engraved with scales (symbol of justice)
- **Lore origin:** Hails from "Great Exardia" (likely a reference to Great Britain). She is human. Despite the nickname "The Scarlet Queen," she is not descended from royalty.
- **Role within lore:** Leader of Justice, dispatched to apprehend the fugitive Advent generation. Her base is "The Lookout" headquarters, equipped with "The Panscope" (a multidimensional telescope).
- **3D model debuted:** August 1, 2025
- **New Year costume:** January 1, 2026 (red kimono with rose pattern, sword-shaped kogai, fan/microphone/cocktail accessories)

## Clarification on "Upload Date vs Debut Date"

The official site lists the "Debut Stream" as **June 22, 2024** (in JST, the stream started late June 21 PDT). The YouTube channel was created on **April 3, 2024**. The generation was announced on **June 18, 2024**. The thumbnail filename pattern (`2023/07/`) in the official site's image URLs refers to the site's asset upload date, NOT the talent's debut year.
