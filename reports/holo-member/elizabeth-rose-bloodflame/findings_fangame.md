## Age of Advent — Precedent Analysis

- **Published:** Under holo indie label (free)
- **Format:** Collection of 4 mini-games, one per Advent member
- **Reception:** Well-received by community; praised for capturing each member's personality
- **Design approach:**
  - Each mini-game is simple (single mechanic)
  - Heavy on character flavor (voice lines, references, inside jokes)
  - Pixel art / retro aesthetic
  - Short playtime per game (5-15 minutes)
- **Takeaway for Justice fan game:** A similar collection format could work for Justice (Elizabeth, Gigi, Cecilia, Raora). Elizabeth's mini-game could focus on singing/rhythm mechanics, castle defense, or a hybrid of both.
- **Alternative:** A single, larger game starring Elizabeth as the protagonist, with the other Justice members as supporting characters/party members
