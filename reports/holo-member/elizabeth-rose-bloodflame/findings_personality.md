# Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame — Personality & Streaming Profile

## Overview

Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame (エリザベス・ローズ・ブラッドフレイム) is a VTuber under **hololive English -Justice-**, the second generation of the hololive EN "Justice" unit. She debuted on **June 21, 2024** as the first member of her generation. Her character is styled as "The Scarlet Queen" — a regal, disciplined leader who coordinates the Justice unit.

**Debut Date:** June 22, 2024
**Birthday:** April 25
**Height:** 171 cm (5'7")
**Unit:** hololive English -Justice-

## Personality Traits & Characteristics

### Official Bio Description
> *"Let my voice be your strength."*
> *"The Scarlet Queen, Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame paves the way for the world and all of its people. Not only does she stand at the forefront during missions with her physical prowess, but she also captures the hearts of all around her with her beautiful singing voice. She is highly self-disciplined and a bit too hard on herself, but, perhaps due to her culture's emphasis on politeness and manners, she tends to go a bit soft on those around her. As a result, she spends her days stressed out with her work coordinating Justice, but it seems like there's nothing a nice nap in a comfy bed can't fix."*

## Streaming Style & Content Themes

### Content Categories
- **Singing streams** — Her singing voice is a core part of her identity ("captures hearts with her beautiful singing voice"); she has released original songs including "ABOVE BELOW," "RENEGADE," "Odyssey," "START AGAIN," "All for One," "Serendipity" (2026/06/20), and "SUPERNOVA SUPER GIRL" (2026/06/29). Notably, her karaoke streams are often labeled "UNARCHIVED" and can run 3+ hours; members frequently remind viewers not to miss them as they will not be saved
- **ASMR/keyboard ASMR** — She has hosted ASMR recital streams (e.g., "KEYBOARD ASMR RECITAL") and pillow talk (枕邊談話) ASMR sessions
- **Gaming streams** — Plays a variety of games; participated in ENigmatic Recollection (TTRPG-style narrative events)
- **Karaoke/cover songs** — Has released covers including "OTONABLUE" (ATARASHII GAKKO!) and "I Wanna Be Your Slave" (Måneskin) with Justice members
- **Chatting/Just Chatting** — Relaxed conversational streams
- **Collaboration streams** — Regularly collabs with other Justice members (Gigi Murin, Cecilia Immergreen, Raora Panthera) and Advent members (especially Nerissa Ravencroft)
- **3D showcases** — Participated in hololive EN 3D showcase events

### Audio & Vocal Style
- Uses a **Neumann U 87** microphone as her primary recording mic (upgraded from earlier budget mics like a Logitech headset, Blue Snowball, and Blue Spark)
- Known for her **beautiful singing voice** — it is explicitly highlighted as a defining trait in her official bio
- Has a habit of **humming** during streams

### Regal Persona
- Maintains a royal, queenly persona
- Speaks in a refined, theatrical register
- Incorporates dramatic laughter ("OH- HOHOHOHO~!")
- Uses poetic/Shakespearean flourishes (e.g., "Parting is such sweet sorrow")

## Trivia

- Her first microphone was a Logitech headset held together with duct tape; she later used a Blue Snowball, then a Blue Spark, before upgrading to a Neumann U 87 (which she financed "halfsies" with her grandfather and a credit card)
- She is a talented **voice mimic** and occasionally uses this for pranks on other talents
- She tends to **troll** both hololive and Holostars members, revealing a playful side beneath the regal exterior
- Despite her queenly persona, she is described as going **"soft" on those around her** due to her cultural emphasis on politeness
- She is a **loyal supporter** of the other Justice members and has a "not-so-secret appreciation" of Advent member Nerissa Ravencroft
