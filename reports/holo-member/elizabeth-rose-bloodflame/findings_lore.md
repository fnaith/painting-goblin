# Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame — Lore & Backstory Research

## Overview

Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame (エリザベス・ローズ・ブラッドフレイム) is a VTuber under **hololive English -Justice-**, the fourth generation of Hololive English (fifth counting Project: HOPE). She debuted on **June 21, 2024** as the first member of Justice to debut, followed by Gigi Murin, Cecilia Immergreen, and Raora Panthera.

- **Debut Date:** June 21, 2024
- **Birthday:** April 25
- **Height:** 171 cm (5'7")
- **Unit:** hololive English -Justice-
- **Illustrator:** MEPHISTO
- **Live2D Modeling:** Zearu
- **Fan Name:** Rosarians
- **Oshi Mark/Emoji:** 💄 (lipstick)
- **Nicknames:** The Scarlet Queen, Liz, ERB
- **Based in:** Great Britain (first Hololive member known to stream from the UK)

## Official Character Lore / Backstory

**Tagline:** *"Let my voice be your strength."*

Elizabeth Rose Bloodflame is known as **"The Scarlet Queen."** She paves the way for the world and all of its people. She stands at the forefront during missions with her physical prowess and captures the hearts of all around her with her beautiful singing voice.

**Personality traits from official bio:**
- Highly self-disciplined and a bit too hard on herself
- Her culture's emphasis on politeness and manners makes her go soft on those around her
- Spends her days stressed out with work coordinating Justice
- Finds solace in a nice nap in a comfy bed

### Debut

She was the first member of Justice to debut on June 21, 2024. The rest of Justice (Gigi Murin, Cecilia Immergreen, Raora Panthera) debuted shortly after across June 21–22, 2024.

## Memorable Moments & Anecdotes

### Microphone History (Trivia)
Her first ever microphone was an unspecified Logitech headset that was "duct taped a lot." She later moved onto a Blue Snowball, followed by a Blue Spark. She finally upgraded to a Neumann U 87 microphone, which she still currently uses for both talking and recording. To afford the U 87 (a much more expensive microphone), she "went halfsies" with money from her grandad and paid the rest with a credit card. (Source: HOLOMELO RADIO Episode 5)

### Debut Song Success
Her debut group original song **"ABOVE BELOW"** (with Gigi Murin, Cecilia Immergreen, and Raora Panthera) reached:
- 1 Million views on YouTube by July 9, 2024 (~2 weeks after release)
- 2 Million views by August 21, 2024
- 3 Million views by October 24, 2024
- 4 Million views by January 23, 2025

### Far East Remix
A remix of "ABOVE BELOW" was released on January 2, 2025 using electric guitar and traditional Japanese instruments. The name "Far East Remix" is a reference to Raora Panthera calling Japan "the Far East."

### 3D Showcase
Justice received its 3D showcase announced in June 2025, with Elizabeth participating in 3D concerts including hololive English 3rd Concert "All for One" and 4th Concert "Serendipity."

### Queen's Speech Tradition
On Christmas Day, Elizabeth uploads a short video as a "Queen's Speech," inspired by the British royal tradition of the monarch's annual Christmas message. This demonstrates her British cultural identity and serves as an annual fan tradition.

## Unique Characteristics for Fan Game Development

- **Visual motifs:** Roses, flames, scarlet/red color scheme, lipstick emoji motif, crown/queen imagery
- **Armor/combat aesthetic:** Her design includes armored elements matching the "Harbinger of Order" theme
- **British mannerisms:** Formal speech patterns, politeness, references to British culture; streams in GMT
- **Dramatic flair:** Her introduction speech ("OH- HOHOHOHO~! Roses are red...") is highly theatrical
- **Thematic role:** Leader of Justice, stands for order and righteousness
- **Cockney dialect:** British RP speaker who learned Cockney for roleplay; recognizable by dropped 'h' and "BO-OH-O-WO-ER" pronunciation of "bottle of water"
- **Queen's Speech tradition:** Annual Christmas video tradition inspired by the British monarchy
- **Voice talent:** Skilled singer with voice mimicry abilities — could translate into in-game voicing or powers
- **Fan mascot:** A "Rosarian" mascot design exists, created by Elizabeth herself and illustrated by badger yekun, available in both unarmored and armored versions
- **Relaxation motif:** Naps in comfy beds as a stress relief — a potential idle animation or recovery mechanic
