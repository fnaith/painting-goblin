### Official Lore / Setting

As the executive officer of Secret Society holoX, Lui serves as holoX's point of contact and the one in charge of external affairs. She is the **de facto leader** of the group, handling matters that the founder (La+ Darknesss) cannot. While she may seem aloof and standoffish at first glance, she is a compassionate person who cares deeply for her subordinates. Unfortunately, she is also rather prone to screwing up in crucial moments (a trait referred to in the community as "PON").

## 2. Character Persona & Personality Traits

### Archetype: The "Onee-san" (Big Sister) Type

Lui is widely recognized as the **mature, cool, "onee-san" figure** of holoX. She projects an image of competence, elegance, and calm authority, but this is frequently undercut by her clumsy, airheaded side (PON). This gap moe (contrast between cool exterior and cute flaws) is central to her character appeal.

### Key Personality Traits

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cool & Mature** | Carries herself with a poised, composed demeanor. Often acts as the responsible adult in holoX. |
| **Compassionate / Maternal** | Genuinely cares for her holoX "subordinates" and looks after them. Described as having a "warmth" to her personality. |
| **PON / Airhead** | Frequently makes mistakes, drops things (especially water on her keyboard), or spaces out at crucial moments. This "gap" is endearing to fans. |
| **Sarcastic / Dry Wit** | Known for sharp, sarcastic remarks. When she tells a bad joke, the air freezes -- she will react with "Cold!" or "Did someone turn off the heating?" |
| **Ad-lib Master** | Skilled at coming up with "hellish adlib" stream ideas (unexpected, chaotic segments). Known for her creativity in variety streams. |
| **Hardworking** | Expressed a desire to become a multifaceted idol and to bring smiles and warmth to everyone. |

### Gap Moe Summary

The core of Lui's appeal lies in the contrast between:
- **Cool, collected executive secretary** vs. **clumsy, PON airhead**
- **Mature onee-san** vs. **person who drops water on her keyboard**
- **Sharp, sarcastic wit** vs. **bad jokes that freeze the room**

## 3. Speech Patterns, Catchphrases & Greetings

### Greeting (Start of Stream)

| Phrase | Japanese | Meaning / Notes |
|---|---|---|

### Farewell (End of Stream)

| Phrase | Japanese | Meaning / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **"I take your Luive."** | "Otsuluilui" (おつるいるい) | A pun combining "Otsukaresama" (good work) with her name. |

### Common Phrases & Verbal Tics

| Phrase | Meaning / Context |
|---|---|
| **"Did you xx, if I'm not mistakane?"** | "xx shitakane?" -- A punny phrasing she uses for rhetorical questions. |
| **"Hype Luivels rising!"** | "Takamattekita!" -- Used when the stream/excitement is ramping up. |
| **"PON"** | Self-deprecating term for her airheaded mistakes. |
| **"K Time"** | "Klarity mode" -- when she enters a focused, sharp-witted state. |
| **"Cold!" / "Uh..." / "Wha?" / "Did someone turn off the heating?"** | Her reaction after telling a bad joke that "freezes" the room. |
| **"Ko! ☆"** | The sparkle/cool sign-off she uses after saying something in a cool voice. |

### Speech Style

- Uses polite but warm Japanese (keigo mixed with casual affection).
- Frequently makes puns incorporating her own name ("Lui" / "rui").
- Known for a somewhat low, calm voice that contrasts with higher-pitched members.
- Tends to narrate her own failures with dry, self-aware humor.
