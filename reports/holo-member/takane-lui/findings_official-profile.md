## Official Lore / Character Setting

Takane Lui is the **executive officer (secretary)** of **Secret Society holoX**. She serves as the organization's point of contact and is in charge of external affairs, effectively acting as the de facto leader who handles matters the founder (La+ Darknesss) cannot.

**Personality description from the official site:**
> "While she may seem aloof and standoffish at first glance, she is a compassionate person who cares a lot for her subordinates. Unfortunately, she is also rather prone to screwing up in crucial moments."

Her design theme revolves around a **black-and-red color scheme** with a stylish office-lady aesthetic — a fitted black dress, long black gloves, and thigh-high boots. She has long, straight black hair with a distinctive red streak, and her design incorporates subtle visual motifs of elegance, mystery, and professional composure.
