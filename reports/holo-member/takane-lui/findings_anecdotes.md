## 2. Catchphrases & Signature Sayings

Lui has a highly distinctive verbal tic built around puns on her own surname (Takane / "kane" = "ka ne?" question suffix) and name (Rui / "rui" = "rui" sound in various words):

| Phrase | Meaning / Context |
|---|---|
| **"Did I Luive you waiting!?" (Mattakane?)** | Start-of-stream greeting. Pun on "mattakane?" (did you wait?) |
| **"I take your Luive." (Otsuluilui)** | End-of-stream greeting. Pun on "otsurui" (otsukaresama) + Lui |
| **"Did you xx, if I'm not mistakane?" (xx shitakane?)** | General questioning tic |
| **"Hype Luivels rising!" (Takamattekita!)** | Excitement/buildup phrase |
| **"K Time"** | When she enters "Klarity mode" -- focused/analytical state |
| **Ko! ☆** | Sparkle sign-off after saying something in a cool voice |
| **"Don't drop your water."** | Self-referential joke -- she frequently drops water on her keyboard |
| **"Cold! / Uh... / Wha? / Did someone turn off the heating?"** | Self-deprecating reaction when she cracks a bad joke that freezes the room |

Lui debuted as the second member of holoX (after La+ Darknesss on Nov 26). Her introduction video established her as the cool, collected executive officer -- a persona that would quickly be subverted by her clumsy ("PON") tendencies.

Her 3D model was revealed, showcasing her full-body design in motion for the first time. The stream was titled "This is the trajectory of my growth."

## 5. Personality, Quirks & Running Gags

### 5.1 The "PON" (Airhead) Gap Moe
Lui's primary character trait is the **gap between her cool executive persona and her frequent clumsy mistakes ("PON")**. The official bio itself states she "is also rather prone to screwing up in crucial moments." This is her defining running gag -- the competent-looking leader who fumbles at key moments.

### 5.2 Keyboard Water Incidents
Lui has a well-known habit of dropping water on her keyboard during streams. It has happened enough times that "Don't drop your water" became an official catchphrase documented on her profile. This is a staple community inside joke.

### 5.3 Dad Jokes / "Cold" Jokes
She frequently makes bad puns or jokes that land with silence, prompting her to dramatically react with "Cold! / Uh... / Wha? / Did someone turn off the heating?" -- a meta-joke about her own joke-telling ability.

### 5.4 The Cool Voice --> "Ko! ☆"
She will sometimes switch to an intentionally cool, low voice for a line, then immediately undercut it with a sparkly "Ko! ☆" sign-off. This has become a signature mannerism.

### 5.5 "K Time" / Klarity Mode
When she shifts into a focused, analytical state during problem-solving or gaming, chat calls it "K Time." It represents her genuine competence shining through the PON persona.

### 5.6 HawkEye
Listed as a "special skill" -- she is observant and has good awareness when she isn't in PON mode. Also used as a nickname/character trait.

### 5.7 Onee-san (Big Sister) Energy
Fans and fellow members refer to her as **Lui-nee (ルイ姉)**. She projects mature, big-sister energy toward her gen-mates and kouhai, consistent with her role as the responsible executive officer.

## 6. Fan Community & Inside Jokes

### 6.1 Lui-tomo (ルイ友)
Her fanbase is called "Lui-tomo" -- a portmanteau of Lui and "tomo" (friend). She also refers to them as "Birds of my feather." The community is known for being relatively tight-knit and supportive, reflecting Lui's more chill streaming style.

### 6.2 The Wilted Rose (🥀)
Lui's oshi mark is a wilted rose. She has explained that she doesn't consider herself a "flower blooming in brilliance yet," but hopes to gain confidence to hold her head up high someday. The rose also fits the holoX "secret society" aesthetic.

### 6.3 Name-Searching (Namesearching)
Lui has listed "namesearching" (searching for her own name on social media) as a hobby. She actively monitors fan discussions, creating a close feedback loop with the community.

### 6.4 Pun-Based Community Language
Much of Lui's fandom communication revolves around her signature pun tics. Fans will intentionally structure sentences to play along with the "-kane?" and "-rui" patterns. The sheer volume of documented catchphrases on her official profile (more than most Hololive members) underscores how central wordplay is to her brand.

### 6.5 Chill Stream Atmosphere
Compared to the high-energy chaos of some holoX members, Lui's streams (tagged `#たかねの見物`) tend toward a more relaxed, chatty vibe. She positions herself as background company for meals or sleepless nights: "Why not come to see Takane Lui on nights you can't sleep, or you need something to watch during your meal?"

### 6.6 HoloX Unit Pride
As the executive officer who handles external affairs, Lui is often the one to coordinate and anchor holoX group activities. Fans see her as the glue holding the generation together, which has fostered strong unit loyalty within her fandom.

### 6.7 Reddit Moderation
Lui is one of the moderators of the official Hololive subreddit, giving her a unique direct presence in the English-speaking fan community.
