# Anya Melfissa — Personality & Style Research

## 1. Personality Description

Anya Melfissa is the second member of Hololive Indonesia 2nd Generation (also called "Holoro"). Her character is an anthropomorphized Indonesian Kris (keris) dagger, awakened into human form by a master's ritual.

**Core personality traits:**
- **Introverted & sleepy** — described as an "introverted, sleepy, calm yandere dagger." Her hobbies include sleeping and playing games.
- **Apathetic by nature** — official bio states: "Living mostly alone for so long has made her tend to be apathetic towards things and people she's uninterested in... Her base personality is actually apathetic, especially towards humans."
- **Childlike & passionate** — when discussing things she likes, she becomes very enthusiastic and childlike.
- **Smug & sadistic** — described as a "smug sadistic loli." During horror game streams, fans often say "the scariest part of the stream is Anya herself." She is notably hard to scare.
- **Nervous mumbler** — tends to mumble and speak less than other Holoro members due to nervousness while streaming.
- **Demeaning humor** — has a surprisingly sharp, demeaning sense of humor that contrasts with her initial shy/insecure first impression.
- **Massive otaku** — loves gacha games (especially Fate/Grand Order), Vocaloid music, anime, and visual novels.

**Fan-name:** **Melfriends** — combination of "Melfissa" and "friends." She chose this because she wants to be friends with viewers.

**Oshi mark / Emoji:** 🍂 (fallen leaves) — Autumn is her favorite season because, in her words, "it's the best season to sleep during."

## 3. Streaming Content & Style

**Preferred game genres:**
- **Horror games** — her specialty. Known for being unflappable; the scariest thing in a horror stream is her reaction (or lack thereof).
- **Shooting/survival games** — Rust, Raft, Half-Life 2.
- **Gacha games** — particularly Fate/Grand Order (FGO).

**Streaming mannerisms:**
- Quiet, mumbly, and nervous during solo streams.
- Can be smug and sadistic during gameplay, especially horror.
- Usually reads Superchats and donations at the end of streams.
- Collab stream Superchats are read during a later solo stream.

**Music:**
- Has released original songs: "id:entity voices" (2021, collab with Holoro), "HI:STREET TIME" (2024), "Senandung Jiwa" (2024), "Juara Khatulistiwa" (2025).
- Has produced solo covers of Vocaloid songs (Gira Gira, Phony, Marshall Maximizer, Schema, DOKUZU).
- Participated in Hololive ID group covers and the cross-branch "Bad ∞ End ∞ Night" cover.

## 4. Notable Interactions with Other Members

**Hololive ID 2nd Gen (Holoro) — Ollie & Reine:**

- Sang "Kibun Jojo↑↑" as a Holoro trio cover.
- Collab regularly in ID group activities.

**Ninomae Ina'nis (EN):**

**Vestia Zeta (ID 3rd Gen):**
- Collaborated on the "Slow Downer" cover together.

**Cross-branch collaborations:**
- Participated in "Bad ∞ End ∞ Night" with EN (Calliope, Ina, Kronii) and ID members.
- Generally collabs with ID and JP members most frequently. When collabing with EN, she is usually invited by the EN member.

## 5. Language Abilities

- **Indonesian:** Native / primary language. Streams primarily in Indonesian.
- **Japanese:** Strong — better than her English. When speaking with EN members who can speak Japanese, she defaults to Japanese.
- **English:** Limited but functional. Can understand and speak some English, but less comfortable than Japanese.

Her stream descriptions are maintained in three languages (Indonesian, English, Japanese), and she may deliver her introduction in all three versions during a single stream.
