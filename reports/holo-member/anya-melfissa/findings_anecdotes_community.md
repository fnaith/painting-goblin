# Anya Melfissa — Research Findings: Anecdotes & Community Culture

## 1. Lore & Character Identity

Anya Melfissa is a member of **hololive Indonesia 2nd Generation** (unit name: "Holoro"). She debuted on **December 5, 2020**. Her character is an ancient Indonesian dagger known as a **Keris (Kris)**, granted human form through a mystical ritual by her master. She is 147 cm tall, with a birthday of March 12. Her fanmark is the fallen leaf emoji (🍂), chosen because autumn is her favorite season for sleeping.

Her official bio describes her as apathetic and sleepy by default, but childlike and passionate when discussing things she likes. Her fan name is **Melfriends** (a portmanteau of "Melfissa" and "friend"). Her art hashtag is **#anyatelier** and stream hashtag is **#Liveissa**.

## 3. Fan Community Culture & Inside Jokes

### Fan Name & Catchphrases
- **Melfriends** — Official fan name
- **Otsukurisu (おつくりす)** / **Otsukeris** — A portmanteau of "otsukaresama" and "keris," used as both a farewell greeting from Anya and a response from fans
- **Wake up, Anya!** — A common waiting room message referencing her lore-accurate love of sleeping

### Personality-Driven Community Dynamics
- Anya is known as a **"calm yandere dagger"** — a contrast to her genmates Ollie (hyper zombie) and Reine (elegant peacock)
- She tends to mumble and speak less due to nervousness, but has a **smug, sadistic side** that fans love
- During horror game streams, fans often joke that **"the scariest part of the stream is Anya herself"** — she is notably hard to scare
- She has a **demeaning sense of humor** that contrasts with her initially shy demeanor

### Fanmark & Aesthetics
- The 🍂 (fallen leaf) emoji is her symbol, tied to autumn being her favorite season for sleeping
- Her membership area is called **"Melhomies Den"**
- Stream rules are posted in Indonesian, English, and Japanese — reflecting her trilingual community

### Community Interaction Style
- She reads superchats at the end of streams (not during), a habit established early
- She is known for being an avid gacha gamer (especially FGO), Vocaloid fan, anime fan, and visual novel enthusiast
- Her English was initially weaker than her Japanese, so she often speaks Japanese when collabing with EN members who know Japanese

### Notable Fan Running Gags
- Her Q&A on the official site includes the memorable exchange:
  - **Q:** How old are you?
  - **A:** Yes.
- Another classic:
  - **Q:** Ummm... waif-
  - **A:** Friends. Know your place, dear.
- **"Toast?" — "Toast"** is an exchange from her official Q&A that became a minor meme

## 6. Summary of Key Trivia

- Shares the same illustrator (Uekura Eku) with VTubers Luca Polka, Korone Pochi, Sawaki Yuko, and Tenteru
- Shares the same Live2D modeler with **Gawr Gura**
- Her name "Melfissa" is derived from "Melfis" (related to the keris/dagger theme)
- She is a huge **Fate/Grand Order** gacha player and Vocaloid enthusiast
- Her channel art and membership badges feature autumn leaf and dagger motifs
- She provided her own **English translation** for her cover of "Phony"
- Alongside Pavolia Reine, she is one of only two ID members whose name letters aren't used as membership emotes
- She is a **YouTube verified** creator
