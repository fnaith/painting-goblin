# Anya Melfissa — Notable Memes & Viral Clips Research

## 1. Classic Memes & Running Gags

### "The Scariest Part of the Stream is Anya Herself"
When Anya plays horror games, she is famously **unflappable** — she rarely gets scared. Fans quickly turned this into a meme: during horror game streams (e.g. *Mad Father*), viewers joke that the scariest thing in the stream is not the game's monsters, but Anya's own smug/sadistic reactions (or total lack of fear). She is one of the hardest VTubers to scare in all of hololive.

**Source context:** Her *Mad Father* playthrough generated comments about her sadistic commentary toward the game's events, cementing the "yandere dagger" image.

### Official Q&A Memes
Anya's official hololive profile Q&A section is a goldmine of memes:

| Q | A | Meme Status |
|---|---|---|
| "How old are you?" | "Yes." | Widely shared as a classic dodging technique |
| "Ummm... waif-" | "Friends. Know your place, dear." | Famous rejection of being called a waifu |
| "Toast?" | "Toast." | Became a minor copypasta / inside joke |

### "Wake Up, Anya!"
A common waiting-room spam before her streams begin. Originates from her lore-accurate hobby of sleeping. Fans use it as a playful greeting to "wake her up" for the stream.

### "Otsukurisu!" / "Otsukeris"
Her signature farewell — a portmanteau of Japanese *otsukaresama* (辛苦了) + *keris* (her dagger identity). Fans reply with the same phrase, making it a mutual sign-off between Anya and Melfriends.

## 2. Notable Stream Events & Clips

### Mad Father Playthrough (Yandere Awakening)
During her playthrough of *Mad Father*, Anya made notably sadistic comments about the game's events (e.g. expressing enjoyment at disturbing situations). This stream is frequently cited as the moment fans began labeling her a "calm yandere" and where the "scariest part of the stream is Anya herself" meme solidified.

- Stream: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HMJU9h1gmdw

### Demeaning Humor Highlights
- **Called Ollie a "cutting board"** (flat chest joke) during a post-debut collab talk.
- **Height comparisons** — during her debut, she listed her height alongside Gura, Shion, and Rushia as a running short-statured members joke.

### First Collab With Gura
Anya was **Gawr Gura's first collab with a kouhai (junior member)**. This is notable because they share the same Live2D modeler (深海潮 / Shin Umiushi), creating a "modeler-sister" bond between EN's first generation and ID's second generation.

### "RIP PC-kun"
Some of Anya's streams suffer from lag or technical issues. Fans coined the phrase **"RIP PC-kun"** as a running joke whenever her computer struggles during a stream.

### First Non-Holoro Collab With Ina
Her first collab outside of Holoro was with **Ninomae Ina'nis**, playing *Unravel Two* — a game Ina herself recommended. This collab is often referenced as the start of their senpai-kouhai relationship.

## 4. Notable Fan Interactions

### Superchat Reading Style
Anya reads Superchats and donations **at the end of streams, not during** — an established habit since early streaming. For collab streams, Superchats are deferred to a later solo stream. This became a known community norm.

### Multilingual Community Dynamics
- Stream rules are posted in **Indonesian, English, and Japanese** reflecting her trilingual audience.
- When collabing with EN members who speak Japanese, she defaults to Japanese (her Japanese is stronger than her English).
- She provided her own **English translation** for her cover of "Phony" (フォニイ).
