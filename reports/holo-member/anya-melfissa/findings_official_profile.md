# Anya Melfissa — Official Profile & Lore Research

## 1. Debut Date & Generation

- **Debut Date:** December 5, 2020
- **Generation:** Hololive Indonesia 2nd Generation (unit name: **holoro**)
- Branch: Hololive Indonesia
- Generation-mates: Kureiji Ollie, Pavolia Reine

## 2. Character Design Theme

- **Core concept:** An ancient weapon known as a **Keris** (a traditional Indonesian wavy-bladed dagger) granted human form through a mystical ritual performed by her master.
- **Influences:** Wayang/Kris mythology — the keris is a culturally significant weapon in Indonesian (Javanese/Malay) folklore, often imbued with spiritual power. Her design evokes shadow puppet (wayang) aesthetics and a mystical "baleful whisper" princess archetype.
- **Personality described in official lore:** She is "almost always sleeping or playing games." Having lived alone for so long, she tends to be apathetic toward things and people she is uninterested in. However, when talking about things she likes, she can be quite childlike and passionate. Her base personality is apathetic, especially toward humans — "Don't let looks fool you — that's what every foolish human always does to themselves somehow."
- **Fan name:** Melfriends
- **Hashtags:** `#Liveissa` (live streams), `#anyatelier` (fan art)
- **Fan mark / motif:** Sleep/autumn — her favorite season is autumn because "it's the best season to sleep during!"

## 5. Official Lore / Setting Description

From the official hololive website (hololivepro.com):

> *"An ancient weapon known as 'Keris,' she was granted human form through a mystical ritual performed by her master. She's just about always sleeping or playing games. Living mostly alone for so long has made her tend to be apathetic towards things and people she's uninterested in, but when talking about things she likes, she can be quite childlike and passionate. Her base personality is actually apathetic, especially towards humans. Don't let looks fool you — that's what every foolish human always does to themselves somehow."*

Her tagline / greeting: **"Good day! This is Anya Melfissa from hololive ID 2nd Generation."**

She describes herself as an idol (with humorous reluctance in her Q&A).

## 6. Name Origin

- **Anya:** A common Slavic-derived name (diminutive of Anna), meaning "grace" or "favor." Widely used across many cultures including in Indonesia.
- **Melfissa:** Likely a constructed/portmanteau name. The "-fissa" root may derive from Latin *fissa* (split/crack) or play on "mellifluous" (sweet-sounding). It evokes a mystical, lyrical quality fitting her keris/spirit theme.
- The full name "Anya Melfissa" flows with a soft, melodic cadence that matches her "baleful whisper" princess archetype.

## 7. Miscellaneous Details

- **Dream:** "To work hard and convince YAGOO to make a personal and BIG sleep place that I can sleep at without any care about the outside world."
