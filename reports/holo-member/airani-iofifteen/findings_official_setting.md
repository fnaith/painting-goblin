## 2. Character Design Theme & Lore Backstory

### Lore (Official)
An **alien princess** who ran away from home and came to Earth. She fell in love with Earth's culture and successfully enrolled in a university to study **Virtual Communication and Designs**. She is described as a "smart alien" who sometimes teases viewers and acts as a caring "Iomama" when her fans need it.

### Character Design Theme
- **Theme:** Alien / Space motif fused with a colorful, artistic aesthetic
- **Design elements:** Space-themed accessories, star patterns, futuristic alien-inspired outfit
- **Illustrator:** Yano Mitsuki (Twitter: @mituk1)
- **Live2D Modeling:** Taniya Raku (Twitter: @taniya001)
- **Oshi Mark / Emoji:** 🎨 (artist palette)

### Debut Date
- **April 12, 2020** — YouTube debut and first uploaded video
- **Birthday:** July 15 (the "15" in her name references July 15)
- **Height:** 150 cm (ongoing gag that it's actually 145cm with 5cm shoes)

## 3. Official Personality Traits, Catchphrases & Motto

### Personality (Official)
- Cheerful, energetic, and talkative
- Described as having a "comfy" streaming atmosphere
- Strong comedic side; playful and flirtatious (earning the nickname **"Erofi"** )
- Acts as a caring "Iomama" — supportive and maternal toward fans
- Gets "crazy over gacha games" and rhythm games
- Multilingual: speaks **Indonesian, Japanese, English, German, Korean, and "Alienish"**
- Has **rhotacism** (difficulty pronouncing /r/ sounds)

### Real-life Background (from interviews)
- Was **homeschooled**; her sister was her only close friend growing up
- Became a VTuber to gain more friends
- Self-taught Japanese and English through anime, video games, and dramas
- Can write in Hiragana and Katakana; still learning Kanji
- Prefers her name spelled completely in lowercase: **"iofi"**
- Has suffered from **tonsillitis** and has been reluctant to have surgery
