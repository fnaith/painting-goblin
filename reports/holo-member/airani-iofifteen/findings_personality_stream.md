## 1. Personality Traits

### Cheerful & Energetic
Iofi has a consistently cheerful, energetic, and talkative presence throughout her streams. She is quite open about her thoughts and feelings. [Source: Hololive Fan Wiki]

### Comedic & Playful ("Erofi")
- Has a strong comedic side, often playing along with or against viewers
- Among hololive ID, she is known as one of the most "down bad" members, capable of creating very provoking but comical situations
- Fans often call her **"Erofi"** due to her flirtatious antics, contrasting with the wholesome advice she gives others
- Openly friends with R-18 artist Pochimaru, leading to the nickname
- Despite this, she aims to keep her content "Seiso" (pure/wholesome) and suitable for younger viewers

### Caring & Supportive ("Iomama")
- Shifts between caring/motherly ("Iomama") and flirtatious during streams
- Frequently posts short videos encouraging viewers
- Very vocal and responsive to chat during streams
- Described as "incredibly supportive and social" with a compassionate nature

### Anxious/Prone to Panic
- Prone to panicking when things don't go in her favor or unexpected events happen on streams
- Particularly sensitive about her height — Moona has teased her for being short
- Can get frustrated with gacha games and rhythm games, staying on the same song repeatedly until satisfied

### Workaholic & Humble
- Has difficulty accepting compliments — when she praises chat, they often turn around and compliment her back
- Known to be a hard worker who pushes herself

### Key Quotes
- "IOFORIA~! OBISA!" (Catchphrase — "OBISA" = "Oh, I can do it!")
- "Bodo!" (Indonesian slang)
- "It should have been me!"

## 2. Streaming Content & Style

### Stream Vibes
- Streams are often described as **calm and "comfy"**
- Mixes gameplay with chatting with viewers
- Loves **free talk streams** — chats are varied and often choose topics from viewers
- Known to host **drawing streams** where she teaches viewers while drawing fellow Hololive members or herself

### Content Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Free Talk / Just Chatting** | Frequent, highly interactive with chat |
| **Drawing Streams** | Teaches art, draws Hololive members; hosts "#holoctober" drawing events |
| **Gacha Games** | "NO GACHA NO LIFE!" — plays *Arknights*, *BanG Dream!*, *THE iDOLM@STER* |
| **Rhythm Games** | *osu!*, *BanG Dream!*, stays on one song for full combo |
| **Karaoke/Singing** | Occasional karaoke, includes a cappella |
| **ASMR** | Relaxing ASMR streams, including "Salonpas ASMR" |
| **Art Tutorials** | Uses hashtag #iorials for art tutorials |
| **Language Streams** | Has taught viewers languages (Indonesian, Japanese, English, etc.) |
| **3D Morning Greetings** | Daily 3D model tweets in the morning |

### Specialty Streams
- **"Area 15"** — Monthly collab with genmates Ayunda Risu and Moona Hoshinova
- **"IoSoRadio"** — Monthly otaku radio show with Tokino Sora
- **"Kira Kira Family"** — Collab group with Risu, Aragami Oga, Arurandeisu
- **"HoloFOI"** — Collab with Shirakami Fubuki and Oga

### Multilingual Streaming
- Speaks **6 languages**: Indonesian, Sundanese, English, Japanese, German, Korean
- Also learning sign language
- Self-taught Japanese and English through anime, video games, and dramas
- Often greets in German ("guten tag") and Korean ("gomawo")
- Explains that she learned languages because "it's fun and I was bored"

### Stream Rules (from descriptions)
- Be nice to fellow viewers, use polite language
- No OOT (out of topic) discussion
- No emoji spam
- No swearing, bashing, or foul language
- No discussion in waiting room
- No mentioning other VTubers unless the streamer starts the topic

## 3. Talents, Skills & Hobbies Outside Streaming

### Artistic Skills
- **Skilled digital artist** — studied Virtual Communication and Design (VCD) at university
- Designed her own costume concepts and the IoSoRadio logo
- Drew her 3rd and 4th costume concepts herself
- Can illustrate at a professional level, frequently shares fan art
- Hosted "#holoctober" drawing event with 31 daily themes

### Linguistic Ability
- Polyglot: Indonesian (native), Sundanese (regional dialect), English (fluent), Japanese (fluent), German (conversational), Korean (learning)
- Uses her multilingual ability as a "bridge" between personalities — helped connect Usada Pekora with Moona, and Yukoku Roberu with Ono Yuuki

### Music
- Original songs: "Bersama Ioforia", "Q&A=E", "Here I Am", "HI:STREET TIME"
- Group songs: "id:entity voices", "HI-15", "Dramatic XViltration", "Senandung Jiwa", "Juara Khatulistiwa"
- First cover was "Alien Alien" by NayutalieN
- Performed songs in sign language during 3D showcase

### Gaming
- Loves **rhythm games** (osu!, BanG Dream!)
- Loves **gacha games** ("NO GACHA NO LIFE!")
- Big fan of **Monster Hunter** franchise — frequently tries to get Hololive members into "Moonhun" collabs
- Loves **visual novels**
- Dislikes FPS games and Minecraft
- Favorite anime: **One Piece**

### Other Hobbies
- Passionate about **beauty and skincare**
- Loves **voice actors** and anime
- Uses **Salonpas** pain relief patches heavily (jokes about it, has done "Salonpas ASMR")

## 4. Voice Characteristics & Appeal

- Has **rhotacism** — difficulty pronouncing "R" sounds, which fans find endearing
- Has suffered from tonsillitis but has been reluctant to have surgery
- Voice is described as cute and energetic
- Switches between Indonesian, English, and Japanese mid-sentence
- Uses the catchphrase **"OBISA!"** (from "Oh, bisa!" — "Oh, it works!")
- Sign-off: **"Otsulonpas"** (portmanteau of "Otsukare" and "Salonpas")

- IOFORIA is known to be a warm, supportive community

- Fans affectionately call her "Erofi" and "Iomama" depending on context
- Her community enjoys her mix of wholesome advice and flirtatious teasing

### Collab Culture
- Very active collab partner — bridges gaps between different VTuber groups
- Close with genmates Risu and Moona (Area 15)
- Monthly show "IoSoRadio" with Tokino Sora

- Part of "Fanfic Club" with Pavolia Reine, Gigi Murin, and Shiori Novella
- Referred to as "tante Iofi" (Auntie Iofi) by Pavolia Reine due to friendship with Pochimaru
