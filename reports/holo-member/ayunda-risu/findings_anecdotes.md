## 2. Notable Collaborations

### 2.1 AREA 15 (Hololive Indonesia 1st Gen)

The three members of Hololive ID Gen 1 — Ayunda Risu, Moona Hoshinova, and Airani Iofifteen — share a special bond known as **AREA 15**.

**Family dynamic (lore/fanon):**
- **Airani Iofifteen** = Dad (the responsible artist)
- **Moona Hoshinova** = Mom (the caring but strict one)
- **Ayunda Risu** = Child (the energetic, mischievous squirrel)

**Key collab events:**
- **Monthly Area 15 streams** — Every 15th of the month. A long-running tradition.
- They share a dormitory (in lore), reinforcing the family setup.
- Risu and Moona have a close rivalry dynamic.
- Iofi often draws art of the three together.

### 2.2 Hololive Meet Ambassador

Risu was selected as one of three **Hololive Meet Ambassadors**, alongside **Tokino Sora** and **Gawr Gura**.

### 2.3 Guest Appearances

### 2.4 Cross-Branch Interactions

- She expressed a strong desire to collab with **Inugami Korone**.

## 3. Memorable Moments & Inside Jokes

### 3.1 The Piggy Bank Incident (July 2020)

One of the most famous early Risu moments:
- Risu streamed counting loose coins.
- **Moona raided** and jokingly claimed the coins were from her piggy bank.
- Became a running gag whenever Risu mentions money.

### 3.2 Non-stop Nut November (NNN)

- Parody series started in 2020.
- Wholesome content about the importance of nuts (cashews, almonds, etc.).
- Nut-themed facts, challenges, and taste tests.
- A yearly tradition.

### 3.3 Voice DLCs

Risu can switch between multiple voice modes at will:

| Voice | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Risu** | Default | High-pitched cute squirrel voice |
| **Wisu** | Child mode | Even higher-pitched, childish voice |
| **Ayunda** | Witch mode | Deep, mature voice |
| **Wisnu** | Ikemen mode | Handsome male voice |

This vocal range is one of her most celebrated skills.

### 3.4 Bonk

- **Bonk** is her catchphrase for kicking/muting misbehaving chat viewers.
- Chat playfully baits bonks by posting memes.

### 3.5 The Warden & Prisuners

- **Warden** — Nickname from strict chat moderation.
- **Prisuners** — YouTube membership name (she is warden, members are prisoners).

### 3.6 BRR

- **BRR** is Risu's signature flustered noise.
- A trilling sound like a squirrel's chattering.

### 3.7 Cactus Language

- Uses **cactus** as a substitute for swearing to maintain a seiso image.

### 3.8 Seiso Degenerate

- Describes her ideal fan as a **Seiso Degenerate** (wholesome outside, chaotic inside).

### 3.9 Favorite Nut: Cashews

- Her stated favorite nut is **cashews**.

### 3.10 ALiCE&u / Alice Name Origin

- Risu once stated she originally planned to name herself **Alice**.
- This is hinted in her debut original song title **ALiCE&u** (ALiCE = Alice).

### 3.11 Korone & Okayu's Child (Running Joke)

- A popular fan joke that Risu is the child of **Inugami Korone** (dog) and **Nekomata Okayu** (cat) — a cat and dog raising a squirrel (rodent) daughter.

### 3.12 4-Ears Confirmation

- In a Q&A, Risu confirmed her in-universe design includes **4 ears**: 2 human ears + 2 squirrel ears on top of her head.

## 4. IRL Backstory & Biography Details

### 4.1 In-Character Lore

Official backstory (from Hololive website):

> **A squirrel girl who came from another world. She was lost and confused in the human world,
> but a kind older sister took her in and took care of her. To repay the favor, she became a VTuber.**

Key elements:
- Originates from a **magical forest** in another world.
- Classic fish out of water isekai setup.
- A kind **Onee-chan (older sister)** took her in.
- Became a VTuber to repay kindness and spread happiness.

### 4.2 Name Etymology

- **Ayunda** — From Ayu meaning **pretty/beautiful** in Bahasa Indonesia.
- **Risu** — From the **ruddy tree shrew** (*Tupaia splendidula*).
- **Full meaning:** Pretty Squirrel

### 4.3 Notable Facts

- **Trilingual:** Indonesian (primary), English, Japanese.
- Her Japanese improved dramatically over her career.
- Acts as a **bridge between Hololive ID, EN, and JP** audiences.
- Considered one of the strongest vocalists in Hololive ID.
