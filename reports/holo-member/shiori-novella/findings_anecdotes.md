## 1. Character Backstory & Lore

### The Archiver

Shiori Novella is **"The Archiver"** -- she turns her favorite stories and treasured memories into bookmarks and saves them. Her catchphrase is:

> *"Don't you think that's a wonderful story?"*

Driven by an insatiable thirst for knowledge, Shiori was imprisoned after she was discovered to have obtained forbidden knowledge hidden within one of her stories. Rather than being frightened by her imprisonment, she saw it as merely another fascinating story. To continue her search for more stories, she planned and executed a prison break -- and she was the **mastermind** behind the entire -Advent- prison break, freeing her fellow genmates from "The Cell," a secret prison deep underground built to contain dangerous beings.

### Key Lore Details

- **The Cell:** All -Advent- members were sealed away in a prison deep underground, built to hold dangerous people and things. Its location is a closely guarded secret.
- **Two-Tone Hair:** Her hair was originally all black, but part of it turned grey from the stress of acquiring too much forbidden knowledge.
- **Glowing Eyes:** Her eyes can glow like a flashlight, allowing her to see in the dark -- another consequence of discovering forbidden knowledge.
- **Nerissa's Horn:** Shiori knows the location of Nerissa Ravencroft's missing horn, a recurring piece of lore between them.
- **Beatrice Niori World Destroyer Novella:** Shiori and Nerissa have a fictional "daughter" together. Shiori claims she is not sure how this happened. Beatrice also has an older brother who remains largely unknown.
- **Narrator:** Shiori served as the narrator for all -Advent- members' introductory lore videos.

### Runes & Debut PV

The golden runes in the *Wanted!* debut promotional video use Anglo-Saxon runes (used in England from the 5th to 11th centuries). The runes spell out Shiori's official English profile text. A book in the PV reads:

> *"In the age of the gods, monsters were sealed in this land after a battle. The impact created by myths, councils and other supernatural beings rocked the seals of [...] and stirred their souls. Its fetal movement frees them from their bondage. Imprisoned in an empty and empty prison, the monsters pledged rebellion and glory in the new land."*

## 2. Personality & Streaming Style

### Core Personality

Shiori is best described as **cheerful and dorky**. She is kind-hearted but frequently teases those she is close with, especially her genmates. Her streams are generally cozy and chill, though she gets excited easily.

### The Infamous Tangents

Shiori is **legendary** among fans for her *tangents* -- rambling discussions about whatever strange topics pop into her head. These can range from:
- Childhood traumas
- Pregnancy
- Cannibalism
- Parasites
- Anatomy and chemistry

Fans joke that her manager ("Henmama") is losing sleep and sanity trying to keep Shiori from being *too* unhinged. Her membership channel includes a **"bonk" emote** specifically for viewers to use when they think she deserves to be reined in.

### Tech-Savvy Creator

Shiori is notably tech-savvy. She creates her own stream titles, thumbnails, overlays, intros, and outros. She has dabbled in game development using RPG Maker, Ren'Py, Unity, and Twine. She produces highly edited videos including stream highlights, vlogs, and prerecorded content.

### Notable Quirks

- She sometimes **eats with scissors**.
- She suffers from **insomnia and headaches**.
- She used to own **over 30 porcelain dolls**.
- She typically doesn't wake up until **at least noon**.
- She takes pictures of **insects** for fun.
- She can **sight-read music** and plays piano.
- Despite her unhinged persona, she is known as having **the sweetest heart** among holomems, with a soft spot for animals and a habit of replying to fan encouragement.

## 6. Mascots & Fan Culture

### Yorick
Shiori's mascot is named **Yorick** -- a small black sphere with two long arms, four long spikes on its head, and a perpetually sad face with two eyes and a mouth. It can be seen on her right shoulder in official art. The name is a reference to Shakespeare's *Hamlet* ("Alas, poor Yorick").

### Nyakuza
Shiori also introduced a pair of cat mascot characters called **Nyakuza**.

### Novelites (Fan Name)
Her fans are known as **Novelites**, pronounced "novel-eets" -- likely a portmanteau of "novel" and "elites." The name reflects her book/archiving theme.

### Membership Tiers
Shiori's YouTube channel has three membership tiers:
1. **Page** -- entry level
2. **Bookwrym** (intentional/accidental typo of "Bookwyrm," a pun on "bookworm" + "wyrm," a type of dragon) -- mid tier
3. **Novelknights** -- top tier

The membership channel includes a **"bonk" emote** for viewers to use when they think Shiori deserves to be reined in during her unhinged tangents.
